# §39 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yangming disease with profuse sweating: because the fluids go outward, the stomach is dry and the stool will certainly be hard; hardness brings delirious speech. It belongs to Xiao Chengqi tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 213 of the yangming chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
