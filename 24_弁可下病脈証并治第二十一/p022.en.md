# §22 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease of six or seven days with the exterior pattern still present, a feeble deep pulse, and, on the contrary, no chest bind, when the person is manic: because the heat is in the lower jiao, the lower abdomen should be hard and full; when urination is spontaneously free, purging the blood cures it. The reason is that the Taiyang follows the meridian and stagnant heat is in the interior. It is appropriate to purge it with Didang tang (Teitoto).

## Notes

The same content as Clause 124 of the middle Taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
