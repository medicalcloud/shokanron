# §16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

After cold damage, the pulse is deep. Deep means internal excess. Purging resolves it; Da Chaihu tang (Daisaikoto) is appropriate.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

"After cold damage" means the exterior has already resolved. A deep pulse means the interior is not yet harmonious. Give Da Chaihu tang to purge the internal excess. The classic says: "When after recovery from cold damage there is fever again and the pulse is deep and excess, resolve it by purgation." [The commentary's "巳" is read as "已."]

## Notes

The clause states that a deep pulse remaining after the exterior pattern of cold damage has resolved is interior excess, purged with Da Chaihu tang. It relates to Clause 394 of the post-recovery chapter.
