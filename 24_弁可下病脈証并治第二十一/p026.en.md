# §26 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In a yangming pattern, when the person is forgetful, there is certainly blood amassment. The reason is that there is long-standing static blood, causing forgetfulness. Although the stool is hard, defecation is, on the contrary, easy, and its color will certainly be black. It is appropriate to purge with Didang tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 237 of the yangming chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
