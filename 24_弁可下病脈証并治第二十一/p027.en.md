# §27 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Sweating (one version writes "lying") with delirious speech is because there is dry stool in the stomach; this is wind. When it must be purged, it may be purged only after the channels have been passed. If it is purged too early, speech will certainly be disordered, because the exterior is vacuous and the interior replete. Purging cures it; Da Chaihu tang or Da Chengqi tang is appropriate.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 217 of the yangming chapter (this paragraph also names Da Chaihu tang). Cheng Wuji's commentary is found there.
