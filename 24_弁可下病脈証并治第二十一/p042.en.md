# §42 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yangming disease with tidal fever and slightly hard stool may be given Da Chengqi tang; if not hard, it must not be given. If there has been no bowel movement for six or seven days and dry stool is feared, the way to know is to give a little Xiao Chengqi tang; if after the decoction enters the abdomen flatus is passed, there is dry stool, and then it may be attacked. If no flatus is passed, only the head of the stool is hard and the rest will certainly be sloppy; it must not be attacked; attacking it will certainly cause distension and inability to eat. If one wants to drink water, giving water will cause hiccup. If fever arises afterward, the stool will certainly become hard and scant again; it is appropriate to harmonize it with Xiao Chengqi tang. If no flatus is passed, be careful not to attack.
