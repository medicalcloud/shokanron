# §14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Diarrhea with, on the contrary, a slippery pulse: there is something to be removed; purging cures it; Da Chengqi tang is appropriate.

## Notes

The clause judges diarrhea from abiding food by a slippery pulse and uses purgation. Cheng Wuji's commentary is recorded in the second half of the commentary on paragraph 12. The same text appears in the *Jingui Yaolüe*.
