# §40 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yangming disease with heart vexation, not having been treated by vomiting or purgation: it belongs to Tiaowei Chengqi tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 207 of the yangming chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
