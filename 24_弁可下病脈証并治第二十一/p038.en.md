# §38 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cold damage of six or seven days with chest bind and heat excess, a deep tight pulse, pain below the heart, and stony hardness on pressure: it belongs to Da Xianxiong tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 135 of the lower Taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
