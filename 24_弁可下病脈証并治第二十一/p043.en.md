# §43 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yangming disease with delirious speech, tidal fever, and a slippery racing pulse: Xiao Chengqi tang governs it. Accordingly give 1 sheng of Chengqi tang; if qi turns over in the abdomen, take another sheng; if qi does not turn over, do not give more. If the next day there is again no bowel movement and the pulse is, on the contrary, faint and rough, the interior is vacuous; it is difficult to treat, and Chengqi tang must not be given again.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 214 of the yangming chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
