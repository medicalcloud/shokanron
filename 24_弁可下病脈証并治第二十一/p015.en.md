# §15 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When abdominal fullness does not diminish, or diminishes too little to mention, it should be purged; Da Chaihu tang or Da Chengqi tang is appropriate.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 255 of the yangming chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
