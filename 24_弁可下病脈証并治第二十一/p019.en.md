# §19 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the pulse is doubly wiry and slow, there will certainly be hardness below the heart. When the pulse is large and tight, there is yin within yang; it may be purged; Da Chengqi tang is appropriate.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The *Jingui Yaolüe* says: "A doubly wiry pulse is cold." The classic says: "Slow means [the disease] is in the viscera." A doubly wiry and slow pulse means yang lies hidden within yin, so there will certainly be hardness below the heart. Large is yang; tight is cold. A large tight pulse means yin lies hidden within yang. Give Da Chengqi tang to separate yin and yang.

## Notes

The clause judges, from the combination of pulses, states in which yin and yang lie hidden within each other, and uses purgation. It is not found in the three yin and three yang chapters, and its meaning leaves room for interpretation.
