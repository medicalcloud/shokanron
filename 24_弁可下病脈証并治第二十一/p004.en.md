# §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyin disease, two or three days after contracting it, with dry mouth and parched throat: purge urgently; Da Chengqi tang is appropriate.

## Notes

The same text as Clause 320 of the shaoyin chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
