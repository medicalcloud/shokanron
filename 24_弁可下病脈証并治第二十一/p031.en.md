# §31 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Two or three days after contracting the disease, the pulse is weak, there is no taiyang or Chaihu pattern, there are vexation and agitation, and glomus below the heart. By the fourth or fifth day, although one can eat, give a little Chengqi tang to harmonize slightly and bring slight relief. By the sixth day, give 1 sheng of Chengqi tang. If there is no bowel movement for six or seven days and urine is scant, although there is no bowel movement, only the head of the stool is hard and the rest will certainly be sloppy; hard stool has not yet formed, and attacking it will certainly produce sloppy stool. One must wait until urination is free and the stool is definitely hard, and then it may be attacked; Da Chengqi tang is appropriate. (One version says Da Chaihu tang.)

## Notes

The same content as Clause 251 of the yangming chapter (Clause 251 reads "hardness below the heart"). Cheng Wuji's commentary is found there.
