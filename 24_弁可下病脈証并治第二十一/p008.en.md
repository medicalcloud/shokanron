# §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Diarrhea with a slow slippery pulse is internal repletion; the diarrhea is not about to stop. It should be purged; Da Chengqi tang is appropriate.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The classic says: "A slow pulse is contracted from eating dry things." The *Jingui Yaolüe* says: "Slippery means grain qi is replete." Diarrhea with a slow slippery pulse means abiding food in the stomach. When the spleen and stomach are damaged by food and do not digest water and grain, causing diarrhea, it is internal repletion. If only center-warming, intestine-thickening medicines are used, the diarrhea will certainly not stop. Give Da Chengqi tang to purge away the abiding food, and the diarrhea will stop by itself.

## Notes

The clause states that diarrhea from abiding food is internal repletion, and removing the cause by purgation also stops the diarrhea (an example of "treating flow by promoting flow"). It is not found in the three yin and three yang chapters.
