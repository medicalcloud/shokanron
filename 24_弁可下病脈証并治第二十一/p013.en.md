# §13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the disease has fullness and pain in the abdomen, this is repletion; it should be purged; Da Chengqi tang or Da Chaihu tang is appropriate.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The *Jingui Yaolüe* says: "When the patient's abdomen is full and not painful on pressure, it is vacuity; when painful, repletion, and it may be purged." Abdominal fullness with pain means the interior qi is congested and replete, so it may be purged.

## Notes

The clause states that abdominal fullness with tenderness is a repletion pattern indicated for purgation. It shows judging vacuity and repletion by abdominal palpation.
