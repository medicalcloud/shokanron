# §35 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cold damage with fever unresolved by sweating, glomus and hardness in the heart, and vomiting with diarrhea: it belongs to Da Chaihu tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 165 of the lower taiyang chapter (Clause 165 reads "below the heart"). Cheng Wuji's commentary is found there.
