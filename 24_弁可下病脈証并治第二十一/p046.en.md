# §46 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

After great purgation, with no bowel movement for six or seven days, unresolved vexation, and abdominal fullness and pain, there is dry stool. The reason is that there was originally abiding food. It belongs to Da Chengqi tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 241 of the yangming chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found. It is the last paragraph of the chapter "Distinguishing Pulse, Signs, and Treatment of Diseases That May Be Purged."
