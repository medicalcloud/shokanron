# §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Diarrhea with the pulses at all three positions even, and hardness below the heart on pressure: purge urgently; Da Chengqi tang (Daijokito) is appropriate.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

With diarrhea the pulse should be feeble with cold limbs. Now, on the contrary, it is harmonious; this is internal excess. Diarrhea with even pulses at the three positions is already excess; when, further, there is hardness below the heart on pressure, the pathogen is severe. Therefore it is appropriate to purge with Da Chengqi tang. [The commentary's "巳" is read as "已."]

## Notes

The clause states that even with diarrhea, if the pulse has not weakened and there is hard resistance below the heart, it is internal excess and should be purged urgently. It is not found in the three yin and three yang chapters; a similar clause appears in the *Jingui Yaolüe* chapter on vomiting, hiccup, and diarrhea.
