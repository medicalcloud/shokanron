# §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Diarrhea with the pulses at all three positions even, and hardness below the heart on pressure: purge urgently; Da Chengqi tang is appropriate.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

With diarrhea the pulse should be faint with reversal. Now, on the contrary, it is harmonious; this is internal repletion. Diarrhea with even pulses at the three positions is already repletion; when, further, there is hardness below the heart on pressure, the evil is severe. Therefore it is appropriate to purge with Da Chengqi tang. [The commentary's "巳" is read as "已."]

## Notes

The clause states that even with diarrhea, if the pulse has not weakened and there is hard resistance below the heart, it is internal repletion and should be purged urgently. It is not found in the three yin and three yang chapters; a similar clause appears in the *Jingui Yaolüe* chapter on vomiting, hiccup, and diarrhea.
