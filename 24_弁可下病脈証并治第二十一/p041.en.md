# §41 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yangming disease with a slow pulse: when there is sweating but no aversion to cold, the body will certainly be heavy, with shortness of breath, abdominal fullness, and panting. When there is tidal fever, the external is about to resolve and the interior may be attacked. When sweat streams from the hands and feet, the stool is already hard; Da Chengqi tang governs it. If sweating is profuse with slight fever and aversion to cold, the external has not yet resolved; Guizhi tang governs it. When the fever is not tidal, Chengqi tang must not yet be given. If the abdomen is greatly full and blocked, give Xiao Chengqi tang to harmonize the stomach qi slightly; do not let it reach heavy draining.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 208 of the yangming chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
