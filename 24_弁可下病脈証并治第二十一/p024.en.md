# §24 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In cold damage with heat and lower-abdominal fullness, urination should be inhibited; now if, on the contrary, it is free, there is blood; it should be purged; Didang wan is appropriate.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 126 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
