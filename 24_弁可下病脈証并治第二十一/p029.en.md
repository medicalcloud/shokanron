# §29 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yangming disease with delirious speech, tidal fever, and, on the contrary, inability to eat: there are five or six pieces of dry stool in the stomach. If one can eat, it is only hard. It belongs to Da Chengqi tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 215 of the yangming chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
