# §21 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the patient has no exterior or interior pattern and has had fever for seven or eight days, even if the pulse is floating and rapid, it may be purged; Da Chaihu tang is appropriate.

## Notes

The same content as the first half of Clause 257 of the yangming chapter (this paragraph names Da Chaihu tang). Cheng Wuji's commentary is found there.
