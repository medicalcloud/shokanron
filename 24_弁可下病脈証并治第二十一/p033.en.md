# §33 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When taiyang disease does not resolve and heat binds in the bladder, the person is as if manic; if blood descends spontaneously, one in whom it descends recovers. When the external has not yet resolved, it still must not be attacked; the external should first be resolved. Once the external has resolved and there is only tension and binding of the lower abdomen, it may be attacked; Taohe Chengqi tang is appropriate.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 106 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
