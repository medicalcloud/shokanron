# §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyin disease with diarrhea of clear water, pure green-blue in color, certain pain below the heart, and a dry mouth may be purged; Da Chaihu tang or Da Chengqi tang is appropriate.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 321 of the shaoyin chapter, which gives Da Chengqi tang with a note "one method uses Da Chaihu tang." Cheng Wuji's commentary is found there.
