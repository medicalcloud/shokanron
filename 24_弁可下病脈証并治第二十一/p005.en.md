# §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyin disease of six or seven days with abdominal fullness and no bowel movement: purge urgently; Da Chengqi tang is appropriate.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 322 of the shaoyin chapter (Clause 322 reads "abdominal distension"). Cheng Wuji's commentary is found there.
