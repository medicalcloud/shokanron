# §25 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yangming disease with fever and sweating: this is heat passing out, so yellowing cannot arise. When there is sweating only on the head, none on the body, stopping at the neck, inhibited urination, and thirst for water and drink, stagnant heat is in the interior, and the body will certainly turn yellow; it is appropriate to purge it with Yinchenhao tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 236 of the yangming chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
