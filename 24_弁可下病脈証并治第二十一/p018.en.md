# §18 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When Taiyang disease has not resolved and the pulse is stopped at both yin and yang (one version writes "feeble"), there will certainly first be shivering and shuddering, and sweating resolves it. When only the yin pulse is feeble (one version writes "the chi pulse is in excess"), purgation resolves it; Da Chaihu tang (Daisaikoto) is appropriate. (One method uses Tiaowei Chengqi tang [Choijokito].)

## Notes

The same content as Clause 94 of the middle Taiyang chapter (Clause 94 gives Tiaowei Chengqi tang). Cheng Wuji's commentary is found there.
