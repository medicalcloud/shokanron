# §44 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Overlap disease of the two yang in which the Taiyang pattern has ceased, with only tidal fever, moist sweating from the hands and feet, difficult stool, and delirium: purging cures it; Da Chengqi tang (Daijokito) is appropriate. [The base has a stop between the two characters of "漐漐," but it is a single word.]

## Notes

The same content as Clause 220 of the Yangming chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
