# §44 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Dual-yang combination disease in which the taiyang pattern has ceased, with only tidal fever, moist sweating from the hands and feet, difficult stool, and delirious speech: purging cures it; Da Chengqi tang is appropriate. [The base has a stop between the two characters of "漐漐," but it is a single word.]

## Notes

The same content as Clause 220 of the yangming chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
