# §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

As a general rule, purgation is appropriate in autumn.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

In autumn yang qi moves downward, and the evil is also below, so purgation is appropriate.

## Notes

The general rule at the beginning of the chapter "Distinguishing Pulse, Signs, and Treatment of Diseases That May Be Purged." Paired with "sweating is appropriate in spring and summer" of the "May Be Sweated" chapter and "vomiting is appropriate in spring" of the "May Induce Vomiting" chapter, it states that purgation suits autumn.
