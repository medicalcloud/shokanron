# §34 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In cold damage of seven or eight days, when the body is yellow like the color of a tangerine, urination is inhibited, and the abdomen is slightly full, it belongs to Yinchenhao tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 260 of the yangming chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
