# §36 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cold damage of more than ten days with heat bound in the interior and also alternating chills and fever: it belongs to Da Chaihu tang.

## Notes

The same content as the first half of Clause 136 of the lower taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
