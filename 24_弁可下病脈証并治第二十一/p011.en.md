# §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Diarrhea with no desire to eat is because there is abiding food; it should be purged; Da Chengqi tang is appropriate.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Damaged by food, one dislikes food, so there is no desire to eat—just as one damaged by wind dislikes wind and one damaged by cold dislikes cold.

## Notes

The clause attributes diarrhea with poor appetite to abiding food and uses purgation. The same text appears in the *Jingui Yaolüe*.
