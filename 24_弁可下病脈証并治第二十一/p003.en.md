# §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yangming disease with fever and profuse sweating: purge urgently; Da Chaihu tang is appropriate. (One method uses Xiao Chengqi tang.)

## Notes

Corresponds to Clause 253 of the yangming chapter, which gives Da Chengqi tang with a note "one version says Da Chaihu tang"; this paragraph gives Da Chaihu tang. Cheng Wuji's commentary is on Clause 253.
