# §20 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In chest binding, the nape is also stiff, like soft tetany; purging it brings harmony. (In the chest-binding section, Da Xianxiong wan is used.)

## Notes

The same content as the second half of Clause 131 of the lower taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
