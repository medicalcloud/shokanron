# §32 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang wind strike with diarrhea and retching counterflow: once the exterior has resolved, it may be attacked. When the person sweats moistly with attacks at fixed times, headache, glomus, hardness, and fullness below the heart pulling with pain below the rib-sides, shortness of breath with dry retching, and sweating without aversion to cold, the exterior has resolved but the interior is not yet harmonious; it belongs to Shizao tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 152 of the lower taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
