# §37 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When there is only chest binding without great heat, water is bound in the chest and rib-sides; when there is only slight sweating on the head, it belongs to Da Xianxiong tang.

## Notes

The same content as the second half of Clause 136 of the lower taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
