# §17 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In cold damage of six or seven days, when the vision is unclear, the eyes are not harmonious, there is no exterior or interior pattern, the stool is difficult, and the body has slight heat, this is repletion; purge urgently. Da Chengqi tang or Da Chaihu tang is appropriate.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 252 of the yangming chapter (this paragraph also names Da Chaihu tang). Cheng Wuji's commentary is found there.
