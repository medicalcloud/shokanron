# §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Combined disease of yangming and shaoyang will certainly have diarrhea. When the pulse is not defeated, it is favorable; when defeated, it is a loss. Mutual restraint and injury is called defeat. When the pulse is slippery and rapid, there is abiding food; it should be purged; Da Chengqi tang is appropriate.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 256 of the yangming chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
