# §23 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease with yellow body, a sunken bound pulse, hard and full lower abdomen, and inhibited urination is not [a blood pattern]. When urination is spontaneously free and the person is as if manic, the blood pattern is certain; it belongs to Didang tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 125 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
