# §45 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the patient's urination is inhibited, the stool is now difficult, now easy, there is periodic slight heat, and panting and veiling with inability to lie down, there is dry stool; it belongs to Da Chengqi tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 242 of the yangming chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
