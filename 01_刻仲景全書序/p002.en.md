# §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

An auspicious day in the third month of the jihai year of Wanli. Preface by Zhao Kaimei, Qingchang Daoren of Haiyu.

## Notes

The date and signature of the preface. The jihai year of Wanli corresponds to the 27th year of Wanli in the Ming (1599). "Gudan" means an auspicious day. Haiyu is an old name for Changshu.
