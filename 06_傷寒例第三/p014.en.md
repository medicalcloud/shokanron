# §14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

(Of the twenty-four qi, there are twelve nodes and twelve center qi. Five days make one period, and the qi are likewise, making seventy-two periods in all. To determine life or death in disease, one must thoroughly understand this.)

## Notes

A small-character note attached to the calendar table. It explains the calendar dividing the year into twenty-four qi (twelve nodes and twelve center qi), with five days as one period making seventy-two periods, and states that this is necessary for judging life or death in disease.
