# §24 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In dual contraction of cold, on the first day the taiyang receives it and is ill together with the shaoyin at once, so there are headache, dry mouth, vexation and fullness, and thirst. On the second day the yangming receives it and is ill together with the taiyin at once, so there are abdominal fullness, body heat, no desire to eat, and delirious speech (讝 pronounced zhi-lian, also nü-jian; likewise below). On the third day the shaoyang receives it and is ill together with the jueyin at once, so there are deafness, retraction of the scrotum with reversal; when water and drink cannot enter and one does not recognize people, death comes on the sixth day. If the three yin and three yang and the five viscera and six bowels all contract disease, the nutritive and defensive do not move, the viscera and bowels do not communicate, and one dies.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

When yin and yang are both diseased and exterior and interior both damaged, it is dual contraction. Because both yin and yang are affected, when ill the signs of both are seen together, and when transmitted through the channels, the yin and yang channels are transmitted together. On the first day, headache is the taiyang; dry mouth, vexation, fullness, and thirst are the shaoyin. By the second day the taiyang is transmitted to the yangming and the shaoyin also to the taiyin; body heat and delirious speech are the yangming; abdominal fullness and no desire to eat are the taiyin. By the third day the yangming is transmitted to the shaoyang and the taiyin also to the jueyin; deafness is the shaoyang; retraction of the scrotum with reversal is the jueyin. Water and drink not entering and not recognizing people mean the stomach qi does not pass. The *Neijing* says: "The five viscera are already damaged, the six bowels do not communicate, the nutritive and defensive do not move; why does death come only three days after this? Qibo said: The yangming is the eldest of the twelve channel vessels; its blood and qi are exuberant, so one does not recognize people; after three days its qi is exhausted, so one dies." This means that in three days the six channels are all diseased, the qi of the nutritive and defensive cannot move inside and outside, the qi of the viscera and bowels cannot communicate above and below, and by the sixth day the qi of the viscera and bowels is all exhausted and the qi of the nutritive and defensive all cut off, and then one dies. [The commentary's "𡵨伯" is read as a variant of "岐伯" (Qibo), and "巳傷" as "已傷."]

## Notes

The clause describes, based on the *Suwen* "Treatise on Heat," the course of "dual contraction," in which the exterior and interior channels are diseased together: on day 1 taiyang with shaoyin, on day 2 yangming with taiyin, on day 3 shaoyang with jueyin, and death on day 6.
