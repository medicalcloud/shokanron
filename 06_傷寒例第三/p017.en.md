# §17 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In general, damage by cold produces heat disease; although the heat may be severe, it is not fatal. If one is ill from dual contraction of cold, it is certainly fatal.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The *Neijing* says: "When wind and cold lodge in a person, they make all the fine hairs stand erect, and the skin closes and produces heat." This is cold damage producing heat disease. The *Zhenjing* says: "One with much heat recovers easily; one with much cold recovers with difficulty." This is "although the heat may be severe, it is not fatal."

When exterior and interior are both diseased, it is called dual contraction.

## Notes

A clause based on the *Suwen* "Treatise on Heat": "When people are damaged by cold, it produces heat disease; although the heat is severe they do not die; those ill from dual contraction of cold cannot escape death." "Dual contraction," in which the exterior and interior channels (such as taiyang and shaoyin) are diseased at once, is regarded as grave.
