# §29 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In general, when the diseases of dual contraction arise together, treatment has an order. Effusing the exterior and attacking the interior are fundamentally different. Yet those who cling to confusion and apply their own ideas say that Shendan and Gansui may be combined and drunk, both resolving the exterior and removing the interior. The words are clever and seem right, but the reasoning is in fact wrong. The conduct of the wise is always careful and cautious; the actions of the foolish are always rash and hasty. How can the change between safety and danger be falsified? The scholars of the world strive only for the glory of crowding [around the powerful] and do not see the ruin of this toppling danger. Only the enlightened can calmly protect their root; take it close at hand in one's own body—what is far about it?

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

When the diseases of dual contraction arise together, they are about to become incurable. The physician should greatly adjust, examine carefully the order of priority, and treat accordingly. One who recklessly attacks according to his own ideas seeking quick effects will surely bring about the ruin of toppling danger.

## Notes

The clause warns that even in dual contraction, where exterior and interior are both diseased, it is wrong to sweat and purge simultaneously, and the order of priority must be observed; it further laments that people pursue fame and profit and neglect their own bodies. It resonates with the purport of Zhongjing's own preface.
