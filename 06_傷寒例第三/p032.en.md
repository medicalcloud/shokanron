# §32 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In general, when one is ill and, on the contrary, can drink water, it is a disease about to recover. Those who do not understand disease, having merely heard that a sick person recovers by drinking water, forcibly give water to one with slight thirst, thereby causing calamity beyond counting.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Slight thirst means the heat in the abdomen is slight. If water is forcibly given, the water-rheum does not disperse and again becomes various rheum diseases.

## Notes

Continuing the previous paragraph, the clause states that being able to drink water is a sign of recovery, but misunderstanding this and forcing water on those with slight thirst causes water to collect and produce disease.
