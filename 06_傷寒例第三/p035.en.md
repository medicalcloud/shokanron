# §35 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

A pulse of four depletions dies in three days. When in the time a normal person breathes four times the patient's pulse arrives once, it is called four depletions.

## Notes

Paragraphs 35–37 are a series stating the degrees of the "depleted pulse," with an extremely low pulse count, and the time of death. This paragraph states that "four depletions," with only one beat in four breaths of a healthy person, is fatal in three days. Cheng Wuji's commentary is attached collectively to paragraph 37.
