# §37 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

A pulse of six depletions dies within one period (*shi*). When in the time a normal person breathes six times the patient's pulse arrives once, it is called six depletions.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

When the qi of four viscera is cut off, the pulse shows four depletions; when the qi of the five viscera is cut off, five depletions; when the five viscera and six bowels are all cut off, six depletions.

## Notes

"Six depletions," one beat in six breaths of a healthy person, is fatal within a short time. Cheng Wuji explains the number of depletions as corresponding to the number of viscera and bowels whose qi has been cut off.
