# §33 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In general, when one is ill and the pulse is stirred and rapid but after taking the decoction becomes slow, when the pulse is floating and large but diminishes and becomes small, and when one is at first agitated but later calm—these are all signs of recovery.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

A stirred rapid pulse means the evil is in yang; when the decoction enters and it changes to slow, the yang evil is healing. A floating large pulse means the evil is in the exterior; when it then diminishes and becomes small, the exterior evil has dissipated. Agitation and disorder at the beginning of illness mean vexation from the evil; becoming calm and quiet when the decoction enters means the medicine has overcome the disease. These are all signs of recovery.

## Notes

The clause states that when after taking medicine the pulse changes from fast to calm and from large to small, and an agitated patient becomes quiet, these are signs the medicine is working and recovery is coming. "厥" in "厥脈" is understood in the sense of "其" (its pulse).
