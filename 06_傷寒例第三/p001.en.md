# §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The method of determining disease by the four seasons, eight nodes, twenty-four qi, and seventy-two periods

## Notes

The title of the calendar table placed at the beginning of the chapter "Examples of Cold Damage." Paragraphs 2–13 form a table dividing the twenty-four solar terms of the year into the "node" and "center" (center qi) of each month, giving the direction pointed to by the handle of the Big Dipper (twenty-four directions by trigrams, heavenly stems, and earthly branches). Together with the small-character note of paragraph 14, it is placed as a basis for judging disease by whether the seasonal climate is regular or irregular. There is no Cheng Wuji commentary.
