# §36 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

A pulse of five depletions dies in one day. When in the time a normal person breathes five times the patient's pulse arrives once, it is called five depletions.

## Notes

Continuing the previous paragraph, "five depletions," one beat in five breaths of a healthy person, is fatal in one day.
