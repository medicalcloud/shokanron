# §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Beginning of Spring (node of the 1st month): the Dipper points to *gen*. Rain Water (center of the 1st month): it points to *yin*.

## Notes

One row of the calendar table. "Node" (*jie*) is the solar term at the beginning of the month, and "center" (*zhong*) is the center qi in the middle of the month. The month and season are fixed by the direction pointed to by the Dipper's handle.
