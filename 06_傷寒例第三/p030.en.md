# §30 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In general, for warming decoctions that induce sweating, although the formula says to take it three times a day, if the disease is severe and does not resolve, the intervals should be shortened, and the three doses may be finished within half a day. If it conflicts with the disease, this will be noticed at once. For one whose disease is heavy, observe for a full day and night; if after taking one preparation the disease signs are still present, the same decoction should be made again and taken. Some will not sweat until three preparations have been taken, and then it resolves. If sweat does not come out, it is a fatal disease.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Sweating medicines should be taken warm so that effusion and dissipation occur easily. Taking them three times a day keeps the medicine's force continuous. When the momentum of the disease is somewhat heavy, one should take it urgently to break the exuberant heat, and not be bound by the original formula. If the medicine does not match the disease, this is known as soon as the decoction enters: when a cool medicine is given to one with much yin, cold counterflow arises at once; when a warm preparation is drunk by one with much yang, heat toxin arises at once. This is "it will be noticed at once." *Zuishi* means a full cycle of time. Within one day and night, take the decoction to finish the preparation, then observe its transmission further; if the disease signs are still present, the same decoction should be made again to induce sweating. If after three preparations it does not resolve and sweat does not come out, the evil qi is extremely severe and the decoction cannot overcome it, and it will certainly become a grave illness. The *Qianjin* says: "In heat disease, when the pulse is agitated and exuberant and sweat cannot be obtained, this is the extreme of the yang pulse; it is fatal."

## Notes

Regarding the administration of sweating formulas, the clause states that if the illness is heavy, the intervals should be shortened; if after observing for a day and night it has not worked, the same formula is taken again; and if sweat does not come out even after three preparations, it is a fatal pattern. It has the same purport as the administration of Guizhi tang in the taiyang chapter (Clause 12).
