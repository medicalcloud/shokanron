# §34 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In general, in treating warm disease, one may needle the fifty-nine points. Also, the body has three hundred sixty-five points; of these, moxibustion at thirty is harmful, and needling at seventy-nine is calamitous, because both strike the marrow.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The fifty-nine points are for draining the warm heat of the channels. The *Zhenjing* says: "In heat disease, take the fifty-nine points of the yang; needle them to drain the heat and bring out sweat, fill the yin and supplement its insufficiency." The so-called fifty-nine needlings are: three on each of the inner and outer sides of both hands, twelve sites; one between each of the five fingers, eight sites; likewise on the feet; on the head, one cun into the hairline, three on each side three fen lateral, six sites; further three cun into the hairline, five on each side, ten sites; one each in front of and behind the ears and below the mouth, and one in the middle of the nape, six sites; one at the vertex, one at Xinhui, one at the hairline, one at Lianquan, two at Fengchi, two at Tianzhu. Also the *Neijing* says: "Of the fifty-nine heat points, the five rows on the head, five per row, drain the heat counterflow of the yang; Dazhu, Yingshu, Quepen, and Beishu—these eight drain heat in the chest; Qichong, Sanli, and the upper and lower Juxu—these eight drain heat in the stomach; Yunmen, Yugu, Weizhong, and Suikong—these eight drain heat of the four limbs; the five beside the shu points of the five viscera—these ten drain heat of the five viscera. All these fifty-nine points are on the left and right of heat."

The points number three hundred sixty-five, corresponding to one year. The prohibitions on moxibustion and needling all concern places where the flesh is thin over bone junctions and where the blood vessels are vacuous and scant; acupuncture or moxibustion there would also strike the marrow. [The commentary's "𩕄会" is read as "顖会" (Xinhui) and "䯞骨" as "髃骨" (Yugu); "鍼灸井中髄" is considered a miscopying of the text's "并中髄."]

## Notes

The clause states that the "fifty-nine heat-disease points" are used in needling for warm disease, and that there are points where moxibustion or needling is contraindicated. As shown in Cheng Wuji's commentary, there are two different traditions of the specific locations of the fifty-nine points, in the *Lingshu* chapter on heat disease and the *Suwen* treatise on water and heat points.
