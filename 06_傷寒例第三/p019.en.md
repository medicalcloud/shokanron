# §19 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When both chi and cun are long, the yangming has contracted disease; it should arise on the second or third day. Because its vessel flanks the nose and connects with the eyes, there are body heat, eye pain, dry nose, and inability to lie down.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The yangming has abundant blood and qi. Both chi and cun being long means the evil has merged with the yangming and blood and qi overflow. When the taiyang has contracted the evil and does not recover, it is transmitted to the yangming, so it should arise on the second or third day. "Its vessel flanks the nose": the yangming vessel starts at the nose, crosses at the root of the nose, and connects with the eyes; the yangming vessel goes straight up to the bridge of the nose and emerges again to connect with the eye system. Body heat is because the yangming governs the flesh of the body. The *Zhenjing* says: "When yangming qi is exuberant, the whole front of the body is hot." Eye pain and dry nose mean evil lodges in the channel. Inability to lie down means the stomach qi counterflows and cannot follow its path. The *Neijing* says: "When the stomach is not harmonious, one cannot lie at ease." [The commentary's "巳" is read as "已."]

## Notes

For the second day of six-channel transmission, the clause explains the pulse (long) and signs (body heat, eye pain, dry nose, sleeplessness) of yangming disease by the course of the yangming channel (nose, eyes).
