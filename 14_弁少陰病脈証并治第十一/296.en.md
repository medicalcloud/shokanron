# Clause 296 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyin disease with vomiting and diarrhea, agitation and vexation, and counterflow of the four limbs is fatal.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Vomiting and diarrhea mean the cold is severe in the interior. Counterflow cold of the four limbs means the cold is severe in the exterior. Agitation and vexation mean the yang qi is about to be cut off. Thus one knows it is fatal.

## Notes

When agitation and vexation appear in addition to vomiting, diarrhea, and counterflow cold of the limbs, it is the stirring of vacuous yang just before yang qi is exhausted, and it is regarded as a fatal pattern. It is distinguished from Clause 289's "periodic spontaneous vexation" (yang returning).
