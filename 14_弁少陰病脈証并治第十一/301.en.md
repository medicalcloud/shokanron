# Clause 301 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyin disease, when just contracted, with fever, on the contrary, and a sunken pulse: Mahuang Xixin Fuzi tang governs it. Formula 1.

Mahuang 2 liang (remove the nodes); xixin 2 liang; fuzi 1 piece (blast-fried, remove the skin, break into eight pieces).

Take the three ingredients above with 1 dou of water; first boil the mahuang to reduce by 2 sheng, remove the foam on top, add the other medicinals, boil to obtain 3 sheng, remove the dregs, and take 1 sheng warm, three times a day.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Shaoyin disease should have no fever and aversion to cold. Fever, on the contrary, means the evil is in the exterior. Although the pulse is sunken, since it has just been contracted the evil qi is not yet deep, so it too should be dissipated by sweating with a warming preparation.

(Nature and flavor) Mahuang: sweet, hot. Xixin: acrid, hot. Fuzi: acrid, hot.

The *Neijing* says: "When cold spreads within, treat with sweet-hot, assist with bitter-acrid, and moisten with acrid." The sweetness of mahuang resolves the shaoyin cold; the acridity of xixin and fuzi warms the shaoyin channel.

## Notes

For the early stage of shaoyin disease (yang vacuity, sunken pulse) accompanied by exterior fever (also explained as "dual contraction" of taiyang and shaoyin), fuzi warms the interior while mahuang and xixin resolve the exterior. It is generally known as "Mahuang Fuzi Xixin tang," but in this clause of the Song edition it is written "Mahuang Xixin Fuzi tang."
