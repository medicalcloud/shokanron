# Clause 321 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyin disease with spontaneous diarrhea of clear water, pure green-blue in color, certain pain below the heart, and a dry mouth may be purged; Da Chengqi tang is appropriate. 20. Use the nineteenth formula above. (One method uses Da Chaihu tang.)

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The shaoyin is kidney water. Green-blue is the color of the liver. Spontaneous diarrhea that is green-blue in color means liver evil is assailing the kidney. The *Nanjing* says: "What comes from in front is replete evil." Since replete evil is accumulated in the kidney, there will certainly be pain below the heart and a dry mouth. Da Chengqi tang is given to purge the replete evil.

## Notes

The second of the shaoyin "three patterns for urgent purgation." It is often interpreted as a state in which dry stool remains bound in the intestines and only foul watery stool flows out around it (later called "heat bind with circumfluence"). Even though there is diarrhea, pain below the heart and a dry mouth lead to judging it as repletion heat and purging.
