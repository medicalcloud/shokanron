# Clause 287 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyin disease with a tight pulse: when by the seventh or eighth day there is spontaneous diarrhea, the pulse suddenly becomes faint, the hands and feet are on the contrary warm, and the tightness of the pulse, on the contrary, departs, it is about to resolve. Even with vexation and diarrhea, it will certainly recover by itself.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Shaoyin disease with a tight pulse means the cold is severe. By the seventh or eighth day, the transmission through the channels is complete and it is the time of resolution. Spontaneous diarrhea with a suddenly faint pulse means the cold qi has been able to drain. If yin cold overcame the upright and the yang were vacuous and draining, the hands and feet would be reversal-cold and the tightness would not depart. Now the hands and feet are, on the contrary, warm and the tightness departs, so one knows the yang qi has returned and the cold qi has gone; therefore it is about to resolve. Diarrhea with vexation-agitation is adverse, but here the upright prevails and the evil is faint, so even with vexation and diarrhea it will certainly stop of itself.

## Notes

During the course of shaoyin disease, when the tight pulse departs with diarrhea and the hands and feet become warm, it is a favorable sign of yang qi returning and cold evil being expelled. It contrasts with the same diarrhea accompanied by persistent cold extremities (reversal) and tight pulse, which signals worsening.
