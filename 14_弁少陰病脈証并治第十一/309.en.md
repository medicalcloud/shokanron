# Clause 309 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyin disease with vomiting and diarrhea, counterflow cold of the hands and feet, and vexation and agitation so severe one feels about to die: Wuzhuyu tang governs it. Formula 8.

Wuzhuyu 1 sheng; renshen 2 liang; shengjiang 6 liang (cut); dazao 12 pieces (broken).

Boil the four ingredients above in 7 sheng of water to obtain 2 sheng, remove the dregs, and take 7 ge warm, three times a day.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Vomiting, diarrhea, and reversal cold of the hands and feet mean the yin cold qi is severe. Vexation and agitation as if about to die mean the yang qi is struggling within. Wuzhuyu tang is given to assist yang and dissipate cold.

## Notes

Although in the shaoyin chapter, the signs are vomiting and diarrhea, cold extremities, and severe vexation and agitation, and it is often interpreted as using Wuzhuyu tang mainly for violent vomiting due to stomach cold (upward counterflow of turbid yin). The signs resemble the fatal pattern of Clause 296 (vomiting and diarrhea, agitation and vexation, counterflow cold), but this clause is distinguished as a stage where yang qi is still struggling and treatment is possible. Wuzhuyu tang also appears in Clause 243 of the yangming chapter and Clause 378 of the jueyin chapter.
