# Clause 320 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyin disease, two or three days after contracting it, with dry mouth and parched throat: purge urgently. Da Chengqi tang is appropriate. Formula 10. [The formula number is "Formula 10" in the base, but from the following clauses' "use the nineteenth formula above," it corresponds to "Formula 19."]

Zhishi 5 pieces (mix-fried); houpo half a jin (remove the bark, mix-fried); dahuang 4 liang (washed with wine); mangxiao 3 ge.

Take the four ingredients above with 1 dou of water; first boil two of them to obtain 5 sheng, remove the dregs, add the dahuang, boil again to obtain 2 sheng, remove the dregs, add the mangxiao, put back on the fire and bring to a boil once or twice, and take warm in two divided doses. If one dose produces a bowel movement, stop the remaining doses.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

In cold damage transmitted through the channels, when the evil is transmitted to the shaoyin on the fifth or sixth day, the mouth is dry, the tongue parched, and there is thirst; this is the evil gradually deepening. Now in shaoyin disease two or three days after contracting it, when the evil qi has not yet entered deeply, dry mouth and parched throat appear at once; the evil heat is already severe and kidney water is drying. Da Chengqi tang is given urgently to purge it and preserve the kidney. [The commentary's "巳甚" is read as "已甚."]

## Notes

One of the shaoyin "three patterns for urgent purgation" (Clauses 320–322). When yangming dryness-heat is exuberant and rapidly consumes kidney yin (shaoyin), one purges urgently to preserve yin. It pairs with the yangming chapter's three patterns for urgent purgation (Clauses 252–254).
