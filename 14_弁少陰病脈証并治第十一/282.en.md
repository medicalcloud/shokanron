# Clause 282 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyin disease with a desire to vomit but no vomiting, heart vexation, and only a desire to sleep, and after five or six days spontaneous diarrhea with thirst, belongs to the shaoyin; because of vacuity one draws in water to rescue oneself. If the urine is white in color, the form of shaoyin disease is fully present. White urine is because the lower burner is vacuous and has cold and cannot control water, so the color is made white.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

A desire to vomit without vomiting and heart vexation mean the exterior evil has been transmitted inward. If there were abdominal fullness and pain it would belong to the taiyin; here there is only a desire to sleep, so one knows it belongs to the shaoyin. Five or six days is when the evil is transmitted to the shaoyin. Spontaneous diarrhea without thirst means cold in the middle burner, belonging to the taiyin; here spontaneous diarrhea with thirst means cold in the lower burner, belonging to the shaoyin. The kidney is vacuous and water is dried, so one is thirsty and draws in water to rescue oneself. The lower burner is vacuous and cold and cannot control water, so the urine is white in color. The classic says: "Diarrhea with a desire to drink water is because there is heat." Here, although there is thirst with diarrhea, the urine is white, so it is clearly not interior heat. This must be carefully examined.

## Notes

The clause describes the mechanism of diarrhea and thirst in shaoyin disease. Even shaoyin diarrhea may involve thirst, but this is not heat; it is because the yang qi of the lower burner (kidney) is vacuous and cannot move the fluids, and clear, copious urine is evidence of vacuity cold. It is read in contrast with taiyin's "spontaneous diarrhea without thirst" (Clause 277).
