# Clause 282 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyin disease with a desire to vomit but no vomiting, heart vexation, and only a desire to sleep, and after five or six days spontaneous diarrhea with thirst, belongs to the Shaoyin; because of deficiency one draws in water to rescue oneself. If the urine is white in color, the form of Shaoyin disease is fully present. White urine is because the lower jiao is deficient and has cold and cannot control water, so the color is made white.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

A desire to vomit without vomiting and heart vexation mean the exterior pathogen has been transmitted inward. If there were abdominal fullness and pain it would belong to the Taiyin; here there is only a desire to sleep, so one knows it belongs to the Shaoyin. Five or six days is when the pathogen is transmitted to the Shaoyin. Spontaneous diarrhea without thirst means cold in the middle jiao, belonging to the Taiyin; here spontaneous diarrhea with thirst means cold in the lower jiao, belonging to the Shaoyin. The kidney is deficient and water is dried, so one is thirsty and draws in water to rescue oneself. The lower jiao is deficient and cold and cannot control water, so the urine is white in color. The classic says: "Diarrhea with a desire to drink water is because there is heat." Here, although there is thirst with diarrhea, the urine is white, so it is clearly not interior heat. This must be carefully examined.

## Notes

The clause describes the mechanism of diarrhea and thirst in Shaoyin disease. Even Shaoyin diarrhea may involve thirst, but this is not heat; it is because the yang qi of the lower jiao (kidney) is deficient and cannot move the fluids, and clear, copious urine is evidence of deficiency cold. It is read in contrast with Taiyin's "spontaneous diarrhea without thirst" (Clause 277).
