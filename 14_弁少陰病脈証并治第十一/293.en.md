# Clause 293 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyin disease of eight or nine days, when the whole body, hands, and feet are all hot, because the heat is in the bladder, there will certainly be bloody discharge.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The bladder is taiyang. Shaoyin and taiyang stand in exterior–interior relation. When shaoyin disease reaches eight or nine days, the cold evil transforms into heat and is transmitted back to the taiyang. The taiyang governs the qi of all the yang, so when heat is in the taiyang, the whole body, hands, and feet are all hot. The taiyang channel has much blood and little qi; when it is assailed by heat, the blood scatters and moves downward, so there will certainly be bloody discharge.

## Notes

In the course of shaoyin disease, when cold turns to heat and the heat moves to the taiyang (bladder), its exterior–interior partner, the whole body becomes hot and bleeding occurs. "Bloody discharge" (*bianxue*) is interpreted either as bleeding with stool or with urine; views differ.
