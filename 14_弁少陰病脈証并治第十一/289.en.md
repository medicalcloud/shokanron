# Clause 289 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyin disease with aversion to cold and curling up, periodic spontaneous vexation, and a desire to remove clothes and bedding can be treated.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Aversion to cold and curling up mean the yin cold is severe. Periodic spontaneous vexation with a desire to remove clothes and bedding means the yang qi has been able to return. Therefore it is said it can be treated.

## Notes

Within aversion to cold and curling up, the periodic appearance of feverish vexation with a desire to throw off clothing is a sign that yang qi is recovering, so it is treatable. It is said to need distinguishing from the "agitation" of yin exuberance repelling yang (fatal patterns such as Clause 296).
