# Clause 312 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyin disease with injury in the throat producing sores, inability to speak, and loss of voice: Kujiu tang (Vinegar Decoction) governs it. Formula 1.

Banxia (washed, broken into pieces the size of jujube pits) 14 pieces; 1 egg (remove the yolk, put vinegar on top, filling the eggshell).

Of the two ingredients above, put the banxia into the vinegar, set the eggshell in the ring of a knife handle, place it over the fire, bring to a boil three times, remove the dregs, and hold a little at a time in the mouth and swallow it. If it does not recover, make three more preparations. [The formula number is "Formula 1" in the base, but from the surrounding numbering it corresponds to "Formula 11."]

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

When heat damages the network vessels, the channels and network vessels become dry, injuring the throat and producing sores. For inability to speak and loss of voice, Kujiu tang is given to resolve the heat of the network vessels and heal the throat sores.

(Nature and flavor) Banxia: acrid, warm. Egg: sweet, slightly cold.

Dissipate with acrid—the acridity of banxia brings out the voice. Moderate with sweet—the sweetness of the egg moderates the sore throat. Contract with sour—the sourness of vinegar astringes the throat sores.

## Notes

For throat ulcers with loss of voice, Kujiu tang is a liquid of banxia, egg white, and *kujiu* (vinegar) cooked in the eggshell, held a little at a time in the mouth. Its use as a medicine held in the mouth to act directly on the throat is considered characteristic. *Kujiu* is understood as vinegar.
