# Clause 285 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyin disease with a thready, deep, rapid pulse: the disease is in the interior, and it must not be sweated.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

When Shaoyin disease is just contracted and, on the contrary, there is fever with a deep pulse, the pathogen is in the meridian, and Mahuang Fuzi Xixin tang (Maobushisaishinto) may be given to induce sweating. Here Shaoyin disease has a thready, deep, rapid pulse, so the disease is in the interior; therefore it must not be sweated.

## Notes

In Shaoyin disease, a thready, deep, rapid pulse means the disease is in the interior and sweating is contraindicated. Cheng Wuji contrasts it with Shaoyin disease accompanied by an exterior pattern (Clause 301, Mahuang Xixin Fuzi tang (Maosaishinbushito)).
