# Clause 288 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyin disease with diarrhea: if the diarrhea stops by itself, and there is aversion to cold with lying curled up but the hands and feet are warm, it can be treated.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Shaoyin disease with diarrhea, aversion to cold, and lying curled up means cold has reached its extreme and yin prevails. When the diarrhea stops by itself and the hands and feet are warm, the interior is harmonized and the yang qi has been able to return; therefore it can be treated.

## Notes

One of the prognostic clauses of shaoyin disease. Even with aversion to cold and lying curled up (shrinking the body from cold), if the diarrhea stops and the hands and feet are warm, yang qi is recovering and it is treatable.
