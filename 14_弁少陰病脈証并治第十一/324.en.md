# Clause 324 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyin disease: when food and drink enter the mouth they are vomited; there is queasiness in the heart with a desire to vomit, yet one cannot vomit. When just contracted, with cold hands and feet and a wiry slow pulse, this is repletion in the chest; it must not be purged but should be treated by vomiting. If there is cold rheum above the diaphragm with dry retching, vomiting must not be induced; it should be warmed. Sini tang is appropriate. 23. The formula follows the method above.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

In cold damage, the exterior evil is transmitted inward and reaches the shaoyin. The shaoyin vessel emerges from the lung, connects with the heart, and pours into the chest. When the evil has lodged in the chest and does not dissipate, food and drink are vomited as soon as they enter the mouth, and there is queasiness in the heart with a desire to vomit. Yang receives its qi in the chest; when the evil has lodged in the chest, the yang qi cannot diffuse outward; therefore when just contracted the hands and feet are cold and the pulse wiry and slow. This is repletion in the chest; it must not be purged but should be treated by vomiting. Cold rheum above the diaphragm also causes queasiness in the heart and cold hands and feet. In vomiting, matter comes out; in retching, no matter comes out—this is the distinction between vomiting and retching. With repletion in the chest, one vomits and matter comes out; with cold rheum above the diaphragm, there is only dry retching without vomiting. This must not be treated by vomiting; Sini tang may be given to warm the diaphragm.

## Notes

Among cold extremities and vomiting resembling shaoyin disease, the clause distinguishes repletion with phlegm or food lodged in the chest (indicated for vomiting therapy) from vacuity cold due to cold rheum above the diaphragm (indicated for warming). The clue is that in the former matter comes out with vomiting, while in the latter there is dry retching with nothing coming out.
