# Clause 311 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyin disease of two or three days with sore throat may be given Gancao tang; if it does not recover, give Jiegeng tang. 10.

Gancao tang formula:

Gancao 2 liang.

Boil the one ingredient above in 3 sheng of water to obtain 1.5 sheng, remove the dregs, and take 7 ge warm, twice a day.

Jiegeng tang formula:

Jiegeng 1 liang; gancao 2 liang.

Boil the two ingredients above in 3 sheng of water to obtain 1 sheng, remove the dregs, and take warm in two divided doses.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

When yang evil is transmitted to the shaoyin and evil heat causes sore throat, taking Gancao tang cures it. If cold and heat contend to cause sore throat and taking Gancao tang does not cure it, Jiegeng tang is given to harmonize the shaoyin qi.

(Nature and flavor) Jiegeng: acrid-sweet, slightly warm. Gancao: sweet, neutral.

Jiegeng, acrid and warm, dissipates cold; gancao, sweet and neutral in flavor, removes heat. Gancao and jiegeng combined regulate cold and heat.

## Notes

For sore throat due to mild heat lodged in the shaoyin channel, the single-ingredient Gancao tang is used, and if it does not cure, Jiegeng tang with jiegeng added. The gancao in Gancao tang is often said to be raw (not mix-fried). Jiegeng tang is widely used in later times for sore, swollen throat.
