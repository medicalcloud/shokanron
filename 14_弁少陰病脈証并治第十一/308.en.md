# Clause 308 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyin disease with diarrhea and stool with pus and blood may be needled.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

When blood and qi stagnate and gather in the lower burner and putrefy, they become pus and blood. Needling it frees the lower burner and diffuses the blood and qi.

## Notes

A short clause stating that needling may be used for stool with pus and blood in shaoyin disease. The site is not indicated. One view interprets that, in contrast with Taohua tang (vacuity cold) of the previous two clauses, needling to drain applies when there is heat or stagnation.
