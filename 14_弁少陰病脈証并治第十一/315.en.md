# Clause 315 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyin disease with diarrhea and a faint pulse: give Baitong tang. When the diarrhea does not stop, with reversal counterflow and no pulse, dry retching, and vexation, Baitong jia Zhudanzhi tang governs it. After taking the decoction, if the pulse suddenly emerges, one dies; if it continues faintly, one lives. Baitong jia Zhudan tang. Formula 14. For Baitong tang, use the formula above.

Congbai 4 stalks; ganjiang 1 liang; fuzi 1 piece (raw, remove the skin, break into eight pieces); human urine 5 ge; pig bile 1 ge.

Boil the five ingredients above in 3 sheng of water to obtain 1 sheng, remove the dregs, add the bile and urine, mix to combine, and take warm in two divided doses. Even without bile, it may be used.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Shaoyin disease with diarrhea and a faint pulse means cold has reached its extreme and yin prevails. Baitong tang is given to restore yang and dissipate cold. When after taking the decoction the diarrhea does not stop, with reversal counterflow and no pulse, dry retching, and vexation, the cold qi is extremely severe and repels [the medicine] within, and the yang qi is in counterflow disorder. Baitong tang with added pig bile is given to harmonize it. The *Neijing* says: "Go against it while following it; follow it while going against it," and also: "Going against is straight treatment; following is contrary treatment." This is what is meant. When after taking the decoction the pulse suddenly emerges, the upright qi is thereby discharged and deserts; therefore one dies. When the pulse continues faintly, the yang qi is gradually returning; therefore one lives.

(Nature and flavor) Human urine: salty, cold. Pig bile: bitter, cold.

The *Neijing* says: "To regulate counterflow of cold and heat, ensure cold and heat will proceed: when a hot thing is taken cold, after it passes down the throat its cold body disperses and its hot nature then emerges; thereby the disease qi accordingly recovers and retching and hiccup are all removed; it does not go against the disposition [of the disease] and brings great benefit." This means that by mixing salty, bitter, cold human urine and pig bile into the hot preparation Baitong tang, making their qi follow [the disease], the repelling cold can be removed.

## Notes

Known as a clause showing the "contrary assistant" method: when Baitong tang worsens the condition because yin cold is so strong it rejects hot medicine (repulsion), cold-natured human urine and pig bile are added so the medicine is accepted.

- It gives a prognostic judgment: if the pulse suddenly emerges strongly after taking the medicine, it is the final outward desertion of vacuous yang and fatal; if it recovers slowly, it is yang returning and one lives.
- The formula name is written two ways in the clause: "Baitong jia Zhudanzhi tang" and "Baitong jia Zhudan tang."
