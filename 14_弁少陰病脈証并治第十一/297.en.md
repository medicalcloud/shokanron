# Clause 297 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyin disease: when the diarrhea stops but there are dizziness and periodic spontaneous clouding, it is fatal.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The diarrhea stopping means water and grain are exhausted; dizziness and clouding mean the yang qi is deserting. Therefore it is fatal.

## Notes

Cessation of diarrhea is usually a good sign (Clause 288), but when accompanied by dizziness and clouding of consciousness, it is not a return of yang but a sign that there is nothing left to discharge and both yin and yang are deserting; the clause deems it fatal.
