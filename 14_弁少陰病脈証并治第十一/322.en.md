# Clause 322 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyin disease of six or seven days with abdominal distension and no bowel movement: purge urgently. Da Chengqi tang is appropriate. 21. Use the nineteenth formula above.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

This is the shaoyin entering the bowel. Six or seven days is when the shaoyin evil enters the bowel. The internal heat of the yangming is congested and severe, so there are abdominal fullness and no bowel movement. When yangming disease's earth prevails, kidney water dries. Da Chengqi tang is given urgently to purge it and rescue kidney water.

## Notes

The third of the shaoyin "three patterns for urgent purgation." When in the course of shaoyin disease it turns into yangming bowel repletion (abdominal distension, constipation), one purges urgently to preserve kidney yin.
