# Clause 286 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyin disease with a faint pulse must not be sweated, because it is yang collapse. When the yang is already vacuous and the chi pulse is weak and rough, it must not be purged either.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

A faint pulse means yang collapse with exterior vacuity, and it must not be sweated. A weak and rough pulse means yang collapse with interior vacuity, and it must not be purged either.

## Notes

In shaoyin disease, sweating is contraindicated when yang qi has declined (faint pulse), and purgation is also contraindicated when yin blood is additionally insufficient (weak and rough chi pulse).
