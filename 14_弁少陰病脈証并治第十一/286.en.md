# Clause 286 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyin disease with a feeble pulse must not be sweated, because it is yang collapse. When the yang is already deficient and the chi pulse is weak and hesitant, it must not be purged either.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

A feeble pulse means yang collapse with exterior deficiency, and it must not be sweated. A weak and hesitant pulse means yang collapse with interior deficiency, and it must not be purged either.

## Notes

In Shaoyin disease, sweating is contraindicated when yang qi has declined (feeble pulse), and purgation is also contraindicated when yin blood is additionally insufficient (weak and hesitant chi pulse).
