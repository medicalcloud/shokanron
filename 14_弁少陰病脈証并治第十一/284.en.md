# Clause 284 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyin disease with cough, diarrhea, and delirium is because it has been forced by fire qi. Urination will certainly be difficult, because sweat has been forcibly demanded of the Shaoyin.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Cough with diarrhea means the interior is cold and the fluids are lost. When, on the contrary, fire is used to force sweat from the Shaoyin, the fluids are exhausted within and fire qi further vexes them, so there are delirium and difficult urination.

## Notes

The clause describes the result of mistaken treatment forcing sweat in Shaoyin disease with fire methods (burning needle, fumigation, etc.): loss of fluids producing delirium and difficult urination. It leads into the prohibition of sweating in Shaoyin disease (the following Clauses 285 and 286).
