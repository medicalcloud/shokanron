# Clause 299 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyin disease of six or seven days with high breathing is fatal.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The kidney is the source of the generative qi and the gate of respiration. When shaoyin disease does not recover after six or seven days and the breathing is high, the generative qi is severed.

## Notes

"High breathing" is understood as shallow breathing using only the upper chest, unable to draw inhalation deep. It is regarded as a fatal sign that the kidney can no longer grasp qi.
