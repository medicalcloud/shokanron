# Clause 281 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyin disease is a faint fine pulse and only a desire to sleep.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

In shaoyin disease, a faint fine pulse means the evil qi has been transmitted deep into the interior. When the defensive qi moves in the yang there is waking; when it moves in the yin there is sleep. When the evil is transmitted to the shaoyin, the qi moves in the yin and not in the yang, so there is only a desire to sleep.

## Notes

Regarded as the outline clause of shaoyin disease. A faint (decline of yang qi) and fine (insufficiency of yin blood) pulse and "only a desire to sleep" (a listless, drowsy state) form the basic picture of shaoyin disease. "Only a desire to sleep" is often interpreted not as sound sleep but as a dull, dozing state of consciousness.
