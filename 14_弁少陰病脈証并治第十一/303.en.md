# Clause 303 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyin disease, two or three days or more after contracting it, with vexation in the heart and inability to lie down: Huanglian Ejiao tang governs it. Formula 3.

Huanglian 4 liang; huangqin 2 liang; shaoyao 2 liang; egg yolk 2 pieces; ejiao 3 liang (one version says "3 sticks").

Take the five ingredients above with 6 sheng of water; first boil the three items to obtain 2 sheng, remove the dregs, add the ejiao and melt it completely, let cool slightly, add the egg yolks, stir to combine, and take 7 ge warm, three times a day.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The *Maijing* says: "Wind damages yang; cold damages yin." When the shaoyin contracts disease, it is contracted through cold. Two or three days or more is when cold reaches its extreme and transforms into heat; the heat vexes within, so there is vexation in the heart with inability to lie down. Huanglian Ejiao tang is given to support yin and dissipate heat.

(Nature and flavor) Huanglian: bitter, cold. Huangqin: bitter, cold. Shaoyao: sour, neutral. Egg yolk: sweet, warm. Ejiao: sweet, warm.

When yang is in excess, remove it with bitter—the bitterness of huangqin and huanglian removes heat. When yin is insufficient, supplement it with sweet—the sweetness of egg yolk and ejiao supplements the blood. Sour contracts and also drains—the sourness of shaoyao contracts the yin qi and drains the evil heat.

## Notes

Regarded as representative of the "shaoyin heat transformation pattern," in which yin (kidney yin, blood) is insufficient and heart fire flares. The main sign is heart vexation with sleeplessness; huanglian and huangqin clear heart fire, while ejiao, egg yolk, and shaoyao supplement yin blood. In later times it has been widely applied to insomnia and the like.
