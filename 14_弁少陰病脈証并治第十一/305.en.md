# Clause 305 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyin disease with generalized body pain, cold hands and feet, joint pain, and a deep pulse: Fuzi tang (Bushito) governs it. 5. Use the fourth formula above.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The Shaoyin is kidney water and governs the bones and joints. Aching pain of the body, cold limbs, and a deep pulse mean cold has formed in the yin. Even with body aches and bone pain, if the pulse were floating and the hands and feet hot, sweating would be permissible; here the hands and feet are cold and the pulse deep, so Fuzi tang should be given to warm the meridians.

## Notes

Following the previous clause (304), this describes the Fuzi tang pattern. Body and joint pain also appear in the Taiyang exterior pattern (Mahuang tang [Maoto] pattern), but cold hands and feet and a deep pulse distinguish pain due to cold-damp with yang deficiency. In later times it is applied to joint pain from cold and similar conditions.
