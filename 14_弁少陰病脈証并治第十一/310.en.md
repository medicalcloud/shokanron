# Clause 310 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyin disease with diarrhea, sore throat, chest fullness, and heart vexation: Zhufu tang (Pork Skin Decoction) governs it. Formula 9.

Pork skin 1 jin.

Boil the one ingredient above in 1 dou of water to obtain 5 sheng, remove the dregs, add 1 sheng of white honey and 5 ge of white flour dry-fried until fragrant, mix to combine, and take warm in six divided doses.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The shaoyin vessel rises from the kidney, penetrates the liver and diaphragm, enters the lung, and follows the throat. Its branch emerges from the lung, connects with the heart, and pours into the chest. When the evil is transmitted from the yang channels to the shaoyin and heat lodges where yin is vacuous, there are diarrhea, sore throat, chest fullness, and heart vexation. Zhufu tang is given to regulate yin and dissipate heat.

(Nature and flavor) Pork skin: sweet, cold.

The pig is a water animal, and its qi first enters the kidney. Since heat lodges in the shaoyin, pork skin resolves it. White honey is added to moisten dryness and remove vexation, and white flour to boost qi and stop the diarrhea.

## Notes

For shaoyin diarrhea that damages yin fluids, with vacuity heat rising along the channel to the throat and chest, Zhufu tang gently moistens and removes heat with pork skin, honey, and rice flour.

- Clauses 310–313 are regarded as a group dealing with sore throat in shaoyin disease.
- "White flour" is generally interpreted as rice flour.
