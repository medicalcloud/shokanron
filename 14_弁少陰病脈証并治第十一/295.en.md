# Clause 295 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyin disease with aversion to cold, a curled-up body with diarrhea, and counterflow cold of the hands and feet is untreatable.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The *Zhenjing* says: "One with much heat recovers easily; one with much cold recovers with difficulty." Here cold has reached its extreme inside and out; it is pure yin without yang, so it is said to be untreatable.

## Notes

Clauses 295–300 are regarded as a group listing fatal and difficult patterns of shaoyin disease. This clause deems untreatable the combination of aversion to cold, lying curled up, diarrhea, and counterflow cold of the extremities, with no sign at all of yang returning (warm hands and feet, Clause 288).
