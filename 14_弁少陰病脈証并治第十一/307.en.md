# Clause 307 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyin disease, from the second or third day to the fourth or fifth day, with abdominal pain, inhibited urination, unceasing diarrhea, and stool with pus and blood: Taohua tang governs it. 7. Use the sixth formula above.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Two or three days to four or five days means the cold evil has entered deep into the interior. Abdominal pain means interior cold. Inhibited urination means water and grain are not separated. Unceasing diarrhea with pus and blood means the intestines and stomach are vacuous and weak and the lower burner is not secure. Taohua tang is given to secure the intestines and stop the diarrhea.

## Notes

The clause describes the Taohua tang pattern of the previous clause in more detail. Abdominal pain and inhibited urination are added, but the inhibited urination is because fluids are taken by the unceasing diarrhea, so it is treated by astringing rather than promoting urination.
