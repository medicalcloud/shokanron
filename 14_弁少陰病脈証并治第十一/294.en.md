# Clause 294 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyin disease with only reversal and no sweating: if sweating is forcibly induced, it will certainly stir the blood. It is not known by which path it will come out; it may come from the mouth and nose or from the eyes. This is called lower reversal with upper exhaustion and is difficult to treat.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Only reversal and no sweating mean heat is moving in the interior. When sweating is nevertheless forced, it makes the channels and network vessels vacuous; the heat takes advantage of the channel vacuity, presses the blood to move recklessly, and it comes out where there is vacuity—from the mouth and nose, or from the eyes. All reversal belongs to the lower; only reversal is lower reversal, and loss of blood above is upper exhaustion. Qi is damaged and blood is harmed; the evil is severe and the upright vacuous, so it is difficult to treat.

## Notes

When sweating is forced in shaoyin disease with cold extremities and no sweat, yang qi becomes vacuous below (lower reversal) and blood comes out from the upper orifices (mouth, nose, eyes), exhausting yin blood (upper exhaustion), a dangerous state. It is regarded as reiterating the prohibition of sweating in shaoyin disease (Clauses 285, 286).
