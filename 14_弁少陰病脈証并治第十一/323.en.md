# Clause 323 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyin disease with a deep pulse: warm it urgently. Sini tang is appropriate. Formula 22.

Gancao 2 liang (mix-fried); ganjiang 1.5 liang; fuzi 1 piece (used raw, remove the skin, break into eight pieces).

Boil the three ingredients above in 3 sheng of water to obtain 1 sheng 2 ge, remove the dregs, and take warm in two divided doses. For a strong person, one large fuzi and ganjiang 3 liang may be used.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Even [the clause on] one who has already vomited and has diarrhea, free urination, great sweating, diarrhea of undigested grain, interior cold and exterior heat, and a feeble pulse verging on expiry does not say "warm urgently." Here, Shaoyin disease with merely a deep pulse is said to need urgent warming because there, although the cold is severe, the pattern has already taken visible form, so there is an established method to treat it; here, at the beginning, there is only a deep pulse and no formed pattern, and it is not known where the pathogenic qi will go or what disease will arise. Therefore Sini tang is given urgently to warm it.

## Notes

In Shaoyin disease, if the pulse is deep (yang deficiency), one should warm early with Sini tang before serious signs such as cold limbs and diarrhea appear. It is cited as an example of anticipating and preventing the progression of disease. This "urgent warming" clause is placed right after the three patterns for urgent purgation, in contrast.
