# Clause 290 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In shaoyin wind strike, when the pulse is faint at yang and floating at yin, it is about to recover.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

In shaoyin wind strike, the yang pulse should be floating; that the yang pulse is faint means the exterior evil has relaxed. The yin pulse should be sunken; that the yin pulse is floating means the interior qi is harmonized. There is yin within yang and yang within yin; yin and yang are in accord, so it is about to recover.

## Notes

In shaoyin wind strike, the cun (yang) pulse becoming faint shows the external evil declining, and the chi (yin) pulse becoming floating shows yang qi recovering, a sign of recovery. It has the same form as the taiyin wind strike clause, 274.
