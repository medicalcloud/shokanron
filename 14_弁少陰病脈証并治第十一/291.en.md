# Clause 291 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The time when shaoyin disease tends to resolve is from zi to yin.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Yang is born at zi. Zi is one yang, chou is two yang, yin is three yang. Shaoyin resolves at this time because yin resolves when it obtains yang.

## Notes

One of the six channels' "times of resolution." Zi to yin corresponds roughly to 11 p.m.–5 a.m., the period when yang qi is born and grows.
