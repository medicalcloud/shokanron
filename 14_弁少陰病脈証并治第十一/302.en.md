# Clause 302 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyin disease, two or three days after contracting it: induce slight sweating with Mahuang Fuzi Gancao tang. Because at two or three days there are no [interior] signs, slight sweating is induced. Formula 2.

Mahuang 2 liang (remove the nodes); gancao 2 liang (mix-fried); fuzi 1 piece (blast-fried, remove the skin, break into eight pieces).

Take the three ingredients above with 7 sheng of water; first boil the mahuang to "one liang" boils, remove the foam on top, add the other medicinals, boil to obtain 3 sheng, remove the dregs, and take 1 sheng warm, three times a day. [The phrase "一両沸" is translated as in the base; it is generally read as "一二沸" (bring to a boil once or twice).]

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

At two or three days the evil is not yet deep. When there are none of the interior signs such as vomiting, diarrhea, or reversal counterflow, Mahuang Fuzi Gancao tang may be given to induce slight sweating and dissipate it.

The sweetness of mahuang and gancao dissipates the exterior cold; the acridity of fuzi warms the cold qi.

## Notes

Like the previous clause, this is early shaoyin disease with an exterior pattern, but regarded as the formula for two or three days after onset when sweating should be gentler. Replacing xixin with gancao weakens the sweating effect, explained as a measure to protect the upright qi. "No signs" is interpreted as the absence of interior signs such as vomiting and diarrhea.
