# Clause 372 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Diarrhea with abdominal distension and fullness and generalized body pain: first warm the interior, then attack the exterior. To warm the interior, Sini tang is appropriate; to attack the exterior, Guizhi tang is appropriate. 13. For Sini tang, use the fifth formula above.

Guizhi tang formula:

Guizhi 3 liang (remove the bark); shaoyao 3 liang; gancao 2 liang (mix-fried); shengjiang 3 liang (cut); dazao 12 pieces (broken).

Boil the five ingredients above in 7 sheng of water to obtain 3 sheng, remove the dregs, and take 1 sheng warm. After a short while, sip 1 sheng of hot thin gruel to assist the power of the medicine.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Diarrhea with abdominal fullness means there is vacuity cold in the interior; first give Sini tang to warm the interior. Generalized body pain means the exterior has not yet resolved; when the diarrhea stops and the interior is harmonized, give Guizhi tang to attack the exterior.

## Notes

When interior vacuity cold (diarrhea, abdominal fullness) and an exterior pattern (body pain) coexist, the clause shows the order of treatment: first warm the interior with Sini tang, then resolve the exterior with Guizhi tang. It is the same principle as Clause 91 in the taiyang chapter.
