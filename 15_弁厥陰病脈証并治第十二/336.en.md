# Clause 336 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In cold damage disease with cold limbs for five days and heat also for five days, if on the sixth day there should be cold limbs again but there is none, it will recover by itself. The coldness of the limbs in the end did not exceed five days, and the heat was five days; therefore one knows it will recover by itself.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

When yin prevails there are cold limbs; when yang prevails there is heat. Cold limbs first for five days means yin prevails. On the sixth day yang prevails again, and after heat also for five days, if cold limbs return, yin has prevailed again; if there are no cold limbs, yang has fully prevailed, so it recovers by itself. The classic says: "Fever for four days, cold limbs, on the contrary, for three days, and heat again for four days: cold limbs are less and heat more, so the disease recovers."

## Notes

When the days of cold limbs and fever are equal and cold limbs do not reappear afterward, yin and yang are balanced and recovery occurs spontaneously. It is regarded as typical of prognosis by the days of alternating cold limbs and fever.
