# Clause 336 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In cold damage disease with reversal for five days and heat also for five days, if on the sixth day there should be reversal again but there is none, it will recover by itself. The reversal in the end did not exceed five days, and the heat was five days; therefore one knows it will recover by itself.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

When yin prevails there is reversal; when yang prevails there is heat. Reversal first for five days means yin prevails. On the sixth day yang prevails again, and after heat also for five days, if reversal returns, yin has prevailed again; if there is no reversal, yang has fully prevailed, so it recovers by itself. The classic says: "Fever for four days, reversal, on the contrary, for three days, and heat again for four days: reversal is less and heat more, so the disease recovers."

## Notes

When the days of reversal and heat are equal and reversal does not reappear afterward, yin and yang are balanced and recovery occurs spontaneously. It is regarded as typical of prognosis by the days of alternating reversal and heat.
