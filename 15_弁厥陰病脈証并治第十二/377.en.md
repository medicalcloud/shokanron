# Clause 377 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Retching with a weak pulse, free urination again, slight body heat, and the appearance of reversal is difficult to treat; Sini tang governs it. 17. Use the fifth formula above.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Retching with a weak pulse means the evil qi has been transmitted inward. Retching makes qi counterflow upward, so urination should be inhibited; free urination again means the interior is vacuous. Slight body heat with the appearance of reversal means yin is overcoming yang, and it is difficult to treat. Sini tang is given to warm the interior and assist yang.

## Notes

Retching accompanied by a weak pulse, excessive urination, slight heat, and cold extremities indicates yang qi so declined that it cannot contain above or below, the slight heat being vacuous yang floating outward; the clause calls it difficult to treat yet warms it with Sini tang.
