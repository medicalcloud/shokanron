# Clause 330 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

All cases of counterflow of the four limbs and reversal must not be purged; the same applies to those of vacuous constitution.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Counterflow of the four limbs means the four limbs are not warm; reversal means the hands and feet are cold. In both, yang qi is scant and yin qi abundant, so they must not be purged. The same applies to those of vacuous constitution; purging them would doubly deplete them. The *Jingui Yuhan* says: "For the vacuous, supplement ten times and do not drain even once."

## Notes

The clause states the principle that purgation must not be used for those with cold extremities (counterflow, reversal) or of weak constitution. Since some reversal should be purged, as in Clause 335's "reversal should be purged" (heat reversal), this is interpreted as referring mainly to cold reversal and vacuity patterns.
