# Clause 330 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

All cases of counterflow of the four limbs and cold limbs must not be purged; the same applies to those of deficient constitution.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Counterflow of the four limbs means the four limbs are not warm; cold limbs means the hands and feet are cold. In both, yang qi is scant and yin qi abundant, so they must not be purged. The same applies to those of deficient constitution; purging them would doubly deplete them. The *Jingui Yuhan* says: "For the deficient, supplement ten times and do not drain even once."

## Notes

The clause states the principle that purgation must not be used for those with cold extremities (counterflow, cold limbs) or of weak constitution. Since some cold limbs should be purged, as in Clause 335's "cold limbs should be purged" (heat-type cold limbs (WHO: heat syncope)), this is interpreted as referring mainly to cold-type cold limbs (WHO: cold syncope) and deficiency patterns.
