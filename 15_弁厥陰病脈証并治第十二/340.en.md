# Clause 340 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the patient's hands and feet are cold, and the patient says "I have no chest bind," and the lower abdomen is full and painful on pressure, this is cold bound at the bladder and Guanyuan.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Cold hands and feet without chest bind mean there is no heat. Lower-abdominal fullness that is painful on pressure means cold is bound in the lower jiao.

## Notes

When cold extremities are accompanied by lower-abdominal distension and tenderness with no symptoms in the chest, it is cold-type cold limbs (WHO: cold syncope) with cold bound in the lower jiao (the region of the bladder and Guanyuan). No formula is given. "Guanyuan" is said to refer to the location three cun below the navel (an acupuncture point name).
