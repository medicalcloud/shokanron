# Clause 340 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the patient's hands and feet are reversal-cold, and the patient says "I have no chest binding," and the lower abdomen is full and painful on pressure, this is cold bound at the bladder and Guanyuan.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Reversal-cold hands and feet without chest binding mean there is no heat. Lower-abdominal fullness that is painful on pressure means cold is bound in the lower burner.

## Notes

When cold extremities are accompanied by lower-abdominal distension and tenderness with no symptoms in the chest, it is cold reversal with cold bound in the lower burner (the region of the bladder and Guanyuan). No formula is given. "Guanyuan" is said to refer to the location three cun below the navel (an acupuncture point name).
