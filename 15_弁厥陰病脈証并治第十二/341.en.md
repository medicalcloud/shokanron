# Clause 341 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In cold damage with fever for four days, reversal, on the contrary, for three days, and heat again for four days, the reversal is less and the heat more, so the disease should recover. When from the fourth to the seventh day the heat is not removed, there will certainly be stool with pus and blood.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The disease should recover, but when from the fourth to the seventh day the heat is not removed, there will certainly be stool with pus and blood afterward. Heat first and reversal afterward means the evil [with] the yang qi is transmitted inward. Fever means the evil qi is in the exterior; reversal after the fourth day means it has been transmitted to the yin. When after three days it is transmitted again to the yang channels, there is heat again. Less reversal means the evil is slight; more heat means yang prevails, so the disease recovers. When by the seventh day the transmission through the channels is complete and the heat is removed, there is recovery. When the heat is not removed, the surplus heat qi contends internally with the blood of the jueyin, and there will certainly be pus and blood in the stool afterward. [The opening phrase of the commentary looks like a summary of the clause, and the phrase "陽気邪伝裏" is hard to construe; this is a tentative translation.]

## Notes

One of the clauses judging prognosis by the days of alternating reversal and heat. If the days of heat exceed those of reversal, yang prevails and there is recovery, but if heat remains too long, yang becomes excessive and causes stool with pus and blood (the same purport as Clause 334).
