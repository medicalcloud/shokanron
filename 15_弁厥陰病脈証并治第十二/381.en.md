# Clause 381 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In cold damage with hiccup and abdominal fullness, examine the front and back, find which part is not free, and free it; then there is immediate recovery.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Hiccup with abdominal fullness means qi rises and does not descend. Examine the front and back; if there is a part that is not free, free it at once to lower the qi. The front part is urine; the back part is stool.

## Notes

In repletion patterns of hiccup with abdominal fullness, one checks whether urination or defecation is obstructed and frees it, so the qi descends and there is recovery. It is contrasted with the vacuity-cold hiccup of the previous clause. It is the last clause of the jueyin chapter.
