# Clause 352 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

If the person has long-standing cold within, Danggui Sini jia Wuzhuyu Shengjiang tang is appropriate and governs it. Formula 4.

Danggui 3 liang; shaoyao 3 liang; gancao 2 liang (mix-fried); tongcao 2 liang; guizhi 3 liang (remove the bark); xixin 3 liang; shengjiang half a jin (cut); wuzhuyu 2 sheng; dazao 25 pieces (broken).

Boil the nine ingredients above in a mixture of 6 sheng of water and 6 sheng of clear wine to obtain 5 sheng, remove the dregs, and take warm in five divided doses. One version: 4 sheng each of water and wine.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Wuzhuyu, acrid and warm, dissipates long-standing cold; shengjiang, acrid and warm, moves the yang qi.

## Notes

For a person with the Danggui Sini tang pattern of the previous clause who also has long-standing internal cold, wuzhuyu and shengjiang are added and the formula is decocted in water and wine to strengthen its warming power. In later times it is applied to abdominal pain, headache, menstrual pain, and the like due to cold.
