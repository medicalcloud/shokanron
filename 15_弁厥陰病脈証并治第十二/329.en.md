# Clause 329 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Jueyin disease with thirst and a desire to drink water: give a little at a time, and it recovers.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

When the evil reaches the jueyin, transmission through the channels is complete, and it is the time when sweating is about to occur. For thirst with a desire to obtain water, give a little at a time; when the stomach qi is moistened, there is recovery.

## Notes

Thirst appearing in the course of jueyin disease is a sign of yang qi recovering, and giving water a little at a time to moisten the stomach leads to recovery. It is interpreted as different in degree from the "dispersion-thirst" of the outline clause (326).
