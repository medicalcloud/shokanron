# Clause 331 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In cold damage, when there are first cold limbs and afterward fever with diarrhea, [the diarrhea] will certainly stop by itself; when cold limbs appears [again], there is diarrhea again.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

When yin qi prevails there are cold limbs and diarrhea; when yang qi returns there is fever and the diarrhea will certainly stop by itself. When cold limbs appears, yin qi has prevailed again, and there is diarrhea again.

## Notes

The first of a group of clauses (331–336 etc.) on "alternation of cold limbs and fever" (cold limbs = yin prevailing; fever = yang returning), considered characteristic of Jueyin disease. Cold limbs and diarrhea indicate yin prevailing; fever and cessation of diarrhea indicate yang returning.
