# Clause 374 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Diarrhea with delirious speech means there is dry stool; Xiao Chengqi tang is appropriate. Formula 15.

Dahuang 4 liang (washed with wine); zhishi 3 pieces (mix-fried); houpo 2 liang (remove the bark, mix-fried).

Boil the three ingredients above in 4 sheng of water to obtain 1 sheng 2 ge, remove the dregs, and take in two divided doses. If after the first dose the delirious speech stops and there is a bowel movement, stop the remaining dose; otherwise, take it all.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The classic says: "Repletion causes delirious speech." Dry stool means the stomach is replete; diarrhea means the intestines are vacuous. Xiao Chengqi tang is given to purge the dry stool.

## Notes

Even with diarrhea, if there is delirious speech, it is a repletion pattern with dry stool bound in the intestines, and Xiao Chengqi tang purges it. Like Clause 321 in the shaoyin chapter (heat bind with circumfluence), it is cited as an example of "treating flow by promoting flow" (treating diarrhea with purgation).
