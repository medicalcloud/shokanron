# Clause 356 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In cold damage with reversal and palpitations below the heart, it is appropriate first to treat the water: Fuling Gancao tang should be taken, and afterward the reversal treated. Otherwise, the water will soak into the stomach and will certainly cause diarrhea. Fuling Gancao tang. Formula 8.

Fuling 2 liang; gancao 1 liang (mix-fried); shengjiang 3 liang (cut); guizhi 2 liang (remove the bark).

Boil the four ingredients above in 4 sheng of water to obtain 2 sheng, remove the dregs, and take warm in three divided doses.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The *Jingui Yaolüe* says: "When water collects below the heart and is severe, there are palpitations." Although reversal means cold prevails, palpitations below the heart mean water-rheum is severe within, so first give Fuling Gancao tang to treat the water and afterward treat the reversal. If the reversal is treated first, the water-rheum will soak into the stomach and will certainly cause diarrhea.

## Notes

Cold extremities with palpitations below the heart are reversal due to collected water-rheum preventing yang qi from circulating (water reversal), and the water should first be removed with Fuling Gancao tang. It is cited as an example showing the order of treatment (water first, reversal afterward). Fuling Gancao tang also appears in Clause 73 of the taiyang chapter.
