# Clause 337 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

As a rule, reversal is when the qi of yin and yang do not connect smoothly with each other; that is reversal. Reversal is counterflow cold of the hands and feet.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The three yin and three yang of the hand connect with each other at the ten fingers; the three yin and three yang of the foot connect with each other at the ten toes. When yang qi sinks inward and yang does not connect smoothly with yin, the hands and feet become reversal-cold.

## Notes

The clause defining "reversal." Whether caused by cold or heat, loss of connection between the qi of yin and yang resulting in cold hands and feet is called reversal. It is regarded as a general statement for the clauses on reversal in the jueyin chapter.
