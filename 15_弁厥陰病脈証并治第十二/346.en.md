# Clause 346 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In cold damage, when there has been no diarrhea for six or seven days and then suddenly there are fever and diarrhea, and the person sweats without stopping, it is fatal, because there is yin without yang.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The seventh day of cold damage is when the evil and the upright struggle. When the upright prevails one lives; when the evil prevails one dies. When at first there was no diarrhea and then suddenly fever and diarrhea arise with unceasing sweating, the evil qi has overcome the upright and the yang qi is deserting. Therefore it is fatal.

## Notes

The sudden appearance of fever, diarrhea, and unceasing sweating is regarded as yang qi deserting outward leaving only yin (yang collapse), and the clause deems it fatal.
