# Clause 348 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Fever with reversal, and diarrhea on the seventh day, is difficult to treat.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Fever with reversal means the evil has been transmitted inward. When by the seventh day the transmission through the channels is complete, the upright qi should overcome the evil, and sweating should resolve it. If, on the contrary, there is diarrhea, the evil qi prevails and the interior qi is vacuous, so it is difficult to treat.

## Notes

When fever and reversal continue and diarrhea is added on the seventh day, it is a sign that the upright qi has failed to overcome the evil and the interior qi has become vacuous, and the clause deems it difficult to treat.
