# Clause 348 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Fever with cold limbs, and diarrhea on the seventh day, is difficult to treat.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Fever with cold limbs means the pathogen has been transmitted inward. When by the seventh day the transmission through the meridians is complete, the healthy qi should overcome the pathogen, and sweating should resolve it. If, on the contrary, there is diarrhea, the pathogenic qi prevails and the interior qi is deficient, so it is difficult to treat.

## Notes

When fever and cold limbs continue and diarrhea is added on the seventh day, it is a sign that the healthy qi has failed to overcome the pathogen and the interior qi has become deficient, and the clause deems it difficult to treat.
