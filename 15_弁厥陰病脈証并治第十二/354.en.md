# Clause 354 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Great sweating, or great diarrhea, with reversal cold: Sini tang governs it. 6. Use the fifth formula above.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Great sweating and great diarrhea differ as inner and outer, but they are the same in losing fluids and damaging yang qi. Yang is vacuous and yin prevails, so reversal counterflow arises. Sini tang is given to secure yang and repel yin.

## Notes

A concise clause in which yang qi is lost through profuse sweating or violent diarrhea with cold extremities, and Sini tang promptly restores yang.
