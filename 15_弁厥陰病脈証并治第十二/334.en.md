# Clause 334 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In cold damage, when there is first reversal and afterward fever, the diarrhea will certainly stop by itself. If, on the contrary, there are sweating and pain in the throat, the throat becomes obstructed (*bi*). When there is fever without sweating, the diarrhea will certainly stop by itself; if it does not stop, there will certainly be stool with pus and blood; and one with stool with pus and blood does not have throat obstruction.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

First reversal with diarrhea in cold damage means yin-cold qi prevails. When cold reaches its extreme and transforms into heat, and afterward there is fever, the diarrhea will certainly stop by itself. When, on the contrary, there are sweating, pain in the throat, and throat obstruction, the heat qi moves upward. When there is fever without sweating and the diarrhea should certainly stop by itself, but it does not stop and there is certainly stool with pus and blood, the heat qi moves downward. When the heat qi moves down and not up, the throat is not obstructed.

## Notes

After reversal, yang qi returns with fever, but if that heat becomes excessive, moving upward it causes painful swelling of the throat (throat obstruction), and moving downward stool with pus and blood. It is regarded as an example of yang's return going too far.
