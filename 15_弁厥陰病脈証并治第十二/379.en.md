# Clause 379 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Retching with fever: Xiao Chaihu tang governs it. Formula 19.

Chaihu 8 liang; huangqin 3 liang; renshen 3 liang; gancao 3 liang (mix-fried); shengjiang 3 liang (cut); banxia half a sheng (washed); dazao 12 pieces (broken).

Boil the seven ingredients above in 1 dou 2 sheng of water to obtain 6 sheng, remove the dregs, decoct again to obtain 3 sheng, and take 1 sheng warm, three times a day.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The classic says: "Retching with fever means the Chaihu pattern is present."

## Notes

It is often interpreted that retching and fever appearing in the course of jueyin disease are a sign that the disease is emerging from yin to yang (shaoyang), and Xiao Chaihu tang harmonizes and resolves it.
