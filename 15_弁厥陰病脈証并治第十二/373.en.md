# Clause 373 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Diarrhea with a desire to drink water is because there is heat; Baitouweng tang governs it. 14. Use the twelfth formula above.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Spontaneous diarrhea without thirst is visceral cold; Sini [tang] is given to warm the viscera. Diarrhea with drinking water means there is heat; Baitouweng tang is given to cool the center.

## Notes

Diarrhea with thirst is judged to be heat diarrhea and treated with Baitouweng tang. It is read in contrast with taiyin's "spontaneous diarrhea without thirst" (Clause 277).
