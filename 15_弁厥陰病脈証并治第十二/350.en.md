# Clause 350 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cold damage with a slippery pulse and reversal: there is heat in the interior; Baihu tang governs it. Formula 2.

Zhimu 6 liang; shigao 1 jin (crushed, wrapped in cotton); gancao 2 liang (mix-fried); jingmi 6 ge.

Boil the four ingredients above in 1 dou of water until the rice is cooked and the decoction is done, remove the dregs, and take 1 sheng warm, three times a day.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Slippery is yang. Reversal means the qi has sunk inward; this is interior heat. Baihu tang is given to dissipate the interior heat.

## Notes

Even with cold extremities, if the pulse is slippery (a yang pulse), it is heat reversal with heat shut in the interior, and Baihu tang clears the heat. It is regarded as one of the specific treatments for the heat reversal of Clause 335.
