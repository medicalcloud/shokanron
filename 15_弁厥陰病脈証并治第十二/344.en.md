# Clause 344 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cold damage with fever, diarrhea with cold limbs, and agitation with inability to lie down is fatal.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Fever in cold damage means the pathogen is in the exterior. Diarrhea with cold limbs means the yang qi is deficient. Agitation with inability to lie down means the disease overcomes the zang organs. Therefore it is fatal.

## Notes

When fever and restless agitation are added to the yin-cold signs of diarrhea and cold limbs, it is not the return of yang but a sign that deficient yang is floating outward and about to desert; the clause deems it fatal.
