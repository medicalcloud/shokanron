# Clause 344 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cold damage with fever, diarrhea with reversal counterflow, and agitation with inability to lie down is fatal.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Fever in cold damage means the evil is in the exterior. Diarrhea with reversal counterflow means the yang qi is vacuous. Agitation with inability to lie down means the disease overcomes the viscera. Therefore it is fatal.

## Notes

When fever and restless agitation are added to the yin-cold signs of diarrhea and reversal, it is not the return of yang but a sign that vacuous yang is floating outward and about to desert; the clause deems it fatal.
