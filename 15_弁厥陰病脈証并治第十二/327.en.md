# Clause 327 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In jueyin wind strike, a slightly floating pulse means it is about to recover; a pulse that is not floating means it has not yet recovered.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The classic says: "When a yang pulse is seen in yin disease, there is life." Floating is yang. A slightly floating pulse in jueyin wind strike is the time when the evil qi returns to the exterior and tends toward sweating, so it is said to be about to recover.

## Notes

In the same form as the wind strike clauses for taiyin (274) and shaoyin (290), this clause judges the prognosis of jueyin wind strike by the pulse. A slight floating (yang) quality appearing in the pulse of yin disease is said to be a sign of improvement, the evil heading outward.
