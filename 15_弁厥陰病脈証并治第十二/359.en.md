# Clause 359 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In cold damage with originally spontaneous cold diarrhea, the physician again induced vomiting and purgation, causing cold repulsion; with further counterflow vomiting and purgation, if food is vomited as soon as it enters the mouth, Ganjiang Huangqin Huanglian Renshen tang governs it. Formula 10.

Ganjiang, huangqin, huanglian, and renshen, 3 liang each.

Boil the four ingredients above in 6 sheng of water to obtain 2 sheng, remove the dregs, and take warm in two divided doses.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

In cold damage, the evil being spontaneously transmitted from the exterior is taken as "originally spontaneous cold diarrhea." When the physician, on the contrary, induces vomiting and purgation and damages the upright qi, the cold qi repels within. The classic says: "Repulsion causes vomiting counterflow." Vomiting as soon as food enters the mouth is called cold repulsion. If vomiting and purgation are induced yet again, there is double vacuity and death; this is "further counterflow vomiting and purgation." Ganjiang Huanglian Huangqin Renshen tang is given to free the cold repulsion.

(Nature and flavor) Ganjiang: acrid, hot. Huanglian: bitter, cold.

(Nature and flavor) Huangqin: bitter, cold. Renshen: sweet, warm.

Dissipate with acrid and moderate with sweet—the sweetness and acridity of ganjiang and renshen supplement the upright qi. Drain with bitter—the bitterness of huanglian and huangqin frees the cold repulsion.

## Notes

For a person with original vacuity-cold diarrhea in whom mistaken vomiting and purgation have separated heat above from cold below, so that food is vomited as soon as it is eaten (heat above, cold below), ganjiang and renshen warm and supplement the middle burner while huangqin and huanglian clear heat above.

- The phrase "寒格，更逆吐下" is difficult; one reading is "because of cold repulsion, vomiting and diarrhea become further aggravated," among others.
- Cheng Wuji's phrase "傷寒邪自伝表為本自寒下" is also hard to construe; a literal translation has been attempted.
