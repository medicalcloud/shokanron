# Clause 345 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cold damage with fever, extremely severe diarrhea, and unceasing reversal is fatal.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The *Jingui Yaolüe* says: "When the qi of the six bowels is cut off outside, the hands and feet are cold; when the qi of the five viscera is cut off inside, diarrhea cannot be contained." Fever in cold damage means the evil qi alone is severe; extremely severe diarrhea with unceasing reversal means the qi of the bowels and viscera is cut off. Therefore it is fatal.

## Notes

Even with fever, violent diarrhea and unceasing cold extremities indicate the exhaustion of visceral and bowel qi, and the clause deems it fatal.
