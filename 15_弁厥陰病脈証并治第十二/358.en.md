# Clause 358 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In cold damage of four or five days, with pain in the abdomen, if qi turns and moves down toward the lower abdomen, this means spontaneous diarrhea is about to occur.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Four or five days of cold damage is when the evil qi is transmitted inward. Pain in the abdomen with qi turning and moving down toward the lower abdomen means the interior is vacuous and meets cold; the cold qi moves downward and spontaneous diarrhea is about to occur.

## Notes

The sensation of qi rumbling and moving down to the lower abdomen with abdominal pain is a precursor of diarrhea.
