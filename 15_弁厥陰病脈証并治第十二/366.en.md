# Clause 366 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Diarrhea with a sunken slow pulse, the person's face slightly red, slight heat in the body, and diarrhea of undigested grain: there will certainly be depressive veiling, and sweating will resolve it; the patient will certainly have slight reversal. The reason is that the face carries upcast yang and the lower part is vacuous.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Diarrhea of undigested grain with a sunken slow pulse means cold in the interior. A slightly red face and slight body heat mean the exterior has not yet resolved. The patient's slight reversal is [as] the *Zhenjing* says: "When the lower part is vacuous there is reversal." When the exterior evil is about to resolve, at the moment of sweating, because the interior is first vacuous, there will certainly be depressive veiling, and afterward sweating resolves it.

## Notes

In interior-cold diarrhea, a slightly red face and slight fever are "upcast yang," vacuous yang floating upward. Since yang qi is not yet completely exhausted, it may resolve through depressive veiling followed by sweating. It is read in connection with the facial redness of the Tongmai Sini tang pattern (Clause 317).
