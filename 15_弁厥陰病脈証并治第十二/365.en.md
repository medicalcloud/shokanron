# Clause 365 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Diarrhea with a deep wiry pulse means rectal heaviness; a large pulse means it has not yet stopped; a feeble, weak, rapid pulse means it is about to stop by itself, and even with fever it is not fatal.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Deep means [the disease] is in the interior; wiry is tension. When the interior qi is insufficient, it governs rectal heaviness. Large means the disease is advancing, so this diarrhea has not yet stopped. A feeble, weak, rapid pulse means the pathogenic qi is slight and the yang qi is returning, so it is about to stop by itself. Even with fever, it is merely due to yang prevailing and is not a major adverse development.

## Notes

The clause judges the signs and prognosis of diarrhea by the pulse: deep and wiry means tenesmus (rectal heaviness); large means the pathogen is still exuberant and it does not stop; feeble, weak, and rapid means the pathogen is declining and yang qi returning, so it stops by itself.
