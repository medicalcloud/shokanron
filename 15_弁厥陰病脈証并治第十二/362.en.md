# Clause 362 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Diarrhea with reversal-cold hands and feet and no pulse: if moxibustion does not warm them and the pulse does not return, and on the contrary there is slight panting, it is fatal. When the shaoyin [pulse] is defeated by the fuyang [pulse], it is favorable.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Diarrhea with reversal counterflow of the hands and feet and no pulse means yin qi alone prevails and yang qi is greatly vacuous. When moxibustion restores the yang qi, the hands and feet become warm, and the pulse returns, it is about to recover. If the hands and feet do not warm and the pulse does not return, the yang is already cut off; slight panting, on the contrary, means the yang qi is deserting. The shaoyin being defeated by the fuyang is favorable because the shaoyin is kidney water and the fuyang spleen earth; diarrhea is kidney evil invading the spleen. When water does not overcome earth, it is a slight evil, so it is favorable.

## Notes

When moxibustion for diarrhea with a lost pulse does not warm the hands and feet or restore the pulse, and shallow panting appears, it is a fatal pattern of deserting yang qi. The second half states that if the foot shaoyin pulse (Taixi) is weaker than the fuyang pulse (Chongyang, the stomach pulse), the stomach qi is preserved, so the prognosis is good.
