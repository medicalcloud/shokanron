# Clause 378 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Dry retching, vomiting of drool and foam, and headache: Wuzhuyu tang governs it. Formula 18.

Wuzhuyu 1 sheng (wash seven times in hot water); renshen 3 liang; dazao 12 pieces (broken); shengjiang 6 liang (cut).

Boil the four ingredients above in 7 sheng of water [to obtain] 2 sheng, remove the dregs, and take 7 ge warm, three times a day. [The base reads "煮二升," lacking the character "取" (obtain).]

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Dry retching and vomiting of drool and foam mean the interior is cold. Headache means the cold qi is attacking upward. Wuzhuyu tang is given to warm the interior and dissipate cold.

## Notes

Regarded as the clause using Wuzhuyu tang when vacuity cold of the liver and stomach causes cold rheum to counterflow upward, producing retching, drool and foam, and headache (especially at the vertex). In later times it is applied to migraine with cold and the like. Wuzhuyu tang also appears in Clause 243 of the yangming chapter and Clause 309 of the shaoyin chapter.
