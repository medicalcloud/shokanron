# Clause 364 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In diarrhea of undigested grain, the exterior must not be attacked; if there is sweating, there will certainly be distension and fullness.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Diarrhea means the spleen and stomach are vacuous. The stomach is the master of the fluids; when sweating is induced and the fluids are lost, the stomach qi becomes ever more vacuous, and there will certainly be distension and fullness.

## Notes

Diarrhea of undigested grain is an interior-cold pattern with vacuity of spleen and stomach yang, and even with an exterior pattern, inducing sweating further damages yang qi and causes abdominal distension and fullness. It is regarded as having the same purport as the principle "first rescue the interior, then the exterior" found in Clause 91 of the taiyang chapter and elsewhere.
