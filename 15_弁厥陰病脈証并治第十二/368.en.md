# Clause 368 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When after diarrhea the pulse is cut off and the hands and feet are reversal-cold, if within a full day the pulse returns and the hands and feet become warm, one lives; if the pulse does not return, one dies.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

When after diarrhea the pulse is cut off and the hands and feet are reversal-cold, there is no yang. *Zuishi* means a full cycle of time (a day and night). When within a full day the reversal heals and the pulse emerges, the yang qi has returned, and one lives. If the hands and feet do not warm and the pulse does not return, the yang qi is cut off, and one dies.

## Notes

Even if the pulse cannot be felt after violent diarrhea, life or death is judged by whether the pulse and warmth of the hands and feet return within a day and night.
