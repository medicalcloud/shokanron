# Clause 335 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In cold damage from the first or second day to the fourth or fifth day, one with reversal will certainly have fever; one with heat before will certainly have reversal afterward. When the reversal is deep, the heat is also deep; when the reversal is slight, the heat is also slight. Reversal should be purged; if, on the contrary, sweating is induced, there will certainly be injury of the mouth with red erosion.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Reversal before and fever afterward means cold reaches its extreme and generates heat. Heat before and reversal afterward means yang qi sinks inward. That deep reversal accompanies deep heat and slight reversal slight heat follows the depth of the sinking of the yang qi. When heat lies deeply hidden, it must be purged to remove it. If, on the contrary, sweating is induced, the heat is drawn upward, and there will certainly be injury of the mouth with red erosion. The *Neijing* says: "When fire qi arises within, it rises to become erosion of the mouth."

## Notes

Regarded as the clause describing "heat reversal," in which heat is deeply shut in the interior and yang qi cannot reach the limbs, making the hands and feet cold.

- It shows the relation that the deeper the heat, the deeper the reversal ("deep reversal, deep heat").
- Heat reversal should be treated by purging the interior heat; inducing sweating makes the heat rise and causes erosion in the mouth.
