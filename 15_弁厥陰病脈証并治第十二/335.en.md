# Clause 335 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In cold damage from the first or second day to the fourth or fifth day, one with cold limbs will certainly have fever; one with heat before will certainly have cold limbs afterward. When the coldness of the limbs is deep, the heat is also deep; when the coldness of the limbs is slight, the heat is also slight. Cold limbs should be purged; if, on the contrary, sweating is induced, there will certainly be injury of the mouth with red erosion.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Cold limbs before and fever afterward means cold reaches its extreme and generates heat. Heat before and cold limbs afterward means yang qi sinks inward. That deep reversal accompanies deep heat and slight reversal slight heat follows the depth of the sinking of the yang qi. When heat lies deeply hidden, it must be purged to remove it. If, on the contrary, sweating is induced, the heat is drawn upward, and there will certainly be injury of the mouth with red erosion. The *Neijing* says: "When fire qi arises within, it rises to become erosion of the mouth."

## Notes

Regarded as the clause describing "heat-type cold limbs (WHO: heat syncope)," in which heat is deeply shut in the interior and yang qi cannot reach the limbs, making the hands and feet cold.

- It shows the relation that the deeper the heat, the deeper the coldness of the limbs ("deep reversal, deep heat").
- Heat-type cold limbs should be treated by purging the interior heat; inducing sweating makes the heat rise and causes erosion in the mouth.
