# Clause 347 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In cold damage of five or six days, when there is no chest binding, the abdomen is soft, the pulse is vacuous, and there is also reversal, it must not be purged. This is blood collapse; purging it is fatal.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Five or six days of cold damage is when the evil qi should form interior repletion. If there is no chest binding and the abdomen is soft, there is no interior heat. A vacuous pulse means blood collapse. Reversal as well means the yang qi is scant. It must not be purged; purging it would doubly deplete, so it is fatal. The *Jingui Yuhan* says: "When the vacuous are drained again, the true qi is then cut off."

## Notes

Even with cold extremities, if the abdomen is soft and the pulse vacuous, it is reversal from insufficiency of blood rather than interior repletion heat, and purgation is contraindicated. It is regarded as having the same purport as Clause 330, "the vacuous must not be purged either."
