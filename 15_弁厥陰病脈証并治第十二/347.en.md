# Clause 347 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In cold damage of five or six days, when there is no chest bind, the abdomen is soft, the pulse is deficient, and there are also cold limbs, it must not be purged. This is blood collapse; purging it is fatal.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Five or six days of cold damage is when the pathogenic qi should form interior excess. If there is no chest bind and the abdomen is soft, there is no interior heat. A deficient pulse means blood collapse. Cold limbs as well means the yang qi is scant. It must not be purged; purging it would doubly deplete, so it is fatal. The *Jingui Yuhan* says: "When the deficient are drained again, the genuine qi is then cut off."

## Notes

Even with cold extremities, if the abdomen is soft and the pulse deficient, it is cold limbs from insufficiency of blood rather than interior excess heat, and purgation is contraindicated. It is regarded as having the same purport as Clause 330, "the deficient must not be purged either."
