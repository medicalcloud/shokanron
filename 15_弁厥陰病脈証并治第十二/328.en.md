# Clause 328 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The time when jueyin disease tends to resolve is from chou to mao.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The jueyin is wood and is in its prime at mao. Chou and yin turn toward the prime, so it is the time of resolution.

## Notes

The last of the six channels' "times of resolution." Chou to mao corresponds roughly to 1–7 a.m., the period when yin is exhausted and yang arises, matched to the jueyin (wood).
