# Clause 370 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Diarrhea of undigested grain with interior cold and exterior heat, sweating, and reversal: Tongmai Sini tang governs it. Formula 11.

Gancao 2 liang (mix-fried); fuzi, a large one, 1 piece (raw, remove the skin, break into eight pieces); ganjiang 3 liang (4 liang may be used for a strong person).

Boil the three ingredients above in 3 sheng of water to obtain 1 sheng 2 ge, remove the dregs, and take warm in two divided doses. When the pulse emerges at once, there is recovery.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Diarrhea of undigested grain is interior cold; unresolved body heat is exterior heat. Sweating means the yang qi is flowing outward, so there should be no reversal; sweating with reversal means the yang qi is greatly vacuous. Tongmai Sini tang is given to secure the yang qi.

## Notes

Like Clause 317 in the shaoyin chapter, this uses Tongmai Sini tang for yin exuberance repelling yang (true cold with false heat). Sweating with cold extremities is regarded as a sign that yang qi is leaking outward and about to desert.
