# Clause 353 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When after great sweating the heat does not leave, with internal tension, pain of the four limbs, and also diarrhea, reversal counterflow, and aversion to cold, Sini tang governs it. Formula 5.

Gancao 2 liang (mix-fried); ganjiang 1.5 liang; fuzi 1 piece (used raw, remove the skin, break into eight pieces).

Boil the three ingredients above in 3 sheng of water to obtain 1 sheng 2 ge, remove the dregs, and take warm in two divided doses. For a strong person, one large fuzi and ganjiang 3 liang may be used.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

After great sweating the heat should leave; when the heat, on the contrary, does not leave, it is yang collapse. Internal tension and diarrhea mean the cold is severe in the interior. Pain of the four limbs, reversal counterflow, and aversion to cold mean the cold is severe in the exterior. Sini tang is given to restore yang and dissipate cold.

## Notes

The clause uses Sini tang when great sweating has lost yang qi, and although a feeling of heat remains, the yin-cold signs of abdominal tension, diarrhea, reversal counterflow, and aversion to cold are present. The remaining heat is often interpreted as vacuous yang floating outward.
