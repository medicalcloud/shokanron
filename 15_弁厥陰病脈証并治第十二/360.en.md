# Clause 360 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Diarrhea with slight heat and thirst and a weak pulse will soon recover by itself.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Diarrhea is a disease of yin cold; great heat, on the contrary, is adverse. Slight heat with thirst means the interior qi is just becoming warm. The classic says: "All weakness with fever and a weak pulse means the yang qi has been able to return." It will certainly soon recover by itself. [The quoted phrase "諸弱発熱" is hard to construe and is translated literally.]

## Notes

Clauses around 360–375 are regarded as a group dealing with the prognosis and treatment of diarrhea. This clause states that when slight heat and thirst appear in vacuity-cold diarrhea and the pulse is gentle (weak), it is a sign that yang qi has moderately returned, and it will recover by itself.
