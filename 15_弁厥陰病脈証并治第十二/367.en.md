# Clause 367 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Diarrhea with a rapid pulse and thirst will soon recover by itself; if it does not get better, there will certainly be discharge of pus and blood, because there is heat.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The classic says: "When the rapid pulse does not resolve and the diarrhea does not stop, there will certainly be heat-associated stool with pus and blood."

## Notes

A rapid pulse and thirst during diarrhea indicate yang returning and are signs of recovery, but if the return overshoots and heat remains, there will be stool with pus and blood. It has the same purport as Clause 258 in the yangming chapter, which Cheng Wuji cites.
