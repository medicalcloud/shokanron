# Clause 342 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In cold damage with reversal for four days, heat, on the contrary, for three days, and reversal again for five days, the disease is advancing. Cold is more and heat less; the yang qi is retreating; therefore it is advancing.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

In cold damage where yin prevails, there is reversal first. By the fourth day the evil is transmitted inward; doubled yin must become yang, so [yang] withdraws and there is heat for three days. By the seventh day the transmission through the channels is complete and it should recover. If it does not recover and reversal returns, it is transmitted through the channels a second time, and by the fourth day there should be heat again. If there is no heat again and by the fifth day the reversal is not removed, yin overcomes yang, and the disease is advancing. [The phrase "重陰必陽却熱三日" in the commentary is hard to construe; this is a tentative translation.]

## Notes

The counterpart of the previous clause. If the days of reversal exceed those of heat, yin prevails and yang qi is retreating, so the disease is worsening.
