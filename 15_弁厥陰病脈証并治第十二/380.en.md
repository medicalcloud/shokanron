# Clause 380 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In cold damage, after great vomiting and great purgation, with extreme vacuity, and again extreme sweating, the person's outer qi is depressed; when water is further given to induce sweating, hiccup results. The reason is that the stomach is cold.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Great vomiting and great purgation make the stomach qi extremely vacuous; extreme sweating again further loses yang qi. When the external evil is depressed in the exterior, the body is hot, so the physician gives water to induce sweating. The vacuous stomach receiving water, vacuity and cold contend and produce hiccup.

## Notes

When a person whose stomach yang is extremely vacuous from repeated mistaken vomiting, purgation, and sweating is further given water to induce sweating, the stomach cold intensifies and produces hiccup.
