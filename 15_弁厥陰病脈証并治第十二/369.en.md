# Clause 369 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cold damage with diarrhea more than ten times a day, when the pulse is, on the contrary, replete, is fatal.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Diarrhea means the interior is vacuous, and the pulse should be faint and weak. When it is, on the contrary, replete, the disease overcomes the viscera; therefore it is fatal. The *Nanjing* says: "When the pulse does not correspond to the disease and the disease does not correspond to the pulse, it is a fatal disease."

## Notes

When the pulse is replete (forceful) even though violent diarrhea should have caused vacuity, it is an adverse pattern in which pulse and signs do not match, a sign of exuberant evil and declining upright qi, and the clause deems it fatal.
