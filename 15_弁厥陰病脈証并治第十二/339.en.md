# Clause 339 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In cold damage with little heat, slight reversal, cold fingertips (one version writes "slightly" for "fingers"), taciturnity with no desire to eat, and vexation and agitation: when after several days urination is free and white in color, the heat has been removed; if one desires food, the disease will recover. If there are reversal with retching and vexing fullness of the chest and rib-sides, there will certainly be bloody stool afterward.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Cold fingertips mean the reversal is slight and the heat little. Taciturnity with no desire to eat and vexation and agitation mean the evil heat has just been transmitted inward. After several days, white urine means the interior heat has gone, and the desire for food means the stomach qi has already been harmonized, so the disease will recover. The jueyin vessel flanks the stomach, penetrates the diaphragm, and spreads over the rib-sides. Reversal with retching and vexing fullness of the chest and rib-sides mean the heat of the transmitted evil is severe in the interior. The jueyin liver governs blood; if after several days the heat does not leave and cannot drain outward, it presses the blood downward and will certainly cause bloody stool. [The commentary's "市脅肋" is read as "布脅肋" and "巳和" as "已和."]

## Notes

The clause describes two courses of mild heat reversal. If the urine becomes clear and appetite returns, the heat has gone and there is recovery; if reversal, retching, and vexing fullness of the chest and rib-sides persist, the heat presses on the blood aspect of the jueyin and causes bloody stool.
