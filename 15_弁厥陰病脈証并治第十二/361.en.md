# Clause 361 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Diarrhea with a rapid pulse, slight heat, and sweating will soon recover by itself; if [the pulse] is tight again (one version says: "if the pulse is floating and again tight"), it has not yet resolved.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Diarrhea is a yin disease; a rapid pulse is a yang pulse; when a yang pulse appears in yin disease, there is life. Slight heat and sweating mean the yang qi has been able to flow through. The diarrhea will certainly recover by itself. All tightness is cold; if the pulse is tight again, the yin qi still prevails, so it is said not yet to have resolved.

## Notes

Like the previous clause, the appearance of a rapid pulse, slight heat, and sweating during diarrhea is a sign of yang returning and recovery, while persistence of a tight (cold) pulse means it has not yet recovered.
