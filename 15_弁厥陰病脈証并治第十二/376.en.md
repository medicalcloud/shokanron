# Clause 376 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When a person prone to retching has a welling-abscess with pus, the retching must not be treated; when the pus is exhausted, it recovers by itself.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

When there is a welling-abscess in the stomach duct, there is retching and vomiting of pus. The retching must not be treated. When retching has expelled all the pus, the retching also [the commentary breaks off here; it is understood as "the retching also recovers by itself"].

## Notes

Retching that expels pus from an internal abscess is a reaction discharging the pathogen, so it must not be stopped; when the pus is exhausted there is spontaneous recovery. It is cited as an example of the idea that one should look at the cause behind a symptom.
