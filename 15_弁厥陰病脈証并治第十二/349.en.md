# Clause 349 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cold damage with a skipping pulse ("skipping" is written "loose" in one version) and reversal counterflow of the hands and feet may be treated with moxibustion.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

A skipping pulse means the yang is vacuous and does not continue. Reversal counterflow means the yang is vacuous and does not connect. Moxibustion is applied to assist the yang qi.

## Notes

A skipping pulse (rapid with occasional pauses) with cold extremities is attributed to vacuity of yang qi, and moxibustion is used to assist yang. Since the skipping pulse generally often indicates yang exuberance or heat, interpretations here vary.
