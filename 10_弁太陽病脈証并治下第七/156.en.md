# Clause 156 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

(Originally because of purgation) glomus below the heart: Xiexin tang was given, but the glomus did not resolve; when the person is thirsty with a dry mouth, vexed, and has inhibited urination, Wuling san governs it. 19. (One formula says: if this is endured for one day, [the patient] recovers.) Use the formula of the seventh pattern above.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Since the glomus formed after purgation, Xiexin tang should be given to remove it. If after taking it the glomus does not resolve, and the person is thirsty with a dry mouth, vexed, and has inhibited urination, water-rheum is amassed within and the fluids do not move; it is not heat glomus. Giving Wuling san to induce sweating and disperse the water will cure it. "If this is endured for one day, he recovers" in one formula means that by not drinking water, no external water enters, the collected water can move, and the glomus is also cured.

## Notes

Even with glomus below the heart, if Xiexin tang does not cure it and there are thirst and inhibited urination, it is glomus due to collected water (water glomus), and Wuling san is used. It is taken as an example of choosing the formula according to the cause of the glomus. "Endure it for one day" is understood as refraining from drinking water for a day (as Cheng Wuji also says).
