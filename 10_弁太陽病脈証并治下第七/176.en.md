# Clause 176 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In cold damage, when the pulse is floating and slippery, it is because there is heat in the exterior and cold in the interior; Baihu tang (White Tiger Decoction) governs it. Formula 38.

Zhimu 6 liang; shigao 1 jin (crushed); gancao 2 liang (mix-fried); jingmi 6 ge.

Of the four ingredients above, with 1 dou of water boil until the rice is cooked and the decoction is done, remove the dregs, and take 1 sheng warm, three times a day. Your servants [Lin] Yi and others respectfully note: the earlier chapter says, "When heat is bound in the interior and both exterior and interior are hot, Baihu tang governs it," and also, "When the exterior has not resolved, Baihu tang must not be given." Here, where it says "the pulse is floating and slippery, with heat in the exterior and cold in the interior," the characters "exterior" and "interior" must have been switched. Further, a yangming pattern says, "With a floating, slow pulse, heat in the exterior and cold in the interior, Sini tang governs it," and a shaoyin pattern says, "With cold in the interior and heat outside, Tongmai Sini tang governs it." From this it is clear that "exterior" and "interior" have been switched. The *Qianjin yi*'s reading "Baitong tang" is wrong.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Floating means in the exterior; slippery means in the interior. "Heat in the exterior" means there is heat outside. "Cold in the interior" means some evil qi has been transmitted inward; since the evil has not yet entered the bowel, it is called merely "cold"—as the Guadi san pattern says, "there is cold above the chest." Baihu tang is given to resolve the evil inside and out.

(Nature and flavor) Zhimu: bitter, cold. Shigao: sweet, cold. Gancao: sweet, neutral. Jingmi: sweet, neutral.

The *Neijing* says: "Where heat excess prevails, assist with bitter and sweet." The bitter sweetness of zhimu and shigao disperses heat. Heat damages the qi, so moderate it with sweet—the sweetness of gancao and jingmi boosts the qi.

## Notes

The clause on Baihu tang, used when yangming heat is exuberant in both exterior and interior.

- "Heat in the exterior and cold in the interior" does not fit the Baihu tang pattern (heat in both), and has long been debated. Lin Yi and others think "exterior" and "interior" have been switched (heat in the interior, cold in the exterior); other views take "cold" to mean "evil" (close to Cheng Wuji) or regard it as an error for "heat in the interior, heat in the exterior."
- Baihu tang (zhimu, shigao, gancao, jingmi) is regarded as the representative formula for clearing great heat in the yangming qi aspect.
