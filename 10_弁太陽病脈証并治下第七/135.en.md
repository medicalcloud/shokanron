# Clause 135 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cold damage of six or seven days with chest bind and heat repletion, a deep and tight pulse, pain below the heart, and stony hardness on pressure: Da Xianxiong tang governs it. 3. Use the second formula above.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

When a disease in the exterior is purged, heat enters and thereby chest bind forms. Here it says not "after purgation" but "cold damage of six or seven days," so it is replete heat transmitted to the interior. Deep means in the interior; tight means interior repletion. Since there is pain below the heart that is replete and hard on pressure, it is chest bind. Da Xianxiong tang is given to purge the bound heat.

## Notes

Da Xianxiong tang is also used when chest bind forms through the natural course, with heat transmitted inward, not through mistaken purgation. A deep, tight pulse, pain below the heart, and stony hardness on pressure are called the "three signs of chest bind," the typical indications for Da Xianxiong tang.
