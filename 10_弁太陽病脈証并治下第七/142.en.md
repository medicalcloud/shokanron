# Clause 142 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In overlap disease of Taiyang and Shaoyang with stiffness and pain of the head and nape, or dizziness and veiling, at times like chest bind, with stuffiness and hardness below the heart, one should needle the first space of Dazhui, Feishu, and Ganshu, and must be careful not to induce sweating. If sweating is induced, there will be delirium and a wiry pulse; if the delirium does not stop after five days, one should needle Qimen. 8.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The Taiyang vessel connects with the head and descends the nape; stiffness and pain of the head and nape are Taiyang exterior disease. The Shaoyang vessel runs through the chest and connects with the rib-side; being like chest bind with stuffiness and hardness below the heart is Shaoyang interior disease. When Taiyang and Shaoyang merge into disease, it is not purely in the exterior, so the head and nape are not only stiff and painful but there may be dizziness and veiling; and since it has not yet fully entered the interior, at times it is like chest bind with stuffiness and hardness below the heart. This is pathogen between half exterior and half interior. Needling the first space of Dazhui and Feishu drains the Taiyang pathogen; needling Ganshu drains the Shaoyang pathogen. When the pathogen is in the exterior, sweating may be induced; when it is half exterior, half interior, sweating must not be induced. Sweating loses the fluids and damages and stirs the stomach qi, so the Shaoyang pathogen invades the stomach; earth is punished by wood, so there will certainly be delirium and a wiry pulse. By the fifth or sixth day, transmission through the meridians is complete, the pathogenic heat departs, and the delirium should stop. If it still does not stop, the Shaoyang pathogenic heat is severe; needle Qimen to drain the qi of liver and gallbladder.

## Notes

A clause treating overlap disease of Taiyang and Shaoyang with acupuncture. For pathogen between exterior and interior, sweating is prohibited, and Dazhui (GV-14) and Feishu (BL-13) and Ganshu (BL-18) (back transport points on the bladder meridian) are needled. If mistaken sweating leads to persistent delirium, Qimen is needled.
