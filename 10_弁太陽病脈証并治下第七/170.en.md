# Clause 170 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In cold damage with a floating pulse, fever, no sweating, and the exterior unresolved, Baihu tang must not be given. When there are thirst and a desire to drink water without an exterior pattern, Baihu jia Renshen tang governs it. 32. Use the formula above.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

In cold damage with a floating pulse, fever, no sweating, and the exterior unresolved: without thirst, Mahuang tang is appropriate; with thirst, Wuling san is appropriate; Baihu [tang] is not suitable. When there are great thirst and a desire for water without an exterior pattern, then Baihu jia Renshen tang may be given to disperse the interior heat. Practitioners facing the disease must distinguish carefully.

## Notes

A clause giving a contraindication of Baihu tang (when an exterior pattern remains). While there is an exterior pattern with a floating pulse, fever, and no sweating, Baihu tang is not used; Baihu jia Renshen tang is used only when there is no exterior pattern and thirst is intense.
