# Clause 130 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In visceral bind, when there is no yang pattern, no alternating chills and fever (one version says "cold without heat"), the person is, on the contrary, quiet, and the tongue coating is slippery, one must not attack.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Visceral bind should, by rule, be purged. No yang pattern means no heat in the exterior. No alternating chills and fever means no heat in the half-exterior half-interior. The person being, on the contrary, quiet means no heat in the interior. The Classic says, "Something like a coating on the tongue is because there is heat in the cinnabar field and cold in the chest." Since both exterior and interior are cold, one must not attack.

## Notes

In visceral bind, when there are no heat signs in the exterior, half-exterior half-interior, or interior and the whole is cold, one must not attack by purgation. Since it is a binding of yin cold, purging would further damage the yang.
