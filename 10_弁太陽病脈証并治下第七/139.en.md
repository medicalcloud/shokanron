# Clause 139 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease of two or three days with inability to lie down and a desire only to sit up, and certainly binding below the heart: if the pulse is faint and weak, there is originally a cold aspect. If it is purged instead, and the diarrhea stops, there will certainly be chest bind; if it does not stop and is purged again on the fourth day, this becomes heat-assisted diarrhea.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

On the second or third day of taiyang disease the evil is in the exterior. Inability to lie down, a desire only to sit up, and certain binding below the heart: since below the heart is bound and full, lying down blocks the qi and makes it worse, so one cannot lie down and wants only to sit up. Binding fullness below the heart may have a water aspect, a cold aspect, or a qi aspect; here the pulse is faint and weak, so one knows there is originally a cold aspect. If the physician, seeing binding below the heart, instead purges, the taiyang exterior evil takes advantage of the vacuity to enter the interior. If the diarrhea stops, the evil qi remains bound and forms chest bind. If the diarrhea does not stop and on the next day it is purged again as before, and the diarrhea still does not stop, the evil heat has descended to attack the intestines and stomach—heat-assisted diarrhea.

## Notes

A clause describing two courses (chest bind and heat-assisted diarrhea) after mistakenly purging someone who originally had cold (such as water-rheum) with binding below the heart. Its meaning is difficult, and the interpretation of "cold aspect" and other points is debated.
