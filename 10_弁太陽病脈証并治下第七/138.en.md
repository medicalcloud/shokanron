# Clause 138 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Minor chest bind is right below the heart and painful on pressure; when the pulse is floating and slippery, Xiao Xianxiong tang (Minor Chest-Draining Decoction) governs it. 6.

Huanglian 1 liang; banxia half a sheng (washed); gualoushi one large fruit.

Of the three ingredients above, with 6 sheng of water first boil the gualou down to 3 sheng, remove the dregs, add the other ingredients, boil down to 2 sheng, remove the dregs, and take warm in three doses.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Hardness and pain below the heart that cannot be approached by the hand is chest bind. Right below the heart and painful on pressure means the heat qi is still shallow; it is called minor chest bind. In chest bind the pulse is deep and tight, or floating at cun and deep at guan; here the pulse is floating and slippery, so one knows the heat is not yet deeply bound. Xiao Xianxiong tang is given to remove the bound heat above the chest and diaphragm.

(Nature and flavor) Huanglian: bitter, cold. Banxia: acrid, warm. Gualoushi: bitter, cold.

Discharge with bitter; disperse with acrid. Huanglian and gualoushi, bitter and cold, discharge heat; the acridity of banxia disperses the binding.

## Notes

The clause on Xiao Xianxiong tang, used for a mild chest bind (minor chest bind).

- Whereas major chest bind is hard from below the heart to the lower abdomen and too painful to touch, minor chest bind is confined to below the heart and painful only on pressure; the pulse is floating and slippery rather than deep and tight.
- Xiao Xianxiong tang (huanglian, banxia, gualoushi) is regarded as removing bound phlegm and heat and has been widely applied in later times to oppression in the chest and coughs with much phlegm.
