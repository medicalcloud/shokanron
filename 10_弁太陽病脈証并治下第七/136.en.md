# Clause 136 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cold damage of ten-odd days with heat bound in the interior and again alternating fever and chills: give Da Chaihu tang (Daisaikoto). When there is only chest bind without great heat, this is water bound in the chest and rib-side; when there is only slight sweating from the head, Da Xianxiong tang (Taikankyoto) governs it. 4. Use the second formula above.

Da Chaihu tang
Chaihu (saiko) half a jin; zhishi (kijitsu) 4 pieces (mix-fried); shengjiang (shokyo) 5 liang (sliced); huangqin (ogon) 3 liang; shaoyao (shakuyaku) 3 liang; banxia (hange) half a sheng (washed); dazao (taiso) 12 pieces (split).

Of the seven ingredients above, boil with 1 dou 2 sheng of water down to 6 sheng, remove the dregs, decoct again, and take 1 sheng warm, three times a day. One version adds 2 liang of dahuang (daio); if it is not added, it probably could not be called Da Chaihu tang.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Heat bound in the interior after ten-odd days of cold damage is a pattern to be purged. Alternating fever and chills again mean the healthy qi and pathogen are contending separately and it has not fully contracted and bound, so Da Chaihu tang is given to purge it. When there is only chest bind without great heat, it is not heat binding but water-fluid retention bound in the chest and rib-side, called water chest bind. If the whole body sweats, the water-fluid retention disperses outward and [the patient] recovers. If there is only slight sweating from the head and none elsewhere, the water-fluid retention cannot drain outward but collects, amasses, and does not move. Da Xianxiong tang is given to expel the water.

## Notes

A clause contrasting the Da Chaihu tang pattern (interior heat binding plus alternating fever and chills) with the Da Xianxiong tang pattern (water chest bind: water and heat bound in the chest and rib-side).

- If there are alternating fever and chills, a Shaoyang pattern remains and Da Chaihu tang is used; if there are no alternating fever and chills but only chest bind with sweating from the head, it is water chest bind and Da Xianxiong tang is used.
- "No great heat" is taken to mean no great heat on the body surface (the heat is bound inside).
