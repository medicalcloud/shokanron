# Clause 145 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

A woman with cold damage and fever, whose menses happen to come, who is clear during the day but has delirious speech in the evening, as if seeing ghosts: this is heat entering the blood chamber. If the stomach qi and the upper two burners are not violated, she will certainly recover spontaneously. 11.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Fever in cold damage means the cold has already become heat. When the menses happen to come, the blood chamber is empty, and the evil heat takes advantage of the vacuity to enter it. Delirious speech in the daytime would mean the evil lodges in the bowel and the yang contends. Here she is clear in the daytime but has delirious speech in the evening as if seeing ghosts, meaning the evil has not entered the bowel but the blood chamber, and the yin contends. Delirious speech from exuberant yang should be purged, but this is heat entering the blood chamber, so purgative medicine must not be given to violate the stomach qi. When heat entering the blood chamber has bound the blood into replete heat, Xiao Chaihu tang is given to disperse the evil and induce sweating; here, although heat has entered the blood chamber, it is not lodged and bound, so sweating medicine must not be given to violate the upper burner. When heat entering the blood chamber causes fullness of the chest and rib-side like chest bind, Qimen may be needled; here, although heat has entered the blood chamber, there is no fullness or binding, so Qimen must not be needled to violate the middle burner. She will certainly recover spontaneously because, as the menses flow, the heat leaves with the blood; when the blood has been discharged, the evil heat is completely removed and she recovers. Sweating violates the upper burner because sweating stirs the defensive qi, and the defensive qi issues from the upper burner. Needling Qimen violates the middle burner because needling Qimen stirs the nutritive qi, and the nutritive qi issues from the middle burner. The *Maijing* says, "If the stomach qi and the upper two burners are not violated, she will certainly recover spontaneously"—how could this refer only to medicines and not to needles?

## Notes

Even with heat entering the blood chamber, if the menses come smoothly and the blood is discharged, the heat leaves with the blood; so if one watches the course without damaging the upright qi by purgation, sweating, or the like, she will recover spontaneously. Delirious speech that appears at night is taken as a sign that the evil is in the blood (yin) aspect. "The upper two burners" are generally taken as the upper and middle burners; Cheng Wuji explains that sweating violates the upper burner and needling Qimen the middle burner.
