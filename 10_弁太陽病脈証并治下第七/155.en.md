# Clause 155 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Glomus below the heart with, in addition, aversion to cold and sweating: Fuzi Xiexin tang (Aconite Heart-Draining Decoction) governs it. Formula 18.

Dahuang 2 liang; huanglian 1 liang; huangqin 1 liang; fuzi 1 piece (blast-fried, remove the skin, break it, boil separately and take the juice).

Of the four ingredients above, cut three and steep them in 2 sheng of boiling water for a short while, wring out and remove the dregs, add the fuzi juice, and take warm in two doses.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Glomus below the heart means vacuous heat hidden within. Aversion to cold with sweating means the yang qi is vacuous outside. Xiexin tang is given to attack the glomus, and fuzi is added to secure the yang.

## Notes

The clause on Fuzi Xiexin tang, used when yang vacuity (aversion to cold, sweating) is added to heat glomus (the Dahuang Huanglian Xiexin tang pattern).

- Its characteristic is separate preparation of cold and hot medicines: the three yellows (dahuang, huanglian, huangqin) are merely steeped in boiling water to clear heat, while fuzi is decocted separately and thoroughly to warm the yang.
- It is often cited as an example of using cold and hot medicines together for a pattern mixing cold and heat, vacuity and repletion.
