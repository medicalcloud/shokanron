# Clause 153 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease: when the physician has induced sweating, followed by fever and aversion to cold, and then purged as well, there is glomus below the heart; exterior and interior are both vacuous, the qi of yin and yang are both exhausted; without yang there is yin alone. If red-hot needling is then added, causing vexation in the chest, with a green-yellow facial color and twitching skin, it is difficult to treat. If now the color is slightly yellow and the hands and feet are warm, it is easy to cure.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

In taiyang disease, fever and aversion to cold following sweating mean the yang qi is vacuous outside and the evil has not been removed. Then purging further vacuates the interior; the vacuous evil in the exterior sinks inward and is transmitted to below the heart, forming glomus. Sweating vacuating the exterior is exhausting the yang; purging vacuating the interior is exhausting the yin. The exterior pattern having ended is "no yang"; glomus in the interior is "yin alone." When red-hot needling is added, the vacuous [body] cannot withstand the fire, and the fire qi attacks within, causing vexation in the chest. In cold-damage diseases yang is primary. When the face is green and the flesh twitches, the yang qi is greatly vacuous; hence "difficult to treat." If the face is slightly yellow and the hands and feet warm, the yang qi has been restored; hence "easy to cure."

## Notes

A clause judging, by facial color and the warmth of the hands and feet, the prognosis of a patient made vacuous in exterior and interior, yin and yang, by repeated mistreatment (sweating, purgation, red-hot needling). A green-yellow face with twitching skin shows great vacuity of yang qi and is hard to treat; a slightly yellow face with warm hands and feet shows the stomach qi (yang qi) recovering and is easy to cure.
