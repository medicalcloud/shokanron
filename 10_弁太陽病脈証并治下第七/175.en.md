# Clause 175 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Wind and dampness contend: the joints ache with vexation, there is pulling pain with inability to bend and stretch, the pain becomes severe when approached (touched), there are sweating and shortness of breath, inhibited urination, aversion to wind with no wish to take off clothes, or slight swelling of the body: Gancao Fuzi tang (Licorice and Aconite Decoction) governs it. Formula 37.

Gancao 2 liang (mix-fried); fuzi 2 pieces (blast-fried, remove the skin, broken); baizhu 2 liang; guizhi 4 liang (remove the bark).

Of the four ingredients above, boil with 6 sheng of water down to 3 sheng, remove the dregs, and take 1 sheng warm, three times a day. If with the first dose a slight sweat is obtained, it resolves. For one who can eat but whose sweating has stopped and who is vexed again, give 5 ge. For one for whom 1 sheng may be too much, it is appropriate to begin with 6 or 7 ge.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Wind damages the defensive; dampness flows into the joints. When wind and dampness contend, the two evils disorder the channels, so the joints ache with vexation, there is pulling pain with inability to bend and stretch, and the pain becomes severe when approached. When wind prevails, the defensive qi is not secure, so there are sweating, shortness of breath, and aversion to wind with no wish to take off clothes—this is wind in the exterior. When dampness prevails, water qi does not move, so there are inhibited urination or slight swelling of the body—this is dampness pressing on the outside. Gancao Fuzi tang is given to disperse dampness and secure the defensive qi.

(Nature and flavor) Gancao: sweet, neutral. Fuzi: acrid, hot. Baizhu: sweet, warm. Guizhi: acrid, hot.

The acrid sweetness of guizhi and gancao effuses and disperses the wind evil and secures the defensive; the acrid sweetness of fuzi and baizhu resolves the damp qi and warms the channels.

## Notes

The clause on Gancao Fuzi tang, used when wind-damp is bound deep in the joints with violent joint pain that cannot bear touch. The disease is regarded as deeper than the Guizhi Fuzi tang pattern of Clause 174, reaching the joints. Fuzi and baizhu remove dampness, and guizhi and gancao free the yang qi. In later times it has been applied to rheumatism-like pain and the like.
