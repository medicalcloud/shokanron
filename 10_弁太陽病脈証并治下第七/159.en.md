# Clause 159 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In cold damage, after taking a decoction, there is incessant diarrhea with glomus and hardness below the heart; after Xiexin tang has been taken, it is purged again with another medicine, and the diarrhea does not stop. The physician gives Lizhong [tang], and the diarrhea becomes even worse. Lizhong regulates the middle burner, but this diarrhea is in the lower burner; Chishizhi Yuyuliang tang (Halloysite and Limonite Decoction) governs it. If it still does not stop, urination should be promoted. Chishizhi Yuyuliang tang formula. 22.

Chishizhi (halloysite) 1 jin (crushed); taiyi yuyuliang (limonite) 1 jin (crushed).

Of the two ingredients above, boil with 6 sheng of water down to 2 sheng, remove the dregs, and take warm in three doses.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

When in cold damage, after purgation with a decoction, the diarrhea does not stop and there are glomus and hardness below the heart, the qi is vacuous and visiting qi counterflows upward. Giving Xiexin tang to attack it would end the glomus, but the physician purged again with another medicine, further vacuating the interior, so the diarrhea did not stop. Lizhong wan cures diarrhea due to spleen-stomach vacuity cold; here the lower burner is vacuous, so giving it makes the diarrhea worse. The *Shengji jing* says: "Slipperiness means the qi deserts, and one wants to contract it. When the intestines are open with throughflux diarrhea and loss of stool and urine, astringent preparations are used to contract it." This diarrhea is due to the lower burner failing to restrain, so Chishizhi Yuyuliang tang is given to astringe the throughflux. The lower burner governs the separation of clear and turbid; diarrhea means water and grain are not separated. If after taking the astringent preparation the diarrhea still does not stop, urination should be promoted to separate the qi.

(Nature and flavor) Chishizhi: sweet, warm. Yuyuliang: sweet, neutral.

The *Bencao* says: "Astringent can remove desertion; heavy can remove timidity." The astringency of chishizhi contracts it; the heaviness of yuyuliang settles and secures it.

## Notes

A clause showing stepwise treatment that changes the formula according to the location of the diarrhea.

- When diarrhea does not stop after mistaken purgation, Lizhong tang is used for vacuity cold of the middle burner (spleen and stomach); if it worsens, the diarrhea is regarded as slippery desertion of an unrestraining lower burner, and the astringent Chishizhi Yuyuliang tang stops it.
- If it still does not stop, it is attributed to water and grain not being separated, and urination is promoted ("promote urination to firm the stool").
- It is valued as an example of reconsidering the location of the pattern during treatment.
