# Clause 165 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cold damage with fever not resolved by sweating, glomus and hardness in the heart, vomiting, and diarrhea: Da Chaihu tang governs it. 27. Use the fourth formula above.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Fever in cold damage means the cold has already become heat. Not resolved by sweating means the exterior is harmonized but the interior is diseased. Vomiting and diarrhea with softness of the heart and abdomen is interior vacuity; vomiting and diarrhea with glomus and hardness of the heart and abdomen is interior repletion. Da Chaihu tang is given to purge the interior heat.

## Notes

Even with vomiting and diarrhea, if below the heart there is glomus and hardness (repletion), Da Chaihu tang is used to purge despite the diarrhea. It is taken as an example of judging not by the presence of diarrhea but by the hardness (repletion) below the heart ("treating flux by promoting flux").
