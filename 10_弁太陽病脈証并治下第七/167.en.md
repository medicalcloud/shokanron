# Clause 167 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When in a disease there has long been glomus below the rib-side, extending to beside the navel, with pain pulling into the lower abdomen and entering the genital sinews, this is called visceral bind; [the patient] dies. 29.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

There was originally a long-standing accumulation bound below the rib-side as glomus. Now, through cold damage, the evil qi enters the interior and assists the old accumulation, binding the true qi of the viscera so that it does not flow; hence it extends to beside the navel, the pain pulls into the lower abdomen and enters the genital sinews, and [the patient] dies.

## Notes

A clause on a severe (fatal) form of visceral bind. When external evil is added in someone who already has glomus (an accumulation mass) below the rib-side, and the pain extends from beside the navel to the lower abdomen and genitals, the visceral qi is regarded as bound and blocked—a dangerous state.
