# Clause 150 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Overlap disease of taiyang and shaoyang that is instead purged forms chest bind, with hardness below the heart, incessant diarrhea, inability to get down water or drink, and vexation of the heart.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Overlap disease of taiyang and shaoyang is evil qi half exterior, half interior. If it is instead purged, the evil of both channels takes advantage of the vacuity to enter. The taiyang exterior evil enters the interior and binds in the chest, forming chest bind with hardness below the heart. The shaoyang interior evil takes advantage of the vacuity to descend and invade the intestines and stomach, and the diarrhea does not stop. If the evil bound in the yin aspect, eating and drinking would be as usual and it would be visceral bind. Here the yang evil is bound within, so water and drink cannot get down and there is vexation.

## Notes

This describes the grave deterioration from mistakenly purging overlap disease of taiyang and shaoyang (for which purgation should be prohibited, as in Clause 142). Chest bind with added diarrhea and inability to take even water is regarded as a dangerous state with the upright qi greatly damaged. No formula is given.
