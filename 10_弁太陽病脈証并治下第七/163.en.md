# Clause 163 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease: when the external pattern has not been removed but it is repeatedly purged, so that heat-assisted diarrhea follows, the diarrhea does not stop, there are glomus and hardness below the heart, and exterior and interior are unresolved, Guizhi Renshen tang (Cinnamon Twig and Ginseng Decoction) governs it. Formula 25.

Guizhi 4 liang (cut separately); gancao 4 liang (mix-fried); baizhu 3 liang; renshen 3 liang; ganjiang 3 liang.

Of the five ingredients above, with 9 sheng of water first boil four of them down to 5 sheng, add the gui, boil again down to 3 sheng, remove the dregs, and take 1 sheng warm, twice by day and once at night.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Repeatedly purging while the external pattern has not been removed doubly vacuates the interior; evil heat takes advantage of the vacuity to enter, the interior vacuity combines with heat, and the diarrhea does not stop, with glomus below the heart. If the exterior has resolved and there are diarrhea and glomus below the heart, Xiexin tang may be given. If there is no diarrhea, the exterior is unresolved, and there is glomus below the heart, one may first resolve the exterior and then attack the glomus. Here both exterior and interior are unresolved, so Guizhi Renshen tang is given to harmonize the interior and resolve the exterior.

(Nature and flavor) Guizhi: acrid, hot. Gancao: sweet, neutral. Baizhu: sweet, neutral. Renshen: sweet, warm. Ganjiang: acrid, hot.

When the exterior has not resolved, disperse it with acrid; when the interior is insufficient, moderate it with sweet. Here the interior qi is greatly vacuous and neither exterior nor interior has resolved, so guizhi and gancao are added to Lizhong tang.

## Notes

The clause on Guizhi Renshen tang, used when repeated mistaken purgation with the exterior pattern still present has made the spleen and stomach vacuous and cold, with incessant diarrhea and glomus below the heart.

- It is Lizhong tang (renshen, baizhu, ganjiang, gancao) with guizhi added, regarded as warming interior vacuity cold while resolving the exterior.
- The "heat" in "heat-assisted diarrhea" is generally interpreted as the exterior fever, the interior being cold.
- Adding guizhi later is explained as preserving its qi to resolve the exterior.
