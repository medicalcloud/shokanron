# Clause 157 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When, after sweating in cold damage has resolved [the disease], the stomach is not in harmony, with glomus and hardness below the heart, dry belching with the smell of food, water qi below the rib-side, thunderous rumbling in the abdomen, and diarrhea, Shengjiang Xiexin tang (Fresh Ginger Heart-Draining Decoction) governs it. Formula 20.

Shengjiang 4 liang (sliced); gancao 3 liang (mix-fried); renshen 3 liang; ganjiang 1 liang; huangqin 3 liang; banxia half a sheng (washed); huanglian 1 liang; dazao 12 pieces (split).

Of the eight ingredients above, boil with 1 dou of water down to 6 sheng, remove the dregs, decoct again down to 3 sheng, and take 1 sheng warm, three times a day. Fuzi Xiexin tang was originally said to "add fuzi." Banxia Xiexin tang and Gancao Xiexin tang are the same body with different names. Shengjiang Xiexin tang was originally said to be "Lizhong Renshen Huangqin tang with guizhi and zhu removed and huanglian added, combined with the method of draining the liver."

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The stomach is the master of the fluids and the root of yang qi. After heavy sweating, the fluids are lost outside, the stomach is empty, and visiting qi counterflows upward, causing glomus and hardness below the heart. The *Jingui yaolüe* says: "When the qi of the middle burner is not yet harmonized and cannot disperse grain, it causes belching." Dry belching with the smell of food means the stomach is vacuous and does not digest the grain. Water qi below the rib-side and thunderous rumbling in the abdomen mean earth is weak and cannot overcome water. Xiexin tang is given to attack the glomus, and shengjiang is added to boost the stomach.

## Notes

The clause on Shengjiang Xiexin tang, used when after sweating the stomach qi has weakened and glomus below the heart is accompanied by water-qi signs such as belching with the smell of food, abdominal rumbling, and diarrhea.

- It is Banxia Xiexin tang with less ganjiang and a large amount of shengjiang added, regarded as strengthening the power to disperse water qi and harmonize the stomach.
- The note after the formula, on the relations among the Xiexin tang group (Fuzi Xiexin tang, Banxia Xiexin tang, Gancao Xiexin tang, and Lizhong tang), is regarded as a later addition.
