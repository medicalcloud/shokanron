# Clause 164 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In cold damage, when after heavy purgation sweating is again induced, and there are glomus below the heart and aversion to cold, the exterior has not yet resolved; one must not attack the glomus but should first resolve the exterior, and only when the exterior has resolved may the glomus be attacked. To resolve the exterior, Guizhi tang is appropriate; to attack the glomus, Dahuang Huanglian Xiexin tang is appropriate. 26. For Xiexin tang use the seventeenth formula above.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Sweating induced again after heavy purgation should have ended all the evil of exterior and interior. Here there are glomus below the heart and aversion to cold, so the evil of both exterior and interior has not resolved. Because it was purged while the exterior was unresolved, glomus formed below the heart. First give Guizhi tang to resolve the exterior; when the exterior has resolved, give Dahuang Huanglian Xiexin tang to attack the glomus. The *Neijing* says: "What enters from outside to inside and becomes exuberant inside—first treat the outside, then regulate the inside."

## Notes

When an exterior pattern (aversion to cold) and heat glomus coexist, first the exterior is resolved with Guizhi tang and then the glomus attacked with Dahuang Huanglian Xiexin tang—a concrete illustration of the principle "exterior first, then interior." Since sweating and purgation have already been applied, Guizhi tang rather than Mahuang tang is used to resolve the exterior.
