# Clause 151 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the pulse is floating and tight and it is then purged, the tightness, on the contrary, enters the interior and stuffiness forms; on pressure it is soft—it is only a qi stuffiness.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Of the floating and tight [pulse], floating means the yang is damaged, tight means the yin is damaged; sweating should be induced, but it is purged instead. If the floating enters the interior, it is yang pathogen entering the interior and forms chest bind. If the floating does not enter the interior but the tightness does, it is yin pathogen entering the interior and forms stuffiness.

## Notes

A clause on the formation and features of stuffiness. When an exterior pattern is mistakenly purged and the pathogen enters below the heart, stuffiness forms; stuffiness is soft on pressure, not a tangible binding (chest bind) but stagnation of qi (qi stuffiness). Hardness and pain on pressure are regarded as the points distinguishing chest bind from stuffiness.
