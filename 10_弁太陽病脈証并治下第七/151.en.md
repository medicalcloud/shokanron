# Clause 151 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the pulse is floating and tight and it is then purged, the tightness, on the contrary, enters the interior and glomus forms; on pressure it is soft—it is only a qi glomus.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Of the floating and tight [pulse], floating means the yang is damaged, tight means the yin is damaged; sweating should be induced, but it is purged instead. If the floating enters the interior, it is yang evil entering the interior and forms chest bind. If the floating does not enter the interior but the tightness does, it is yin evil entering the interior and forms glomus.

## Notes

A clause on the formation and features of glomus. When an exterior pattern is mistakenly purged and the evil enters below the heart, glomus forms; glomus is soft on pressure, not a tangible binding (chest bind) but stagnation of qi (qi glomus). Hardness and pain on pressure are regarded as the points distinguishing chest bind from glomus.
