# Clause 133 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the chest-bind pattern is complete and there is restlessness, [the patient] also dies.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

That the chest-bind pattern is complete means the pathogen is already deeply bound. Restlessness means the healthy qi is scattered and disordered. The pathogenic qi overcomes the healthy qi, so the patient will certainly die.

## Notes

When restlessness is added to a fully developed chest-bind pattern, the pathogen is extremely exuberant and the healthy qi scattered—a dangerous state with poor prognosis. With Clause 132, it describes the fatal patterns of chest bind.
