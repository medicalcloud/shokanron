# Clause 133 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the chest-bind pattern is complete and there is vexation and agitation, [the patient] also dies.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

That the chest-bind pattern is complete means the evil is already deeply bound. Vexation and agitation mean the upright qi is scattered and disordered. The evil qi overcomes the upright, so the patient will certainly die.

## Notes

When vexation and agitation are added to a fully developed chest-bind pattern, the evil is extremely exuberant and the upright qi scattered—a dangerous state with poor prognosis. With Clause 132, it describes the fatal patterns of chest bind.
