# Clause 128 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Question: Among diseases there are chest bind (*jiexiong*) and visceral bind (*zangjie*); what are their characteristics? Answer: When it is painful on pressure, with a floating cun pulse and a deep guan pulse, it is called chest bind.

## Notes

The opening clause of the lower taiyang chapter, a question-and-answer defining "chest bind" in contrast with "visceral bind" (continued in Clause 129).

- Chest bind: evil (heat) bound from the chest to below the heart, painful on pressure. A floating cun pulse and a deep guan pulse are taken as its pulse signs.
- Cheng Wuji's commentary is given together with the next clause (129).
