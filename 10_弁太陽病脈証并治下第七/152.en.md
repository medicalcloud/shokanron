# Clause 152 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang wind-strike with diarrhea and retching counterflow: when the exterior has resolved, it may then be attacked. When the person sweats lightly, with attacks at fixed times, headache, glomus, hardness, and fullness below the heart, pain pulling to below the rib-side, dry retching, shortness of breath, and sweating without aversion to cold, this is the exterior resolved but the interior not yet harmonized; Shizao tang (Ten Jujubes Decoction) governs it. Formula 16.

Yuanhua (genkwa flower) (dry-fried); gansui; daji (euphorbia).

Take equal amounts of the three ingredients above, pound each separately into powder. With 1.5 sheng of water first boil ten plump jujubes down to 8 ge, remove the dregs, and add the powdered medicine: a strong person takes one qianbi, a weak person half a qian. Take it warm, at dawn. If there is little purgation and the disease is not removed, take it again the next day, adding half a qian. After satisfactory diarrhea, nourish oneself with gruel.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Diarrhea with retching counterflow means the interior has received the evil. When the evil is in the interior it may be purged, but one must wait until the exterior has resolved before attacking. Light sweating with attacks at fixed times and no aversion to cold mean the exterior has already resolved. Headache, glomus, hardness, and fullness below the heart, pain pulling to below the rib-side, dry retching, and shortness of breath mean evil heat is accumulated within with hidden rheum (water); this is the interior not yet harmonized. Shizao tang is given to purge heat and expel rheum.

(Nature and flavor) Yuanhua: acrid-bitter. Gansui: bitter, cold. Daji: bitter, cold. Dazao: sweet, warm.

Disperse with acrid: the acridity of yuanhua disperses rheum. Discharge with bitter: the bitterness of gansui and daji discharges water. Water is governed by the kidney; sweet is the flavor of the spleen; the sweetness of dazao boosts earth to overcome water.

## Notes

The clause on Shizao tang, used for "suspended rheum," water-rheum collected in the chest and rib-side.

- A drastic formula in which the strong purgative, water-expelling yuanhua, gansui, and daji are taken with a jujube decoction; it cautions that it should be used only after the exterior pattern has resolved.
- Even with symptoms resembling an exterior pattern, such as sweating and headache, if there is no aversion to cold, the exterior has already resolved and the symptoms are attributed to water-rheum.
- Detailed instructions for handling a drastic formula are given: take it on an empty stomach at dawn, adjust the dose to avoid excessive purgation, and recuperate with gruel afterward.
