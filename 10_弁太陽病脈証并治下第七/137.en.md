# Clause 137 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease: when sweating has been induced repeatedly and then purgation applied, with no bowel movement for five or six days, a dry tongue and thirst, slight tidal fever in the late afternoon (one version says "great vexation in the heart and chest in the late afternoon"), and hardness, fullness, and pain from below the heart to the lower abdomen so that it cannot be approached (touched), Da Xianxiong tang governs it. 5. Use the second formula above.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Repeated sweating followed by purgation doubly loses the fluids inside and out; the evil heat binds within, leading to no bowel movement for five or six days and a dry tongue with thirst. Tidal fever in the late afternoon belongs to the stomach; here there is only slight tidal fever in the late afternoon, so it is not only in the stomach. Hardness, fullness, and pain from below the heart to the lower abdomen that cannot be approached mean the evil qi is severe both above and below in the abdomen. Da Xianxiong tang is given to purge the evil.

## Notes

Da Xianxiong tang is used for a severe chest bind extending from below the heart to the lower abdomen. Although it is accompanied by signs resembling yangming bowel repletion (constipation, dry tongue, tidal fever), pain so severe that the area from below the heart to the lower abdomen cannot be touched is characteristic of chest bind and is regarded as the point distinguishing it from yangming disease (the Da Chengqi tang pattern).
