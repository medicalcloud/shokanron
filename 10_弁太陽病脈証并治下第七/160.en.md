# Clause 160 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In cold damage, when after vomiting and purgation sweating is induced, with deficiency vexation and an extremely feeble pulse, and after eight or nine days there are stuffiness and hardness below the heart, pain below the rib-side, qi surging up to the throat, dizziness and veiling, and twitching of the meridians and vessels, it will in time become wilting (*wei*).

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Inducing sweating after vomiting and purgation in cold damage makes the qi of both exterior and interior deficient. Deficiency vexation with an extremely feeble pulse means the healthy qi is deficient within and only the pathogenic qi remains. By the seventh or eighth day the healthy qi should recover and the pathogenic qi end; stuffiness below the heart, pain below the rib-side, qi surging up to the throat, and dizziness and veiling mean the healthy qi is deficient within and does not recover, while the pathogenic qi remains bound and does not leave. Twitching of the meridians and vessels means the qi of the meridians and collaterals is extremely deficient; in time the heat qi returns to the meridians, and there will certainly be wilting and weakness.

## Notes

This clause states that repeated mistreatment (vomiting, purgation, sweating) greatly damages yang qi and fluids so that the sinews and vessels, no longer nourished, twitch and, if prolonged, the limbs wilt. Some regard it as a further deterioration through mistreatment of the Ling Gui Zhu Gan tang pattern of Clause 67. No formula is given.
