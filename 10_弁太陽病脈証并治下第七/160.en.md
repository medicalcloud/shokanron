# Clause 160 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In cold damage, when after vomiting and purgation sweating is induced, with vacuity vexation and an extremely faint pulse, and after eight or nine days there are glomus and hardness below the heart, pain below the rib-side, qi surging up to the throat, dizziness and veiling, and twitching of the channels and vessels, it will in time become wilting (*wei*).

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Inducing sweating after vomiting and purgation in cold damage makes the qi of both exterior and interior vacuous. Vacuity vexation with an extremely faint pulse means the upright qi is vacuous within and only the evil qi remains. By the seventh or eighth day the upright qi should recover and the evil qi end; glomus below the heart, pain below the rib-side, qi surging up to the throat, and dizziness and veiling mean the upright qi is vacuous within and does not recover, while the evil qi remains bound and does not leave. Twitching of the channels and vessels means the qi of the channels and network vessels is extremely vacuous; in time the heat qi returns to the channels, and there will certainly be wilting and weakness.

## Notes

This clause states that repeated mistreatment (vomiting, purgation, sweating) greatly damages yang qi and fluids so that the sinews and vessels, no longer nourished, twitch and, if prolonged, the limbs wilt. Some regard it as a further deterioration through mistreatment of the Ling Gui Zhu Gan tang pattern of Clause 67. No formula is given.
