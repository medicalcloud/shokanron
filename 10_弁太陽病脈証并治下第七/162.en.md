# Clause 162 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

After purgation, Guizhi tang must not be used again. If there are sweating and panting without great heat, Mahuang Xingzi Gancao Shigao tang may be given. Formula 24.

Mahuang 4 liang; xingren 50 kernels (remove the skins and tips); gancao 2 liang (mix-fried); shigao half a jin (crushed, wrapped in cotton).

Of the four ingredients above, with 7 sheng of water first boil the mahuang, reducing by 2 sheng; remove the white froth, add the other ingredients, boil down to 3 sheng, remove the dregs, and take 1 sheng warm. Originally it said "yellow-ear cup."

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The sixteenth pattern of the third volume above says, "After sweating, Guizhi tang must not be used again; for sweating and panting without great heat...," and its treatment is the same as this pattern. Although sweating and purgation differ, they are alike in that the upright qi should not be damaged, and the transmission of the evil qi is also the same, so one method treats both. This is what the Classic means by "after sweating, or purgation, or vomiting."

## Notes

The same pattern as Clause 63 (after sweating), occurring after purgation, is also treated with Ma Xing Gan Shi tang. Whether after sweating or purgation, if heat is trapped in the lung with sweating and panting and no great heat on the body surface, the same formula is used—an example of the idea that "the same pattern calls for the same treatment."
