# Clause 140 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease: after purgation, if the pulse is skipping (one version writes *zong*) and there is no chest bind, it is about to resolve. If the pulse is floating, there will certainly be chest bind. If tight, there will certainly be sore throat. If wiry, there will certainly be tension of both rib-sides. If fine and rapid, the headache has not stopped. If deep and tight, there will certainly be a desire to retch. If deep and slippery, heat-assisted diarrhea. If floating and slippery, there will certainly be passing of blood.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

This is the transmission and transformation of the evil qi after purging taiyang disease. A skipping pulse means exuberant yang; a skipping pulse after purgation means the yang prevails over the yin, so chest bind does not form and it is about to resolve. A floating pulse after purgation means the yang evil of the upper burner binds and forms chest bind; the Classic says, "In chest bind the cun pulse is floating and the guan pulse deep." A tight pulse after purgation means the taiyang evil has been transmitted to the shaoyin; the Classic says, "A tight pulse belongs to shaoyin." The *Neijing* says, "When the evil lodges in the network vessel of the shaoyin, it makes the throat sore so that food cannot be taken." Hence a tight pulse certainly means sore throat. A wiry pulse means the taiyang evil has been transmitted to the shaoyang; the Classic says, "When the chi and cun are both wiry, the shaoyang has contracted the disease." Its vessel runs along the rib-side and connects with the ear; hence a wiry pulse certainly means tension of both rib-sides. After purgation, when the evil qi is transmitted inward, the headache should stop. A fine, rapid pulse means the evil has not yet been transmitted inward and has damaged the qi; fine is scant qi, rapid is [the evil] in the exterior, so the headache has not stopped. A deep, tight pulse means the taiyang evil has been transmitted to the yangming and become interior repletion; deep is in the interior, tight is interior repletion; with yangming interior repletion there will certainly be a desire to retch. A slippery pulse means the taiyang evil has been transmitted to the intestines and stomach; slippery means yin qi in surplus, so one knows the evil qi has entered the interior and invaded the lower burner. Deep means blood prevails and qi is vacuous—this is heat-assisted diarrhea. Floating means qi prevails and blood is vacuous—so one knows there will certainly be passing of blood. The Classic says, "When one attacks at once though purgation is not appropriate, the various deteriorations are innumerable"—this is what is meant.

## Notes

A clause predicting, by the pulse, the changes after taiyang disease has been mistakenly purged. For each pulse it gives chest bind, sore throat, tension of the rib-sides, headache, retching, heat-assisted diarrhea, or passing of blood. Some commentators regard it as a later addition because the correspondence between pulse and signs is too mechanical.
