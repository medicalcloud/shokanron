# Clause 132 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In a chest-bind pattern, if the pulse is floating and large, one must not purge; if it is purged, [the patient] dies.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Chest bind is evil bound in the chest, belonging to the upper burner. With a floating cun pulse and a deep guan pulse, it is in the interior and may be purged. If the pulse is floating and large, although there is binding below the heart, much is still in the exterior and it is not yet fully bound. Purging it vacuates doubly; the evil qi binds again and is hard to control; hence "if it is purged, he dies."

## Notes

Even in chest bind, if the pulse is floating and large, exterior evil is taken to remain or the upright qi to be vacuous and floating outward, so purgation is prohibited. One of the contraindications of purgation.
