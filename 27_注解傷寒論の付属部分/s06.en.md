# 各巻末の釈音 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Sound Glosses at the End of Each Scroll

(This section is a list giving the pronunciation of difficult characters by *fanqie* or direct-sound glosses, sometimes adding the meaning. For example, "見（音現，下同）" means "見 is pronounced like 現; likewise below," and "讝（職廉切，病人寐而自語也）" means "讝 is pronounced by the *fanqie* of 職 and 廉; it means a patient talking to oneself while asleep." Since the list gives the sound and meaning of characters, item-by-item translation is omitted.)

## Notes

The "sound glosses" (lists of pronunciation notes) appended at the end of each scroll of the annotated edition. For difficult characters in the text they show the reading by *fanqie* (indicating a character's sound with two characters) or direct sound (indicating it with a homophone), sometimes adding the meaning. They are not Cheng Wuji's commentary, and the author has not been identified.

- Pronunciation notes such as "讝（之廉切，又女監切，下同。）" also remain within the text and Cheng Wuji's commentary of various chapters of this work.
- Because characters were unified to new forms, the character forms glossed may differ from the original.
- The meaning glosses include items relevant to interpreting the text, such as "痓 means bad; one says a wind disease" and "痙 means stiff and tense."
