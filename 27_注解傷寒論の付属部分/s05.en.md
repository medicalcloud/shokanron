# 各巻の巻頭の題署と巻末の題 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Title Inscriptions at the Head and End of Each Scroll

(Since each scroll follows the same format, only the example of the first scroll is translated.)

*Zhujie Shanghan lun*, Scroll One / Authored by Zhang Zhongjing of the Han; compiled by Wang Shuhe of the Jin / Annotated by Cheng Wuji of the Song; collated by Wang Jichuan of the Ming. … At the end of the scroll: *Zhujie Shanghan lun*, Scroll One (end).

## Notes

The title inscriptions placed at the head and end of each scroll of the annotated edition. They name Zhang Zhongjing of the Han as author, Wang Shuhe of the Jin as compiler, Cheng Wuji as annotator, and Wang Jichuan of the Ming as collator.

- Cheng Wuji was a man of the Jin, but here he is called "Song" (thought to follow the tradition of the Song edition).
- The variation in the characters "成無巳" and "成無已" is as in the original (Kanripo).
