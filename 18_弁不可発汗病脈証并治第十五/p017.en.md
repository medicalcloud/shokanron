# §17 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease with fever and aversion to cold, more heat than cold, and a faint weak pulse: there is no yang, and it must not be sweated.

## Notes

The same content as the first half of Clause 27 of the upper taiyang chapter. Cheng Wuji's commentary is found there.
