# §20 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

One prone to nosebleed must not be sweated; when sweat comes out, there will certainly be sinking at the forehead, an urgent tight pulse, staring eyes unable to blink, and inability to sleep. (For the pronunciation, see above.)

## Notes

The same text as Clause 86 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found. "For the pronunciation, see above" is a note indicating that the pronunciation gloss for a difficult character appears earlier.
