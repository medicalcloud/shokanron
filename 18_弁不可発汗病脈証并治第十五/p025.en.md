# §25 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

One who coughs with free urination, or with loss of urine, must not be sweated; when sweat comes out, there will be reversal counterflow cold of the four limbs.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

When the lung channel is vacuous and cold, and the upper part is vacuous and cannot govern the lower, there is cough with free urination or loss of urine. If sweating is induced while the upper part is vacuous, yang qi is lost outward. The four limbs are the root of all yang; when yang is vacuous it does not connect with yin, so the four limbs are reversal counterflow cold.

## Notes

The clause prohibits sweating for one who, from lung vacuity cold, has copious or leaking urine along with cough, since yang qi is insufficient and sweating would cause cold limbs.
