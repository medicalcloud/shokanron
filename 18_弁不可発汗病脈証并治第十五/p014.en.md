# §14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Reversal with a tight pulse must not be sweated. If sweating is induced, the voice becomes disordered, the throat hoarse, and the tongue limp, and the voice cannot come forth.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Reversal with a tight pulse is shaoyin cold damage. By rule the interior should be warmed; if sweating is, on the contrary, induced, the shaoyin qi is damaged. The shaoyin vessel enters the lung, follows the throat, and flanks the root of the tongue. The kidney is the root and the lung the branch; when the root is vacuous the branch is weak, so the voice becomes disordered, the throat hoarse, the tongue limp, and the voice cannot come forth.

## Notes

The clause states that sweating a shaoyin cold pattern with cold extremities and a tight pulse impairs the throat and tongue through which the shaoyin channel passes, so that the voice is lost.
