# §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyin disease with a fine, sunken, rapid pulse: the disease is in the interior, and it must not be sweated.

## Notes

The same text as Clause 285 of the shaoyin chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
