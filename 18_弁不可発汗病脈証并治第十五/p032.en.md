# §32 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyin disease with only cold limbs and no sweating: if sweating is forcibly induced, it will certainly stir the blood; it is not known by which path it will come out; when it comes from the mouth and nose or from the eyes, this is called cold limbs below with exhaustion above and is difficult to treat. [The final "”" is a symbol present in the base and has no meaning.]

## Notes

The same text as Clause 294 of the Shaoyin chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found. It is the last paragraph of the chapter "Distinguishing Pulse, Signs, and Treatment of Diseases That Must Not Be Sweated."
