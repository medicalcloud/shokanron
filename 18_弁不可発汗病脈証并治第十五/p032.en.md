# §32 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyin disease with only reversal and no sweating: if sweating is forcibly induced, it will certainly stir the blood; it is not known by which path it will come out; when it comes from the mouth and nose or from the eyes, this is called lower reversal with upper exhaustion and is difficult to treat. [The final "”" is a symbol present in the base and has no meaning.]

## Notes

The same text as Clause 294 of the shaoyin chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found. It is the last paragraph of the chapter "Distinguishing Pulse, Signs, and Treatment of Diseases That Must Not Be Sweated."
