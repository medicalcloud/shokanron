# §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the stirring qi is above, sweating must not be induced. If sweating is induced, qi surges up right at the tip of the heart.

## Notes

The clause states the prohibition of sweating for one with stirring qi above the navel (heart). Cheng Wuji's commentary is recorded in the second half of the commentary on the previous paragraph.
