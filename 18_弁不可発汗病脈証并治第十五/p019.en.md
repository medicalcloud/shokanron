# §19 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

One with blood loss must not be sweated; if sweating is induced, there will be cold shuddering and trembling.

## Notes

Nearly the same text as Clause 87 of the middle taiyang chapter (Clause 87 reads "persons prone to blood loss"). Cheng Wuji's commentary is found there.
