# §16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease of eight or nine days, malaria-like, with fever and aversion to cold, more heat than cold, no retching, normal stool continuing, attacks two or three times a day: when the pulse is faint with aversion to cold, both yin and yang are vacuous, and it must not be sweated again.

## Notes

The same content as part of Clause 23 of the upper taiyang chapter (with slight differences in wording). Cheng Wuji's commentary is found there.
