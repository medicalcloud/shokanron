# §26 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In cold damage from the first or second day to the fourth or fifth day, one with reversal will certainly have fever; one with reversal before will certainly have heat afterward. When the reversal is deep, the heat is also deep; when the reversal is slight, the heat is also slight. Reversal should be purged; if, on the contrary, sweating is induced, there will certainly be injury of the mouth with red erosion.

## Notes

Nearly the same text as Clause 335 of the jueyin chapter (Clause 335 reads "one with heat before will certainly have reversal afterward"). Cheng Wuji's commentary is found there.
