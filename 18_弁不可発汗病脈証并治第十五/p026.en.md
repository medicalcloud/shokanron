# §26 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In cold damage from the first or second day to the fourth or fifth day, one with cold limbs will certainly have fever; one with cold limbs before will certainly have heat afterward. When the coldness of the limbs is deep, the heat is also deep; when the coldness of the limbs is slight, the heat is also slight. Cold limbs should be purged; if, on the contrary, sweating is induced, there will certainly be injury of the mouth with red erosion.

## Notes

Nearly the same text as Clause 335 of the Jueyin chapter (Clause 335 reads "one with heat before will certainly have cold limbs afterward"). Cheng Wuji's commentary is found there.
