# §24 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

One with diarrhea must not be sweated; when sweat comes out, there will certainly be distension and fullness.

## Notes

A clause with the same purport as Clause 364 of the jueyin chapter ("diarrhea of undigested grain must not have the exterior attacked; if there is sweating, there will certainly be distension and fullness").
