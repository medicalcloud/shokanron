# §27 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cold damage with a wiry thready pulse, headache, and fever belongs to the Shaoyang. Shaoyang must not be sweated.

## Notes

The same text as the first half of Clause 265 of the Shaoyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
