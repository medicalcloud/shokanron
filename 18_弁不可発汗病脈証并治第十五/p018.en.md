# §18 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

One with a dry throat must not be sweated.

## Notes

The same text as Clause 83 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found. Paragraphs 18–23 correspond to Clauses 83–89, which list the "persons prone to …" (contraindications by constitution or history) for whom sweating is prohibited.
