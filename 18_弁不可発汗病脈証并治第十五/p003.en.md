# §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the pulse is floating and tight, by rule the body should ache, and it is appropriate to resolve it by sweating. But if the chi pulse is slow, sweating must not be induced. How is this known? Because the nutritive qi is insufficient and the blood scant.

## Notes

The same text as Clause 50 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
