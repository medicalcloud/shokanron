# §31 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyin disease with cough and "not free" (diarrhea) and delirious speech is because it has been forced by fire qi. Urination will certainly be difficult, because sweat has been forcibly demanded of the shaoyin.

## Notes

Nearly the same text as Clause 284 of the shaoyin chapter. However, Clause 284 reads "cough with diarrhea" (咳而下利), whereas this paragraph reads "咳而不利." Since "diarrhea" fits the sense better, the translation renders it as "not free (diarrhea)." Cheng Wuji's commentary is on Clause 284.
