# §21 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

One prone to sweating must not be sweated; if sweating is induced, there will certainly be abstraction and mental disorder, and pain of the genitals after urination. Yuyuliang wan is appropriate. (The formula is originally missing.)

## Notes

The same text as Clause 88 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found. The formula of Yuyuliang wan has not been transmitted.
