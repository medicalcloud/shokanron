# §28 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cold damage with headache, feathery fever, a form resembling wind strike, constant slight sweating, and spontaneous retching: if purged, there will be increased vexation and anguish in the heart as if hungry; if sweated, there will be tetany, with a stiff body hard to bend and stretch; if fumigated, there will be yellowing and no urination; if prolonged, there will be cough and spitting.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Cold damage should have no sweating and aversion to cold. Now headache, fever, slight sweating, and spontaneous retching mean the evil of cold damage has been transmitted, become heat, and is about to move into the interior. If, on the contrary, it is purged, the evil heat takes advantage of the vacuity and flows into the chest, producing vacuity vexation with anguish in the heart as if hungry. If sweating is induced, the exterior is made vacuous, the heat returns to the channels and network vessels, and the intense heat generates wind, so the body becomes rigid with tetany. If fumigated, fire and heat combine to consume the fluids, so urination is inhibited and yellowing arises. The lung dislikes fire; moxibustion lets fire-heat damage the lung, and there will certainly be cough with spitting of pus.

## Notes

The clause states that in cold damage resembling wind strike, at the stage where the evil has become heat and is heading inward, purgation, sweating, and fumigation are all mistaken, producing respectively vacuity vexation, tetany, yellowing, and cough with spitting.
