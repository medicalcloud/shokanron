# §29 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Combined disease of taiyang and shaoyang with stiff painful head and nape, perhaps dizziness and veiling, at times something like chest binding, and glomus and hardness below the heart: it must not be sweated.

## Notes

The same content as part of Clause 142 of the lower taiyang chapter. Cheng Wuji's commentary is found there.
