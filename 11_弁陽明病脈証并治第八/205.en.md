# Clause 205 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In yangming disease with hardness and fullness below the heart, one must not attack. If it is attacked and diarrhea then does not stop, [the patient] dies; if the diarrhea stops, he recovers.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Abdominal fullness in yangming disease means the evil qi has entered the bowel, and it may be purged. Hardness and fullness below the heart mean the evil qi is still shallow and has not fully entered the bowel, so it must not be purged at once. If the diarrhea stops, the evil qi has gone and the upright qi is at peace; with the upright qi at peace, he recovers. If because of purgation the diarrhea does not stop, the upright qi has deserted, and he dies.

## Notes

Even in yangming disease, if the hardness and fullness are below the heart (epigastrium) rather than in the abdomen, the evil has not fully entered the bowel (intestines), so one must not purge. If mistaken purgation causes unstoppable diarrhea, it is desertion of the upright qi with poor prognosis. One of the contraindications of purgation.
