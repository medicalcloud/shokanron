# Clause 261 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In cold damage with yellowing of the body and fever, Zhizi Bopi tang governs it. Formula 43.

Plump zhizi 15 pieces (broken); gancao 1 liang (mix-fried); huangbai 2 liang.

Boil the three ingredients above in 4 sheng of water to obtain 1.5 sheng, remove the dregs, and take warm in two divided doses.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Yellowing of the body in cold damage means there is stagnant heat in the stomach, which must be purged and removed. Here, because there is fever, the heat is regarded as not yet replete, and Zhizi Bopi tang is given to resolve and dissipate it.

(Nature and flavor) Zhizi: bitter, cold. Gancao: sweet, neutral.

## Notes

A clause using Zhizi Bopi tang to clear heat in jaundice with fever but without interior repletion such as abdominal fullness or constipation. It is contrasted with Yinchenhao tang (with interior repletion) and the next clause's Mahuang Lianyao Chixiaodou tang (with an exterior pattern) as the three formulas for yang jaundice. "Bopi" in the formula name refers to huangbai (phellodendron bark), which is written "huangbai" in the formula.
