# Clause 189 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yangming wind-strike with a bitter taste in the mouth, dry throat, abdominal fullness, slight panting, fever, aversion to cold, and a floating, tight pulse: if it is purged, there will be abdominal fullness and difficult urination.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Of the pulse, floating means in the exterior and tight means interior repletion. In yangming wind-strike, a bitter taste, dry throat, abdominal fullness, and slight panting mean the heat has been transmitted inward. Fever and aversion to cold mean the exterior has still not resolved. If it is purged, although the interior evil is removed, the exterior evil again enters the interior and the fluids are further lost, causing abdominal fullness and difficult urination.

## Notes

In yangming wind-strike where shaoyang signs (bitter taste, dry throat) and yangming signs (abdominal fullness, panting) coexist with a remaining taiyang exterior pattern (fever, aversion to cold, floating and tight pulse), one must not purge. It warns that purging while the exterior pattern remains causes abdominal fullness and difficult urination instead. It is taken as an example of mixed signs of the three yang.
