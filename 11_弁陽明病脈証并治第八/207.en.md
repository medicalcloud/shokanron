# Clause 207 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In yangming disease, when there has been no vomiting and no purgation but there is vexation of the heart, Tiaowei Chengqi tang may be given. Formula 1.

Gancao 2 liang (mix-fried); mangxiao half a sheng; dahuang 4 liang (washed in clear wine).

Cut the three ingredients above; with 3 sheng of water boil two of them down to 1 sheng, remove the dregs, add the mangxiao, put on a gentle fire again and boil once or twice, and take warm in a single dose to regulate the stomach qi.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Vexation after vomiting is called internal vexation; vexation after purgation is called vacuity vexation. Here, in yangming disease without vomiting or purgation, there is vexation, meaning there is constrained heat in the stomach. Tiaowei Chengqi tang is given to purge the constrained heat.

## Notes

Yangming disease with vexation, without previous vomiting or purgation, is regarded as replete vexation from heat trapped in the stomach, and Tiaowei Chengqi tang regulates the stomach qi. It is read in contrast with the vacuity vexation of Zhizi Chi tang (after sweating, vomiting, or purgation).
