# Clause 241 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When after heavy purgation there is no bowel movement for six or seven days, the vexation does not resolve, and the abdomen is full and painful, there is dry stool. The reason is that there was originally retained food. Da Chengqi tang is appropriate. 27. Use the second formula above.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

After heavy purgation the stomach is weak and cannot digest grain. When there is no bowel movement by the sixth or seventh day, the retained food has already bound and is not digested, so the vexing heat does not resolve and the abdomen is full and painful; from this one knows there is dry stool. Da Chengqi tang is given to purge and remove it.

## Notes

Even after one heavy purgation, if dry stool has formed again (because retained food remained), it may be purged again with Da Chengqi tang. It shows that one should follow the pattern without fearing a second purgation merely because one has already been given.
