# Clause 259 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In cold damage, after sweating the body and eyes become yellow. The reason is that cold-damp (one version writes "warmth") is in the interior and does not resolve. Considering that it must not be purged, seek [the treatment] among [the methods for] cold-damp.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The *Jingui Yaolüe* says: "Jaundice arises, being contracted from dampness." When sweat comes out and the heat goes, there cannot be yellowing. When the body and eyes become yellow after sweating, the wind qi has gone but the damp qi remains. The spleen dislikes dampness; when damp qi lodges within and the color of the spleen is drawn outward, the body and eyes become yellow. If yellowing arises from static blood in the interior it may be purged, but here cold-damp is in the interior, so it must not be purged and should be treated by the method for cold-damp.

## Notes

Distinguishing it from jaundice due to heat (yang jaundice, which may be purged), the clause states that jaundice due to cold-damp (later called "yin jaundice") must not be purged but should be treated by warming and removing dampness. No specific formula is given.
