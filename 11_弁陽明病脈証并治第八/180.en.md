# Clause 180 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The disease of yangming is stomach-family repletion (one version writes "cold").

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

When the evil is transmitted into the stomach and heat toxin remains and binds, the stomach family becomes replete. Hua Tuo said, "When heat toxin enters the stomach, it must be purged and removed; it must not be left in the stomach." From this one knows that evil in the yangming is stomach-family repletion.

## Notes

Regarded as the outline (*tigang*) of yangming disease. "Stomach family" is often taken to mean the whole stomach and intestines (stomach, large intestine, small intestine). "Repletion" refers to a state in which the evil heat is exuberant while the upright qi is also full; the general interpretation is that it expresses the essence of yangming disease, including not only bowel repletion with tangible dry stool but also the channel pattern with exuberant formless heat (the Baihu tang pattern).
