# Clause 180 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The disease of Yangming is stomach-family excess (one version writes "cold").

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

When the pathogen is transmitted into the stomach and heat toxin remains and binds, the stomach family becomes in excess. Hua Tuo said, "When heat toxin enters the stomach, it must be purged and removed; it must not be left in the stomach." From this one knows that pathogen in the Yangming is stomach-family excess.

## Notes

Regarded as the outline (*tigang*) of Yangming disease. "Stomach family" is often taken to mean the whole stomach and intestines (stomach, large intestine, small intestine). "Excess" refers to a state in which the pathogenic heat is exuberant while the healthy qi is also full; the general interpretation is that it expresses the essence of Yangming disease, including not only bowel excess with tangible dry stool but also the meridian pattern with exuberant formless heat (the Baihu tang pattern).
