# Clause 222 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

If there are thirst with a desire to drink water and a dry mouth and tongue, Baihu jia Renshen tang governs it. Formula 12.

Zhimu 6 liang; shigao 1 jin (crushed); gancao 2 liang (mix-fried); jingmi 6 ge; renshen 3 liang.

Of the five ingredients above, with 1 dou of water boil until the rice is cooked and the decoction is done, remove the dregs, and take 1 sheng warm, three times a day.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

If after purgation the pathogenic heat lodges in the upper jiao, it is deficiency vexation. Here, after purgation, the pathogenic heat does not lodge in the upper jiao but in the middle jiao, causing dryness, vexation, and thirst. Baihu jia Renshen tang is given to disperse the heat and moisten the dryness.

## Notes

Following Clause 221, when after purgation the heat is in the middle jiao (stomach) with strong thirst and a dry mouth and tongue, Baihu jia Renshen tang is used. It is regarded as part of the choice: Zhizi Chi tang for heat in the upper jiao, Baihu jia Renshen tang for the middle jiao, and Zhuling tang (Clause 223) for the lower jiao.
