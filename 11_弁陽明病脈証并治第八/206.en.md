# Clause 206 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In yangming disease with the whole face red, one must not attack. [If attacked,] one who certainly develops fever and yellowing has inhibited urination.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

*He* means throughout. A face red throughout in yangming disease means the heat is in the channel. It must not be purged. If purged, the stomach qi is vacuated and the fluids consumed; the heat in the channel takes advantage of the vacuity to enter the stomach, and there will certainly be fever with yellowing and inhibited urination.

## Notes

A face red all over is taken to show that the heat is in the channel (near the exterior) and not yet replete in the bowel, so one must not purge. Mistaken purgation lets the heat enter the stomach, causing inhibited urination and jaundice. One of the contraindications of purgation.
