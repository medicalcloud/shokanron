# Clause 206 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In Yangming disease with the whole face red, one must not attack. [If attacked,] one who certainly develops fever and yellowing has inhibited urination.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

*He* means throughout. A face red throughout in Yangming disease means the heat is in the meridian. It must not be purged. If purged, the stomach qi is vacuated and the fluids consumed; the heat in the meridian takes advantage of the deficiency to enter the stomach, and there will certainly be fever with yellowing and inhibited urination.

## Notes

A face red all over is taken to show that the heat is in the meridian (near the exterior) and not yet excess in the bowel, so one must not purge. Mistaken purgation lets the heat enter the stomach, causing inhibited urination and jaundice. One of the contraindications of purgation.
