# Clause 203 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In yangming disease, when there was originally spontaneous sweating but the physician again repeatedly induced sweating, and the disease has been cured, but there is still slight vexation and [the patient] does not feel clear, it must be because the stool is hard. Because the fluids were lost and the stomach became dry, the stool was made hard. One should ask how many times a day he urinates. If originally he urinated three or four times a day and today only twice, one knows the stool will come out before long. Now that urination has become less frequent, the fluids should return into the stomach, so one knows there will surely be a bowel movement before long.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The fluids were lost earlier, making the stool hard; when urination becomes less frequent, the fluids are redistributed [to the intestines], and the stool will certainly descend spontaneously.

## Notes

In one whose stool has become hard from loss of fluids through excessive sweating, even without purgatives, the fluids return to the intestines and the bowels move spontaneously when urination becomes less frequent; so a bowel movement can be predicted from the frequency of urination. It is taken as an example of waiting for natural recovery instead of purging indiscriminately.
