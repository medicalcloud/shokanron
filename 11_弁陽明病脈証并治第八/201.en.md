# Clause 201 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In Yangming disease with a floating and tight pulse, there will certainly be tidal fever coming at set times; when it is only floating, there will certainly be night sweats.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Floating means in the meridian; tight means interior excess. A floating and tight pulse means heat in the exterior and excess in the interior, so there will certainly be tidal fever coming at set times. If the pulse is only floating and not tight, there is only exterior heat, and there will certainly be night sweats. Night sweats is sweating during sleep. In Yangming disease, interior heat produces spontaneous sweating; exterior heat produces night sweats.

## Notes

A clause on the relation between the pulse, fever pattern, and sweating in Yangming disease. A floating, tight pulse means interior excess heat with tidal fever; a pulse that is only floating means heat in the meridian (exterior) with night sweats.
