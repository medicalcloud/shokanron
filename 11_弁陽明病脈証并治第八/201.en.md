# Clause 201 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In yangming disease with a floating and tight pulse, there will certainly be tidal fever coming at set times; when it is only floating, there will certainly be night sweating.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Floating means in the channel; tight means interior repletion. A floating and tight pulse means heat in the exterior and repletion in the interior, so there will certainly be tidal fever coming at set times. If the pulse is only floating and not tight, there is only exterior heat, and there will certainly be night sweating. Night sweating is sweating during sleep. In yangming disease, interior heat produces spontaneous sweating; exterior heat produces night sweating.

## Notes

A clause on the relation between the pulse, fever pattern, and sweating in yangming disease. A floating, tight pulse means interior replete heat with tidal fever; a pulse that is only floating means heat in the channel (exterior) with night sweating.
