# Clause 227 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

With a floating pulse, fever, dry mouth, dry nose, and ability to eat, there will be nosebleed.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

A floating pulse with fever, dry mouth, and dry nose means the heat is in the channel. Ability to eat means the interior is in harmony. When the heat is severe in the channel, it presses the blood, causing nosebleed. [In the preceding clause] stomach vacuity cold is yin prevailing; water enters the channels and then blood is formed; drinking water assists the yin, and the qi counterflows as hiccup. Fever with a dry mouth is yang prevailing; food enters the yin and grows qi in the yang; ability to eat assists the yang, and the blood moves frenetically as nosebleed. These two [the preceding clause and this one] are diseases inclined toward yin and toward yang.

## Notes

One in whom yangming heat is in the channel while the interior is harmonized (able to eat) has the heat rise along the channel and cause nosebleed (the same purport as Clause 202). Cheng Wuji pairs it with the preceding clause (stomach vacuity cold with hiccup) and explains them by the inclination of yin and yang.
