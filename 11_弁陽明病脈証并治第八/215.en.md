# Clause 215 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In Yangming disease with delirium and tidal fever, one who, on the contrary, cannot eat must have five or six pieces of dry stool in the stomach; one who can eat only has hard stool. It is appropriate to purge with Da Chengqi tang (Daijokito). 7. Use the second formula above.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Delirium and tidal fever are stomach heat, which should digest grain and draw food. One who, on the contrary, cannot eat has dry stool in the stomach and the stomach is in excess. One who can eat has deficiency heat in the stomach; although [the stool] is hard, it cannot be regarded as dry stool. In miscellaneous diseases, deficiency means no desire to eat and excess means a desire to eat; in cold damage, one whose stomach is in excess with severe heat cannot eat, while one with severe deficiency heat in the stomach can eat—different from miscellaneous diseases. Da Chengqi tang purges the dry stool and expels the bound heat.

## Notes

In Yangming disease with delirium and tidal fever, if the patient cannot eat, dry stool has fully formed (bowel excess); if he can eat, the stool is merely hard—judging the presence of dry stool by appetite. "It is appropriate to purge with Da Chengqi tang" is generally read as belonging to the first half (the case with dry stool).
