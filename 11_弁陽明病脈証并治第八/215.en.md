# Clause 215 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In yangming disease with delirious speech and tidal fever, one who, on the contrary, cannot eat must have five or six pieces of dry stool in the stomach; one who can eat only has hard stool. It is appropriate to purge with Da Chengqi tang. 7. Use the second formula above.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Delirious speech and tidal fever are stomach heat, which should digest grain and draw food. One who, on the contrary, cannot eat has dry stool in the stomach and the stomach is replete. One who can eat has vacuity heat in the stomach; although [the stool] is hard, it cannot be regarded as dry stool. In miscellaneous diseases, vacuity means no desire to eat and repletion means a desire to eat; in cold damage, one whose stomach is replete with severe heat cannot eat, while one with severe vacuity heat in the stomach can eat—different from miscellaneous diseases. Da Chengqi tang purges the dry stool and expels the bound heat.

## Notes

In yangming disease with delirious speech and tidal fever, if the patient cannot eat, dry stool has fully formed (bowel repletion); if he can eat, the stool is merely hard—judging the presence of dry stool by appetite. "It is appropriate to purge with Da Chengqi tang" is generally read as belonging to the first half (the case with dry stool).
