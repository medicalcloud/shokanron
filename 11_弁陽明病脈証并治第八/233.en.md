# Clause 233 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In yangming disease, when there is spontaneous sweating, or sweating has been induced, and urination is spontaneously free, the fluids are exhausted within; although [the stool] is hard, it must not be attacked. One must wait until there is a spontaneous urge to defecate, and it is appropriate to guide it out with Mijian (honey pastille). Gualougen [tugua root] or the bile of a large pig can both also serve as a guide (enema/suppository). 20.

Mijian formula
Edible honey 7 ge.
Boil the one ingredient above in a copper vessel over a gentle fire until it congeals like malt sugar, stirring so that it does not burn; when it can be rolled, twist it by hand into a stick with a pointed tip, as thick as a finger and about 2 cun long. Make it quickly while hot; when cold it becomes hard. Insert it into the anus, hold it quickly with the hand, and remove it when there is an urge to defecate. Doubtfully Zhongjing's intent, but it has been tried and is very good.

Also, take the bile of one large pig, mix it with a little vinegar, and pour it into the anus; within about the time of a meal, the stool should come out with retained food and foul matter—very effective.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The fluids are exhausted within and the intestines and stomach dry, so the stool becomes hard. This is not bound heat, so it must not be attacked; it is appropriate to treat externally with medicine to guide it out. The Mijian dao formula: put 7 ge of honey alone in a copper vessel and boil it over a gentle fire until it congeals slightly like malt sugar.

Zhudanzhi (pig bile) formula. [The commentary breaks off here.]

## Notes

When the stool is hard merely because the fluids are scanty and there is no bound heat (dry stool), one does not attack with Chengqi tang but assists defecation with a suppository of boiled-down honey (Mijian dao) or an enema of pig bile and vinegar. The clause is known as the record of suppositories and enemas in the *Shanghan lun*. The formula name "Mijian" is "Mijian dao" in the other sources.
