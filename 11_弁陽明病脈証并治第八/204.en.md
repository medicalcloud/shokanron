# Clause 204 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In cold damage with much vomiting, although there is a yangming pattern, it must not be attacked.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Vomiting means the heat is in the upper burner and has not fully entered the bowel, so it must not be purged.

## Notes

One of the contraindications of purgation. Much vomiting is taken to show that the evil is above (in the chest and upper stomach) or that a shaoyang pattern remains, so even with a yangming pattern one must not purge.
