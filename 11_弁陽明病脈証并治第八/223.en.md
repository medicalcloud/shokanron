# Clause 223 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

If the pulse is floating, with fever, thirst with a desire to drink water, and inhibited urination, Zhuling tang (Polyporus Decoction) governs it. Formula 13.

Zhuling (remove the skin), fuling, zexie, ejiao, and huashi (talc) (crushed), 1 liang each.

Of the five ingredients above, with 4 sheng of water first boil four of them down to 2 sheng, remove the dregs, add the ejiao and dissolve it, and take 7 ge warm, three times a day.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

This is visiting heat lodging in the lower burner after purgation. The evil qi has entered from the exterior into the interior and lodges in the lower burner, all three burners carrying heat. A floating pulse with fever is heat of the upper burner. Thirst with a desire to drink water is heat of the middle burner. Inhibited urination means the evil lodges in the lower burner and the fluids cannot pass downward. Zhuling tang is given to free urination and drain the heat of the lower burner.

(Nature and flavor) Zhuling: sweet, neutral. Fuling: sweet, neutral. Ejiao: sweet, neutral. Huashi: sweet, cold. Zexie: sweet-salty, cold.

When sweet is extreme it becomes bland; bland flavor percolates and drains and is yang—the sweetness of zhuling and fuling moves urination. Salty flavor causes upwelling and discharge and is yin—the saltiness of zexie drains hidden water. Slippery frees the orifices—the slipperiness of ejiao and huashi frees the waterways.

## Notes

The clause on Zhuling tang, used when yangming heat has reached the lower burner and water and heat have bound, with inhibited urination and thirst.

- Similar to Wuling san (Clause 71), Zhuling tang is used when there is heat with damaged yin (fluids); it differs in supplementing yin with ejiao and clearing heat with huashi.
- In later times it has been widely applied to symptoms from damp-heat of the lower burner, such as painful and frequent urination.
