# Clause 214 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In yangming disease with delirious speech, tidal fever, and a slippery and racing pulse, Xiao Chengqi tang governs it. When 1 sheng of Chengqi tang has been given and qi turns in the abdomen (flatus), take another sheng; if qi does not turn, do not give more. If the next day there is still no bowel movement and the pulse is, on the contrary, faint and rough, the interior is vacuous; it is difficult to treat, and Chengqi tang must not be given again. 6. Use the second formula above.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

In yangming disease with delirious speech and tidal fever, a deep and replete pulse means internal repletion, which may be purged. A slippery and racing pulse means the interior heat is not yet replete, so it must not yet be purged; first give Xiao Chengqi tang to harmonize it. When the decoction enters the abdomen and flatus is passed, there is dry stool within, and another sheng of Xiao Chengqi tang may be given to remove it. If flatus is not passed, there is no dry stool, and Xiao Chengqi tang must not be given again. The next day, at the time of the evil's transmission, if the pulse is deep, replete, tight, or firm, it is interior repletion; if, on the contrary, it is faint and rough, the interior qi is greatly vacuous. If the pulse is faint and rough after the bowels have moved, it is merely interior vacuity and still manageable; here there has been no bowel movement yet the pulse is faint and rough, meaning the upright qi has declined within and is overcome by the evil qi—hence "difficult to treat."

## Notes

Even with delirious speech and tidal fever, if the pulse is slippery and racing (the dry stool not yet formed), Xiao Chengqi tang is tried, and whether to continue is decided by the passing of flatus. If the next day there is still no bowel movement and the pulse becomes faint and rough, the upright qi is vacuous and the condition, which cannot be purged, is difficult to treat. Like Clause 209, it shows the careful use of purgation.
