# Clause 246 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The pulse is floating and hollow (hollow). Floating is yang; hollow is yin. When floating and hollow contend, the stomach qi generates heat, and its yang is cut off.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

When floating and hollow contend, yin and yang are not in accord; the stomach qi governs alone, becomes constrained and generates heat, consuming the fluids, so its yang is cut off.

## Notes

A short clause stating that a pulse combining floating (exuberant yang heat) and hollow (deficiency of yin blood and fluids) is a sign that heat arising in the stomach consumes the fluids and the disease shifts to Yangming. As in the preceding clause, "yang cut off" is understood as due to exhaustion of fluids.
