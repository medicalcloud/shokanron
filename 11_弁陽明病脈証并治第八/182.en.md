# Clause 182 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Question: What is the external pattern of yangming disease? Answer: Generalized heat, spontaneous sweating, no aversion to cold, but, on the contrary, aversion to heat.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Yangming disease is evil that has entered the bowel. When the evil is in the exterior, there are generalized heat, sweating, and aversion to cold. Once the evil has entered the bowel, the exterior pattern has ended, so there is no aversion to cold but only generalized heat, sweating, and aversion to heat.

## Notes

A clause showing the external signs of yangming disease (generalized heat, spontaneous sweating, no aversion to cold, aversion to heat). Aversion to heat rather than to cold is regarded as the greatest difference from taiyang disease (aversion to cold).
