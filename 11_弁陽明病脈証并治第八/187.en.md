# Clause 187 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cold damage with a floating, moderate pulse and spontaneously warm hands and feet: this is [the disease] tied to the taiyin. In the taiyin, the body should turn yellow; if urination is spontaneously free, it cannot turn yellow. When by the seventh or eighth day the stool is hard, it is yangming disease.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Floating is yang evil; moderate is the spleen pulse. A floating, moderate pulse in cold damage means heat lodges in the taiyin. When the evil is in the three yang, the hands and feet are hot; when in the three yin, the hands and feet are cold. Here the hands and feet are spontaneously warm, so one knows it is tied to the taiyin. The taiyin is earth; when steamed by evil, its color appears outside, so the body should turn yellow. When urination is spontaneously free, the heat is not amassed within, so it cannot turn yellow. When by the seventh or eighth day the stool is hard, the taiyin evil has entered the bowel and shifted to belong to the yangming.

## Notes

A clause distinguishing, from a state in which the evil remains in the taiyin (spleen), the course to jaundice from the course to yangming with constipation. If urination is free, damp-heat does not accumulate and there is no jaundice; when the stool eventually becomes hard, it is yangming disease. Warm hands and feet are taken as a sign distinguishing taiyin (warm limbs) from shaoyin and jueyin (cold limbs).
