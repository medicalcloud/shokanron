# Clause 209 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In yangming disease with tidal fever and slightly hard stool, Da Chengqi tang may be given; if the stool is not hard, it must not be given. If there has been no bowel movement for six or seven days and there may be dry stool, the way to find out is to give a little Xiao Chengqi tang. If, when the decoction enters the abdomen, flatus is passed, there is dry stool, and then it may be attacked. If flatus is not passed, only the head of the stool is hard and the rest will certainly be sloppy; it must not be attacked—if attacked, there will certainly be distension and fullness with inability to eat. If one who wants to drink is given water, there will be hiccup. If fever arises afterward, the stool will certainly be hard again and scant; harmonize it with Xiao Chengqi tang. If flatus is not passed, be careful not to attack. Xiao Chengqi tang. 3. Use the second formula above.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Tidal fever is repletion. When the stool is found to be slightly hard, it may be attacked at once. If the stool is not hard, the heat has not yet become repletion, and even with tidal fever it must not yet be attacked. If there has been no bowel movement for six or seven days and there may be dry stool, first give Xiao Chengqi tang to probe it. If there is dry stool, Xiao Chengqi tang, being gentle in force, cannot flush it out, and the qi will certainly turn and escape below (flatus). If flatus is not passed, there is no dry stool in the stomach, only slight hardness in the intestines—only the head of the stool is hard and the rest certainly sloppy. Attacking it vacuates the stomach qi, causing distension and fullness with inability to eat. When the stomach is dry, one wants to drink water, but when water enters the stomach, vacuity and cold contend, the qi counterflows, and there is hiccup. When fever arises afterward, the heat qi takes advantage of the vacuity to gather again in the stomach; the dry stomach obtains heat, and the stool will certainly be hard again and scant. Xiao Chengqi tang is given to cause slight diarrhea and harmonize it. Therefore it repeats, "if flatus is not passed, it must not be attacked"—the utmost caution.

## Notes

A famous clause: to confirm whether to use Da Chengqi tang, first give a small amount of Xiao Chengqi tang; if flatus is passed, there is dry stool and Da Chengqi tang may be used to attack; if not, one must not attack. It is often cited as an example of "trial administration." "Passing flatus" (*zhuan shiqi* 転矢気) in the text emends the base's 転失気 according to the general text (see the emendation note).
