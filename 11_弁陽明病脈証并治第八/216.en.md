# Clause 216 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In yangming disease with passing of blood and delirious speech, this is heat entering the blood chamber. When there is sweating only from the head, needle Qimen and drain according to the repletion; with continuous sweating [the patient] recovers.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

In yangming disease, heat enters the blood chamber and presses the blood downward, causing passing of blood and delirious speech. Yangming disease by rule has much sweating, but one robbed of blood has no sweat, so there is sweating only from the head. Needle Qimen to disperse the heat of the blood chamber, draining according to the repletion to remove the yangming evil heat. When the evil is dispersed and the heat removed, the nutritive and defensive flow, the fluids are restored, and with continuous sweating it resolves.

## Notes

This clause states that heat entering the blood chamber can also occur in yangming disease and, as with heat entering the blood chamber in the taiyang chapter (Clause 143), Qimen is needled to drain the heat. Some hold that it can occur in both sexes, others that it is limited to women. "Drain" (瀉) in the text emends the base's 写 according to the general text (see the emendation note).
