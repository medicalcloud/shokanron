# Clause 248 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease of three days not resolved by sweating, with steaming fever: it belongs to the stomach; Tiaowei Chengqi tang governs it. 32. Use the first formula above.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

*Zhengzheng* means like heat fuming and steaming—that is, intense heat. When taiyang disease is not resolved by sweating on the third day, the exterior evil has already ended; steaming fever means the stomach heat is severe. Tiaowei Chengqi tang is given to purge the stomach heat.

## Notes

When after sweating the exterior pattern has gone but a steaming fever rising from within persists, the heat is taken to have moved to the yangming (stomach), and Tiaowei Chengqi tang is used.
