# Clause 258 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

If the rapid pulse does not resolve and the diarrhea does not stop, there will certainly be heat-associated stool with pus and blood.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

When after purgation the rapid pulse does not resolve and there is no bowel movement, the heat cannot drain, and blood amasses below to form static blood. If after purgation the rapid pulse does not resolve and the diarrhea does not stop, the heat is able to drain downward and presses the blood to move down, so there will certainly be stool with pus and blood.

## Notes

Continuing the previous clause: when heat has entered the blood aspect and diarrhea continues, the heat pushes the blood downward producing stool with pus and blood. It pairs with the previous clause's "no bowel movement → static blood."
