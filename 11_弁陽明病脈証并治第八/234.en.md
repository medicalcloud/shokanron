# Clause 234 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In Yangming disease with a slow pulse, much sweating, and slight aversion to cold, the exterior has not yet resolved; sweating may be induced, and Guizhi tang (Keishito) is appropriate. 21.

Guizhi (keishi) 3 liang (remove the bark); shaoyao (shakuyaku) 3 liang; shengjiang (shokyo) 3 liang; gancao (kanzo) 2 liang (mix-fried); dazao (taiso) 12 pieces (split).

Of the five ingredients above, boil with 7 sheng of water down to 3 sheng, remove the dregs, take 1 sheng warm, and after a short while sip 1 sheng of hot thin porridge to assist the power of the medicine and obtain sweat.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

A slow pulse with much sweating in Yangming disease should be attributed to pathogen in the interior, but from the slight aversion to cold one knows the exterior has not resolved. Guizhi tang is given to harmonize the exterior.

## Notes

Even in Yangming disease, if there is slight aversion to cold, an exterior pattern remains, so the exterior is first resolved with Guizhi tang. It pairs with the next clause (235, Mahuang tang [Maoto]) and shows that the Yangming chapter also includes treatment of exterior patterns.
