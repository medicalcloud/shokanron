# Clause 252 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In cold damage of six or seven days, when the vision is unclear, the eyes are not harmonious, there is no exterior or interior pattern, the stool is difficult, and the body has slight heat, this is repletion; purge urgently. Da Chengqi tang is appropriate. 36. Use the second formula above.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The *Neijing* says: "All vessels belong to the eyes." Six or seven days of cold damage is when the evil qi enters the interior. Unclear vision and disharmony of the eyes mean evil heat is severe within and fumes up to the eyes. No exterior or interior pattern with difficult stool is interior repletion. Great heat of the body is exterior heat; slight heat of the body is interior heat. The *Zhenjing* says: "In heat disease, when the eyes are not clear and the heat does not stop, one dies." This unclear vision and disharmony of the eyes means the pattern is close to danger; Da Chengqi tang must be given urgently to purge it.

## Notes

One of the "three patterns for urgent purgation" in yangming disease (Clauses 252–254). Although the body heat is slight and abdominal signs are not prominent, blurred vision and unnatural eye movement are regarded as a dangerous sign that heat is violently consuming the yin (fluids), so urgent purgation with Da Chengqi tang is used.
