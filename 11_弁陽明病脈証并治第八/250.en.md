# Clause 250 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease: when after vomiting, purgation, or sweating there are slight vexation, frequent urination, and consequently hard stool, give Xiao Chengqi tang; harmonizing it cures it. 34. Use the second formula above.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Vomiting, purgation, and sweating all damage the fluids, and the exterior evil takes advantage of the vacuity to be transmitted inward. Great vexation means the evil is in the exterior; slight vexation means the evil has entered the interior. Frequent urination and consequently hard stool mean the spleen is restrained. Harmonizing it with Xiao Chengqi tang cures it.

## Notes

For mild constipation (close to spleen restraint) after loss of fluids through sweating, vomiting, or purgation, Xiao Chengqi tang harmonizes lightly. It is regarded as a concrete example of the taiyang yangming (spleen restraint) of Clause 179, and Cheng Wuji cites it as such.
