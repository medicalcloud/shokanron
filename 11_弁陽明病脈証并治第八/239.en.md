# Clause 239 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When a patient has had no bowel movement for five or six days, with pain around the navel and vexation and agitation coming at set times, there is dry stool, which causes the absence of bowel movement.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

With no bowel movement for five or six days, the stool must bind into dry stool. The stomach is dry and replete and the qi cannot pass downward, so there are pain around the navel and vexation and agitation coming at set times.

## Notes

A clause describing signs indicating dry stool (pain around the navel and vexation and agitation occurring periodically). It is regarded as one of the indications for Da Chengqi tang.
