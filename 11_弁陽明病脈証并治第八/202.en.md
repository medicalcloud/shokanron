# Clause 202 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In yangming disease with a dry mouth and a desire only to rinse the mouth with water but not to swallow it, there will certainly be nosebleed.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The yangming vessel arises at the nose and connects with the mouth. When the yangming interior is hot, there is thirst with a desire to drink water. Here the mouth is dry and one wants only to rinse with water and not swallow it, meaning the heat is in the channel and there is no heat in the interior. The yangming has abundant qi and blood; when the heat in the channel is severe, it presses the blood to move frenetically, and there will certainly be nosebleed.

## Notes

A dry mouth with no desire to drink but only to rinse indicates that the heat is not in the qi aspect (interior) but in the blood aspect or channels, a sign of impending nosebleed. It is often cited in later times as an example of locating heat by the presence or absence of thirst (some take it as a guide in diagnosing static blood).
