# Clause 211 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When there is much sweating, or sweating is repeatedly induced, the yang is lost; with delirious speech, one whose pulse is short dies; one whose pulse is spontaneously harmonious does not die.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

In one who has lost yang, with a dry stomach and delirious speech, a short pulse means the fluids are already exhausted and it cannot be treated. A spontaneously harmonious pulse means the upright qi has not yet declined, and he can still live.

## Notes

A clause judging by the pulse the prognosis of one with delirious speech after losing yang qi and fluids through excessive sweating. A short pulse (not reaching cun and chi) means exhaustion of qi and blood with poor prognosis; a harmonious pulse means the upright qi remains and the patient will live.
