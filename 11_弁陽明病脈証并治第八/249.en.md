# Clause 249 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In cold damage, when after vomiting there is abdominal distension and fullness, give Tiaowei Chengqi tang (Choijokito). 33. Use the first formula above.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The *Neijing* says: "All distension and enlargement of the abdomen belong to heat." When heat is in the upper jiao there is vomiting. When after vomiting it does not resolve and the abdomen again becomes distended and full, the pathogenic heat has entered the stomach. Tiaowei Chengqi tang is given to purge the stomach heat.

## Notes

Abdominal distension and fullness after the vomiting method are regarded as pathogenic heat entering the stomach and becoming excess, and Tiaowei Chengqi tang is given. It is contrasted with Clause 66 (distension after sweating: deficiency with excess, Houpo Shengjiang Banxia Gancao Renshen tang (Kobokushokyohangekanzoninjinto)).
