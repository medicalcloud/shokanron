# Clause 253 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yangming disease with fever and profuse sweating: purge urgently. Da Chengqi tang is appropriate. 37. Use the second formula above. (One version says Da Chaihu tang.)

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The evil heat has entered the bowel; when there are external fever and profuse sweating, the heat presses on the fluids, which are about to be exhausted. Da Chengqi tang is given urgently to purge the bowel heat.

## Notes

The second of the "three patterns for urgent purgation." When high fever and profuse sweating continue, the fluids are soon exhausted, so one purges urgently to remove the heat without waiting for abdominal signs (the idea called "urgent purgation to preserve yin").
