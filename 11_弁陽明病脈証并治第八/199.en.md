# Clause 199 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In yangming disease with no sweating, inhibited urination, and anguish in the heart, the body will certainly turn yellow.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

In yangming disease, no sweating and inhibited urination mean the heat is accumulated within and cannot pass out. Anguish in the heart means the heat qi is constrained and steaming, trying to erupt outward, and becomes yellow. [Next clause:] In yangming disease subjected to fire, with slight sweating on the forehead and inhibited urination, there will certainly be yellowing. Yangming disease is internal heat; when subjected to fire, fire and heat combine and become severe. If the whole body sweats and urination is free, the heat can drain and pass out, so there cannot be yellowing. Here there is slight sweating on the forehead and inhibited urination, so the heat cannot pass out and is constrained and steaming in the stomach [and turns yellow].

## Notes

In yangming disease, with neither sweating nor urination there is no exit for the heat, so damp-heat accumulates and causes jaundice. The idea of predicting jaundice from sweating and urination is common to the jaundice clauses of the yangming chapter (e.g., Clause 236). Cheng Wuji's commentary also covers the next clause (200).
