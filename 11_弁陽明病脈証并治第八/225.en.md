# Clause 225 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the pulse is floating and slow, with heat in the exterior and cold in the interior, and diarrhea with undigested food, Sini tang governs it. Formula 14.

Gancao 2 liang (mix-fried); ganjiang 1.5 liang; fuzi 1 piece (used raw, remove the skin, broken into eight pieces).

Of the three ingredients above, boil with 3 sheng of water down to 1 sheng 2 ge, remove the dregs, and take warm in two doses. For a strong person, a large piece of fuzi and 3 liang of ganjiang may be used.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Floating is exterior heat; slow is interior cold. Diarrhea with undigested food means the interior cold is severe. Sini tang is given to warm the interior and disperse the cold.

## Notes

Although placed in the yangming chapter, this clause states that one with diarrhea of undigested food and interior cold, even with heat in the exterior (false heat), has not yangming heat but a yin-cold pattern, and Sini tang warms the interior. It is taken as an example of urgently rescuing interior cold (the same purport as Clause 91).
