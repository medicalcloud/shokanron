# Clause 212 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In cold damage, after vomiting or purgation, [the disease] does not resolve; there is no bowel movement for five or six days up to more than ten days, tidal fever in the late afternoon, no aversion to cold, and talking to oneself as if seeing ghosts. In severe cases, during attacks [the patient] does not recognize people, fiddles with the clothes and gropes at the bed, is fearful and restless (one version says "strokes the clothes and grabs at random, fearful and restless"), with slight panting and a fixed stare: if the pulse is wiry, he lives; if rough, he dies. In mild cases with only fever and delirious speech, Da Chengqi tang governs it. If one dose produces diarrhea, stop the later doses. 4. Use the second formula above.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Vomiting or purgation both damage the stomach qi. No bowel movement for five or six days up to more than ten days means the fluids are lost, the stomach qi vacuous, and the evil heat bound within. The yangming flourishes at *shen*, *you*, and *xu*, so there is tidal fever in the late afternoon; the fever means yangming heat is severe. No aversion to cold means the exterior pattern has ended. Talking to oneself as if seeing ghosts means the yangming is replete within, the heat qi being in excess. In severe cases the heat qi is extremely great; extreme heat within clouds and covers the upright qi, so that one does not recognize people, fiddles with the clothes and gropes at the bed, is fearful and restless, with slight panting and a fixed stare. In cold damage, when yang prevails and yin is cut off, one dies; when yin prevails and yang is cut off, one also dies. Violent heat is yang prevailing. A wiry pulse means yin is in surplus; a rough pulse means yin is insufficient. Though the yang heat is violent, if the pulse is wiry, one knows the yin is not yet cut off, and he can still live; if the pulse is rough, the yin is cut off, so it cannot be treated. When the evil heat is mild and has not become violent, with only fever and delirious speech, Da Chengqi tang may be given to purge the heat in the stomach. The Classic says, "Whenever purgatives are taken, stop as soon as they hit the disease; it is not necessary to finish the preparation." Here the heat is not yet violent, so it says, "If one dose produces diarrhea, stop the later doses."

## Notes

A clause describing a severe state of yangming bowel repletion: prolonged constipation, tidal fever, delirious speech, and, when severe, disturbed consciousness (not recognizing people, fiddling with clothes and groping at the bed, fearful restlessness).

- In severe cases the prognosis is judged by the pulse: a wiry pulse (yin still remaining) means life; a rough pulse (yin exhausted) means death.
- Mild cases (only fever and delirious speech) are purged with Da Chengqi tang, stopping once one dose produces purgation.
- "Fiddling with the clothes and groping at the bed" describes a deranged state of stroking clothes and bedding; the phrase is often used in later literature as a grave sign.
