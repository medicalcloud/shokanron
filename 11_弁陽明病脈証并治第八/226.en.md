# Clause 226 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

If the stomach is vacuous and cold and one cannot eat, drinking water causes hiccup.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

*Yue* is hiccup (*keni*). The *Qianjin* says, "*Keni* is another name for *yueni*." When the stomach is vacuous and cold and receives water, water and cold contend, the stomach qi counterflows, and there is hiccup.

## Notes

A short clause stating that giving water to one whose stomach is vacuous and cold and who cannot eat intensifies the cold, makes the stomach qi counterflow, and causes hiccup. It is read in connection with the interior-cold pattern of the preceding clause.
