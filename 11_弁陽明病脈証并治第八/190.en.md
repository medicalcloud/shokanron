# Clause 190 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In Yangming disease, if one can eat, it is called wind attack; if one cannot eat, it is called cold-strike.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

In Yangming disease, wind and cold are distinguished by eating and drinking because the stomach is the sea of water and grain. Wind is a yang pathogen, and yang digests grain, so one with wind attack can eat. Cold is a yin pathogen, and yin pathogen does not digest grain, so one with cold damage cannot eat.

## Notes

A clause dividing Yangming disease by appetite into "wind attack" (heat, yang) and "cold-strike" (cold, yin). If there is heat in the stomach, grain is digested and one can eat; if the stomach is cold, one cannot.
