# Clause 183 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Question: Among diseases, there is the case in which, on the first day after contracting it, there is aversion to cold without fever; why? Answer: Although it is the first day after contracting it, the aversion to cold will soon end by itself, and then there will be spontaneous sweating and aversion to heat.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

When the evil lodges in the yangming, there should be fever without aversion to cold. That on the first day after contracting it there is still no fever but aversion to cold means the evil has not fully entered the bowel and still carries exterior evil. When the exterior evil has fully entered, there is no more aversion to cold; there will certainly be spontaneous sweating and aversion to heat.

## Notes

Even in yangming disease there may be aversion to cold on the first day of onset, but it soon stops of itself and changes into the yangming external pattern (spontaneous sweating, aversion to heat). The clause calls attention to distinguishing it from the aversion to cold of taiyang disease.
