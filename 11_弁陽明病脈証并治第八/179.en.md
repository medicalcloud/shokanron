# Clause 179 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Question: Among diseases there are taiyang yangming, true yang yangming, and shaoyang yangming; what are they? Answer: Taiyang yangming is spleen restraint (*piyue*) (*yue* is written *luo* in one version). True yang yangming is stomach-family repletion. Shaoyang yangming is when, after sweating and promoting urination, the stomach is dry, vexed, and replete, and the stool is difficult.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Yangming is the stomach. When the evil is transmitted from the taiyang channel into the bowel, it is called taiyang yangming. The Classic says, "In taiyang disease, when after vomiting, purgation, or sweating there are slight vexation, frequent urination, and consequently hard stool, give Xiao Chengqi tang"—this is the spleen-restraint disease of taiyang yangming.

When the evil is transmitted from the yangming channel into the bowel, it is called true yang yangming. The Classic says, "In yangming disease with a slow pulse, sweating without aversion to cold, the body will certainly be heavy, with shortness of breath, abdominal fullness, panting, and tidal fever; the exterior is about to resolve and the interior may be attacked. When the hands and feet sweat profusely, the stool is already hard; Da Chengqi tang governs it"—this is the stomach-family repletion of true yang yangming.

When the evil is transmitted from the shaoyang channel into the bowel, it is called shaoyang yangming. The Classic says, "Cold damage with a wiry, fine pulse, headache, and fever belongs to shaoyang. Shaoyang must not be sweated; if sweated, there will be delirious speech; this belongs to the stomach"—this is shaoyang yangming disease.

## Notes

The opening question-and-answer of the yangming chapter, classifying the origins of yangming disease into three.

- Taiyang yangming (spleen restraint): arising from taiyang disease, with the spleen failing to move the fluids so that the stool becomes hard; regarded as the Maziren wan pattern (Clause 247).
- True yang yangming (stomach-family repletion): replete heat of the yangming itself; the Chengqi tang pattern.
- Shaoyang yangming: the stomach made dry by mistakenly sweating or promoting urination in shaoyang disease.
- Cheng Wuji explains each by citing the corresponding clauses.
