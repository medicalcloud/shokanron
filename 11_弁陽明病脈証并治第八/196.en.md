# Clause 196 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yangming disease by rule has much sweating; when, on the contrary, there is no sweating and the body feels as if insects were crawling in the skin, it is because of long-standing vacuity.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The stomach is the bowel of the fluids. When qi is vacuous and fluids scant, there is, on the contrary, no sweating in illness. The stomach governs the flesh of the body; when the body feels as if insects were crawling in the skin, one knows the stomach qi has long been vacuous.

## Notes

When sweat that should appear in yangming disease does not, and there is a sensation of insects crawling in the skin, it is because long-standing vacuity of stomach qi and fluids prevents producing sweat. The clause shows that yangming disease also has vacuity patterns.
