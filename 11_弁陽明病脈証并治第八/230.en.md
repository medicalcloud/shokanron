# Clause 230 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In yangming disease with hardness and fullness below the rib-side, no bowel movement, retching, and a white tongue coating, Xiao Chaihu tang may be given. The upper burner is freed, the fluids can descend, the stomach qi is thereby harmonized, the body sweats continuously, and it resolves. 17. Use the formula above.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

In yangming disease with abdominal fullness, no bowel movement, and a yellow tongue coating, the evil heat has entered the bowel and it may be purged. If there are hardness and fullness below the rib-side, and though there is no bowel movement there is retching, with a white tongue coating, the evil has not yet entered the bowel but is between exterior and interior; Xiao Chaihu tang is given to harmonize and resolve it. When the upper burner is freed, the retching stops; when the fluids can descend, the stomach qi is thereby harmonized and [the body sweats continuously and it resolves].

## Notes

Even with constipation, hardness and fullness below the rib-side, retching, and a white coating constitute a shaoyang pattern; freeing the upper burner with Xiao Chaihu tang lets the fluids descend, regulates the bowels, and resolves the disease with sweating. The clause is often cited to explain the action of Xiao Chaihu tang (freeing the triple burner). It is also taken as an example of distinguishing by tongue coating (yellow: yangming bowel repletion; white: shaoyang).
