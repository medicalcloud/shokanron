# Clause 219 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Combined disease of the three yang: abdominal fullness, heaviness of the body with difficulty turning over, insensitivity of the mouth, a grimy face (also written "withered"; one version says *xiangjing*), delirium, and enuresis. If sweating is induced, there will be delirium; if purged, sweat will appear on the forehead with counterflow cold of the hands and feet. If there is spontaneous sweating, Baihu tang governs it. Formula 9.

Zhimu 6 liang; shigao 1 jin (crushed); gancao 2 liang (mix-fried); jingmi 6 ge.

Of the four ingredients above, with 1 dou of water boil until the rice is cooked and the decoction is done, remove the dregs, and take 1 sheng warm, three times a day.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Abdominal fullness, heaviness of the body with difficulty turning over, insensitivity of the mouth, and delirium are Yangming. The *Zhenjing* says: "When Shaoyang disease is severe, the face looks slightly dusty"; this grimy face is Shaoyang. Enuresis is Taiyang. Since Yangming signs predominate among the three, it appears in the Yangming chapter. Combined disease of the three yang has pathogen in both exterior and interior. If sweating is induced to attack the exterior, the dry heat becomes even more severe and there will certainly be more delirium. If purged to attack the interior, the exterior heat takes advantage of the deficiency to sink inward, and there will certainly be sweating on the forehead with counterflow cold of the hands and feet. Spontaneous sweating means the heat of the three yang meridians is severe. The *Neijing* says: "With heat, the interstices open, the ying-nutrient and wei-defense flow, and sweat drains greatly." Baihu tang is given to resolve the heat inside and out.

## Notes

The clause on Baihu tang for combined disease of the three yang (Taiyang, Shaoyang, Yangming) in which Yangming heat predominates.

- Both sweating and purgation are mistakes; if there is spontaneous sweating, the heat is exuberant and trying to escape, and Baihu tang clears the heat.
- "If there is spontaneous sweating, Baihu tang governs it" is generally read as following the first part of the clause (the list of signs), an inverted construction.
