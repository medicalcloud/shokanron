# Clause 194 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In Yangming disease, when one who cannot eat has the heat attacked, there will certainly be hiccup. The reason is that the stomach is deficient and cold. Because the person is originally deficient, attacking the heat will certainly cause hiccup.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Inability to eat means the stomach is originally cold. Attacking the heat further vacuates the stomach, and deficiency and cold contend, causing hiccup. The Classic says, "A weak guan pulse means the stomach qi is deficient; even with heat, it must not be greatly attacked; when the heat goes, cold arises"—this is what is meant.

## Notes

A warning that attacking the heat (purging with cold medicines) in Yangming disease when the patient cannot eat (cold-strike, stomach deficiency cold) further chills the stomach and causes hiccup. It corresponds to Clause 190, "cannot eat: called cold-strike."
