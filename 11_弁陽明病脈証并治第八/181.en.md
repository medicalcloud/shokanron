# Clause 181 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Question: How does one come to have yangming disease? Answer: When in taiyang disease sweating, or purgation, or promotion of urination has caused loss of fluids, the stomach becomes dry, and consequently [the disease] shifts to belong to the yangming; when there is no "changing of clothes" (no bowel movement), internal repletion, and difficult stool, it is called yangming.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Originally taiyang disease did not resolve, and through sweating and promoting urination the fluids were lost; the stomach became dry, the taiyang evil entered the bowel, and [the disease] shifted to belong to the yangming. In ancient times people always changed their clothes when going to the privy, so "not changing clothes" means, in general, not having a bowel movement. Without a bowel movement, the contents of the stomach cannot drain, so there is internal repletion. With no fluids in the stomach and accumulated heat besides, the stool becomes difficult—yangming interior repletion.

## Notes

A clause on how taiyang disease shifts into yangming disease. When sweating, purgation, or diuresis has lost the fluids, the stomach and intestines dry out, causing constipation and internal repletion: yangming disease. "Changing clothes" is a euphemism for defecation (Cheng Wuji's explanation is generally accepted).
