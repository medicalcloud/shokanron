# Clause 181 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Question: How does one come to have Yangming disease? Answer: When in Taiyang disease sweating, or purgation, or promotion of urination has caused loss of fluids, the stomach becomes dry, and consequently [the disease] shifts to belong to the Yangming; when there is no "changing of clothes" (no bowel movement), internal excess, and difficult stool, it is called Yangming.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Originally Taiyang disease did not resolve, and through sweating and promoting urination the fluids were lost; the stomach became dry, the Taiyang pathogen entered the bowel, and [the disease] shifted to belong to the Yangming. In ancient times people always changed their clothes when going to the privy, so "not changing clothes" means, in general, not having a bowel movement. Without a bowel movement, the contents of the stomach cannot drain, so there is internal excess. With no fluids in the stomach and accumulated heat besides, the stool becomes difficult—Yangming interior excess.

## Notes

A clause on how Taiyang disease shifts into Yangming disease. When sweating, purgation, or diuresis has lost the fluids, the stomach and intestines dry out, causing constipation and internal excess: Yangming disease. "Changing clothes" is a euphemism for defecation (Cheng Wuji's explanation is generally accepted).
