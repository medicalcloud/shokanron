# Clause 242 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When a patient has inhibited urination, stool now difficult and now easy, occasional slight fever, and panting and veiling (one version writes "depression") so that he cannot lie down, there is dry stool; Da Chengqi tang is appropriate. 28. Use the second formula above.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

When urination is free, the stool becomes hard. Here, because there is dry stool, urination is inhibited and the stool is now difficult, now easy. One with stomach heat has fever, panting, and veiling at all times and likes to lie down; here dry stool is in the stomach, so there are occasional slight fever and panting and veiling, and he cannot lie down. Da Chengqi tang is given to purge the dry stool.

## Notes

This clause states that even with dry stool, inhibited urination may make the stool pass at times (stool passing around the dry stool) and not at others; for one with panting and veiling who cannot lie down, Da Chengqi tang is used. It is taken as an example that passing stool does not necessarily mean there is no dry stool.
