# Clause 232 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the pulse is only floating with no other signs, give Mahuang tang. If there is no urine, with abdominal fullness and hiccup added, it cannot be treated. Mahuang tang formula. 19.

Mahuang 3 liang (remove the nodes); guizhi 2 liang (remove the bark); gancao 1 liang (mix-fried); xingren 70 kernels (remove the skins and tips).

Of the four ingredients above, with 9 sheng of water boil the mahuang, reducing by 2 sheng; remove the white froth, add the other ingredients, boil down to 2.5 sheng, remove the dregs, and take 8 ge warm. Cover to obtain a slight sweat.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Floating and large are yang—wind in the exterior; wiry is yin—wind in the interior. Shortness of breath, abdominal fullness, and pain from below the rib-side to the heart mean wind-heat is blocking the abdomen and not flowing. If cold lodged within and caused pain, pressing would disperse the cold qi and stop the pain; here wind-heat blocks within, so even long pressure does not let the qi through. In yangming disease, a dry nose with inability to lie down and spontaneous sweating mean the evil is in the exterior; here a dry nose without sweating and a liking to lie down mean wind-heat is attacking within and not [reaching] the exterior. Yellowing of the whole body, face, and eyes, difficult urination, tidal fever, and occasional hiccup mean wind-heat is attacking the stomach. The yangming vessel emerges at Daying, runs along Jiache, rises before the ear, and passes Kezhuren; when heat prevails there is swelling. Here wind-heat is in the channel, so there is swelling before and behind the ears; needling frees the channel qi and the swelling improves slightly. In such a case, once the external pattern has ended, it may be attacked. If the external pattern does not resolve and after ten days the pulse is still floating, the evil qi is still half exterior, half interior; Xiao Chaihu tang is given to harmonize and resolve it. If the pulse is only floating, not wiry or large, and there are none of the interior signs, the evil is only in the exterior, and Mahuang tang may be given to induce sweating. If there is no urine, with abdominal fullness and hiccup added, it is the disease of block and repulsion (*guange*); hence "it cannot be treated." The *Nanjing* says: "One with block and repulsion cannot live out his life and dies."

## Notes

Following Clause 231: if the pulse is only floating with no interior signs, Mahuang tang induces sweating; if there is no urine and hiccup is added to abdominal fullness, it is the dangerous state of "block and repulsion," with obstruction above and below, and it cannot be treated.
