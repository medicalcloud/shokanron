# Clause 220 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Overlap disease of the two yang: when the taiyang pattern has ended and there is only tidal fever, with continuous sweating of the hands and feet, difficult stool, and delirious speech, purging it cures it; Da Chengqi tang is appropriate. 10. Use the second formula above.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

When originally taiyang disease merges into the yangming, it is called overlap disease. The taiyang pattern having ended means there is no exterior pattern. Only tidal fever means the heat has merged into the yangming. Sweating of the whole body is heat passing out; here there is continuous sweating of the hands and feet, meaning the heat has gathered in the stomach. There will certainly be difficult stool and delirious speech. The Classic says, "When the hands and feet sweat continuously, the stool is certainly already hard." Da Chengqi tang is given to purge the replete heat in the stomach.

## Notes

In overlap disease of taiyang and yangming, once the taiyang pattern (exterior pattern) has ended and only the yangming bowel-repletion signs remain (tidal fever, sweating of the hands and feet, constipation, delirious speech), purging with Da Chengqi tang is permitted. It shows that the presence of an exterior pattern decides whether purgation is allowed.
