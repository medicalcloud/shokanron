# Clause 193 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The time when yangming disease tends to resolve is from shen to xu.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The fourth month is yang, and earth flourishes at *shen*, *you*, and *xu*. The time approaching flourishing is the time of resolution.

## Notes

The "time of tending to resolve" for yangming disease is from *shen* to *xu* (roughly 3 p.m. to 9 p.m.), regarded as the time when yangming qi flourishes. The same purport as Clause 9 (taiyang).
