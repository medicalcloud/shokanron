# Clause 243 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Eating grain with a desire to retch belongs to the Yangming; Wuzhuyu tang (Goshuyuto; Evodia Decoction) governs it. One who becomes worse after the decoction belongs to the upper jiao. Wuzhuyu tang formula. 29.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The upper jiao governs taking in, and the stomach is its market. Eating grain with a desire to retch means the stomach does not accept it. Wuzhuyu tang is given to warm the stomach qi. One who becomes worse after the decoction does not take in at the upper jiao; treat it by the method for treating the upper jiao.

(Nature and flavor) Wuzhuyu (evodia): acrid, hot. Renshen (ninjin): sweet, warm. Shengjiang (shokyo): acrid, warm. Dazao (taiso): sweet, warm.

The *Neijing* says: "When cold runs to excess within, treat with sweet and hot, assisted by bitter and acrid." The acridity of wuzhuyu and shengjiang warms the stomach; the sweetness of renshen and dazao moderates the spleen.

## Notes

Nausea on eating is attributed to deficiency cold of the stomach (middle jiao), and Wuzhuyu tang warms the stomach. If Wuzhuyu tang makes it worse, it is due not to cold but to heat in the upper jiao, requiring other treatment.

- The base text lacks the Wuzhuyu tang formula (composition and preparation) here. For the formula in the other sources, see the notes of this unit (wuzhuyu 1 sheng, renshen 3 liang, shengjiang 6 liang, dazao 12 pieces).
- Wuzhuyu tang also appears in the Shaoyin chapter (Clause 309) and the Jueyin chapter (Clause 378).
