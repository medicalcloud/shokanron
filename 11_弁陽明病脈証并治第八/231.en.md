# Clause 231 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yangming wind-strike with a wiry, floating, and large pulse, shortness of breath, fullness of the whole abdomen, pain from below the rib-side to the heart that, though pressed long, does not let the qi through, a dry nose, inability to sweat, a liking to lie down, yellowing of the whole body and eyes, difficult urination, tidal fever, occasional hiccup, and swelling before and behind the ears: needling it brings slight improvement, but when the exterior does not resolve, the disease has passed ten days, and the pulse continues floating, give Xiao Chaihu tang. 18. Use the formula above.

## Notes

A clause describing the course and treatment of a grave disease mixing signs of yangming, shaoyang, and taiyang (jaundice of combined disease of the three yang); it is read together with the next clause (232). The swelling before and behind the ears is needled, and if the pulse is still floating, Xiao Chaihu tang is given. Cheng Wuji's commentary is given together with Clause 232.
