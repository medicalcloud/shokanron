# Clause 224 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In yangming disease with much sweating and thirst, Zhuling tang must not be given, because with much sweating the stomach is dry, and Zhuling tang further promotes urination.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The *Zhenjing* says: "Water and grain enter the mouth and are conveyed to the intestines and stomach; their fluid divides into five. When the weather is cold and clothing thin, it becomes urine; when the weather is hot and clothing thick, it becomes sweat." That is, sweat and urine are one fluid. Much sweating means the fluids drain outward and the stomach is dry, so Zhuling tang must not be given to promote urination.

## Notes

A contraindication of Zhuling tang. Much sweating with thirst is due to insufficient fluids, and a diuretic must not further deplete them. It rests on the idea that sweat and urine come from the same source (fluids).
