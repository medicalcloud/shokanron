# Clause 254 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When sweating does not resolve it and there are abdominal fullness and pain, purge urgently. Da Chengqi tang is appropriate. 38. Use the second formula above.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

When sweating does not resolve it and evil heat is transmitted into the bowel, causing abdominal fullness and pain, the transmission is rapid; therefore it must be purged urgently.

## Notes

The third of the "three patterns for urgent purgation." Abdominal fullness and pain appearing soon after sweating mean the evil heat has rapidly entered the bowel and become replete, and it is purged without hesitation.
