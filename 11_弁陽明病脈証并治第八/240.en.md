# Clause 240 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When a patient has vexing heat that resolves with sweating, but again, like malaria, has fever in the late afternoon, it belongs to the yangming. If the pulse is replete, it is appropriate to purge; if the pulse is floating and vacuous, it is appropriate to induce sweating. To purge, give Da Chengqi tang; to induce sweating, Guizhi tang is appropriate. 26. For Da Chengqi tang use the second formula above; for Guizhi tang use the twenty-first formula above.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Even with a yangming pattern, one cannot at once regard it as interior repletion; examine the pulse to distinguish inside from outside. When the pulse is replete, the heat has already entered the bowel and become repletion, and Da Chengqi tang may be given to purge it. When the pulse is floating and vacuous, the heat has not yet entered the bowel and is still in the exterior; giving Guizhi tang to induce sweating will cure it.

## Notes

Even with fever in the late afternoon (resembling tidal fever), if the pulse is replete it is yangming bowel repletion to be purged with Da Chengqi tang; if the pulse is floating and vacuous it is an exterior pattern to be sweated with Guizhi tang. It is taken as an example of distinguishing exterior and interior by the pulse to choose the treatment.
