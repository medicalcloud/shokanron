# Clause 218 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In cold damage of four or five days with a deep pulse and panting fullness—deep means in the interior—if, on the contrary, sweating is induced, the fluids pass out, the stool becomes difficult, the exterior is vacuous and the interior replete, and in time there will be delirious speech.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

When the evil qi has entered within, a deep pulse with panting and fullness means the interior pattern is complete, and it should be purged. If, on the contrary, sweating is induced so that the fluids pass out, the stomach becomes dry and the stool will certainly be difficult; in time the stool dries, the stomach becomes replete, and there will certainly be delirious speech.

## Notes

A warning that mistakenly sweating a patient with an interior pattern (deep pulse, panting fullness) loses the fluids and hardens the stool, leading to exterior vacuity with interior repletion and delirious speech. An example of misuse of sweating.
