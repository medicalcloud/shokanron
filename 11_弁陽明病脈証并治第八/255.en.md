# Clause 255 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When abdominal fullness does not diminish, or diminishes too little to mention, it should be purged. Da Chengqi tang is appropriate. 39. Use the second formula above.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Abdominal fullness that does not diminish means the evil qi is replete. The classic says: "Great fullness and great repletion may of course be removed by purgation." Da Chengqi tang purges the fullness and repletion. If the abdominal fullness diminishes at times, it is not interior repletion and must not be purged. The *Jingui Yaolüe* says: "Abdominal fullness that diminishes at times and then returns as before is cold; warming medicinals should be given." This is [what is distinguished from] "diminishes too little to mention."

## Notes

A clause giving the key distinction: persistent, undiminishing abdominal fullness is repletion and may be purged, whereas fullness that sometimes eases is vacuity cold and should be warmed.

- Cheng Wuji cites the *Jingui Yaolüe* chapter on abdominal fullness ("abdominal fullness that diminishes at times and returns as before is cold") for contrast.
