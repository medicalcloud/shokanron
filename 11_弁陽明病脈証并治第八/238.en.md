# Clause 238 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In yangming disease, after purgation, when there is anguish and vexation in the heart and there is dry stool in the stomach, it may be attacked. When the abdomen is slightly full and only the head of the stool is hard and the rest will certainly be sloppy, it must not be attacked. If there is dry stool, Da Chengqi tang is appropriate. 25. Use the second formula above.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Anguish and vexation in the heart after purgation is vacuity vexation, for which Zhizi Chi tang should be given. If there is dry stool in the stomach, it is not vacuity vexation, and Da Chengqi tang may be given to purge it. When the abdomen is slightly full, hard at first and sloppy afterward, there is no dry stool; the heat is not in the stomach but above, so it must not be attacked.

## Notes

Even for anguish and vexation after purgation, if there is dry stool, it may be attacked again with Da Chengqi tang; if not, one must not attack. Cheng Wuji adds that anguish without dry stool is the vacuity vexation of Zhizi Chi tang.
