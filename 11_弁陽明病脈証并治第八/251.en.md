# Clause 251 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Two or three days after contracting the disease, the pulse is weak, there is no taiyang or Chaihu pattern, there are vexation and agitation, and hardness below the heart. By the fourth or fifth day, although one can eat, give a little Xiao Chengqi tang to harmonize slightly and bring slight relief. By the sixth day, give 1 sheng of Chengqi tang. If there is no bowel movement for six or seven days and urine is scant, although food is not accepted (one version says "no bowel movement"), only the head of the stool is hard and the rest will certainly be sloppy; hard stool has not yet formed, and attacking it will certainly produce sloppy stool. One must wait until urination is free and the stool is definitely hard, and then it may be attacked; Da Chengqi tang is appropriate. 35. Use the second formula above.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The *Zhenjing* says: "When the pulse is soft, the disease is about to [move] down." Weak is a yin pulse, and the evil should be regarded as in the interior. A weak pulse two or three days after contracting the disease means that, although few days have passed, the evil qi has already entered the interior. No taiyang pattern means the exterior pattern has already ended; no Chaihu pattern means there is no half-exterior half-interior pattern. Vexation, agitation, and hardness below the heart mean the evil qi is severe within. When the stomach is replete with severe heat, one cannot eat; when the stomach is vacuous with severe heat, even though one can eat by the fourth or fifth day, Xiao Chengqi tang should still be given to harmonize slightly. By the sixth day the heat becomes severe, so 1 sheng of Da Chengqi tang is given. If there is no bowel movement for six or seven days and urine is copious, the fluids are exhausted within and the stool is certainly hard, so it may be purged. When urine is scant, water and grain are not separated in the stomach, and the stool will certainly be hard at first and sloppy afterward; even if one cannot eat because the stomach is replete, with scant urine hard stool has not yet formed, and it must not be attacked. One must wait until urination is free and the stool is definitely hard, and then it may be attacked.

## Notes

A clause showing a procedure of cautiously strengthening purgation as yangming bowel repletion progresses: a little Xiao Chengqi tang, then 1 sheng of Chengqi tang, then Da Chengqi tang. The key point is judging from the volume of urine whether the stool has hardened and set.
