# Clause 244 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease with a moderate cun pulse, floating guan pulse, and weak chi pulse: when the person has fever and sweating, again aversion to cold, no retching, and only glomus below the heart, it is because the physician purged. If it was not purged and the patient has no aversion to cold but is thirsty, it has shifted to belong to the yangming. When urination is frequent, the stool will certainly be hard, but even without a bowel movement for ten days there is no discomfort. For thirst with a desire to drink water, give a little at a time; simply rescue according to the method. For thirst, Wuling san is appropriate. Formula 30.

Zhuling (remove the skin), baizhu, and fuling, 18 zhu each; zexie 1 liang 6 zhu; guizhi half a liang (remove the bark).

Make the five ingredients above into powder and take a square-inch spoonful mixed with white drink, three times a day.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

In taiyang disease, a pulse floating at yang and weak at yin means the evil is in the exterior. Here the cun is moderate, the guan floating, and the chi weak, so the evil qi is gradually being transmitted inward. Fever, sweating, and again aversion to cold mean the exterior has not resolved. When the evil transmitted through the channels enters the interior and the interior is not harmonized, there must be retching; here there is no retching but only glomus below the heart, because the physician purged too early and the evil qi remains below the heart. If it was not purged, there will gradually be no aversion to cold but thirst—the taiyang evil has shifted to belong to the yangming. If after vomiting, purgation, or sweating there are frequent urination and hard stool, Xiao Chengqi tang should be given to harmonize it. Here frequent urination and hard stool do not follow vomiting, purgation, or sweating, so if there is no fullness or repletion, even without a bowel movement for ten days there is no discomfort; wait for the fluids to return into the stomach and urination to become less frequent, and the stool will certainly come out spontaneously. For thirst with a desire to drink water, give a little at a time to moisten the stomach qi. Simply examine where the evil qi is and rescue according to the method—as when thirst does not stop, Wuling san is given.

## Notes

A clause on various courses in the shift from taiyang to yangming disease (glomus below the heart from mistaken purgation, constipation from natural shift, thirst) and their handling.

- One who has frequent urination and hard stool but no discomfort may be left without purgation (the same purport as Clause 203).
- Thirst is met by giving water a little at a time; if thirst does not stop and there is collected water, Wuling san is used.
