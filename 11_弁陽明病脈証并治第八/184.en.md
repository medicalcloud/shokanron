# Clause 184 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Question: Why does the aversion to cold end by itself? Answer: The Yangming dwells in the center and governs earth; it is where all things return, and from there nothing is transmitted further. Although there is aversion to cold at first, it stops by itself on the second day; this is Yangming disease.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The stomach is the sea of water and grain and governs nourishing the four sides. When there is disease in the four sides, it can all be transmitted into the stomach, and once in the stomach it is not transmitted further—just as when Taiyang [disease] is transmitted into the stomach it is no longer transmitted to the Yangming, when Yangming disease is transmitted into the stomach it is no longer transmitted to the Shaoyang, and when Shaoyang disease is transmitted into the stomach it is no longer transmitted to the three yin.

## Notes

Since the Yangming (stomach) corresponds to earth among the five phases and is in the center where all things return, pathogen that has entered the stomach bowel is not transmitted to other meridians. The clause is regarded as explaining, by five-phase theory, why the aversion to cold of Yangming stops early.
