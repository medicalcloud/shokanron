# Clause 197 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In yangming disease (one version says "winter yangming"), when, on the contrary, there is no sweating, urination is free, and after two or three days there are retching, cough, and reversal-cold of the hands and feet, there will certainly be suffering from headache; if there is no cough or retching and the hands and feet are not reversal-cold, the head does not ache.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Yangming disease by rule has much sweating; one with, on the contrary, no sweating and free urination has yangming cold damage, with cold qi attacking within. When after two or three days there are retching, cough, and reversal of the limbs, the cold evil has erupted outward, and there will certainly be suffering from headache. If there is no cough or retching and the limbs are not reversal-cold, the cold evil only attacks the interior and does not erupt outward, so the head does not ache either.

## Notes

In yangming cold-strike, one in whom cold qi counterflows upward, with retching, cough, and reversal-cold of the limbs, has headache; otherwise there is no headache. The presence of headache is explained by whether the cold counterflows upward. It pairs with the next clause (198, wind-strike).
