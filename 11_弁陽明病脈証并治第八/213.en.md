# Clause 213 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In yangming disease, when the person sweats much, the fluids go out, the stomach becomes dry, and the stool will certainly be hard; with hardness there is delirious speech; Xiao Chengqi tang governs it. If after one dose the delirious speech stops, do not take it again. 5. Use the second formula above.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Loss of fluids with a dry stomach, hard stool, and delirious speech: even without great heat bound within, Xiao Chengqi tang should be given to harmonize the stomach qi. If after one dose the delirious speech stops, the dryness of the stomach has been moistened, and Chengqi tang must not be given again, because originally there is no replete heat.

## Notes

For one who has lost fluids through heavy sweating, with hard stool and delirious speech, even without great heat binding, Xiao Chengqi tang purges lightly and is stopped as soon as the delirious speech stops. It is taken as an example of warning against over-purgation.
