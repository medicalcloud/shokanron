# Clause 237 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In a yangming pattern, one who is forgetful certainly has blood amassment. The reason is that there is originally long-standing static blood, which makes him forgetful. Although the stool is hard, it passes, on the contrary, easily, and its color will certainly be black; it is appropriate to purge with Didang tang. Formula 24.

Shuizhi (dry-fried) and mengchong (remove the wings and legs, dry-fried), 30 each; dahuang 3 liang (washed in wine); taoren 20 kernels (remove the skins, tips, and double kernels).

Of the four ingredients above, boil with 5 sheng of water down to 3 sheng, remove the dregs, and take 1 sheng warm. If there is no purgation, take it again.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The *Neijing* says: "When blood merges below, there is derangement and forgetfulness." Here there is originally long-standing static blood below, hence forgetfulness. With scant fluids the stool is hard, but because amassed blood is within, though the stool is hard it passes easily and is black. Didang tang is given to purge the static blood.

## Notes

In yangming disease, one with forgetfulness whose stool, though hard, passes easily and is black has old static blood (blood amassment), which is purged with Didang tang. Whereas blood amassment in the taiyang chapter (Clauses 124–126) is marked by lower abdominal and mental signs (as if manic, mania), here forgetfulness and black stool are the marks.
