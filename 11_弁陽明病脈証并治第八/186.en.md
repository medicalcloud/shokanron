# Clause 186 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cold damage of three days: the yangming pulse is large.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Three days of cold damage is the time when the evil is transmitted to the yangming. The Classic says, "When the chi and cun are both long, the yangming has contracted the disease; it should manifest on the second or third day." The yangming has abundant qi and blood, and moreover the evil merges into the channel, so the pulse is large.

## Notes

A short clause stating that the main pulse of yangming disease is "large." Since the yangming is rich in qi and blood and the evil heat is exuberant, the pulse becomes large and forceful.
