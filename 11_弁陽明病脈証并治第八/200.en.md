# Clause 200 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In yangming disease subjected to fire, with slight sweating on the forehead and inhibited urination, there will certainly be yellowing.

## Notes

When fire therapy is added to yangming disease, fire and heat combine to intensify the heat; if sweat comes only from the forehead and urination does not flow, the heat has no exit and jaundice results. Cheng Wuji's commentary is given together with the preceding Clause 199.
