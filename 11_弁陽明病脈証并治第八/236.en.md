# Clause 236 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In yangming disease with fever and sweating, the heat passes out and there cannot be yellowing. When there is sweating only from the head, none from the body, stopping at the neck, with inhibited urination and thirst with a craving for fluids, there is static heat in the interior and the body will certainly turn yellow; Yinchenhao tang (Virgate Wormwood Decoction) governs it. Formula 23.

Yinchenhao (virgate wormwood) 6 liang; zhizi 14 fruits (split); dahuang 2 liang (remove the bark).

Of the three ingredients above, with 1 dou 2 sheng of water first boil the yinchen, reducing by 6 sheng, add the other two, boil down to 3 sheng, remove the dregs, and take in three doses. Urination should become free, the urine like the juice of gleditsia pods and deep red in color; after one night the abdomen [fullness] diminishes and the yellow leaves through the urine.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Sweating only from the head and none from the body, stopping at the neck, means the heat cannot pass out. Inhibited urination with thirst and craving for fluids means the heat is severe in the stomach and the fluids exhausted within. The stomach is earth and its color is yellow; when the stomach is steamed by heat, its color is drawn out to the outside and there will certainly be yellowing. Yinchen tang is given to expel the heat and remove the yellow.

(Nature and flavor) Yinchenhao: bitter, slightly cold. Zhizi: bitter, cold. Dahuang: bitter, cold.

The qi of slight heat is harmonized with coolness; the qi of great heat is taken with cold. The bitter cold of yinchen and zhizi expels stomach dryness. Where purging is appropriate, bitter must be used; where supplementing is appropriate, sour must be used. The bitter cold of dahuang purges the static heat.

## Notes

The clause on Yinchenhao tang, the representative formula for jaundice due to damp-heat (yang jaundice).

- When the whole body sweats, the heat leaves and there is no jaundice; when only the head sweats, urination is inhibited, and there is thirst, heat and dampness are trapped (static heat) and jaundice results.
- Yinchenhao, zhizi, and dahuang are regarded as expelling damp-heat through urine and stool; it records that after taking it, deep red urine is passed and the jaundice recedes.
