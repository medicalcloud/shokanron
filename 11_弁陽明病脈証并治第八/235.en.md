# Clause 235 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In yangming disease with a floating pulse, no sweating, and panting, sweating will cure it; Mahuang tang is appropriate. 22. Use the nineteenth formula above.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Yangming cold damage with exterior repletion: a floating pulse, no sweating, and panting. Mahuang tang is given to induce sweating.

## Notes

Even in yangming disease, if there is the taiyang cold-damage exterior-repletion pattern (floating pulse, no sweating, panting), Mahuang tang induces sweating. Paired with Clause 234.
