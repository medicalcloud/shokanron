# Clause 235 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In Yangming disease with a floating pulse, no sweating, and dyspnea, sweating will cure it; Mahuang tang is appropriate. 22. Use the nineteenth formula above.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Yangming cold damage with exterior excess: a floating pulse, no sweating, and dyspnea. Mahuang tang is given to induce sweating.

## Notes

Even in Yangming disease, if there is the Taiyang cold-damage exterior-excess pattern (floating pulse, no sweating, dyspnea), Mahuang tang induces sweating. Paired with Clause 234.
