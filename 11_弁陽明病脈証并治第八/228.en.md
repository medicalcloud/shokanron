# Clause 228 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In yangming disease, after purgation, when there is heat on the outside, the hands and feet are warm, there is no chest bind, there is anguish in the heart, hunger with inability to eat, and sweating only from the head, Zhizi Chi tang governs it. 15. Use the eleventh formula above.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Purging while the exterior has not ended should make the evil heat sink inward. When heat sinks inward, there is heat outside without cold hands and feet. Here there is heat outside and the hands and feet are warm, meaning that although the heat has sunk inward it is not deep, so chest bind does not form. Anguish in the heart and hunger with inability to eat mean heat lodges in the chest as vacuity vexation. The heat fumes and steams upward from the chest, so there is sweating only from the head and none from the body. Zhizi Chi tang is given to make [the patient] vomit the vacuity vexation in the chest.

## Notes

After purgation of yangming disease, when the heat does not bind deeply but stays in the chest (no chest bind), with anguish, hunger but inability to eat, and sweating from the head, Zhizi Chi tang is used. Like Clause 221, an example of clearing heat in the chest (vacuity vexation).
