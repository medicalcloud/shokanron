# Clause 260 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In cold damage of seven or eight days, when the body is yellow like the color of a tangerine, urination is inhibited, and the abdomen is slightly full, Yinchenhao tang governs it. 42. Use the thirteenth formula above.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

At the time when the heat is severe, the body being yellow like a tangerine means the heat toxin is effusing outward. The *Neijing* says: "The bladder stores the fluids; when qi transformation occurs, they can come out." Inhibited urination and lower-abdominal fullness mean the heat qi is severe outside and the fluids cannot move downward. Yinchen tang is given to free urination, abate the yellowing, and expel the heat.

## Notes

Bright yellow jaundice (tangerine color) is yang jaundice due to heat; Yinchenhao tang is used when it is accompanied by inhibited urination and abdominal fullness. Together with Clause 236 it is regarded as a representative clause for Yinchenhao tang. Cheng Wuji's commentary reads "inhibited urination and slight abdominal fullness" as "inhibited urination and lower-abdominal fullness."
