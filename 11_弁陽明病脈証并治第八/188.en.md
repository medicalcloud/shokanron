# Clause 188 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When cold damage shifts to be tied to the yangming, the person sweats slightly and continuously.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Cold damage has no sweating; yangming by rule has much sweating. Here the cold-damage evil has shifted to be tied to the yangming, so there is slight continuous sweating.

## Notes

A short clause stating that when sweatless cold damage shifts into yangming, sweating begins (the same purport as Clause 185).
