# Clause 112 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In cold damage with a floating pulse, when the physician has pressed and forced it with fire, causing loss of yang, there will certainly be fright mania with restlessness whether lying or rising; Guizhi qu Shaoyao jia Shuqi Muli Longgu Jiuni tang (Cinnamon Twig Decoction minus Peony plus Dichroa Leaf, Oyster Shell, and Dragon Bone, Counterflow-Rescuing) governs it. Formula 60.

Guizhi 3 liang (remove the bark); gancao 2 liang (mix-fried); shengjiang 3 liang (sliced); dazao 12 pieces (split); muli 5 liang (dry-fried); shuqi (dichroa leaf) 3 liang (washed to remove the rank smell); longgu 4 liang.

Of the seven ingredients above, with 1 dou 2 sheng of water first boil the shuqi, reducing by 2 sheng, add the other ingredients, boil down to 3 sheng, remove the dregs, and take 1 sheng warm. Originally called Guizhi tang; now shaoyao is removed and shuqi, muli, and longgu are added.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

A floating pulse in cold damage means the evil should be regarded as in the exterior. When the physician forces sweating with fire and the sweating is profuse, the yang is lost. Sweat is the fluid of the heart; loss of yang makes the heart qi vacuous. The heart dislikes heat; when fire evil presses inward, the heart spirit floats and escapes, so there are fright mania and restlessness whether rising or lying. Guizhi tang is given to resolve the unexhausted exterior evil, and shaoyao is removed because shaoyao boosts yin and is unsuitable for loss of yang. Since the fire evil is confused and counterflowing, the acridity of shuqi is added to disperse it. Since the yang qi is lost and deserting, the astringency of longgu and muli is added to secure it. The *Bencao* says: "Astringent can remove desertion; longgu and muli are of this kind."

(Nature and flavor) Muli: sour and salty. Longgu: sweet, neutral. Shuqi: acrid, neutral.

## Notes

The clause on Jiuni tang (Guizhi qu Shaoyao jia Shuqi Muli Longgu Jiuni tang), used when forcing sweat with fire has lost the heart yang, causing easy fright, manic agitation, and restlessness.

- It is generally explained that guizhi and gancao restore the heart yang, longgu and muli calm and secure the spirit, and shuqi removes phlegm.
- "Jiuni" means rescuing from mistreatment (counterflow).
