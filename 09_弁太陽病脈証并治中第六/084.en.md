# Clause 84 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

A person with strangury (*linjia*) must not be made to sweat; if made to sweat, there will certainly be blood in the urine.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Heat in the interior of the bladder causes strangury. If instead a decoction is used to induce sweating, the fluids are lost, the visiting heat increases, the bladder becomes vacuous and dry, and there will certainly be blood in the urine.

## Notes

A person with chronic strangury (painful, frequent urination, etc.) often has heat in the lower burner and yin vacuity, so sweating, by further depleting the fluids and intensifying the heat, would cause hematuria; hence it is prohibited.
