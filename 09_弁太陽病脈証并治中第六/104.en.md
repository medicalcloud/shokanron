# Clause 104 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cold damage unresolved after thirteen days, with fullness of the chest and rib-side and retching, tidal fever in the late afternoon, and then slight diarrhea. This was originally a Chaihu pattern; purging it [with Chaihu tang] would not cause diarrhea. That there is diarrhea instead shows that the physician purged with pills; this is not the right treatment. Tidal fever is repletion. It is appropriate first to take Xiao Chaihu tang to resolve the exterior, and afterward Chaihu jia Mangxiao tang (Bupleurum Decoction plus Mirabilite) governs it. 54.

Chaihu 2 liang 16 zhu; huangqin 1 liang; renshen 1 liang; gancao 1 liang (mix-fried); shengjiang 1 liang (sliced); banxia 20 zhu (originally said to be 5 pieces; washed); dazao 4 pieces (split); mangxiao 2 liang.

Of the eight ingredients above, boil with 4 sheng of water down to 2 sheng, remove the dregs, add the mangxiao, boil again to a slight simmer, and take warm in two doses. If it does not resolve, make it again.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The thirteenth day of cold damage is when the second transmission through the channels is complete and it should resolve. If it does not resolve and there are fullness of the chest and rib-side and retching, the evil qi is still between exterior and interior; this is the Chaihu tang pattern. If it were purged with Chaihu tang, there should be no tidal fever or spontaneous diarrhea. The physician instead purged with pills, vacuating the intestines and stomach; the evil qi took advantage of the vacuity to enter the bowel, producing tidal fever in the late afternoon and diarrhea after the fever. Although tidal fever is heat repletion, the evil of the chest and rib-side has not ended, so first Xiao Chaihu tang is given to resolve the exterior, and afterward Chaihu jia Mangxiao [tang] to purge the stomach heat.

## Notes

When yangming replete heat (tidal fever) has been added to a shaoyang pattern (fullness of chest and rib-side, retching), but mistaken purgation with pills has caused diarrhea, first Xiao Chaihu tang and then Chaihu jia Mangxiao tang are used.

- Tidal fever (fever rising at a set time, especially late afternoon) is taken as a mark of yangming replete heat.
- Chaihu jia Mangxiao tang is a reduced dose of Xiao Chaihu tang with mangxiao added, regarded as purging interior heat more gently than Da Chaihu tang. It is often explained that the strong Da Chaihu tang is not used because the upright qi has already been damaged by mistaken purgation.
