# Clause 107 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cold damage of eight or nine days: when after purgation there are fullness of the chest, vexation and fright, inhibited urination, delirious speech, heaviness of the whole body, and inability to turn over, Chaihu jia Longgu Muli tang (Bupleurum Decoction plus Dragon Bone and Oyster Shell) governs it. Formula 57.

Chaihu 4 liang; longgu (dragon bone), huangqin, shengjiang (sliced), qiandan (minium), renshen, guizhi (remove the bark), and fuling, 1.5 liang each; banxia 2.5 ge (washed); dahuang 2 liang; muli 1.5 liang (dry-fried); dazao 6 pieces (split).

Of the twelve ingredients above, boil with 8 sheng of water down to 4 sheng, add the dahuang cut into pieces the size of go stones, boil once or twice more, remove the dregs, and take 1 sheng warm. Originally called Chaihu tang; now longgu and the others are added.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Eight or nine days of cold damage is when the evil qi has already become heat and is transmitted again to the yang channels. Purging it vacuates the interior without removing the heat. Fullness of the chest with vexation means yang heat lodges in the chest. Fright means the heart dislikes heat and the spirit is not kept. Inhibited urination means the interior is vacuous and the fluids do not move. Delirious speech is stomach heat. Heaviness of the whole body with inability to turn over means the yang qi moves internally in the interior and does not nourish the exterior. Chaihu tang is given to remove the chest fullness and vexation; longgu, muli, and qiandan are added to contain the spirit qi and settle fright; fuling is added to move fluids and free urination; dahuang is added to expel stomach heat and stop delirious speech; guizhi is added to move yang qi and relieve the heaviness of the body. [In this way] the intermixed evils are all cured.

Banxia (2 ge, washed); dazao (6 pieces); chaihu (4 liang); shengjiang (1.5 liang); renshen (1.5 liang); longgu (1.5 liang). [Part of the formula has slipped into the commentary.]

## Notes

The clause on Chaihu jia Longgu Muli tang, used after mistaken purgation for an intermixed pattern of exterior and interior, vacuity and repletion: chest fullness, vexation and fright (anxiety, being easily startled), inhibited urination, delirious speech, and heaviness of the body.

- It is explained as based on Xiao Chaihu tang, with longgu, muli, and qiandan to calm the spirit, fuling to move water, dahuang to purge heat, and guizhi to move yang qi.
- It has been widely applied in later times to anxiety, insomnia, palpitations, and the like. Since qiandan contains lead and is toxic, it is usually omitted today.
