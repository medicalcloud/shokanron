# Clause 69 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When after sweating, or purgation, the disease still has not resolved and there is vexation and agitation, Fuling Sini tang (Poria Counterflow Cold Decoction) governs it. Formula 32.

Fuling 4 liang; renshen 1 liang; fuzi 1 piece (used raw, remove the skin, broken into eight pieces); gancao 2 liang (mix-fried); ganjiang 1.5 liang.

Of the five ingredients above, boil with 5 sheng of water down to 3 sheng, remove the dregs, and take 7 ge warm, twice a day.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Sweating or purgation should resolve the disease. If it still does not resolve, sweating has vacuated the yang qi outside and purgation has vacuated the yin qi inside; yin and yang are both vacuous and only the evil is unresolved, so vexation and agitation arise. Fuling Sini tang is given to restore the qi of yin and yang.

(Nature and flavor) Fuling: sweet, neutral. Renshen: sweet, warm. Gancao: sweet, neutral. Ganjiang: acrid, hot. Fuzi: acrid, hot.

Sini tang supplements the yang; fuling and renshen are added to boost the yin.

## Notes

The clause on Fuling Sini tang, used for vexation and agitation when, after mistaken sweating and purgation, both yin and yang are vacuous. It is Sini tang plus fuling and renshen, and is often explained as being used when the vacuity is deeper than in the Ganjiang Fuzi tang pattern of Clause 61 (daytime vexation and agitation), with the yin also damaged.
