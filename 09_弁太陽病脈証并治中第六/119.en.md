# Clause 119 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When warm needling is applied to taiyang cold damage, there will certainly be fright.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Cold damages the nutritive. When red-hot needling is applied to one whose nutritive qi is faint, the blood stagnates and does not move. Fright is because warm needling has damaged the nutritive blood and stirred the heart qi. The *Jingui yaolüe* says: "Scant blood and qi belong to the heart."

## Notes

A short clause warning that using warm (red-hot) needling for taiyang cold damage damages the nutritive blood and disturbs the heart qi, causing easy fright. One of the fire-counterflow clauses.
