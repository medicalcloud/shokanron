# Clause 119 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When warm needling is applied to Taiyang cold damage, there will certainly be fright.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Cold damages the ying-nutrient. When red-hot needling is applied to one whose ying-nutrient qi is feeble, the blood stagnates and does not move. Fright is because warm needling has damaged the ying-nutrient blood and stirred the heart qi. The *Jingui yaolüe* says: "Scant blood and qi belong to the heart."

## Notes

A short clause warning that using warm (red-hot) needling for Taiyang cold damage damages the ying-nutrient blood and disturbs the heart qi, causing easy fright. One of the fire-counterflow clauses.
