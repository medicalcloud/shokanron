# Clause 54 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When a patient has no other disease of the viscera but has periodic fever and spontaneous sweating that does not get better, this is disharmony of the defensive qi. Inducing sweating before the time [of the attack] will bring recovery; Guizhi tang is appropriate. 20. Use the twelfth formula above.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

No other disease of the viscera means the interior is in harmony. Disharmony of the defensive qi is a disease of the exterior. The *Waitai* says: "When the interior is in harmony and the exterior diseased, sweating it brings recovery." "Before the time" means that inducing sweating before the time of fever and sweating brings recovery.

## Notes

This clause has Guizhi tang taken before the attacks by someone without visceral disease who has recurring periodic fever and spontaneous sweating. Like Clause 53, it uses Guizhi tang for spontaneous sweating due to disharmony of the defensive qi; its distinctive point is the timing of administration (before the attack).
