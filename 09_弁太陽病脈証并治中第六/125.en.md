# Clause 125 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease with a yellow body, a deep and bound pulse, hardness of the lower abdomen, and inhibited urination: there is no blood [pattern]. When urination is spontaneously free and the person is as if manic, the blood pattern is certain; Didang tang governs it. 65. Use the formula above.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

A yellow body, a deep and bound pulse, hardness of the lower abdomen, and inhibited urination mean yellowing from stomach heat; Yinchen tang may be given. A yellow body, a deep and bound pulse, hardness of the lower abdomen, spontaneously free urination, and being as if manic mean not static heat in the stomach but heat bound in the lower burner forming blood amassment; Didang tang is given to purge the amassed blood.

## Notes

When there are a yellow body and hardness of the lower abdomen, this clause distinguishes jaundice from damp-heat (the Yinchenhao tang pattern) from blood amassment by the urine: inhibited urination means damp-heat; free urination with mental symptoms means blood amassment.
