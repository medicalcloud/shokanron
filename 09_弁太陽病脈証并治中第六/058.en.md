# Clause 58 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In any disease, whether sweating has been induced, or vomiting, or purgation, or there has been loss of blood or loss of fluids, if yin and yang spontaneously harmonize, [the patient] will certainly recover spontaneously.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

When fluids have been repeatedly lost, sweat cannot be produced; one must wait for yin and yang to harmonize spontaneously, and then [the patient] recovers spontaneously.

## Notes

A clause stating the fundamental principle of cure: even if the upright qi and fluids have been damaged by sweating, vomiting, purgation, or bleeding, the disease will be cured if yin and yang harmonize spontaneously. It is valued as showing the importance of not piling on medicines and of waiting for the body's natural power of recovery.
