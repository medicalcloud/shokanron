# Clause 118 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When fire counterflow has been purged, and then red-hot needling causes restlessness, Guizhi Gancao Longgu Muli tang (Keishikanzoryukotsuboreito; Cinnamon Twig, Licorice, Dragon Bone, and Oyster Shell Decoction) governs it. Formula 62.

Guizhi (keishi) 1 liang (remove the bark); gancao (kanzo) 2 liang (mix-fried); muli (borei) 2 liang (dry-fried); longgu (ryukotsu) 2 liang.

Of the four ingredients above, boil with 5 sheng of water down to 2.5 sheng, remove the dregs, and take 8 ge warm, three times a day.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

First there was counterflow from fire [therapy], then purgation was used to remove it, so the interior qi became deficient; then red-hot needling was added, so the deficient interior is vexed by fire-heat, producing restlessness. Guizhi Gancao Longgu Muli tang is given to disperse the pathogenic fire.

Acrid and sweet disperse: the acrid-sweetness of guizhi and gancao effuses and disperses the pathogenic fire in the meridians. Astringent can remove desertion: the astringency of longgu and muli contains the healthy qi that has floated off.

## Notes

The clause on Guizhi Gancao Longgu Muli tang, used for restlessness when repeated mistreatment—fire therapy, purgation, red-hot needling—has damaged the heart yang. It is regarded as Guizhi Gancao tang (Keishikanzoto) (Clause 64) to supplement the heart yang, with longgu and muli to calm the spirit. It is often explained as being for a lighter pattern than Jiuni tang (Kyugyakuto) in Clause 112.
