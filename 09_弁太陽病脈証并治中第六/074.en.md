# Clause 74 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Wind attack with fever unresolved after six or seven days, with vexation, having both exterior and interior patterns, thirst with a desire to drink water, and vomiting as soon as water enters: this is called water counterflow; Wuling san (Goreisan) governs it. Formula 37. Use the thirty-fourth formula above.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Fever in wind attack should resolve by the sixth or seventh day. If it does not resolve and there is vexation, the pathogen is in the exterior. Thirst with a desire to drink water means the pathogen has been transmitted inward. If the interior heat is severe, it can disperse water, and water does not cause vomiting once it enters. If the interior heat is slight, it cannot disperse water; the water collects, accumulates, and does not disperse, so drinking leads to vomiting of water. Because the vomiting is caused by water, it is called water counterflow. Wuling san is given to harmonize exterior and interior and disperse the collected retained fluid.

## Notes

This clause uses Wuling san for "water counterflow," in which there is thirst and water is drunk but vomited as soon as it is drunk. Because water has collected in the body, newly entering water is not accepted and is vomited. Water counterflow is known as a representative indication for Wuling san.
