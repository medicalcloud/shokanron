# Clause 57 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In cold damage, after sweating has been induced and [the disease] has resolved, when about half a day later there is vexation again and the pulse is floating and rapid, sweating may be induced again; Guizhi tang is appropriate. 23. Use the twelfth formula above.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Vexation is heat. That the body became cool after sweating means it had resolved. When about half a day later the body becomes hot again and the pulse is floating and rapid, the evil has not been exhausted. Sweating may be induced again; give Guizhi tang.

## Notes

When, after sweating with Mahuang tang or the like had once resolved the disease, fever returns with a floating, rapid pulse, exterior evil is taken to remain, and sweating is induced again, this time with the gentle Guizhi tang. Since sweat has already been induced once, the key point is to use Guizhi tang rather than the strong Mahuang tang.
