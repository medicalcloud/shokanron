# Clause 78 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cold damage of five or six days: when, after heavy purgation, the generalized heat does not go and there is bound pain in the heart, [the disease] is not yet about to resolve; Zhizi Chi tang governs it. 40. Use the first formula above.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Five or six days of cold damage is the time when the evil qi is in the interior. If after heavy purgation the generalized heat goes and the heart and chest are empty, it is about to resolve. If after heavy purgation the generalized heat goes but there is bound pain in the heart, it is chest bind. If the generalized heat does not go and there is bound pain in the heart, it is vacuity vexation. Chest bind is heat bound in the chest forming repletion; the heat qi has already contracted inward, so the external generalized heat goes. In vacuity vexation, heat lodges in the chest but does not bind into repletion; it is diffuse and causes vexation, so the generalized heat does not go. The sixth or seventh day is the time for resolution, but since the heat has become vacuity vexation, it says "not yet about to resolve." Zhizi Chi tang is given to remove it by vomiting.

## Notes

As a Zhizi Chi tang pattern it gives bound pain in the heart (chest). Cheng Wuji distinguishes chest pain after purgation with persistent generalized heat as vacuity vexation (Zhizi Chi tang), and chest pain with the generalized heat gone as chest bind (Da Xianxiong tang, etc.).
