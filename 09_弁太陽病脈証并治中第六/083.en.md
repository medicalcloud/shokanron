# Clause 83 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

One whose throat is dry must not be made to sweat.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The fluids are insufficient.

## Notes

From here to Clause 89 are listed cases in which sweating must be prohibited (the clauses on contraindications of the sweating method). A dry throat is a sign of insufficient fluids or yin, and sweating would further damage the fluids, so it is prohibited.
