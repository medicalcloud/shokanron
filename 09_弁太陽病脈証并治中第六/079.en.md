# Clause 79 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When after purgation in cold damage there are vexation of the heart, abdominal fullness, and restlessness whether lying or rising, Zhizi Houpo tang (Gardenia and Magnolia Bark Decoction) governs it. Formula 41.

Zhizi 14 fruits (split); houpo 4 liang (mix-fried, remove the bark); zhishi (unripe bitter orange) 4 pieces (soaked in water, mix-fried until yellow).

Of the three ingredients above, boil with 3.5 sheng of water down to 1.5 sheng, remove the dregs, divide into two doses, and give one dose warm. If vomiting occurs, stop the later dose.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

After purgation, abdominal fullness alone without vexation means the evil qi has entered the interior and become interior repletion. Vexation alone without abdominal fullness means the evil qi is in the chest and has become vacuity vexation. Vexation with fullness means the evil qi is blocked between the chest and abdomen. With fullness one cannot sit; with vexation one cannot lie down; hence restlessness whether lying or rising. Zhizi Houpo tang is given to make the vexation vomited out and discharge the fullness.

(Nature and flavor) Zhizi: bitter, cold. Houpo: bitter, warm. Zhishi: bitter, cold.

Sour and bitter cause upwelling and discharge. The bitterness of zhizi brings up the vacuity vexation; the bitterness of houpo and zhishi discharges the abdominal fullness.

## Notes

The clause on Zhizi Houpo tang, used when after purgation there are both vexation in the chest (the zhizi sign) and abdominal fullness (the houpo and zhishi sign). It is taken as heat blocking from the chest to the abdomen. Although there is abdominal fullness, it is not repletion from dry stool, so one does not purge with dahuang or the like.
