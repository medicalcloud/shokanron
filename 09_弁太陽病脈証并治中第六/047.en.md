# Clause 47 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease with a floating, tight pulse, fever, and no sweating on the body: one who has a spontaneous nosebleed recovers.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Wind and cold in the channels, not resolved by sweating, are constrained and turn into heat; with the nosebleed the heat disperses with the blood. Hence "one who has a spontaneous nosebleed recovers."

## Notes

This clause states that when there is no sweating in taiyang cold damage, a spontaneous nosebleed may let the heat out and resolve the disease. As in Clause 46, it rests on the idea that the nosebleed is a route by which the evil leaves in place of sweat ("red sweat").
