# Clause 102 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cold damage of two or three days with palpitations and vexation in the heart: Xiao Jianzhong tang governs it. 52. Use the fifty-first formula above.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Two or three days of cold damage is when the evil qi is in the exterior and should not yet be transmitted inward. Palpitations and vexation in the heart are not caused by the evil qi assailing. Palpitations are qi vacuity; vexation is blood vacuity. Since qi and blood are vacuous within, Xiao Jianzhong tang is given first to fortify the interior.

## Notes

Even in the early stage of an externally contracted disease, when someone whose qi and blood (middle burner) are originally vacuous develops palpitations and vexation, one should not attack the exterior but first supplement the interior with Xiao Jianzhong tang. It is taken as an example of supporting the upright to remove the evil.
