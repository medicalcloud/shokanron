# Clause 88 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When a person who sweats habitually (*hanjia*) is made to sweat again, there will certainly be abstraction and confusion of the heart, and pain in the genitals after urination; give Yuyuliang wan (Limonite Pill). 44. (The formula was originally missing.)

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Sweat is the fluid of the heart. When a person who sweats habitually is made to sweat again, the heart becomes vacuous, with abstraction and confusion of the heart. Robbing the sweat leaves no water, so there is pain in the genitals after urination.

## Notes

This clause states that making a person who habitually sweats a lot sweat further damages the yin and yang of the heart, causing mental instability, and also depletes the fluids, causing pain in the urethra after urination.

- The Yuyuliang wan formula is noted as "originally missing" even in the Song edition. For the formula found only in Wikisource, see the notes of this unit.
