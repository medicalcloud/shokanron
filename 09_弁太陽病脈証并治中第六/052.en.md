# Clause 52 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the pulse is floating and rapid, sweating may be induced; Mahuang tang is appropriate. 18. Use the fifth formula above.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Floating [shows that] the defensive is damaged; rapid [shows that] the nutritive is damaged. When the nutritive and defensive receive the evil, the disease is in the exterior, so it should be dispersed by sweating.

## Notes

Like Clause 51, a brief clause showing by the pulse whether sweating may be induced. A floating, rapid pulse with an exterior pattern permits sweating, but in practice it is judged together with the Mahuang tang pattern, such as absence of sweating.
