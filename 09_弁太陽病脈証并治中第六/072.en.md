# Clause 72 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When after sweating the pulse is floating and rapid and there are vexation and thirst, Wuling san governs it. 35. Use the thirty-fourth formula above.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

A floating, rapid pulse after sweating means the exterior evil has not been exhausted. Vexation and thirst mean the fluids have been lost and the stomach is dry. Wuling san is given to harmonize the exterior and moisten the dryness.

## Notes

A brief clause supplementing Clause 71. Wuling san is used when after sweating the pulse is still floating and rapid with a remaining exterior pattern and there are vexation and thirst. Inhibited urination is not mentioned, but the clause is generally read together with Clause 71.
