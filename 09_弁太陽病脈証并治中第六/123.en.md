# Clause 123 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease more than ten days past the channel [period], with nausea below the heart and a desire to vomit, pain in the chest, on the contrary sloppy stool, slight abdominal fullness, and depressed, slight vexation: if before this time there was spontaneous extreme vomiting and diarrhea, give Tiaowei Chengqi tang; if not so, it must not be given. When there are only a desire to retch, pain in the chest, and slightly sloppy stool, this is not a Chaihu tang pattern; from the retching one knows there was extreme vomiting and diarrhea. Tiaowei Cheng tang. 63. Use the thirty-third formula above.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Nausea below the heart with a desire to vomit, depressed slight vexation, and pain in the chest should be attributed to evil heat lodging in the chest. Sloppy stool on the contrary and slight abdominal fullness mean the evil heat has already descended into the stomach. Though many days have passed, if there has been no vomiting or purgation, it is merely transmitted evil and must not yet be purged; Chaihu tang should be given to remove the evil of the upper and middle burners. If there was previous vomiting and purgation that damaged the stomach qi, the stomach is vacuous and the evil has taken advantage of the vacuity to enter the stomach and become repletion, which Chaihu tang cannot remove; Tiaowei Chengqi tang purges the stomach heat. From the retching one knows that the stomach qi was previously damaged and stirred.

## Notes

Even with symptoms resembling the Chaihu tang pattern (nausea, chest pain, slight vexation), if there is a history of severe vomiting and diarrhea, it is the Tiaowei Chengqi tang pattern of stomach dryness-heat; the clause distinguishes formulas by the medical history. Its meaning is difficult and interpretations differ. The formula name "Tiaowei Cheng tang" follows the base text (the character *qi* is missing).
