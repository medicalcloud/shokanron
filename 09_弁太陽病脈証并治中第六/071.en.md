# Clause 71 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease: after sweating, when there has been profuse sweating, the stomach is dry, there is restlessness with inability to sleep, and [the patient] wants to drink water, give him a little to drink at a time; when the stomach qi is harmonized he will recover. If the pulse is floating, urination is inhibited, there is slight fever, and wasting and thirst, Wuling san (Goreisan; Five-Ingredient Poria Powder) governs it. Formula 34. (This is the Zhuling san [Choreisan].)

Zhuling (chorei; polyporus) 18 zhu (remove the skin); zexie (takusha; alisma) 1 liang 6 zhu; baizhu (byakujutsu) 18 zhu; fuling (bukuryo) 18 zhu; guizhi (keishi) half a liang (remove the bark).

Pound the five ingredients above into powder and take a square-inch spoonful mixed with white drink (rice water), three times a day. Drink plenty of warm water; when sweat comes out [the patient] recovers. Care according to the method.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

When sweating has resolved [the disease] but the stomach is dry, with restlessness and inability to sleep, and [the patient] wants to drink water, give him a little at a time; when the stomach qi is moistened he recovers. If the pulse is floating, the exterior has not yet resolved. Drinking much water with scant urine is called wasting and thirst, meaning the interior heat is severe and excess. Slight fever with wasting and thirst means the heat has not yet become excess and the upper jiao is dry. Wuling san is given to engender fluids and harmonize exterior and interior.

(Nature and flavor) Zhuling: sweet, neutral. Zexie: sour and salty. Fuling: sweet, neutral. Gui: acrid, hot. Baizhu: sweet, neutral.

Bland is "one." When "one" enters the mouth it becomes sweet; when sweetness is extreme it becomes bland. Sweet moderates and bland percolates. The sweetness of the three ingredients zhuling, baizhu, and fuling moistens deficient dryness and frees the fluids. Salty flavor drains downward and is yin; the saltiness of zexie drains hidden water. Acrid and sweet disperse and are yang; the acrid-sweetness of guizhi harmonizes the flesh and exterior.

## Notes

This clause divides thirst after sweating into two kinds.

- If the thirst is simply due to the stomach fluids being depleted by sweating, giving water a little at a time will cure it.
- If there are a floating pulse, inhibited urination, slight fever, and wasting and thirst, the exterior pathogen remains and the bladder's qi transformation has failed so that water collects—the "water-amassment pattern"—and Wuling san moves the water and resolves the exterior. Its characteristic is thirst with inhibited urination.
- Wuling san has been widely applied in later times as the representative formula for thirst, inhibited urination, vomiting, and the like due to collected water.
