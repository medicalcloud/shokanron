# Clause 121 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When taiyang disease has been made to vomit: taiyang disease should have aversion to cold, but now on the contrary there is no aversion to cold and no wish to have clothes near; this is internal vexation from induced vomiting.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

In a taiyang exterior disease, the physician instead induced vomiting, damaging the stomach qi, and evil heat took advantage of the vacuity to enter the stomach. Because the stomach is internally vexed by evil heat, there is no aversion to cold and no wish to have clothes near.

## Notes

This describes the case in which mistakenly inducing vomiting in taiyang disease damages the stomach fluids so that internal heat arises (internal vexation); the aversion to cold disappears and clothes are disliked.
