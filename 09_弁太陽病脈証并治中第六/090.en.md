# Clause 90 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When sweating should originally be induced but purgation is applied instead, it is a mistake; if sweating is induced first, the treatment is not a mistake. When purgation should originally come first but sweating is induced instead, it is a mistake; if purgation is applied first, the treatment is not a mistake.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

When the disease is in the exterior, sweating is appropriate and purgation is a mistake. When the disease is in the interior, purgation is appropriate and sweating is a mistake. The Classic says: "When yang is exuberant and yin vacuous, sweating kills and purgation cures. When yang is vacuous and yin exuberant, sweating cures and purgation kills."

## Notes

A clause stating the principle of the order of sweating and purgation. If the exterior pattern predominates, sweating comes first; if the interior pattern predominates, purgation comes first. Getting the order wrong is called a "mistake." The general rule is to treat the exterior first, but when the interior is urgent the interior may be treated first (Clause 91).
