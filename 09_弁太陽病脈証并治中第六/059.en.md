# Clause 59 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When, after heavy purgation, sweating is again induced and urination is inhibited, it is because of loss of fluids. Do not treat it; when urination becomes free, [the patient] will certainly recover spontaneously.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

When urination is inhibited because of loss of fluids, one must not free it with medicines. Wait for the fluids to become sufficient; urination will become free, and [the patient] will certainly recover spontaneously.

## Notes

When fluids have been lost through repeated purgation and sweating and urination is inhibited, one must not force urination with diuretics but wait for the fluids to recover. It is taken as a concrete example of the principle of Clause 58. Distinguishing it from inhibited urination due to collected water (such as the Wuling san pattern) is regarded as important.
