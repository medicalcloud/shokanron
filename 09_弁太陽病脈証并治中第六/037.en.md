# Clause 37 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease, after ten days or more: when the pulse is floating and fine and [the patient] likes to lie down, the exterior has already resolved. If there are fullness in the chest and pain in the rib-side, give Xiao Chaihu tang (Minor Bupleurum Decoction). If the pulse is only floating, give Mahuang tang. 7. Use the fifth formula above.

Xiao Chaihu tang
Chaihu (bupleurum) half a jin; huangqin, renshen, gancao (mix-fried), and shengjiang (sliced), 3 liang each; dazao 12 pieces (split); banxia half a sheng (washed).

Of the seven ingredients above, boil with 1 dou 2 sheng of water down to 6 sheng, remove the dregs, decoct again down to 3 sheng, and take 1 sheng warm, three times a day.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

After ten days or more is the time when [the disease] tends to resolve. A floating, fine pulse with a liking to lie down means the exterior evil has ended. The disease is already harmonized, so it is resolved. If the pulse is only floating and not fine, the evil qi is still in the exterior alone, and Mahuang tang is given to effuse and disperse it.

## Notes

This clause states three possibilities for taiyang disease after ten days or more.

- A floating, fine pulse and a liking to lie down: the exterior pattern has resolved and the upright qi is recovering; no treatment is needed.
- Fullness in the chest and rib-side pain: the evil has entered the shaoyang, so Xiao Chaihu tang is used. The Xiao Chaihu tang formula appears here for the first time.
- A pulse that is only floating: still an exterior pattern, so Mahuang tang.
- Xiao Chaihu tang has a distinctive method: remove the dregs and decoct again to concentrate.
