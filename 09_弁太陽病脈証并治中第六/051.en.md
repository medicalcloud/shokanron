# Clause 51 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the pulse is floating, the disease is in the exterior and sweating may be induced; Mahuang tang is appropriate. 17. Use the fifth formula above. (One method uses Guizhi tang.)

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

A floating pulse is one obtained with light finger pressure; it is used to observe the qi of the skin. The *Neijing* says: "What is in the skin is effused by sweating."

## Notes

A brief statement of the principle that a floating pulse shows the disease is in the exterior and can be resolved by sweating. Prescribing Mahuang tang on a floating pulse alone is an abbreviated way of writing; in practice it should be judged together with other signs (such as no sweating). The original note "one method uses Guizhi tang" is understood in this light.
