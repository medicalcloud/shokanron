# Clause 73 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In cold damage, when there are sweating and thirst, Wuling san governs it; when there is no thirst, Fuling Gancao tang (Poria and Licorice Decoction) governs it. Formula 36.

Fuling 2 liang; guizhi 2 liang (remove the bark); gancao 1 liang (mix-fried); shengjiang 3 liang (sliced).

Of the four ingredients above, boil with 4 sheng of water down to 2 sheng, remove the dregs, and take warm in three doses.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

In cold damage, sweating with thirst means the fluids have been lost, the stomach is dry, and the evil qi is gradually being transmitted inward; Wuling san harmonizes exterior and interior. If there is sweating without thirst, the evil qi has not been transmitted inward but is only in the exterior, and the exterior is vacuous; Fuling Gancao tang is given to harmonize the exterior and unite the defensive.

(Nature and flavor) Fuling: sweet, neutral. Guizhi: acrid, hot. Shengjiang: acrid, warm. Gancao: sweet, neutral.

The sweetness of fuling and gancao boosts the fluids and harmonizes the defensive; the acridity of guizhi and shengjiang assists the yang qi and resolves the exterior.

## Notes

This clause divides water collection with sweating into Wuling san and Fuling Gancao tang by the presence or absence of thirst.

- Wuling san: water has collected in the lower burner (bladder), fluids do not rise, and there is thirst.
- Fuling Gancao tang: water has collected in the middle burner (stomach), and there is no thirst. In Clause 356 it is used for cold hands and feet with palpitations below the heart.
