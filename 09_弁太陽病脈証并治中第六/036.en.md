# Clause 36 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In combined disease of Taiyang and Yangming, when there are dyspnea and fullness in the chest, one must not purge; Mahuang tang is appropriate. 6. Use the fifth formula above.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The yang receives qi in the chest. Dyspnea with fullness in the chest means that the yang qi, which should not be effused, is blocked and counterflows. Fullness below the heart and abdominal fullness are both excess and should be purged. Here it is fullness in the chest, not interior excess, so one must not purge. Although Yangming is involved, since it is a combined disease with Taiyang, it belongs to the exterior, and Mahuang tang is given to induce sweating.

## Notes

Even in a combined disease involving Yangming, if dyspnea and chest fullness are the main signs, the pathogen is still in the exterior and chest, so one does not purge but induces sweating with Mahuang tang. Distinguishing chest fullness from abdominal fullness (Yangming interior excess) is taken as the key point.
