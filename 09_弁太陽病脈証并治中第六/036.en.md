# Clause 36 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In combined disease of taiyang and yangming, when there are panting and fullness in the chest, one must not purge; Mahuang tang is appropriate. 6. Use the fifth formula above.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The yang receives qi in the chest. Panting with fullness in the chest means that the yang qi, which should not be effused, is blocked and counterflows. Fullness below the heart and abdominal fullness are both repletion and should be purged. Here it is fullness in the chest, not interior repletion, so one must not purge. Although yangming is involved, since it is a combined disease with taiyang, it belongs to the exterior, and Mahuang tang is given to induce sweating.

## Notes

Even in a combined disease involving yangming, if panting and chest fullness are the main signs, the evil is still in the exterior and chest, so one does not purge but induces sweating with Mahuang tang. Distinguishing chest fullness from abdominal fullness (yangming interior repletion) is taken as the key point.
