# Clause 42 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease with the external pattern not yet resolved and a floating, weak pulse should be resolved by sweating; Guizhi tang is appropriate. Formula 12.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

A floating, weak pulse means weak nutritive and strong defensive.

## Notes

If the exterior pattern remains and the pulse is floating and weak, it is resolved with Guizhi tang, whose sweating power is weak. The clause is taken to show the choice by pulse: Mahuang tang for a floating, tight pulse, Guizhi tang for a floating, weak one.
