# Clause 97 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the blood is weak and the qi exhausted, the interstices open; the evil qi thereupon enters, contends with the upright qi, and binds below the rib-side. The upright and the evil contend apart, so chills and fever alternate, ceasing and recurring at fixed times, with taciturnity and no desire for food or drink. The viscera and bowels are connected with each other, and the pain must be lower; the evil is high and the pain is low, so it causes retching (one version says, "the viscera and bowels are at odds; the disease must be lower, with pain in the rib-side and diaphragm"). Xiao Chaihu tang governs it. If after taking Chaihu tang there is thirst, it belongs to the yangming and is treated by its method. 49. Use the formula above.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

A person's qi and blood wax and wane with the times. When the moon's disk is empty, the blood is weak and the qi exhausted, and the interstices are open and loose. When the evil qi takes advantage of the vacuity to harm a person, [it enters] deep. The *Zhenjing* says: "When the moon's disk is empty, the sea water swells in the east, a person's blood and qi are vacuous, the defensive qi departs and the body is left alone, the flesh diminishes, the skin slackens, the interstices open, the hair thins, the skin texture is thin, and dirt falls off. If at this time one meets a robber wind, it enters deep"—this is it. Because of vacuity of the upright, the evil goes from exterior to interior, binds below the rib-side, and contends separately with the upright, producing alternating chills and fever and taciturnity without desire for food or drink. "Lower" means from outside to inside. The channels and network vessels are connected with the viscera and bowels, and the qi, following the channels, must be transmitted inward; hence "the pain must be lower." (*Tong*, pain, is written *bing*, disease, in one version.) The evil being in the upper burner is "the evil is high"; the evil gradually being transmitted inward is "the pain is low." The interior qi contends with the evil qi and counterflows upward, so it causes retching. Xiao Chaihu tang is given to resolve the half-exterior half-interior evil.

When after taking Xiao Chaihu tang the exterior evil has ended but there is thirst, the interior evil has been transmitted to the yangming; treat it [by the method] of yangming.

## Notes

A clause explaining, from the standpoint of pathology, how the Xiao Chaihu tang pattern arises.

- It explains that when qi and blood are weak and the interstices open, the evil enters and contends with the upright qi below the rib-side, producing alternating chills and fever and the like.
- "The evil is high and the pain is low" is variously interpreted, e.g., the evil is in the gallbladder (above) and the pain extends to the abdomen (below).
- If thirst appears after taking Xiao Chaihu tang, the evil has moved to the yangming, so it is treated by the method of yangming disease.
