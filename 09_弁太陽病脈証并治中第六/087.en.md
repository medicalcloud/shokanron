# Clause 87 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

A person who has lost blood (*wangxuejia*) must not be made to sweat; if made to sweat, there will be shivering and trembling with cold.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The *Zhenjing* says: "One robbed of blood has no sweat; one robbed of sweat has no blood." Inducing sweating when blood has been lost makes both yin and yang vacuous; hence shivering with cold and trembling.

## Notes

Sweating a person who has lost blood through bleeding depletes the yang qi along with the blood, causing shivering with cold; hence it is prohibited. "One robbed of blood has no sweat; one robbed of sweat has no blood" is often cited, along with Clause 50, as expressing the idea that sweat and blood come from the same source.
