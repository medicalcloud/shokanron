# Clause 99 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cold damage of four or five days with generalized heat, aversion to wind, stiffness of the neck and nape, fullness below the rib-side, and warm hands and feet with thirst: Xiao Chaihu tang governs it. 50. Use the formula above.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Generalized heat, aversion to wind, and stiffness of the neck and nape mean the exterior has not resolved. Fullness below the rib-side with thirst means the interior is not in harmony. When the evil is in the exterior, the hands and feet are hot throughout; when in the interior, the hands and feet are reversal-cold. Here the hands and feet are warm, so one knows the evil is between exterior and interior. Xiao Chaihu tang is given to resolve the evil of exterior and interior.

## Notes

When a taiyang exterior pattern (generalized heat, aversion to wind, stiff neck and nape), a shaoyang sign (fullness below the rib-side), and mild interior heat (thirst) coexist, Xiao Chaihu tang harmonizes and resolves exterior and interior together. It is taken as an example of the idea that when signs of the three yang coexist, shaoyang harmonization is primary.
