# Clause 60 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When, after purgation, sweating is again induced, there will certainly be shivering with cold and a faint, fine pulse. The reason is that both interior and exterior are vacuous.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Sweating vacuates the exterior and causes loss of yang; purgation vacuates the interior and causes loss of blood. Shivering with cold means the yang qi is faint; a faint, fine pulse means the yin blood is weak.

## Notes

This describes the state of vacuity of both exterior and interior, yin and yang, after repeated purgation and sweating. Shivering cold is taken as yang vacuity and the faint, fine pulse as vacuity of yin blood; the clause is read as a warning against mistreatment.
