# Clause 60 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When, after purgation, sweating is again induced, there will certainly be shivering with cold and a feeble, thready pulse. The reason is that both interior and exterior are deficient.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Sweating vacuates the exterior and causes loss of yang; purgation vacuates the interior and causes loss of blood. Shivering with cold means the yang qi is feeble; a feeble, thready pulse means the yin blood is weak.

## Notes

This describes the state of deficiency of both exterior and interior, yin and yang, after repeated purgation and sweating. Shivering cold is taken as yang deficiency and the feeble, thready pulse as deficiency of yin blood; the clause is read as a warning against mistreatment.
