# Clause 55 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In cold damage with a floating, tight pulse, when sweating was not induced and a nosebleed resulted, Mahuang tang governs it. 21. Use the fifth formula above.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

In cold damage a floating, tight pulse means the evil is in the exterior, and Mahuang tang should be given to induce sweating. If sweating is not induced, the evil has no exit, is severely blocked in the channels, and presses the blood to move frenetically, resulting in nosebleed.

## Notes

When taiyang cold damage that should have been sweated was not, and a nosebleed resulted while the exterior pattern still remains, sweating is induced with Mahuang tang. Unlike Clause 47 (spontaneous resolution with nosebleed), this is taken as the case in which the nosebleed does not resolve the disease. Later authors have expressed caution about using Mahuang tang for patients with nosebleed.
