# Clause 100 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In cold damage, when the yang pulse is hesitant and the yin pulse wiry, there should, by rule, be tense pain in the abdomen; first give Xiao Jianzhong tang (Minor Center-Fortifying Decoction). If it does not get better, Xiao Chaihu tang governs it. 51. Use the formula above.

Xiao Jianzhong tang
Guizhi 3 liang (remove the bark); gancao 2 liang (mix-fried); dazao 12 pieces (split); shaoyao 6 liang; shengjiang 3 liang (sliced); jiaoyi (malt sugar) 1 sheng.

Of the six ingredients above, boil with 7 sheng of water down to 3 sheng, remove the dregs, add the malt sugar, put it again over a gentle fire to dissolve it, and take 1 sheng warm, three times a day. For a person prone to retching, Jianzhong tang must not be used, because it is sweet.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

When the yang pulse is hesitant and the yin pulse wiry with tense pain in the abdomen, it should be treated as interior deficiency cold; Xiao Jianzhong tang is given to warm the middle and disperse the cold. If it does not get better, it is not interior cold; it must be caused by the pathogenic qi going from exterior to interior so that the interior qi is not flowing. Xiao Chaihu tang with huangqin removed and shaoyao added is given to remove the pathogen transmitted inward.

(Nature and flavor) Guizhi: acrid, hot. Gancao: sweet, neutral. Dazao: sweet, warm. Shaoyao: sour, slightly cold. Shengjiang: acrid, warm. Jiaoyi: sweet, warm.

"Fortifying the center" (*jianzhong*) means fortifying the spleen. The *Neijing* says: "The spleen desires moderation; quickly eat sweet to moderate it." The sweetness of jiaoyi, dazao, and gancao moderates the middle. Acrid moistens and disperses; when the ying-nutrient and wei-defense are insufficient, moisten and disperse them—the acridity of guizhi and shengjiang moves the ying-nutrient and wei-defense. Sour contracts and drains; when the healthy qi is weak, contract it and move it—the sourness of shaoyao contracts the healthy qi.

## Notes

A clause showing the treatment order for abdominal pain—first supplement the deficiency of the middle jiao with Xiao Jianzhong tang, then use Xiao Chaihu tang if it does not get better ("supplement first, then harmonize").

- Xiao Jianzhong tang is Guizhi tang with the shaoyao doubled and malt sugar added; it has been widely applied in later times as the representative formula for abdominal pain due to spleen-stomach deficiency (deficiency/consumption abdominal pain).
- Cheng Wuji holds that the Xiao Chaihu tang used when Xiao Jianzhong tang fails follows the modification for abdominal pain (remove huangqin, add shaoyao).
- Because it is sweet, it is not used for people prone to vomiting.
