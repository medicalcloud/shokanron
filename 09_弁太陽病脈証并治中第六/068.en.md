# Clause 68 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When after sweating the disease has not resolved and, on the contrary, there is aversion to cold, it is because of vacuity; Shaoyao Gancao Fuzi tang (Peony, Licorice, and Aconite Decoction) governs it. Formula 31.

Shaoyao and gancao (mix-fried), 3 liang each; fuzi 1 piece (blast-fried, remove the skin, broken into eight pieces).

Of the three ingredients above, boil with 5 sheng of water down to 1 sheng 5 ge, remove the dregs, and take warm in three doses. Doubtfully a formula of Zhongjing.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

When sweating resolves the disease, there is no aversion to cold. Even when sweating does not resolve it, one with exterior repletion also has no aversion to cold. Here sweating has not resolved the disease, and on the contrary there is aversion to cold: both nutritive and defensive are vacuous. Sweating vacuates the nutritive; aversion to cold means the defensive is vacuous. Shaoyao Gancao Fuzi tang is given to supplement the nutritive and defensive.

(Nature and flavor) Shaoyao: sour, slightly cold. Gancao: sweet, neutral. Fuzi: acrid, hot.

The sourness of shaoyao astringes the fluids and boosts the nutritive; the acridity of fuzi warms and secures the yang qi and supplements the defensive; the sweetness of gancao harmonizes the acrid and sour and calms the upright qi.

## Notes

When after sweating the disease does not resolve and aversion to cold appears instead, it is attributed not to exterior evil but to vacuity of yang qi, and Shaoyao Gancao Fuzi tang is used.

- It is Shaoyao Gancao tang (supplementing yin blood) plus fuzi (warming yang), regarded as a formula supplementing both yin and yang.
- "Doubtfully a formula of Zhongjing" at the end is taken as a note by later editors (such as Lin Yi).
