# Clause 33 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In combined disease of taiyang and yangming, when there is no diarrhea but only vomiting, Gegen jia Banxia tang (Pueraria Decoction plus Pinellia) governs it. Formula 3.

Gegen 4 liang; mahuang 3 liang (remove the nodes); gancao 2 liang (mix-fried); shaoyao 2 liang; guizhi 2 liang (remove the bark); shengjiang 2 liang (sliced); banxia (pinellia) half a sheng (washed); dazao 12 pieces (split).

Of the eight ingredients above, with 1 dou of water first boil the gegen and mahuang, reducing by 2 sheng; remove the white froth, add the other ingredients, boil down to 3 sheng, remove the dregs, and take 1 sheng warm. Cover to obtain a slight sweat.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The evil qi is severe on the outside, the yang does not govern the interior, and the interior qi is not in harmony. When the qi descends and does not rise, there is only diarrhea and no vomiting. When the interior qi counterflows upward and does not descend, there is only vomiting and no diarrhea. Gegen tang is given to disperse the evil, and banxia is added to lower the counterflowing qi.

## Notes

The same taiyang-yangming combined disease as Clause 32, but with vomiting instead of diarrhea; banxia is added to Gegen tang. Banxia is regarded as a representative medicine for stopping vomiting.
