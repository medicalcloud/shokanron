# Clause 89 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When a patient has cold and is again made to sweat, the stomach becomes cold, and he will certainly vomit roundworms (one version writes "counterflow").

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

When a patient has cold, it should be warmed and dispersed. If sweating is induced instead, the yang qi is damaged, the stomach becomes cold, and he will certainly vomit roundworms.

## Notes

Sweating a person with internal cold (yang vacuity) further damages the yang qi, chills the stomach, and causes vomiting (roundworms in the body are said to rise to avoid the cold). One of the contraindications of sweating.
