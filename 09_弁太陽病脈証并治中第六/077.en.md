# Clause 77 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When after sweating, or purgation, there are vexation and heat with stuffiness in the chest, Zhizi Chi tang governs it. 39. Use the first formula above.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The yang receives qi in the chest. When sweating or purgation makes the yang qi insufficient, evil heat lodges in the chest, binds, and does not disperse, so there are vexation and heat with blockage in the chest. Zhizi Chi tang is given to make [the patient] vomit the evil in the chest.

## Notes

Following Clause 76, it adds "stuffiness in the chest" as a sign of the Zhizi Chi tang pattern, taken as heat trapped in the chest obstructing the flow of qi.
