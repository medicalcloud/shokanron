# Clause 41 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cold damage with water qi below the heart, cough with slight panting, fever without thirst: if thirst appears after taking the decoction, this is the cold departing and [the disease] is about to resolve. Xiao Qinglong tang governs it. 11. Use the tenth formula above.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Cough with slight panting is water and cold shooting into the lung. Fever without thirst means the exterior pattern has not yet ended. Xiao Qinglong tang is given to effuse the exterior and disperse the water. Thirst after taking the decoction means the interior qi is warmed and the water qi dispersed; it is about to resolve.

## Notes

This clause describes the Xiao Qinglong tang pattern and the course after taking it.

- Thirst after taking the medicine is taken as a sign of recovery: the water-rheum has been removed and the warming medicine has warmed the interior.
- "Xiao Qinglong tang governs it" comes at the end, but it is generally read as following "fever without thirst" (an inverted construction).
