# Clause 120 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease should have aversion to cold and fever; when now there is spontaneous sweating, and on the contrary no aversion to cold or fever, and the pulse at the guan position is thready and rapid, it is due to the physician's error of inducing vomiting. One who was made to vomit on the first or second day feels hunger in the abdomen but cannot eat. One who was made to vomit on the third or fourth day does not like gruel, wants to eat cold food, and vomits in the evening what he ate in the morning. This is caused by the physician inducing vomiting; it is a minor counterflow.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Aversion to cold with fever is Taiyang exterior disease. Spontaneous sweating without aversion to cold or fever is a Yangming pattern. It was originally a Taiyang exterior disease, but the physician instead induced vomiting, damaging and stirring the stomach qi, and the exterior pathogen took advantage of the deficiency to be transmitted to the Yangming. From the thready, rapid guan pulse one knows it was caused by the physician's induced vomiting. On the first or second day of the disease the exterior pathogen is still cold and has not yet become heat; inducing vomiting transmits the exterior cold into the stomach, which becomes deficient and cold, so there is hunger in the abdomen but inability to eat. On the third or fourth day the exterior pathogen has already been transmitted and become heat; inducing vomiting lets the exterior heat take advantage of the deficiency to enter the stomach, which becomes deficient and hot, so there is dislike of gruel, desire for cold food, and vomiting in the evening of what was eaten in the morning. This is because the morning food enters the stomach, but the deficient stomach cannot digest it; in the evening, when the stomach qi moves in the interior and contends with the pathogenic qi, the stomach qi counterflows. Because the stomach qi is still present, it is called only a "minor counterflow."

## Notes

From here come clauses on deteriorations from misuse of the vomiting method. Mistakenly inducing vomiting in Taiyang disease damages the stomach qi, causing stomach deficiency cold or deficiency heat depending on the timing. Since the stomach qi is still preserved, it is called a "minor counterflow" (a mild mistreatment).
