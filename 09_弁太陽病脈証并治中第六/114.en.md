# Clause 114 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease: when it is fumigated with fire but no sweat is obtained, the person will certainly become agitated; if it does not resolve when the channel [period] arrives, there will certainly be blood in the stool. This is called fire evil.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

This is fire evil pressing the blood so that the blood moves downward. When taiyang disease is fumigated with fire and no sweat is obtained, the heat has no exit; the yin is vacuous and receives fire, so there will certainly be agitation. In six days transmission through the channels is complete; on the seventh day it reaches the taiyang channel again, and the heat qi should resolve. If it does not resolve, the heat qi presses the blood to move downward, and there will certainly be *qingxue*. *Qing* means the privy.

## Notes

When fumigation with fire fails to produce sweat and the heat presses inward causing blood in the stool, it is called "fire evil." *Qing* is taken as interchangeable with *qing* 圊 (privy), and *qingxue* is generally understood as blood in the stool (as Cheng Wuji also says).
