# Clause 115 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the pulse is floating and the heat severe, yet moxibustion is applied instead, this is repletion treated as vacuity; the fire stirs [the blood], and there will certainly be dry throat and spitting of blood.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

This is fire evil pressing the blood so that the blood moves upward. A floating pulse with severe heat is exterior repletion. When the physician takes the floating pulse as vacuity and applies moxibustion with fire, the fire qi stirs the blood, pressing it upward; hence dry throat and spitting of blood.

## Notes

This describes the case in which moxibustion is mistakenly applied to exterior replete heat, and the heat presses the blood to bleed upward (hematemesis from fire evil). It pairs with the downward bleeding (blood in the stool) of the previous clause (114). It warns against mistaking repletion for vacuity.
