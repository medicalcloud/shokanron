# Clause 93 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease: when first purged without recovery, and then sweating was induced, so that exterior and interior are both vacuous, the person consequently develops veiling (*mao*, a clouded, stuffy head). A person with veiling recovers spontaneously when sweat comes out. The reason is that sweating harmonizes the exterior. If the interior is not yet harmonized, purge again afterward.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Veiling is constraint. Purging vacuates the interior and loses blood; sweating vacuates the exterior and loses yang. With both exterior and interior vacuous, the cold qi is obstructed and constrained, and the person develops veiling. The *Jingui yaolüe* says: "After loss of blood, sweating again with much cold causes depressed veiling." When sweat comes out, the obstructed, constrained evil is resolved, and the veiling is cured. The *Jingui yaolüe* says: "When a person with veiling is about to resolve, there must be heavy sweating." When sweating has harmonized the exterior but the interior is not yet harmonized, purge again afterward.

## Notes

When exterior and interior have both become vacuous through the wrong order of purgation and sweating, causing "veiling" (a clouded, stuffy head), the person recovers when spontaneous sweating harmonizes the exterior; if the interior is still not regulated, purgation follows afterward. It is taken as another example of the principle of treatment order (exterior first, then interior).
