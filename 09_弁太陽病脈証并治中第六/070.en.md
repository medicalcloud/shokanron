# Clause 70 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Aversion to cold after sweating is due to vacuity. No aversion to cold but only heat is repletion; the stomach qi should be harmonized, and Tiaowei Chengqi tang given. Formula 33. (The *Yuhan* says to give Xiao Chengqi tang.)

Mangxiao half a sheng; gancao 2 liang (mix-fried); dahuang 4 liang (remove the bark, wash in clear wine).

Of the three ingredients above, boil with 3 sheng of water down to 1 sheng, remove the dregs, add the mangxiao, boil twice more, and take in a single dose.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Sweating with aversion to cold is exterior vacuity. Sweating without aversion to cold but only heat is interior repletion. The Classic says: "When there is sweating without aversion to cold, the exterior has resolved but the interior is not yet in harmony." Tiaowei Chengqi tang is given to harmonize the stomach qi.

## Notes

This clause divides the course after sweating into vacuity and repletion by the presence or absence of aversion to cold. With aversion to cold it is yang vacuity (as in Clause 68); with only heat and no aversion to cold it is replete heat in the stomach, and Tiaowei Chengqi tang harmonizes the stomach qi. It is taken as the case in which sweating has lost the fluids and the stomach has become dry and replete.
