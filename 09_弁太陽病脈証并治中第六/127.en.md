# Clause 127 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease: even when urination is free, because of drinking much water there will certainly be palpitations below the heart; when urine is scant, there will certainly be suffering from interior urgency.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

When much water is drunk and urination is spontaneously free, the water is not amassed internally, but the large amount of water in the abdomen causes palpitations below the heart. The *Jingui yaolüe* says: "When one eats little and drinks much, water collects below the heart; in severe cases there are palpitations." When much water is drunk and urination is inhibited, the water is amassed internally and does not move, so there will certainly be suffering from interior urgency.

## Notes

A clause on how water collection (water-rheum) appears after drinking much water. Even with urination, excessive drinking lets water collect in the middle burner, causing palpitations below the heart; with scant urine, water collects in the lower burner, causing painful tension of the lower abdomen. It is the last clause of the middle taiyang chapter.
