# Clause 81 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Whenever Zhizi tang is used, if the patient has habitually had slightly loose stools, it must not be given.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

A patient who has habitually had slightly loose stools has interior vacuity with cold below. Even if there is vexation, it is not accumulated heat, so Zhizi tang must not be given. The *Neijing* says: "When there is first diarrhea and later another disease arises, treat the root; one must first regulate it, and only afterward treat the other disease."

## Notes

A contraindication of the Zhizi Chi tang group. Since zhizi is a bitter, cold medicine, using it for someone whose spleen and stomach are habitually cold with loose stools may further damage the spleen and stomach and cause diarrhea.
