# §34 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cold damage of five or six days, already sweated and then purged, with fullness and slight binding of the chest and rib-sides, inhibited urination, thirst without retching, sweating only on the head, alternating chills and fever, and heart vexation: it has not yet resolved; it belongs to Chaihu Guizhi Ganjiang tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 147 of the lower taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
