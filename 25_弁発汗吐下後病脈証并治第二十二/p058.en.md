# §58 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cold damage on the thirteenth day, past the meridians, with delirium: there is heat, and it should be purged with a decoction. If urination is free, the stool should be hard; when, on the contrary, there is diarrhea and the pulse is regulated and harmonious, one knows the physician purged with pills, which is not the right treatment. In spontaneous diarrhea the pulse should be feeble with cold limbs; now, on the contrary, it is harmonious—this is internal excess. It belongs to Tiaowei Chengqi tang (Choijokito).

## Notes

The same content as Clause 105 of the middle Taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
