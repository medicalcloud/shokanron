# §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease of two or three days with inability to lie down, only a desire to get up, certain binding below the heart, and a faint weak pulse: there is originally a portion of cold. If, on the contrary, it is purged and the diarrhea stops, there will certainly be chest binding; if it does not stop and it is purged again on the fourth day, heat-associated diarrhea results.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 139 of the lower taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
