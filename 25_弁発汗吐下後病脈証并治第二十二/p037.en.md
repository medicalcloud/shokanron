# §37 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cold damage after vomiting or purgation, unresolved after seven or eight days, with heat bound in the interior, heat in both exterior and interior, periodic aversion to wind, great thirst, dry tongue and vexation, and a desire to drink several sheng of water: it belongs to Baihu jia Renshen tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 168 of the lower taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
