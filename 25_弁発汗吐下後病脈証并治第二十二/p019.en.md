# §19 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease with a moderate cun pulse, floating guan pulse, and weak chi pulse: when the person has fever and sweating, again aversion to cold, no retching, and only glomus below the heart, it is because the physician purged.

## Notes

The same content as the first half of Clause 244 of the yangming chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
