# §69 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cold damage with abdominal distension and fullness after vomiting: it belongs to Tiaowei Chengqi tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 249 of the yangming chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
