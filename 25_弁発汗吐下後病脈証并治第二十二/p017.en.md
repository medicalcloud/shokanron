# §17 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yangming disease with a slow pulse, difficulty eating to fullness; when full, vexation arises, with dizziness and certainly difficult urination; this is about to become grain jaundice. Even if purged, the abdominal fullness remains as before. The reason is that the pulse is slow. [The base writes "穀疽" for "穀疸" (grain jaundice).]

## Notes

The same content as Clause 195 of the yangming chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found. The base's "穀疽" is "穀疸" in Clause 195, and "疽" is considered an error for "疸."
