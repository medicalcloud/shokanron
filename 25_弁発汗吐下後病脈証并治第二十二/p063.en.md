# §63 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Because it was originally purged, there was glomus below the heart; Xiexin tang was given but the glomus did not resolve; when the person is thirsty with a dry mouth and vexation and urination is inhibited, it belongs to Wuling san. (One version says: "Endure it for one day and it will recover.")

## Notes

The same content as Clause 156 of the lower taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
