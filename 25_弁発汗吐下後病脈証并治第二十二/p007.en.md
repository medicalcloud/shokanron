# §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When after purgation sweating is induced again, there will certainly be shaking with cold and a feeble thready pulse. The reason is that inside and outside are both deficient.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 60 of the middle Taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
