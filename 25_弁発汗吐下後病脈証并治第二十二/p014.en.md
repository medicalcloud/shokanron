# §14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the pulse is floating and tight and it is purged again, the tightness, on the contrary, enters the interior and forms stuffiness; it is soft on pressure and is only qi stuffiness.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 151 of the lower Taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
