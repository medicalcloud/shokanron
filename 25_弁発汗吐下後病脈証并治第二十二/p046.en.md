# §46 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease, after purgation, with a skipping pulse and chest fullness: it belongs to Guizhi qu Shaoyao tang. ("Skipping" is written "loose" in one version.)

## Notes

The same content as Clause 21 of the upper taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
