# §71 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When a disease originally taiyang is, on the contrary, purged by the physician, and as a result there are abdominal fullness and periodic pain, it belongs to the taiyin; it belongs to Guizhi jia Shaoyao tang.

## Notes

The same content as the first half of Clause 279 of the taiyin chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
