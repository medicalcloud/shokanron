# §55 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In cold damage, when the physician purged and continuing diarrhea of undigested grain does not stop, with body pain, one should urgently rescue the interior; afterward, when there is body pain and the stool has spontaneously become regular, one should urgently rescue the exterior. To rescue the interior, Sini tang is appropriate; to rescue the exterior, Guizhi tang is appropriate.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 91 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
