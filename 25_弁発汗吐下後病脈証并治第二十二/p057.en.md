# §57 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cold damage unresolved on the thirteenth day, with fullness of the chest and rib-sides and retching, tidal fever around late afternoon, followed by slight diarrhea: this was originally [a] Chaihu [pattern], and purging it should not produce diarrhea; now, on the contrary, there is diarrhea, so one knows the physician purged with pills, which is not the right treatment. Tidal fever is repletion. First take Xiao Chaihu tang to resolve the external, then govern it with Chaihu jia Mangxiao tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 104 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
