# §44 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Great sweating, or great purgation, with cold limbs: it belongs to Sini tang (Shigyakuto).

## Notes

The same content as Clause 354 of the Jueyin chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
