# §61 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease with a floating, stirred, rapid pulse. Floating means wind; rapid means heat; stirred means pain; rapid means vacuity. With headache, fever, slight night sweating, and, on the contrary, aversion to cold, the exterior has not yet resolved. The physician, on the contrary, purged, the stirred rapid pulse changed to slow, there is resisting pain within the diaphragm (one version says "headache with immediate dizziness"), the stomach is empty, guest qi stirs the diaphragm, there are shortness of breath, agitation and vexation, anguish in the heart, yang qi sinks inward, and the area below the heart becomes hard, which is chest binding; it belongs to the Da Xianxiong tang pattern. If there is no chest binding, but only sweating on the head with none elsewhere, stopping at the neck, and urination inhibited, the body will certainly turn yellow.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 134 of the lower taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
