# §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease of three days that has already been sweated, or vomited, or purged, or treated with warm needling, yet still does not resolve, is a spoiled disease; Guizhi [tang] is not suitable to give. Observe the pulse and signs, know which error was committed, and treat according to the pattern.

## Notes

The same content as the first half of Clause 16 of the upper taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
