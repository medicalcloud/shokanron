# §41 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yangming disease with a floating tight pulse, dry throat, bitter mouth, abdominal fullness with panting, fever and sweating, no aversion to cold but, on the contrary, aversion to heat, and a heavy body. If sweated, there is agitation, the heart is confused, and, on the contrary, there is delirious speech; if warm needling is added, there will certainly be fear, vexation, and agitation with inability to sleep; if purged, the stomach is empty, guest qi stirs the diaphragm, there is anguish in the heart, and when there is fur on the tongue, it belongs to Zhizi Chi tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 221 of the yangming chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
