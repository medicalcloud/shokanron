# §68 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yangming disease after purgation with heat outside, warm hands and feet, no chest binding, anguish in the heart, hunger without being able to eat, and sweating only on the head: it belongs to Zhizi Chi tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 228 of the yangming chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
