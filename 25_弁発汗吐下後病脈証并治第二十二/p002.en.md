# §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The cun kou pulse is floating and large, yet the physician, on the contrary, purged it; this is a great adverse treatment. Floating means there is no blood; large means cold. When cold qi contends, there is rumbling of the intestines. The physician, not knowing this, on the contrary gave cold water to drink to cause great sweating. The water obtains the cold qi, the cold will certainly contend, and the person chokes.

## Notes

Nearly the same text as paragraph 28 of the "Distinguishing the Pulse" chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
