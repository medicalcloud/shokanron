# §66 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease with the external pattern not yet removed, purged repeatedly, so that heat-associated diarrhea results, the diarrhea does not stop, there are glomus and hardness below the heart, and exterior and interior are unresolved: it belongs to Guizhi Renshen tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 163 of the lower taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
