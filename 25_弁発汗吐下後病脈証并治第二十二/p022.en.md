# §22 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

After vomiting, diarrhea, and sweating, when the pulse is normal but there is slight vexation, it is because one is newly vacuous and cannot overcome the grain qi.

## Notes

The same text as Clause 391 of the sudden turmoil chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
