# §60 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

After fire counterflow, it was purged; when vexation and agitation arise from the burning needle, it belongs to Guizhi Gancao Longgu Muli tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 118 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
