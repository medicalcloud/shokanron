# §60 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

After fire counterflow, it was purged; when restlessness arises from the burning needle, it belongs to Guizhi Gancao Longgu Muli tang (Keishikanzoryukotsuboreito).

## Notes

The same content as Clause 118 of the middle Taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
