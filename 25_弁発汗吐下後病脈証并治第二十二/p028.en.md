# §28 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cold damage after vomiting or purgation, with counterflow fullness below the heart, qi surging up into the chest, dizziness on rising, and a sunken tight pulse: sweating would stir the channels and make the body sway and shake. It belongs to Fuling Guizhi Baizhu Gancao tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 67 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
