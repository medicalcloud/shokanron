# §67 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

After purgation, Guizhi tang must not be used again. When there are sweating and panting without great heat, it belongs to Mahuang Xingren Gancao Shigao tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 162 of the lower taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
