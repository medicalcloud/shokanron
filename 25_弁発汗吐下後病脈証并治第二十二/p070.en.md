# §70 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the patient has no exterior or interior pattern and has had fever for seven or eight days, even if the pulse is floating and rapid, it may be purged. Suppose it has already been purged and the rapid pulse does not resolve; now with heat, grain is dispersed and one is prone to hunger; when by the sixth or seventh day there is no bowel movement, there is static blood; it belongs to Didang tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 257 of the yangming chapter (Clause 257 reads "combined heat," this paragraph "now with heat"). Cheng Wuji's commentary is found there.
