# §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Six or seven days after contracting the disease, with a slow, floating, weak pulse, aversion to wind and cold, and warm hands and feet, the physician purged two or three times; when one cannot eat, with fullness and pain below the rib-sides, yellow face, eyes, and body, stiff neck and nape, and difficult urination, giving Chaihu tang will certainly cause rectal heaviness afterward. For one who originally retches upon drinking water when thirsty, Chaihu is not suitable; eating grain causes hiccup.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 98 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
