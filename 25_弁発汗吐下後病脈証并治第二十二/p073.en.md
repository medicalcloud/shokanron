# §73 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In cold damage with originally spontaneous cold diarrhea, the physician again induced vomiting and purgation, causing cold repulsion; with further counterflow vomiting and purgation, if food is vomited as soon as it enters the mouth, it belongs to Ganjiang Huangqin Huanglian Renshen tang. [The base punctuates "本自寒。下医復吐下之," but following Clause 359 of the jueyin chapter, "本自寒下" is read as one phrase.]

## Notes

The same content as Clause 359 of the jueyin chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found. It is the last paragraph of the chapter "Distinguishing Pulse, Signs, and Treatment of Diseases After Sweating, Vomiting, and Purgation," and also the last paragraph of the main text of the *Shanghan lun* (ten scrolls, twenty-two chapters).
