# §27 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

After purgation, sweating again, with restlessness and inability to sleep during the day but calm at night, no retching, no thirst, no exterior pattern, a deep feeble pulse, and no great body heat: it belongs to Ganjiang Fuzi tang (Kankyobushito).

## Notes

The same content as Clause 61 of the middle Taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
