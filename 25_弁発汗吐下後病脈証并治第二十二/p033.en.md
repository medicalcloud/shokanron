# §33 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease sweated repeatedly and purged again, with no bowel movement for five or six days, dry tongue and thirst, slight tidal fever around late afternoon (one version says: "arising in the late afternoon, with great vexation in the heart and chest"), and hardness, fullness, and pain from below the heart to the lower abdomen that cannot be approached: it belongs to Da Xianxiong tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 137 of the lower taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
