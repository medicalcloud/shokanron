# §13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Overlap disease of Taiyang and Shaoyang which, on the contrary, is purged forms chest bind, with hardness below the heart, unceasing diarrhea, water and drink not going down, and heart vexation.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 150 of the lower Taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
