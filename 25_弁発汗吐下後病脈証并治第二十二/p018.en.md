# §18 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In disease where yang predominates there is heat; purging it causes hardness. When sweating is profuse and sweating is induced to the extreme, there is also hardness.

## Notes

The text of paragraph 28 of the "Must Not Be Purged" chapter with a phrase on sweating added. Cheng Wuji's commentary is found there.
