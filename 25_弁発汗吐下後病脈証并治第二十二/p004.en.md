# §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the pulse is floating and rapid, by rule sweating should cure it. If it has been purged and there are heaviness of the body and palpitations, sweating must not be induced; one should wait for spontaneous sweating to resolve it. The reason is that the chi pulse is faint, which is interior vacuity; one must wait until exterior and interior are replete and the fluids harmonious, and then spontaneous sweating will cure it.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 49 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
