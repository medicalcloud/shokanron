# §45 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease, after purgation, when the qi surges upward, Guizhi tang may be given; if it does not surge upward, it must not be given.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 15 of the upper taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
