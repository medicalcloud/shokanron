# §43 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease: when after vomiting, purgation, or sweating there are slight vexation, frequent urination, and consequently hard stool, give Xiao Chengqi tang; harmonizing it cures it.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 250 of the yangming chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
