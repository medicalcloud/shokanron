# §53 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cold damage heavily purged by the physician with pills, with body heat not leaving and slight vexation: it belongs to Zhizi Ganjiang tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 80 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
