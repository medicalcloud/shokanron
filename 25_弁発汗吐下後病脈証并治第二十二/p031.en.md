# §31 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When after sweating or purgation there are vexing heat and blockage in the chest, it belongs to Zhizi Chi tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 77 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
