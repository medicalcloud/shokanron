# §24 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease of eight or nine days, malaria-like, with fever and aversion to cold, more heat than cold, no retching, stool tending to be normal, attacks two or three times a day: when the pulse is faint and moderate, it is about to recover. When the pulse is faint with aversion to cold, both yin and yang are vacuous, and it must not be sweated, purged, or vomited again. When the complexion, on the contrary, has a hot color, it is not yet about to resolve; because one cannot get a slight sweat, the body will certainly itch. It belongs to Guizhi Mahuang Geban tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 23 of the upper taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
