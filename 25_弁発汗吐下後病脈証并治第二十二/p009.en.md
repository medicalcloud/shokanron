# §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease first purged without recovery, then sweated again: thereby exterior and interior are both vacuous, and the person consequently has veiling. One with veiling recovers spontaneously when sweat comes out. The reason is that sweating harmonizes the exterior. Once the exterior is harmonized, then purge it again.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 93 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
