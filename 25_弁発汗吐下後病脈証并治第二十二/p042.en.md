# §42 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yangming disease after purgation with anguish and vexation in the heart: when there is dry stool in the stomach, it may be attacked. When the abdomen is only slightly full, with the head of the stool hard and the rest certainly sloppy, it must not be attacked. If there is dry stool, Da Chengqi tang is appropriate.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 238 of the yangming chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
