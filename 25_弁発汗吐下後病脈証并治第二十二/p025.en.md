# §25 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When after taking Guizhi tang or purgation there are still stiff and painful head and nape, feathery fever, no sweating, fullness and slight pain below the heart, and inhibited urination, it belongs to Guizhi qu Gui jia Fuling Baizhu tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 28 of the upper taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
