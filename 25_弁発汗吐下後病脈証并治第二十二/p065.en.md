# §65 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cold damage: after taking decoctions, the diarrhea does not stop and there are glomus and hardness below the heart. After taking Xiexin tang, it was purged again with other medicines, and the diarrhea does not stop. The physician gave Lizhong, and the diarrhea became worse. Lizhong regulates the middle burner; this diarrhea is in the lower burner. It belongs to Chishizhi Yuyuliang tang. If it still does not stop, urination should be promoted.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 159 of the lower taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
