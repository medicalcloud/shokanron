# §52 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cold damage after purgation, with heart vexation, abdominal fullness, and restlessness whether lying or rising: it belongs to Zhizi Houpo tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 79 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
