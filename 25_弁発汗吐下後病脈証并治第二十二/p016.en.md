# §16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yangming disease in which one can eat but purging does not resolve it: the person [later] cannot eat; if the heat is attacked, there will certainly be hiccup. The reason is that the stomach is vacuous and cold. Because the person is originally vacuous, attacking the heat will certainly cause hiccup.

## Notes

Content corresponding to Clause 194 of the yangming chapter (with differences in wording). Cheng Wuji's commentary is found there.
