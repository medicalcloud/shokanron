# §35 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In cold damage, when after sweating, vomiting, or purgation it has resolved but there are glomus and hardness below the heart and belching that does not stop, it belongs to Xuanfu Daizhe tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 161 of the lower taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
