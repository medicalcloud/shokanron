# §15 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cold damage after vomiting, purgation, and sweating, with vacuity vexation and an extremely faint pulse: when after eight or nine days there are glomus and hardness below the heart, pain below the rib-sides, qi surging up to the throat, dizziness and veiling, and twitching of the channels, over time it becomes atrophy.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 160 of the lower taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
