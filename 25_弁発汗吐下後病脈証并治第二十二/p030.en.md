# §30 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

After sweating, vomiting, and purgation, with deficiency vexation and inability to sleep; in severe cases there will certainly be tossing and turning and anguish in the heart: it belongs to Zhizi Chi tang (Shishishito). For shortness of breath, Zhizi Gancao Chi tang (Shishikanzoshito); for retching, Zhizi Shengjiang Chi tang (Shishishokyoshito).

## Notes

The same content as the second half of Clause 76 of the middle Taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
