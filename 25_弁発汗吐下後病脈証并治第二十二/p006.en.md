# §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When after heavy purgation sweating is induced again and urination is inhibited, it is due to loss of fluids. Do not treat it; when urination becomes free, it will certainly recover by itself.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 59 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
