# §39 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Combined disease of the three yang with abdominal fullness, heavy body, difficulty turning over, insensibility of the mouth, and a grimy face (one version writes "withered"; another says "向経").

## Notes

The same content as the first half of Clause 219 of the yangming chapter. In this chapter Clause 219 is split into two paragraphs, continuing in the next. Cheng Wuji's commentary is on Clause 219.
