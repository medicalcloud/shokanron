# §38 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cold damage after vomiting or purgation, unresolved, with no bowel movement for five or six days up to more than ten days, tidal fever around late afternoon, no aversion to cold, and talking to oneself as if seeing ghosts. In severe cases, during attacks one does not recognize people, fumbles with the clothes and gropes at the bed, is fearful and uneasy (one version says: "plucking aimlessly along the clothes, fearful and uneasy"), with slight panting and staring eyes; with a wiry pulse one lives, with a rough pulse one dies. In mild cases with only fever and delirious speech, it belongs to Da Chengqi tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 212 of the yangming chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
