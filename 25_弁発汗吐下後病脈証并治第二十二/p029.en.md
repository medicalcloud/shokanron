# §29 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When after sweating or purgation the disease still does not resolve and there are vexation and agitation, it belongs to Fuling Sini tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 69 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
