# §32 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease more than ten days past the channels, with queasiness below the heart and a desire to vomit, pain in the chest, stool, on the contrary, sloppy, slight abdominal fullness, and depressed slight vexation: if before this there was extreme vomiting or purgation, give Tiaowei Chengqi tang; if not, it must not be given. Only a desire to retch, chest pain, and slightly sloppy stool are not the Chaihu tang pattern; from the retching one knows there was extreme vomiting and purgation.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 123 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
