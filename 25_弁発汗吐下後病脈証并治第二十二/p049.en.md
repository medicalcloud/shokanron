# §49 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease that after purgation has slight panting: it is because the exterior has not yet resolved; it belongs to Guizhi jia Houpo Xingzi tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 43 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
