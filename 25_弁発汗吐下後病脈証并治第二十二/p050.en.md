# §50 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cold damage with no bowel movement for six or seven days, headache, and heat: give Chengqi tang. When the urine is clear (one version says "the stool is green-blue"), one knows [the disease] is not in the interior but still in the exterior, and sweating must be induced. If there is headache, there will certainly be nosebleed. Guizhi tang is appropriate.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 56 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
