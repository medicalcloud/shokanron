# §36 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cold damage heavily purged and then sweated, with stuffiness below the heart and aversion to cold: the exterior has not yet resolved; the stuffiness must not be attacked; the exterior should first be resolved, and when it is resolved, attack the stuffiness. To resolve the exterior, Guizhi tang is appropriate—use the previous formula; to attack the stuffiness, Dahuang Huanglian Xiexin tang is appropriate.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 164 of the lower Taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
