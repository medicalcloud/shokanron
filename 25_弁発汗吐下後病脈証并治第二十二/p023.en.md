# §23 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease: the physician induced sweating, then there was fever and aversion to cold, so it was purged again, producing glomus below the heart; exterior and interior are both vacuous, and the qi of yin and yang are both exhausted; without yang there is yin alone. If a burning needle is further applied, causing vexation in the chest, a green-yellow complexion, and twitching of the skin, it is difficult to treat; if now the color is slightly yellow and the hands and feet are warm, it recovers easily.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 153 of the lower taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
