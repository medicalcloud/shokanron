# §59 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cold damage of eight or nine days, purged, with chest fullness, vexation and fright, inhibited urination, delirious speech, heaviness of the whole body, and inability to turn over: it belongs to Chaihu jia Longgu Muli tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 107 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
