# §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In general, in any disease, whether after sweating, vomiting, purgation, or blood loss, with no fluids, when the yin and yang pulses spontaneously harmonize, there will certainly be spontaneous recovery.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 58 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
