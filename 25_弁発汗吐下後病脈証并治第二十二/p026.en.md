# §26 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease first sweated without resolution and then purged: when the pulse is floating, it does not recover. Floating means [the disease] is outside, yet it was, on the contrary, purged, so it does not recover. Now the pulse is floating, so it is outside; resolving the external will cure it; Guizhi tang is appropriate.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 45 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
