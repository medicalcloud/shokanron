# §56 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease more than ten days past the channels, which was, on the contrary, purged two or three times: when four or five days later the Chaihu pattern is still present, first give Xiao Chaihu [tang]. When retching does not stop, with urgency below the heart (one version says: "retching stops with slight ease") and depressed slight vexation, it has not yet resolved; Da Chaihu tang may be given; purging it cures it.
