# §72 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In cold damage of six or seven days, after great purgation, when the cun pulse is sunken and slow, the hands and feet show reversal counterflow, the pulse of the lower part does not arrive, the throat is inhibited, pus and blood are spat, and diarrhea does not stop, it is difficult to treat; it belongs to Mahuang Shengma tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 357 of the jueyin chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
