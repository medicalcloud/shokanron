# §51 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cold damage of five or six days, after heavy purgation, with body heat not leaving and binding pain in the heart: it is not yet about to resolve; it belongs to Zhizi Chi tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 78 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
