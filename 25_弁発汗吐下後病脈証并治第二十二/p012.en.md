# §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease purged: when the pulse is skipping (one version writes "loose") without chest bind, it is about to resolve. With a floating pulse, there will certainly be chest bind; tight, certainly sore throat; wiry, certainly tension of both rib-sides; thready and rapid, the headache has not yet stopped; deep and tight, certainly a desire to retch; deep and slippery, heat-associated diarrhea; floating and slippery, certainly bloody discharge.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 140 of the lower Taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
