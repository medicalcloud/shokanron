# §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease purged: when the pulse is skipping (one version writes "loose") without chest binding, it is about to resolve. With a floating pulse, there will certainly be chest binding; tight, certainly sore throat; wiry, certainly tension of both rib-sides; fine and rapid, the headache has not yet stopped; sunken and tight, certainly a desire to retch; sunken and slippery, heat-associated diarrhea; floating and slippery, certainly bloody discharge.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 140 of the lower taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
