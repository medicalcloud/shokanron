# §40 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Delirious speech and enuresis. If sweated, there is delirious speech; if purged, sweat appears on the forehead and the hands and feet are counterflow cold. If there is spontaneous sweating, it belongs to Baihu tang.

## Notes

The same content as the second half of Clause 219 of the yangming chapter (continuing the previous paragraph). Cheng Wuji's commentary is on Clause 219.
