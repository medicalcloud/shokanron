# §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In taiyang disease, excessive sweating leads to tetany.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Excessive sweating in taiyang disease causes yang collapse. The *Neijing* says: "Yang qi, when refined, nourishes the spirit; when soft, nourishes the sinews." When yang is faint and cannot nourish the sinews, the sinews and vessels become tense and tetany results.

## Notes

The clause states that excessive sweating loses yang qi and fluids, leaving the sinews unnourished and causing tetany. It is regarded as an example of tetany due to mistaken treatment.
