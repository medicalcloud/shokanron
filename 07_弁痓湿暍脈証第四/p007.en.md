# §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease with painful joints and vexation and a deep thready pulse (one version writes "slowdown") is called damp impediment (one version says "damp strike"). The signs of damp impediment are inhibited urination and, on the contrary, free stool; one should simply promote urination. The disease of one with damp constitution is pain all over the body, fever, and a body color like smoky yellow. In one with damp constitution, when there is sweating only on the head, stiffness of the back, and a desire to be covered with bedding and face the fire, if one purges too early, there will be hiccup, chest fullness, inhibited urination, and something like fur on the tongue; because there is heat in the cinnabar field and cold in the chest, one is thirsty and wants water but cannot drink, and the mouth is dry and vexed.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The *Jingui Yaolüe* says: "Fog damages the skin and interstices; dampness flows into the joints." Pain with vexation means damp qi flows within. Dampness is the same as water; a deep thready pulse is because water's nature tends downward. *Bi* means pain. It is called damp impediment because of the vexing pain in the joints; it is not the impediment of leg qi. The *Neijing* says: "When dampness prevails there is soggy diarrhea." Inhibited urination with, on the contrary, free stool means damp qi prevails within. One should simply promote urination to diffuse and drain the damp qi in the abdomen. Of old it is said: "In treating damp disease, not to promote urination is not the right treatment."

A body yellow like a tangerine is Yangming stagnant heat; this body color like smoky yellow is not Yangming stagnant heat. For yellowing of the body with fever, Zhizi Bopi tang (Shishihakuhito) governs it. When there is heat in exterior and interior, the body does not ache; this pain all over the body is not lodged heat of cold damage, and one knows that pathogenic damp in the meridians causes it. The spleen dislikes dampness; damaged by dampness, the spleen is diseased and its color appears, so the body becomes yellow. The yellow is like smoke-stained, not a true yellow.

Damp constitutions have wind-damp and cold-damp; this is cold and damp contending. When dampness prevails there is much sweating; with cold damage there is no sweating. When cold and damp contend, although there is sweat it cannot spread over the whole body, so there is sweating only on the head. The back is yang; the abdomen is yin. The Taiyang vessel flanks the spine and reaches the lumbus; when cold-damp lodges in the Taiyang, the exterior qi is not free and the back is stiff. One with pathogen in the interior has no aversion to cold outside; one with pathogen in the exterior has aversion to cold. Wanting to be covered and face the fire means cold-damp is in the exterior with aversion to cold. Purging too early damages and stirs the stomach qi and harms the fluids, so there are hiccup, chest fullness, and inhibited urination. After purgation the interior is deficient, and the yang qi of the upper jiao, through deficiency, sinks into the lower jiao—this is heat in the cinnabar field; cold in the exterior takes advantage and enters the chest—this is cold in the upper chest, producing a white, slippery fur on the tongue. When the zang organs are dry one wants to drink water, but because cold-damp lodges in the upper chest one cannot drink, and there is only dryness of the mouth and vexation. [The commentary's "匈" is read as "胸" (chest).]

## Notes

The clause describes the signs of damp disease (damp impediment) and its principle of treatment (promoting urination), and further explains yellowing in damp constitutions and the changes from mistaken purgation. Yellowing due to dampness (smoky yellow) is distinguished from the bright yellow (tangerine color) due to Yangming stagnant heat.
