# §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When one with damp constitution is purged, with sweating on the forehead, slight panting, and free urination (one version says "inhibited"), it is fatal; if there is unceasing diarrhea, it is also fatal.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Damp constitutions recover with sweating. The *Jingui Yaolüe* says: "For one with damp constitution with vexing body pain, it is appropriate to give Mahuang [tang] with four liang of zhu added to induce sweating." Reckless purgation is a great adverse treatment. Sweating on the forehead with slight panting means yang qi counterflows upward; spontaneously free urination or diarrhea means yin qi flows downward. Yin and yang separate, so it is said to be fatal. The *Neijing* says: "When yin and yang separate and are lacking, the essential qi is then cut off."

## Notes

The clause states that when purgation is mistakenly applied in damp disease, yang qi floats up and deserts (forehead sweating, slight panting) while yin qi deserts downward (excessive urination, diarrhea), a fatal pattern in which yin and yang separate.
