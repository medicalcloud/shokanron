# §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang heat strike is summerheat stroke (*ye*). The person sweats with aversion to cold, has a hot body, and is thirsty.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Sweating with aversion to cold and a hot body without thirst is wind strike; sweating with aversion to cold and a hot body with thirst is summerheat stroke. Baihu jia Renshen tang governs it; it is found among the formulas of the *Jingui Yaolüe*.

## Notes

The clause describes the basic signs of "summerheat stroke," being struck by summer heat. Sweating, aversion to cold, and body heat resemble wind strike, but it is distinguished by the presence of thirst. In the *Jingui Yaolüe*, Baihu jia Renshen tang is used.
