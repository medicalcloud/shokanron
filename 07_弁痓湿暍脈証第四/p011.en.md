# §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the patient's whole body aches and there is fever that becomes severe around late afternoon, it is called wind-damp. This disease is caused by being exposed to wind while sweating, or by prolonged damage from taking in cold.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Aching of the whole body is dampness; fever severe in the late afternoon is wind. If contracted by being exposed to wind while sweating, dampness lodged first and wind was contracted afterward. If contracted by prolonged damage from taking in cold, wind damaged first and dampness struck afterward. Mahuang Xingren Yiyiren Gancao tang may be given; it is found in the *Jingui Yaolüe*.

## Notes

The clause describes the signs and causes of wind-damp disease (exposure to wind while sweating, prolonged exposure to cold). In the *Jingui Yaolüe*, Mahuang Xingren Yiyi Gancao tang is used.
