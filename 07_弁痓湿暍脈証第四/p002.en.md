# §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease with fever, no sweating, and, on the contrary, aversion to cold is called hard tetany.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The *Qianjin* says: "When taiyang wind strike is compounded by contraction of cold-damp, it transforms into tetany." Taiyang disease with fever and no sweating is exterior repletion and should not have aversion to cold. Now there is, on the contrary, aversion to cold, so taiyang wind strike has been compounded by contraction of cold and has become tetany. Because the exterior is replete and has contracted cold, it is called hard tetany.

Taiyang disease with fever and sweating is exterior vacuity and should have aversion to cold; without aversion to cold, it is yangming disease. Now when there are fever and sweating without aversion to cold, if it is not a yangming pattern, taiyang wind strike has been compounded by contraction of dampness and has become soft tetany. Because the exterior is vacuous and has contracted dampness, it is called soft tetany.

## Notes

The clause names "hard tetany" the form of tetany with fever, no sweating, and aversion to cold (exterior repletion). In the *Jingui Yaolüe*, Gegen tang is used. The second half of Cheng Wuji's commentary explains the soft tetany of the next paragraph.
