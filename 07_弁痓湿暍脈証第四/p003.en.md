# §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease with fever and sweating but no aversion to cold (the *Bingyuan* says "aversion to cold") is called soft tetany.

## Notes

The clause names "soft tetany" the form of tetany with fever and sweating (exterior vacuity). Cheng Wuji's explanation is in the latter half of the commentary on the previous paragraph. In the *Jingui Yaolüe*, Gualou Guizhi tang is used.
