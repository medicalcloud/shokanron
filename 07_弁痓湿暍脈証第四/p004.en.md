# §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease with fever and a deep thready pulse is called tetany.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The Taiyang governs the exterior. Fever in Taiyang disease is an exterior disease, and the pulse should be floating and large. Now the pulse is, on the contrary, deep and thready and it does not recover, so Taiyang wind attack has been compounded by contraction of dampness and has become tetany. The *Jingui Yaolüe* says: "Taiyang disease with the signs complete, the body stiff and rigid, and the pulse, on the contrary, deep and slow—this is tetany; Gualou Guizhi tang governs it."

## Notes

The clause states that a deep thready pulse despite exterior fever indicates tetany.
