# §13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang summer heat stroke with body heat, aching heaviness, and a feeble weak pulse is caused by being damaged by cold water in the summer months, with water moving in the skin.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The classic says: "A deficient pulse with body heat is contracted from summer heat damage." Body heat with a feeble weak pulse is summer heat stroke; aching heaviness of the body is water. It is contracted by bathing in water during summer heat. Yiwu Guadi san (Kateisan) (single-ingredient melon stalk powder) governs it; it is found among the formulas of the *Jingui Yaolüe*.

## Notes

The clause describes summer heat stroke in which water-damp lodges in the skin after bathing in or drinking cold water in summer. In the *Jingui Yaolüe*, Yiwu Guadi tang (Kateito) is used.
