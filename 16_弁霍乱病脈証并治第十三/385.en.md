# Clause 385 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Aversion to cold with a faint pulse (one version writes "moderate") and diarrhea again; when the diarrhea stops, it is blood collapse. Sini jia Renshen tang governs it. Formula 1.

Gancao 2 liang (mix-fried); fuzi 1 piece (raw, remove the skin, break into eight pieces); ganjiang 1.5 liang; renshen 1 liang.

Boil the four ingredients above in 3 sheng of water to obtain 1 sheng 2 ge, remove the dregs, and take warm in two divided doses.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Aversion to cold with a faint pulse and diarrhea means yang is vacuous and yin prevails. When the diarrhea stops, the fluids are exhausted within, so it is called blood collapse. The *Jingui Yuhan* says: "When water is exhausted there is no blood." Sini tang is given to warm the channels and assist yang, with renshen added to generate fluids and boost blood.

## Notes

When vomiting and diarrhea of sudden turmoil have lost both yang qi and fluids, the cessation of diarrhea is not recovery but because the fluids to be discharged are exhausted (blood collapse); renshen is added to Sini tang to restore yang while supplementing fluids.
