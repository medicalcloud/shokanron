# Clause 388 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Vomiting and diarrhea, sweating, fever and aversion to cold, tension of the four limbs, and reversal-cold hands and feet: Sini tang governs it. Formula 4.

Gancao 2 liang (mix-fried); ganjiang 1.5 liang; fuzi 1 piece (raw, remove the skin, break into eight pieces).

Boil the three ingredients above in 3 sheng of water to obtain 1 sheng 2 ge, remove the dregs, and take warm in two divided doses. For a strong person, one large fuzi and ganjiang 3 liang may be used.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Vomiting above and diarrhea below are interior vacuity. Sweating, fever, and aversion to cold mean the exterior has not yet resolved. Tension of the four limbs and reversal-cold hands and feet mean yang is vacuous and yin prevails. Sini tang is given to assist yang and repel yin.

## Notes

When the violent vomiting, diarrhea, and sweating of sudden turmoil have lost yang qi and fluids, with tension and cold of the limbs appearing, priority is given to restoring yang with Sini tang even if an exterior pattern remains.
