# Clause 391 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

After vomiting, diarrhea, and sweating, when the pulse is normal but there is slight vexation, it is because one is newly vacuous and cannot overcome the grain qi.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The *Neijing* says: "Food enters the yin and grows qi in the yang." Being newly vacuous and unable to overcome the grain qi, slight vexation arises.

## Notes

Slight vexation after sudden turmoil has healed and the pulse has returned to normal is because the stomach qi is still weak after illness and cannot fully digest food; moderating diet suffices. It is the last clause of the sudden turmoil chapter.
