# Clause 387 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When vomiting and diarrhea have stopped but body pain does not cease, one should adjust carefully and harmonize and resolve the exterior; it is appropriate to harmonize it slightly with Guizhi tang. Formula 3.

Guizhi 3 liang (remove the bark); shaoyao 3 liang; shengjiang 3 liang; gancao 2 liang (mix-fried); dazao 12 pieces (broken).

Boil the five ingredients above in 7 sheng of water to obtain 3 sheng, remove the dregs, and take 1 sheng warm.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Cessation of vomiting and diarrhea means the interior is harmonized. Unceasing body pain means the exterior has not yet resolved. Guizhi tang is given to harmonize it slightly. The *Waitai* says: "When the interior is harmonized and the exterior diseased, sweating cures it."

## Notes

When the vomiting and diarrhea of sudden turmoil have stopped and the interior is harmonized but body pain of the exterior pattern remains, Guizhi tang gently harmonizes the exterior. "Adjust carefully" (*xiaoxi*) is understood as modifying according to the condition, and "harmonize slightly" as harmonizing a little without inducing heavy sweating.
