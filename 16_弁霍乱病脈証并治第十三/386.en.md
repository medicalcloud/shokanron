# Clause 386 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Sudden turmoil with headache, fever, and body aches: when heat predominates and there is a desire to drink water, Wuling san governs it; when cold predominates and water is not wanted, Lizhong wan (Center-Regulating Pill) governs it. 2.

Wuling san formula:

Zhuling (remove the skin), baizhu, and fuling, 18 zhu each; guizhi half a liang (remove the bark); zexie 1 liang 6 zhu.

Make the five ingredients above into powder, process further, and take a square-inch spoonful mixed with white drink, three times a day. Drink plenty of warm water; when sweat comes out, there is recovery.

Lizhong wan formula (below are the method of making it as a decoction and the modifications):

Renshen, ganjiang, gancao (mix-fried), and baizhu, 3 liang each.

Pound and sift the four ingredients above, mix with honey to make pills about the size of an egg yolk. Mix one pill with several ge of boiling water, grind it, and take warm, three or four times during the day and twice at night. If the abdomen is not yet warm, increase to three or four pills; but it is not as good as the decoction. Decoction method: cut the four items according to the liang amounts, boil in 8 sheng of water to obtain 3 sheng, remove the dregs, and take 1 sheng warm, three times a day. If there is pounding above the navel, the kidney qi is stirring: remove the zhu and add gui 4 liang. For much vomiting, remove the zhu and add shengjiang 3 liang. For much diarrhea, still use the zhu. For palpitations, add fuling 2 liang. For thirst with a desire for water, add zhu to make 4.5 liang together with the previous amount. For pain in the abdomen, add renshen to make 4.5 liang together with the previous amount. For cold, add ganjiang to make 4.5 liang together with the previous amount. For abdominal fullness, remove the zhu and add one fuzi. About a mealtime after taking the decoction, drink about 1 sheng of hot gruel, keep slightly warm, and do not throw off the clothes and bedding.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Headache and fever mean the evil came from wind-cold. The middle burner is the region where cold and heat are half and half. When the evil is slightly higher, in the yang region, it becomes heat; when heat predominates with a desire to drink water, Wuling san is given to dissipate it. When the evil is slightly lower, in the yin region, it becomes cold; when cold predominates and water is not wanted, Lizhong wan is given to warm it.

(Nature and flavor) Renshen: sweet, warm. Gancao: sweet, neutral. Baizhu: sweet, warm. Ganjiang: acrid, hot.

The *Neijing* says: "The spleen desires moderation; quickly eat sweet to moderate it; use sweet to supplement it." The sweetness of renshen, baizhu, and gancao moderates spleen qi and regulates the center. When cold prevails, level it with acrid-hot—the acridity of ganjiang warms the stomach and dissipates cold.

When the spleen is vacuous and the kidney qi stirs, there is pounding movement above the navel. The *Neijing* says: "Sweet causes fullness in the center." Zhu is sweet, congesting and supplementing; gui drains running piglet; so one is exchanged for the other.

Those prone to retching dislike sweet, so zhu is removed; those prone to retching take much shengjiang, dissipating with its acridity.

For much diarrhea, zhu is used to remove dampness. For palpitations, fuling is added to conduct the qi.

When fluids are insufficient there is thirst; the sweetness of zhu moderates it.

When the interior is vacuous there is pain; renshen is added to supplement it.

When cold prevails, level it with acrid-hot.

When the stomach is vacuous, qi congests and the abdomen is full. Sweet causes fullness in the center, hence zhu is removed. The acridity of fuzi supplements yang and dissipates the congestion.

## Notes

The clause divides sudden turmoil with an exterior pattern into those with predominant heat and thirst (Wuling san) and those with predominant cold and no thirst (Lizhong wan).

- Lizhong wan (Renshen tang) is regarded as the basic formula for warming and supplementing vacuity cold of the middle-burner spleen and stomach, widely used in later times.
- It states that the decoction is stronger than the pill and gives detailed modifications according to symptoms.
- The instruction to drink hot gruel after taking the medicine is shared with the administration of Guizhi tang.
