# Clause 271 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In cold damage on the third day, when the shaoyang pulse is small, it is about to cease.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The *Neijing* says: "[When the pulse is] large, the evil arrives; when small, there is calm." When on the third day of cold damage the evil is transmitted to the shaoyang, the pulse should be wiry and tight. Now the pulse is small, meaning the evil qi is faint and about to cease.

## Notes

A small, calm pulse in the shaoyang period is regarded as a sign that the evil qi has declined and the disease will cure itself.
