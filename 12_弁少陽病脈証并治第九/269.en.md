# Clause 269 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In cold damage of six or seven days, when there is no great heat but the person is agitated and vexed, it is because [the evil] has left the yang and entered the yin.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The exterior is yang; the interior is yin. When the evil is in the exterior, there is heat outside. Six or seven days is when the evil qi enters the interior; when there is no great heat outside but agitation and vexation within, the exterior evil has been transmitted inward. Therefore it is said "leaving the yang and entering the yin." [The commentary's "燥煩" is read in the sense of the clause's "躁煩."]

## Notes

When the surface heat subsides but agitation and vexation appear instead, it is not improvement but a sign that the evil has entered from the exterior (yang) into the interior (yin). There are views that "yin" here refers to the three yin channels and views that it simply refers to the interior.
