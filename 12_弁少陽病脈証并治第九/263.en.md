# Clause 263 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyang disease is bitter taste in the mouth, dry throat, and dizzy vision.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

[Shaoyang is] the foot shaoyang gallbladder channel. The *Neijing* says: "One who has the disease of a bitter mouth is called gallbladder *dan*." The *Jiayi jing* says: "The gallbladder is the bowel of central essence; the five viscera take their decisions from the gallbladder, and the throat serves as its messenger." The shaoyang vessel starts at the outer canthus of the eye. Because the shaoyang has contracted the evil, there are bitter mouth, dry throat, and dizzy vision.

## Notes

Regarded as the outline (general principle) clause of shaoyang disease. Bitter mouth, dry throat, and dizzy vision are commonly explained as due to the evil being half-exterior half-interior and gallbladder heat rising upward. It is usual to understand shaoyang disease together with the main signs of Xiao Chaihu tang (alternating chills and fever, chest and rib-side fullness, etc., Clause 96).
