# Clause 264 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyang wind strike with inability to hear in both ears, red eyes, and fullness and vexation in the chest: one must not induce vomiting or purge; if one induces vomiting or purges, there will be palpitations and fright.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The shaoyang vessel starts at the canthus, runs into the ear, and its branch descends into the chest and penetrates the diaphragm. Wind damages qi, and wind becomes heat. In shaoyang wind strike, the qi is congested and heated, so there are deafness, red eyes, and fullness and vexation in the chest. The evil is in the shaoyang, half-exterior half-interior; if vomiting is induced to remove vexation, vomiting damages qi, and one with qi vacuity has palpitations; if purgation is used to remove fullness, purgation loses blood, and one with blood vacuity is frightened.

## Notes

The clause states the signs of shaoyang wind strike (deafness, red eyes, chest fullness and vexation) and the prohibition of vomiting and purgation (two of the "three prohibitions" of shaoyang). Mistaking the chest fullness for repletion and using vomiting or purgation is said to damage qi and blood, causing palpitations and fright.
