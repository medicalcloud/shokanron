# Clause 270 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In cold damage on the third day, the three yang are exhausted and the three yin should receive the evil; when the person, on the contrary, can eat and does not retch, the three yin have not received the evil.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

In cold damage on the fourth day, the exterior evil is transmitted inward; when the interior is not harmonized, one cannot eat and retches. Now one can eat, on the contrary, and does not retch, so the evil has not been transmitted to the yin but is only in the yang. [The clause says "third day," but the commentary says "fourth day."]

## Notes

Based on the day-count scheme of transmission in the *Suwen* "Treatise on Heat" (day 1 taiyang, day 2 yangming, day 3 shaoyang, day 4 onward the three yin), the clause states that in practice whether the disease has passed to the three yin should be judged by appetite and the presence or absence of retching.
