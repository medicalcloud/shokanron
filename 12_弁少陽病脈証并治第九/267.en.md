# Clause 267 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

If vomiting, purgation, sweating, or warm needling has already been used, and there is delirious speech and the Chaihu tang pattern has ceased, this is a spoiled disease. Know which error was committed and treat it according to the method.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The shaoyang evil is between the exterior and interior. If vomiting, purgation, sweating, or warm needling is used recklessly, consuming the fluids, the stomach becomes dry, the wood evil invades the stomach, and there will certainly be delirious speech. If the Chaihu pattern has not ceased, it is not counted as an adverse development; if the Chaihu pattern has ceased, it is a spoiled disease. Examine in detail which treatment error caused it, and rescue it according to the method.

## Notes

When mistaken treatment is applied to the shaoyang disease of the previous clause, the Chaihu tang pattern is lost and delirious speech etc. arise; this is called a "spoiled disease," and it should be handled according to the nature of the error. It is regarded as having the same purport as the spoiled-disease clause in the taiyang chapter (Clause 16: "observe the pulse and signs, know which error was committed, and treat according to the pattern").
