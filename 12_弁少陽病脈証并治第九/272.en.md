# Clause 272 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The time when shaoyang disease tends to resolve is from yin to chen.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The *Neijing* says: "The shaoyang within yang communicates with the qi of spring." Yin, mao, and chen are the times when shaoyang wood is in its prime.

## Notes

One of the clauses assigning a "time of resolution" to each of the six channels. Yin to chen corresponds roughly to 3–9 a.m., the time of day when yang qi begins to arise, matched to spring (wood) and the shaoyang.
