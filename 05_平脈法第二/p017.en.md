# §17 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Question: When a downy floating pulse is obtained in the second month, how can one say there will be death in autumn? The master said: In the second month the pulse should be soft and weak; obtaining, on the contrary, a downy floating pulse is how one knows there will be death in autumn. In the second month the liver is in charge; the liver belongs to wood, and the pulse should correspond as soft and weak. Obtaining, on the contrary, a downy floating pulse means it is the lung pulse; the lung belongs to metal, and metal comes to overcome wood; thus one knows there will be death in autumn. All others follow this.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

In the spring season, seeing, on the contrary, the autumn pulse means metal qi is assailing wood; the lung comes to overcome the liver, seizing [the liver's] prime pulse and appearing. When autumn comes and the lung is in its prime, the liver qi will be cut off; thus one knows there will be death in autumn.

## Notes

A prognostic clause stating that the appearance of a pulse out of season (the autumn lung pulse in spring) is a sign that the viscus is damaged by five-phase restraint, and death occurs in the season when the restraining viscus is in its prime.
