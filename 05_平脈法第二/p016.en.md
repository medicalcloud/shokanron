# §16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

What does the western lung pulse resemble? The master said: The lung is metal and is called taiyin. Its pulse is downy and floating. In lung disease, spontaneously obtaining this pulse, or obtaining a moderate slow pulse, means recovery in all cases; obtaining a rapid pulse means it becomes severe. How is this known? Rapid is southern fire; fire overcomes western metal; by rule there should be welling-abscess swelling, and it is difficult to treat.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Light, vacuous, and floating is called downy; it is the normal pulse of the lung. Moderate and slow is the spleen pulse; the spleen is the mother of the lung, and child and mother engender each other, so all recover. Rapid is the heart pulse; fire restraining metal is ghost and thief punishing each other, so it becomes severe. The lung governs the skin and hair; rapid means heat; when heat lodges in the skin and stays without leaving, it becomes welling-abscesses and sores. The classic says: "When a rapid pulse [appears] out of season, malign sores arise."

## Notes

The clause describes the normal lung pulse (light and floating like down) and the prognosis by five-phase generation (the spleen's moderate slow pulse = earth generates metal) and restraint (the heart's rapid pulse = fire restrains metal).
