# §18 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The master said: Regarding the pulse, in a fat person floating is to be questioned; in a thin person sunken is to be questioned. A fat person should be sunken, yet now is, on the contrary, floating; a thin person should be floating, yet now is, on the contrary, sunken; therefore it is questioned.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

A fat person has thick flesh, so the pulse should be sunken; a thin person has thin flesh, so the pulse should be floating. Now if a fat person's pulse is, on the contrary, floating and a thin person's, on the contrary, sunken, evil qi must be interfering and making the pulse contrary to normal; therefore it should be questioned.

## Notes

Recognizing that the normal depth of the pulse differs with body build, the clause treats a pulse contrary to the build as pathological.
