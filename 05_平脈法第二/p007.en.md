# §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Question: When a person is ill with fear, what is the pulse like? The master said: The pulse is shaped like stroking a thread, strung together, and the face is white and drained of color.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The *Neijing* says: "Blood and qi are a person's spirit." One who is fearful has insufficient blood and qi and weak spirit qi. A pulse shaped like stroking a thread, strung together, with a white face drained of color—[as] the *Zhenjing* says: "One deprived of blood has a withered, lusterless complexion and an empty pulse." From this one knows fear is insufficiency of blood and qi.

## Notes

Paragraphs 7–9 are regarded as dialogues describing how emotions and states such as fear, not drinking, and shame appear in the pulse and complexion. This paragraph describes changes in pulse and complexion due to fear.
