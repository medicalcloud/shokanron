# §27 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The fuyang pulse is hidden and rough. Hidden means vomiting counterflow and failure to transform water and grain; rough means food cannot enter. This is called block and repulsion.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Hidden means the stomach qi is hidden and not diffused; the middle burner is blocked and repelled and the upright qi congested, so there is vomiting counterflow and water and grain are not transformed. Rough means the spleen qi is rough and does not spread; the evil qi repels at the upper burner, so food cannot enter.

## Notes

The clause explains block and repulsion, in which the qi of the spleen and stomach is congested, by the fuyang (stomach) pulse being hidden (deep and hard to feel) and rough.
