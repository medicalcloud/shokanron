# §30 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the fuyang pulse is large and tight, there should be diarrhea at once, and it is difficult to treat.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Large is vacuity; tight is cold. When the stomach is vacuous and cold, there should be diarrhea at once. With diarrhea the pulse should be faint and small; when it is, on the contrary, tight, the evil prevails, so it is said to be difficult to treat. The classic says: "Diarrhea with a large pulse has not yet stopped."

## Notes

A large (vacuity) and tight (cold) fuyang pulse means diarrhea from stomach vacuity cold, and a strong pulse disproportionate to the diarrhea is a sign of exuberant evil, so the clause deems it difficult to treat.
