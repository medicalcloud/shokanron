# §31 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The cun kou pulse is weak and moderate. Weak means yang qi is insufficient; moderate means stomach qi is in surplus. There are belching and acid swallowing; food does not go down quickly, and qi fills the area above (one version writes "below") the diaphragm.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Weak means yang qi is insufficient. Yang can digest grain; when yang qi is insufficient, grain and food cannot be digested. Moderate means stomach qi is in surplus—there is undigested grain in the stomach. Therefore there are belching and acid swallowing, food does not go down quickly, and qi fills the area above the diaphragm. The *Jingui Yaolüe* says: "When the middle burner is not yet harmonious, it cannot digest grain, hence belching."

## Notes

The clause explains by the pulse how insufficient yang qi fails to drive digestion, so that food stagnates in the stomach and produces belching, acid reflux, and blockage.
