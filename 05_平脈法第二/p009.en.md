# §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Question: When a person is ashamed, what kind of pulse is it? The master said: The pulse is floating, and the complexion turns alternately white and red.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Shame is embarrassment. When ashamed, the spirit qi is timid and weak, so the pulse is floating and the complexion changes without constancy.

## Notes

The clause states that the emotion of shame appears as a floating pulse and changes of complexion.
