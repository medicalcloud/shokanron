# §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The master said: Disease of hidden qi is examined by inference. When within this month hidden qi is about [to emerge], suppose there was previously hidden qi; then one must take the pulse. If the pulse is faint and weak, there should be pain in the throat as if injured, but it is not throat obstruction. The patient says: "My throat truly hurts." Even so, now there will also be diarrhea.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

When cold is contracted in winter and hides in the channels without arising at once, it is called hidden qi. At the spring equinox, the hidden cold is about to arise, so it is said "within this month hidden qi is about [to emerge]." If the hidden qi has already arisen, one must take the pulse to determine in which channel it lies. A faint weak pulse shows the evil is in the shaoyin. The shaoyin vessel follows the throat; when cold qi lodges there, sore throat will certainly arise. The kidney governs opening and closing, and the shaoyin governs the lower burner. When cold evil is severe within, opening and closing are not governed and the lower burner is not contained, so there will certainly be diarrhea. Therefore it is said: "Even so, the throat hurts, and there will also be diarrhea."

## Notes

Regarding diseases of "hidden qi," in which cold evil received in winter lies latent and arises in spring, the clause states that a faint weak pulse means the evil is in the shaoyin, producing sore throat and diarrhea. It is read in connection with the sore-throat and diarrhea clauses of the shaoyin chapter.
