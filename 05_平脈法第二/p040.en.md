# §40 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the fuyang pulse does not emerge, the spleen does not move up and down, and the body is cold and the skin hard.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The spleen and stomach are the root of the nutritive and defensive. When the spleen can move up and down, water and grain are digested and the qi of the nutritive and defensive can move. When the spleen qi is vacuous and debilitated and cannot move up and down, the qi of the nutritive and defensive cannot pass and circulate outward, so the fuyang pulse does not emerge. A cold body means the defensive qi does not warm; hard skin means the nutritive blood does not moisten.

## Notes

When the qi of the spleen and stomach declines and fails to circulate the nutritive and defensive, the fuyang pulse cannot be felt, and coldness of the body and hardness of the skin arise.
