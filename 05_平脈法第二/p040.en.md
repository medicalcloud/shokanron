# §40 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the fuyang pulse does not emerge, the spleen does not move up and down, and the body is cold and the skin hard.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The spleen and stomach are the root of the ying-nutrient and wei-defense. When the spleen can move up and down, water and grain are digested and the qi of the ying-nutrient and wei-defense can move. When the spleen qi is deficient and debilitated and cannot move up and down, the qi of the ying-nutrient and wei-defense cannot pass and circulate outward, so the fuyang pulse does not emerge. A cold body means the wei-defensive qi does not warm; hard skin means the ying-nutrient blood does not moisten.

## Notes

When the qi of the spleen and stomach declines and fails to circulate the ying-nutrient and wei-defense, the fuyang pulse cannot be felt, and coldness of the body and hardness of the skin arise.
