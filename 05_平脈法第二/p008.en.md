# §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Question: When a person does not drink, what kind of pulse is it? The master said: The pulse is spontaneously rough, and the lips and mouth are dry.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Rough is yin. Although it governs loss of fluids and the lips and mouth are dry, because yin governs the interior, one does not drink.

## Notes

The pulse of one who takes no fluids is rough (stagnant flow), and the lips and mouth become dry. Cheng Wuji's explanation is terse and leaves room for interpretation.
