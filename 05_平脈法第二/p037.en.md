# §37 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The cun kou pulse is faint and moderate. Faint means the defensive qi is sparse; when it is sparse, the skin is empty. Moderate means the stomach qi is replete; when it is replete, grain is digested and water transformed. When grain enters the stomach, the pathways of the vessels then move; when water enters the channels, the blood is then formed. When the nutritive is exuberant, the skin will certainly be sparse, and the triple burner breaks its norm; this is called blood flooding.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The defensive is yang; faint is yang collapse. A faint pulse means the defensive qi is sparse. The defensive warms the parting flesh and fattens the interstices; when the defensive qi is sparse, the skin cannot be warmed and fattened, so it is empty. The classic says: "Moderate means stomach qi in surplus." Surplus is repletion, so it is said "moderate means the stomach qi is replete." The *Neijing* says: "When food enters the stomach, it spreads essence into the vessels"; this is "when grain enters the stomach, the pathways of the vessels then move." The *Zhenjing* says: "Drink [enters], its fluid seeps into the network vessels, and combines with the blood in harmony"; this is "when water enters the channels, the blood is then formed." In the stomach, grain is digested and water transformed into blood and qi. Now the defensive is sparse and the nutritive exuberant, meaning the nutritive qi is strong and the defensive qi weak. When the defensive qi is weak, outside it cannot make the skin secure and dense, which is called sparse qi; inside it cannot guard and protect the blood, and the blood floods as a result. *Jing* means norm. The triple burner is the pathway of qi; when the defensive qi is sparse, qi does not follow its normal measure, and the triple burner breaks its normal measure.

## Notes

After describing how blood is produced from food and drink, the clause states that because the defensive qi is weak and cannot protect the blood, massive bleeding (blood flooding) occurs. "Blood flooding" is sometimes read in connection with later gynecological "flooding and spotting" (massive abnormal uterine bleeding).
