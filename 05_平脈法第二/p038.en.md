# §38 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The *fuyang* [fuyang] pulse is faint and tight. Tight means cold; faint means vacuity. When faint and tight contend, there is shortness of breath. ["膚陽" is considered an error for "趺陽" (fuyang).]

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

When the center is vacuous and also cold, the qi naturally becomes short.

## Notes

A short clause stating that when the fuyang (spleen-stomach) pulse is faint (vacuity) and tight (cold), vacuity cold of the middle burner causes shortness of breath. The base's "膚陽" is regarded as an error for the similar-looking "趺陽."
