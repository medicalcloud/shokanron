# §34 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The fuyang pulse is deep and rapid. Deep means excess; rapid disperses grain. When it is tight, the disease is difficult to treat.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Deep means excess because deep governs the interior. Rapid disperses grain because rapid is heat. Tight is the liver pulse; its appearing in the spleen region means wood comes to restrain earth, ghost and thief punishing each other, so it is said to be difficult [to treat]. [The end of the commentary breaks off as "故云難" in the base; it is understood as "difficult to treat."]

## Notes

A deep rapid fuyang pulse indicates stomach excess heat with hyperactive digestion; a tight pulse is the liver (wood) pulse appearing in the spleen (earth) region, an adverse restraint relation, and is difficult to treat.
