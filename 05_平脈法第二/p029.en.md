# §29 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The cun kou pulse is weak and slow. Weak means the defensive qi is faint; slow means there is cold in the nutritive. The nutritive is blood; when the blood is cold there is fever. The defensive is qi; when qi is faint there is hunger in the heart, but with hunger there is vacuous fullness and one cannot eat.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The defensive is yang; the nutritive is yin. Weak means the defensive qi is faint and yang qi insufficient. Slow means cold in the nutritive, evil lodging in the channels. When cold evil lodges in the nutritive and contends, there is fever. When yang qi is faint within, although there is hunger in the heart, with hunger there is vacuous fullness and one cannot eat.

## Notes

The clause explains fever and the state of being hungry yet distended and unable to eat by faintness of the defensive qi (weak pulse) and cold in the nutritive blood (slow pulse).
