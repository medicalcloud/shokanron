# §29 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The cun kou pulse is weak and slow. Weak means the wei-defensive qi is feeble; slow means there is cold in the ying-nutrient. The ying-nutrient is blood; when the blood is cold there is fever. The wei-defense is qi; when qi is feeble there is hunger in the heart, but with hunger there is deficient fullness and one cannot eat.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The wei-defense is yang; the ying-nutrient is yin. Weak means the wei-defensive qi is feeble and yang qi insufficient. Slow means cold in the ying-nutrient, pathogen lodging in the meridians. When pathogenic cold lodges in the ying-nutrient and contends, there is fever. When yang qi is feeble within, although there is hunger in the heart, with hunger there is deficient fullness and one cannot eat.

## Notes

The clause explains fever and the state of being hungry yet distended and unable to eat by feebleness of the wei-defensive qi (weak pulse) and cold in the ying-nutrient blood (slow pulse).
