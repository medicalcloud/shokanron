# §44 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Question: Why does the soft weak [pulse], on the contrary, fit the eleven heads? The master said: The five viscera and six bowels assail one another, so it makes eleven.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Soft and weak are qi and blood. Going and returning, there are eleven heads. The heads are the five viscera and six bowels, eleven in all.

## Notes

A short, difficult dialogue stating that the soft weak pulse (qi and blood) can appear in any of the eleven (five viscera and six bowels). Cheng Wuji's commentary is also terse, and interpretations vary.
