# §26 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The cun kou pulse is floating and large. Floating is vacuity; large is repletion. At the chi it is block; at the cun it is repulsion. With block there is no urination; with repulsion there is vomiting counterflow.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The classic says: "Floating is vacuity." The *Neijing* says: "Large means the disease advances." Floating means the upright qi is vacuous; large means the evil qi is replete. At the chi, the evil qi blocks and closes the lower burner, and the interior qi cannot pass downward, so there is no urination. At the cun, the evil qi repels at the upper burner, preventing food from entering, so there is vomiting counterflow.

## Notes

The clause explains the disease of "block and repulsion," combining urinary retention (block) and vomiting (repulsion), by the position in the cun kou pulse. It pairs with the next paragraph (block and repulsion by the fuyang pulse).
