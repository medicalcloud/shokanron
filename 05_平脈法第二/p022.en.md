# §22 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Question: I was once challenged by someone: from what does the tight pulse arise? The master said: Suppose there is loss of sweat or vomiting; because the interior of the lung is cold, the pulse is made tight. Suppose there is cough; because one drank cold water, the pulse is made tight. Suppose there is diarrhea; because the stomach is vacuous and cold, the pulse is made tight.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The *Jingui Yaolüe* says: "Cold makes the pulse urgent." The classic says: "All tightness is cold."

## Notes

The clause shows that the tight pulse arises from cold, with concrete examples: lung cold after sweating or vomiting, cough from cold water, and diarrhea from stomach vacuity cold.
