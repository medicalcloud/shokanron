# §39 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The shaoyin pulse is weak and rough. Weak means slight vexation; rough means reversal counterflow.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Vexation is heat. A weak shaoyin pulse means yin vacuity; yin vacuity produces fever. A yang pulse appearing in a yin position is not great vacuity, so slight vexation arises. Reversal counterflow means cold limbs. The classic says: "When the qi of yin and yang do not connect smoothly, there is reversal; reversal is reversal cold of the hands and feet." A rough shaoyin pulse means the yin qi is rough and cannot connect smoothly with yang, so there is reversal counterflow.

## Notes

The clause explains slight vexation (heat of yin vacuity) and cold extremities (reversal counterflow) by the shaoyin (kidney) pulse being weak and rough. Cheng Wuji cites the definition of reversal from Clause 337 of the jueyin chapter.
