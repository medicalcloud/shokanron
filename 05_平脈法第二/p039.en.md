# §39 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Shaoyin pulse is weak and hesitant. Weak means slight vexation; hesitant means cold limbs.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Vexation is heat. A weak Shaoyin pulse means yin deficiency; yin deficiency produces fever. A yang pulse appearing in a yin position is not great deficiency, so slight vexation arises. Reversal means cold limbs. The classic says: "When the qi of yin and yang do not connect smoothly, there is reversal; reversal is cold hands and feet." A hesitant Shaoyin pulse means the yin qi is rough and cannot connect smoothly with yang, so there are cold limbs.

## Notes

The clause explains slight vexation (heat of yin deficiency) and cold extremities by the Shaoyin (kidney) pulse being weak and hesitant. Cheng Wuji cites the definition of reversal (cold limbs) from Clause 337 of the Jueyin chapter.
