# §45 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Question: How does one know [the evil] has assailed the bowels, and how does one know it has assailed the viscera? The master said: All yang pulses, floating and rapid, mean assailing the bowels; all yin pulses, slow and rough, mean assailing the viscera.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The bowels are yang; when yang pulses are seen, the bowels are assailed. The viscera are yin; when yin pulses are seen, the viscera are assailed.

## Notes

The clause judges that the evil has reached the bowels if the pulse is floating and rapid (yang pulse), and the viscera if slow and rough (yin pulse). It is the last paragraph of the "Balancing the Pulse" chapter and has the same purport as paragraph 21 of the "Distinguishing the Pulse" chapter.
