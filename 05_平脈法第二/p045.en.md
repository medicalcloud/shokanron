# §45 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Question: How does one know [the pathogen] has assailed the bowels, and how does one know it has assailed the zang organs? The master said: All yang pulses, floating and rapid, mean assailing the bowels; all yin pulses, slow and hesitant, mean assailing the zang organs.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The bowels are yang; when yang pulses are seen, the bowels are assailed. The zang organs are yin; when yin pulses are seen, the zang organs are assailed.

## Notes

The clause judges that the pathogen has reached the bowels if the pulse is floating and rapid (yang pulse), and the zang organs if slow and hesitant (yin pulse). It is the last paragraph of the "Balancing the Pulse" chapter and has the same purport as paragraph 21 of the "Distinguishing the Pulse" chapter.
