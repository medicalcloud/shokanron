# §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The master said: Breathing is the head of the pulse. When first taking the pulse, if it comes fast and goes slow, it goes out fast and comes in slow; this is called interior vacuity with exterior repletion. When first taking the pulse, if it comes slow and goes fast, it goes out slow and comes in fast; this is called interior repletion with exterior vacuity.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The *Nanjing* says: "With one exhalation the pulse moves three cun; with one inhalation the pulse moves three cun." Because the pulse moves with breathing, it is called the head of the pulse.

The outside is yang; the inside is yin. The *Neijing* says: "What comes is yang; what goes is yin." Going out examines the outside; coming in examines the inside. Fast is surplus, and surplus is repletion; slow is insufficiency, and insufficiency is vacuity. Coming fast and going slow means yang is in surplus and yin insufficient, so it is called interior vacuity with exterior repletion. Coming slow and going fast means yang is insufficient and yin in surplus, so it is called interior repletion with exterior vacuity.

## Notes

The clause describes judging the vacuity or repletion of inside and outside by the speed of the "coming" (rise) and "going" (fall) of the pulse beat.
