# §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

After sweating, when water and medicine cannot enter the mouth, it is adverse. If sweating is induced again, there will certainly be unceasing vomiting and diarrhea.

## Notes

The same content as the first half of Clause 76 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
