# §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Before the pulse is taken, the patient crosses the hands to cover the heart. When the master tells the patient to try coughing and the patient does not cough at once, this is certainly deafness in both ears. The reason is that repeated sweating has caused deficiency, so it is like this.

## Notes

The same content as the first half of Clause 75 of the middle Taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
