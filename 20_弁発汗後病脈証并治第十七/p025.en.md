# §25 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cold damage with sweating and thirst: Wuling san is appropriate; without thirst, it belongs to Fuling Gancao tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 73 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
