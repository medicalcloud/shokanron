# §18 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When sweating has been excessive and the person crosses the hands to cover the heart, with palpitations below the heart and a desire for pressure, it belongs to Guizhi Gancao tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 64 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
