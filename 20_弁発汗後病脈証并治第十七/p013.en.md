# §13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cold damage with a floating pulse, spontaneous sweating, frequent urination, heart vexation, slight aversion to cold, and spasm of the legs: to give, on the contrary, Guizhi [tang] to attack the exterior is a mistake. Upon taking it there is at once reversal, dryness in the throat, vexation and agitation, and vomiting counterflow; make Gancao Ganjiang tang and give it to restore the yang. If the reversal heals and the feet become warm, make Shaoyao Gancao tang and give it, and the legs will at once stretch. If the stomach qi is not harmonious with delirious speech, give a little Tiaowei Chengqi tang. If sweating has been repeated and a burning needle further applied, give Sini tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 29 of the upper taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
