# §26 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease: after sweating, the sweat comes out but it does not resolve; the person still has fever, palpitations below the heart, dizziness, twitching of the body, and trembling as if about to fall to the ground ("fall" is written differently in one version): it belongs to Zhenwu tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 82 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
