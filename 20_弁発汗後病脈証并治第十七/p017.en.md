# §17 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

After sweating, Guizhi tang must not be used again. When there are sweating and dyspnea without great heat, Mahuang Xingren Gancao Shigao tang may be given.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 63 of the middle Taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
