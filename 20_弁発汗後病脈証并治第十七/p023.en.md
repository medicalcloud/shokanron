# §23 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease: after sweating, with great sweating, dryness in the stomach, vexation and agitation with inability to sleep, and a desire to drink water, give a little at a time to drink; when the stomach qi is harmonized there is recovery. If the pulse is floating, urination inhibited, and there are slight heat and dispersion-thirst, it belongs to Wuling san.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 71 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
