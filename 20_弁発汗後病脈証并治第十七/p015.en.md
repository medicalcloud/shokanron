# §15 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cold damage that has resolved after sweating, but after about half a day there is vexation again and the pulse is floating and rapid: sweating may be induced again; it belongs to Guizhi tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 57 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
