# §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the patient has cold and sweating is induced again, the stomach becomes cold and there will certainly be vomiting of roundworms.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 89 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
