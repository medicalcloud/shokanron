# §29 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yangming disease with spontaneous sweating, or after sweating, with spontaneously free urination: the fluids are exhausted within, and although [the stool] is hard, it must not be attacked. Wait until one spontaneously wants to defecate, and it is appropriate to guide and free it with a honey suppository. Tuguagen and large pig bile can also all be used as guides.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 233 of the yangming chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
