# §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease: when after first taking Guizhi tang there is, on the contrary, vexation and no resolution, first needle Fengchi and Fengfu, then give Guizhi tang, and there will be recovery.

## Notes

The same text as Clause 24 of the upper taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
