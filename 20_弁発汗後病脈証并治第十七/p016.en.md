# §16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

After sweating, with body aches and a deep slow pulse: it belongs to Guizhi (keishi) jia Shaoyao (shakuyaku) Shengjiang (shokyo) ge Yi Liang Renshen San Liang Xinjia tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 62 of the middle Taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
