# §16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

After sweating, with body aches and a sunken slow pulse: it belongs to Guizhi jia Shaoyao Shengjiang ge Yi Liang Renshen San Liang Xinjia tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 62 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
