# §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease: after sweating, [sweat] leaks without stopping, the person has aversion to wind, difficult urination, and slight tension of the four limbs, making bending and stretching difficult: it belongs to Guizhi jia Fuzi tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 20 of the upper taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
