# §32 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When after sweating it does not resolve and there are abdominal fullness and pain, purge urgently; Da Chengqi tang is appropriate.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 254 of the yangming chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
