# §21 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When sweating does not resolve the disease and there is, on the contrary, aversion to cold, it is due to vacuity; it belongs to Shaoyao Gancao Fuzi tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 68 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
