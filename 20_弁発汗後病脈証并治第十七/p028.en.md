# §28 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cold damage with fever unresolved by sweating, stuffiness and hardness in the heart, and vomiting with diarrhea: it belongs to Da Chaihu tang (Daisaikoto).

## Notes

The same content as Clause 165 of the lower Taiyang chapter (Clause 165 reads "below the heart"). Cheng Wuji's commentary is found there.
