# §33 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When sweating has been profuse and there is yang collapse with delirium, it must not be purged. Give Chaihu Guizhi tang (Saikokeishito) to harmonize the ying-nutrient and wei-defense and thereby free the fluids, and afterward it will recover by itself.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The stomach is the sea of water and grain and the master of the fluids. Profuse sweating loses the fluids, the stomach becomes dry, and there will certainly be delirium. This is not excess heat, so it must not be purged. Give Chaihu Guizhi tang to harmonize the ying-nutrient and wei-defense and make the fluids flow; when the fluids are generated, the stomach is moistened and the delirium stops by itself.

## Notes

Delirium from loss of fluids through excessive sweating is not excess heat, so instead of purging one harmonizes the ying-nutrient and wei-defense with Chaihu Guizhi tang and circulates the fluids, and recovery follows. This clause does not appear in the three yin and three yang chapters, and is the last paragraph of the chapter "Distinguishing Pulse, Signs, and Treatment of Diseases After Sweating."
