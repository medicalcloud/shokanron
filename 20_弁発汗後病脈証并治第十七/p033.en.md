# §33 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When sweating has been profuse and there is yang collapse with delirious speech, it must not be purged. Give Chaihu Guizhi tang to harmonize the nutritive and defensive and thereby free the fluids, and afterward it will recover by itself.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The stomach is the sea of water and grain and the master of the fluids. Profuse sweating loses the fluids, the stomach becomes dry, and there will certainly be delirious speech. This is not repletion heat, so it must not be purged. Give Chaihu Guizhi tang to harmonize the nutritive and defensive and make the fluids flow; when the fluids are generated, the stomach is moistened and the delirious speech stops by itself.

## Notes

Delirious speech from loss of fluids through excessive sweating is not repletion heat, so instead of purging one harmonizes the nutritive and defensive with Chaihu Guizhi tang and circulates the fluids, and recovery follows. This clause does not appear in the three yin and three yang chapters, and is the last paragraph of the chapter "Distinguishing Pulse, Signs, and Treatment of Diseases After Sweating."
