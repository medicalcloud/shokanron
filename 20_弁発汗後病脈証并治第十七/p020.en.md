# §20 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

After sweating, abdominal distension and fullness belong to Houpo Shengjiang Banxia Gancao Renshen tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 66 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
