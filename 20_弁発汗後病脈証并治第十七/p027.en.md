# §27 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In cold damage, after sweating has resolved it, when the stomach is not harmonious, with glomus and hardness below the heart, dry belching with the smell of food, water qi below the rib-sides, thunderous rumbling in the abdomen, and diarrhea: it belongs to Shengjiang Xiexin tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 157 of the lower taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
