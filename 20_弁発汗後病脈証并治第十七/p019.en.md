# §19 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

After sweating, when the person has palpitations below the navel, running piglet is about to occur; it belongs to Fuling Guizhi Gancao Dazao tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 65 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
