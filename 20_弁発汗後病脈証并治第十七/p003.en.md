# §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

After sweating, drinking much water will certainly cause dyspnea; pouring water over [the body] will also cause dyspnea.

## Notes

The same content as the second half of Clause 75 of the middle Taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
