# §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yangming disease with originally spontaneous sweating, which the physician again sweated repeatedly; the disease has recovered, but slight vexation persists without clearing up—this is certainly because the stool is hard. Because fluids were lost and the stomach is dry, the stool is made hard. One should ask how many times a day the person urinates. If originally three or four times a day and today twice, one knows the stool will come out before long. Now the frequency of urination has decreased because the fluids should return into the stomach; thus one knows there will certainly be a bowel movement before long.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 203 of the yangming chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
