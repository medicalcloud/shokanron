# §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When after taking Guizhi tang there is great sweating and the pulse is surging and large, give Guizhi tang as in the previous method. If the form resembles malaria with attacks twice a day, sweating will certainly resolve it; it belongs to Guizhi Er Mahuang Yi tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 25 of the upper taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
