# §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When sweating is profuse, or sweating has been induced repeatedly, yang is lost and there is delirious speech. With a short pulse it is fatal; when the pulse is spontaneously harmonious, it is not fatal.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 211 of the yangming chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
