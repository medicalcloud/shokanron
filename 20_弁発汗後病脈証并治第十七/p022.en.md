# §22 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

After sweating, aversion to cold is due to deficiency; no aversion to cold but only heat is excess, and the stomach qi should be harmonized; it belongs to Tiaowei Chengqi tang (Choijokito). (One method uses Xiao Chengqi tang (Shojokito).)

## Notes

The same content as Clause 70 of the middle Taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
