# §22 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

After sweating, aversion to cold is due to vacuity; no aversion to cold but only heat is repletion, and the stomach qi should be harmonized; it belongs to Tiaowei Chengqi tang. (One method uses Xiao Chengqi tang.)

## Notes

The same content as Clause 70 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
