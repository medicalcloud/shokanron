# §30 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease of three days not resolved by sweating, with steaming fever: it belongs to the stomach; it belongs to Tiaowei Chengqi tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 248 of the yangming chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
