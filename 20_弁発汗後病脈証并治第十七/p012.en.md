# §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When after taking Guizhi tang and great sweating there are great vexation and unresolved thirst, and the pulse is surging and large, it belongs to Baihu jia Renshen tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 26 of the upper taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
