# §31 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When after great sweating the heat does not leave, with internal tension, pain of the four limbs, and also diarrhea, cold limbs, and aversion to cold: it belongs to Sini tang (Shigyakuto).

## Notes

The same content as Clause 353 of the Jueyin chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
