# §25 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease with an external pattern not yet resolved must not be purged; purging it is adverse.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

When the exterior has not yet resolved, even if there is an interior pattern, it must not be purged; first resolving the external is the proper order. If, on the contrary, it is purged, it is adverse. The classic says: "Where sweating should be induced, purging it again is adverse; if sweating is induced first, the treatment is not adverse."

## Notes

The same content as Clause 44 of the middle taiyang chapter. It states the principle of not purging while an exterior pattern remains.
