# §24 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

One who is ill and wants to vomit must not be purged.

## Notes

A short clause stating that wanting to vomit means the evil is in the chest (above), so purgation is unsuitable. Cheng Wuji's commentary appears in the latter half of the commentary on paragraph 17 ("because the evil is still in the chest"). It has the same purport as Clause 204 of the yangming chapter: "In cold damage with much retching, even if there is a yangming pattern, it must not be attacked."
