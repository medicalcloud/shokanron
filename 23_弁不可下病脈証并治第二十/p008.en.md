# §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

All who are deficient must not be purged; purging causes great thirst. Those who seek water recover easily; those who dislike water become severe.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The *Jingui Yuhan* says: "For the deficient, supplement ten times and do not drain even once." Purging those of deficient constitution is double deficiency, exhausting the fluids within, so it causes great thirst. Those who seek water still have yang qi not exhausted and can still recover; those who dislike water have yang qi already exhausted and are hard to control.

## Notes

The clause states that purging the deficient loses fluids and causes great thirst, and that the remaining yang qi and prognosis are judged by whether water is sought or disliked.
