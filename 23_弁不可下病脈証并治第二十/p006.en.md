# §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the throat is blocked (written "閉寒"; "寒" is thought to be an error for "塞"), it must not be purged. Purging it makes the upper part light and the lower heavy; water and drink do not go down; when lying one wants to curl up; the body is tense and painful; and there is diarrhea dozens of times a day.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Blockage of the throat means the stomach is already not harmonious. Purging it lightens the blocking evil above but further damages the stomach qi, making the lower part heavy, until water and drink do not go down, one wants to curl up when lying, the body is tense and painful, and there is diarrhea dozens of times a day. One knows it is vacuity cold. [The commentary's "巳" is read as "已."]

## Notes

Pairing with paragraph 10 of the "Must Not Be Sweated" chapter, the clause states the prohibition of purging one whose throat is blocked. The base's "閉寒" is considered an error for "閉塞," as Cheng Wuji's commentary also has "閉塞."
