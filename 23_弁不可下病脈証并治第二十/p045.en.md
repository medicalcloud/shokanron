# §45 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yangming disease with hardness and fullness below the heart must not be attacked. If attacked and the diarrhea does not stop, it is fatal; if the diarrhea stops, there is recovery.

## Notes

The same text as Clause 205 of the yangming chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
