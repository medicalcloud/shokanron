# §39 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Visceral binding without yang signs, without alternating chills and fever, the person, on the contrary, quiet, and the tongue fur slippery: it must not be attacked.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 130 of the lower taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
