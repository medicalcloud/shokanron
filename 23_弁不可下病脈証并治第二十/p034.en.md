# §34 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In cold damage of five or six days, when there is no chest binding, the abdomen is soft, the pulse is vacuous, and there is also reversal, it must not be purged. This is blood collapse; purging it is fatal.

## Notes

The same text as Clause 347 of the jueyin chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
