# §33 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyin disease: when food and drink enter the mouth they are vomited; there is queasiness in the heart with a desire to vomit, yet one cannot vomit. When just contracted, with cold hands and feet and a wiry slow pulse, this is repletion in the chest and must not be purged.

## Notes

The same content as the first half of Clause 324 of the shaoyin chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
