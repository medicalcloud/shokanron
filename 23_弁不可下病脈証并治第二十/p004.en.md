# §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the stirring qi is above, it must not be purged; purging it causes heat and vexation in the palms, floating cold on the body surface, spontaneous leakage of hot sweat, and a desire to obtain water and pour it over oneself.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Stirring qi above is the stirring of the heart. Purging it damages the stomach and stirs the heart qi within. The heart is fire and governs heat. The *Zhenjing* says: "Diseases produced by the heart include heat in the palms." The liver is the yin among the viscera; when diseased, although there is body heat, when lying one wants to curl up—this is exterior heat with interior cold. The heart is the yang among the viscera; when diseased, there are floating cold on the body surface, spontaneous leakage of hot sweat, and a desire to obtain water and pour it over oneself—this is exterior cold with interior heat. The yin and yang, cold and heat of the two viscera can be clearly seen.

## Notes

The clause states the prohibition of purging one with stirring qi above the navel (heart). Cheng Wuji contrasts it with the previous paragraph (liver): the liver produces exterior heat with interior cold, the heart exterior cold with interior heat.
