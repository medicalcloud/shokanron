# §42 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In cold damage or wind strike, when the physician, on the contrary, purged, the person has diarrhea dozens of times a day, grain is not transformed, the abdomen rumbles like thunder, there are glomus, hardness, and fullness below the heart, dry retching, and heart vexation without rest. The physician, seeing the glomus below the heart, says the disease is not exhausted and purges again, and the glomus becomes worse. This is not bound heat; it is only because the stomach is vacuous and guest qi counterflows upward that it is made hard. It belongs to Gancao Xiexin tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 158 of the lower taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
