# §46 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yangming disease with spontaneous sweating, or after sweating, with spontaneously free urination: the fluids are exhausted within, and although [the stool] is hard, it must not be attacked. Wait until one spontaneously wants to defecate, and it is appropriate to guide and free it with a honey suppository. Tuguagen and pig bile can also all be used as guides.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 233 of the yangming chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found. It is the last paragraph of the chapter "Distinguishing Pulse, Signs, and Treatment of Diseases That Must Not Be Purged."
