# §20 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the chest-binding pattern, when the pulse is floating and large, it must not be purged; purging it causes immediate death.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 132 of the lower taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
