# §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the stirring qi is on the right, it must not be purged; purging it exhausts the fluids within, with dry throat, dry nose, dizziness, and palpitations of the heart.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Stirring qi on the right is the stirring of the lung. Purging it damages the stomach and stirs the lung, and the fluids are exhausted within. Dry throat and dry nose are because the lung belongs to metal and governs dryness. Dizziness and palpitations of the heart are because the lung governs qi and [it] is vacuous.

## Notes

Paragraphs 2–5 pair with paragraphs 6–9 of the "Must Not Be Sweated" chapter, forming a group describing the harm of purging one with stirring qi around the navel. This paragraph describes stirring qi to the right of the navel (lung).
