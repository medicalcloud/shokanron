# §17 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the pulse is floating and large, sweating should be induced; if the physician, on the contrary, purges it, this is a great adverse treatment.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Floating and large belong to the exterior, so it must not be purged. One who is ill and wants to vomit must not be purged.

Because the evil is still in the chest.

## Notes

The clause warns that using purgation for an exterior-pattern pulse (floating and large) is a great adverse treatment. It is also quoted as "the classic says" in Cheng Wuji's commentary on paragraph 28 of the "Distinguishing the Pulse" chapter. The part of Cheng Wuji's commentary from "one who is ill and wants to vomit must not be purged" onward is thought to be the commentary on paragraph 24 ("one who is ill and wants to vomit must not be purged") placed here.
