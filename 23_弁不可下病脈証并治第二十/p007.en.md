# §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

All with external repletion must not be purged; purging causes slight fever. When the pulse is lost with reversal, there will be heat when grasping the navel.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

External repletion is exterior heat. Sweating cures it; purging is adverse. After purgation the interior is vacuous and the exterior heat sinks inward, so there is slight fever. When the reversal is deep, the heat is also deep. When the pulse is lost with reversal, the yang qi has sunk deeply and lodges in the lower burner, so there is heat when grasping the navel.

## Notes

The clause states that when one with exterior repletion is purged, the exterior heat sinks inward, so that even when the pulse cannot be felt and the limbs are cold, heat is trapped at the navel. "斉" is understood to mean "臍" (navel).
