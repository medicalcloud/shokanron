# §15 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the pulse is rapid and remains rapid for a long time without stopping, and when it stops the evil binds and the upright qi cannot recover, the upright qi instead binds in the viscera, so the evil qi floats it, matching with the skin and hair. When the pulse is rapid, it must not be purged; purging it will certainly cause vexation and unceasing diarrhea.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Rapid is heat. When it stops, the evil qi binds between the channels and network vessels, the upright qi cannot move again to the exterior and instead binds in the viscera, and the evil qi alone floats in the skin and hair. Purging it depletes the interior; the evil heat takes advantage of the vacuity and enters; the vacuous interior carries heat, and there will certainly be vexation and unceasing diarrhea. [The commentary's "叶熱" is read as "協熱" (heat-associated).]

## Notes

The clause states that purging one with a rapid (heat) pulse whose evil floats in the skin and hair lets the evil heat enter the interior, producing vexation and diarrhea. It is not found in the three yin and three yang chapters.
