# §15 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the pulse is rapid and remains rapid for a long time without stopping, and when it stops the pathogen binds and the healthy qi cannot recover, the healthy qi instead binds in the zang organs, so the pathogenic qi floats it, matching with the skin and hair. When the pulse is rapid, it must not be purged; purging it will certainly cause vexation and unceasing diarrhea.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Rapid is heat. When it stops, the pathogenic qi binds between the meridians and collaterals, the healthy qi cannot move again to the exterior and instead binds in the zang organs, and the pathogenic qi alone floats in the skin and hair. Purging it depletes the interior; the pathogenic heat takes advantage of the deficiency and enters; the deficient interior carries heat, and there will certainly be vexation and unceasing diarrhea. [The commentary's "叶熱" is read as "協熱" (heat-associated).]

## Notes

The clause states that purging one with a rapid (heat) pulse whose pathogen floats in the skin and hair lets the pathogenic heat enter the interior, producing vexation and diarrhea. It is not found in the three yin and three yang chapters.
