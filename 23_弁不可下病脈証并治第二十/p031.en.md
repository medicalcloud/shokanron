# §31 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyin disease is abdominal fullness with vomiting, food not going down, spontaneous diarrhea that becomes increasingly severe, and periodic spontaneous abdominal pain. Purging it will certainly cause binding and hardness below the chest.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 273 of the taiyin chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
