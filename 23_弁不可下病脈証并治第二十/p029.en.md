# §29 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When there is original vacuity and the heat is attacked, there will certainly be hiccup.

## Notes

A short clause with the same purport as Clause 194 of the yangming chapter ("yangming disease with inability to eat: attacking the heat will certainly cause hiccup, because the stomach is vacuous and cold").
