# §29 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When there is original deficiency and the heat is attacked, there will certainly be hiccup.

## Notes

A short clause with the same purport as Clause 194 of the Yangming chapter ("Yangming disease with inability to eat: attacking the heat will certainly cause hiccup, because the stomach is deficient and cold").
