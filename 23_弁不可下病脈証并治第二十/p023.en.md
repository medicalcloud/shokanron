# §23 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

All cases of counterflow of the four limbs and reversal must not be purged; the same applies to those of vacuous constitution.

## Notes

The same text as Clause 330 of the jueyin chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
