# §28 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In disease where yang predominates there is heat; purging it causes hardness.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

When yang-heat signs predominate, the fluids are scant. Purging may remove the heat but further damages the fluids, so the stool will certainly be difficult. Some say that "yang predominating" means exterior heat, and purging it causes hardness below the heart.

## Notes

A short clause stating that purging one with predominant yang heat damages the fluids and makes the stool hard (or makes the area below the heart hard). Cheng Wuji presents both interpretations.
