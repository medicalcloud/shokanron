# §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the stirring qi is below, it must not be purged; purging it causes abdominal distension and fullness, dizziness on suddenly rising, diarrhea of undigested grain upon eating, and glomus below the heart.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Stirring qi below is the stirring of the kidney. Purging it damages the spleen and stirs the kidney qi; the kidney's cold assails the spleen, so there are the signs of abdominal fullness, dizziness, diarrhea of undigested grain, and glomus below the heart.

## Notes

The clause states the prohibition of purging one with stirring qi below the navel (kidney).
