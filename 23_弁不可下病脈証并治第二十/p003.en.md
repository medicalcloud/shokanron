# §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the stirring qi is on the left, it must not be purged; purging it causes internal tension of the abdomen, food not going down, and further intensification of the stirring qi; although there is body heat, when lying one wants to curl up.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Stirring qi on the left is the stirring of the liver. Purging it damages the spleen, and the liver qi prevails further and moves again into the spleen, so there are internal tension of the abdomen, food not going down, and further intensification of the stirring qi. Although there is body heat, because the interior qi is insufficient, when lying one wants to curl up.

## Notes

The clause states the prohibition of purging one with stirring qi to the left of the navel (liver).
