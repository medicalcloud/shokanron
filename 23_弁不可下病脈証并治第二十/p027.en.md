# §27 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the disease has a floating tight pulse and it is purged again, the tightness, on the contrary, enters the interior and forms glomus.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 151 of the lower taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
