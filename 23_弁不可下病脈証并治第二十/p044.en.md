# §44 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yangming disease with the whole body red must not be attacked; [if attacked] there will certainly be fever, and when the color turns yellow, urination is inhibited.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 206 of the yangming chapter (Clause 206 reads "the whole face red"). Cheng Wuji's commentary is found there.
