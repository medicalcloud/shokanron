# §43 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Diarrhea with a large pulse is vacuity, because it was forcibly purged. If the pulse is floating and drumskin and there is consequently rumbling of the intestines, it belongs to Danggui Sini tang.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

A large pulse is vacuity. It was purged before it should have been, so the diarrhea consequently does not stop. Floating means insufficient on pressure. Drumskin means replete, large, long, and slightly wiry. Floating is vacuity; drumskin is cold. When cold and vacuity contend, the intestines rumble. Danggui Sini tang is given to supplement vacuity and dissipate cold.

## Notes

The clause uses Danggui Sini tang for vacuity-cold diarrhea from mistaken purgation with a floating drumskin pulse and intestinal rumbling. It is not found in the three yin and three yang chapters.
