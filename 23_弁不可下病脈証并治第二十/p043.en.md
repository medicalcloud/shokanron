# §43 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Diarrhea with a large pulse is deficiency, because it was forcibly purged. If the pulse is floating and tympanic and there is consequently rumbling of the intestines, it belongs to Danggui Sini tang (Tokishigyakuto).

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

A large pulse is deficiency. It was purged before it should have been, so the diarrhea consequently does not stop. Floating means insufficient on pressure. Tympanic means excess, large, long, and slightly wiry. Floating is deficiency; tympanic is cold. When cold and deficiency contend, the intestines rumble. Danggui Sini tang is given to supplement deficiency and dissipate cold.

## Notes

The clause uses Danggui Sini tang for deficiency-cold diarrhea from mistaken purgation with a floating tympanic pulse and intestinal rumbling. It is not found in the three yin and three yang chapters.
