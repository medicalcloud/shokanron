# §19 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Dual-yang combination disease: when taiyang first contracted the disease, sweating was induced, but the sweat came out first without thoroughness, and so it shifted to belong to the yangming, with continuing spontaneous slight sweating and no aversion to cold. If the taiyang pattern has not ceased, it must not be purged; purging it is adverse.

## Notes

The same content as the first half of Clause 48 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
