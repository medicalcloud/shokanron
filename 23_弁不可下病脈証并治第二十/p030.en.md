# §30 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When there is no yang and yin is strong, with hard stool, purging it will certainly cause diarrhea of undigested grain and abdominal fullness.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

"No yang" means loss of fluids; "yin strong" means much cold. Hard stool is yin bind. Purging it makes the stomach vacuous, and yin cold becomes severe within, so there will certainly be diarrhea of undigested grain and abdominal fullness.

## Notes

The clause states that purging constipation from cold (yin bind) intensifies stomach vacuity cold, producing diarrhea of undigested grain and abdominal fullness. It relates to the explanation of yin bind in paragraph 2 of the "Distinguishing the Pulse" chapter.
