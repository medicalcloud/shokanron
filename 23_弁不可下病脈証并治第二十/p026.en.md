# §26 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the disease arises in yang and, on the contrary, it is purged, heat enters and thereby forms chest bind. When the disease arises in yin and, on the contrary, it is purged, stuffiness is thereby formed.

## Notes

The same content as the first half of Clause 131 of the lower Taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
