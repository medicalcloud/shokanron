# §13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The pulse is floating and tight. Floating means wind; tight means cold. Wind damages the wei-defense; cold damages the ying-nutrient. When both the ying-nutrient and the wei-defense are diseased, there is vexing pain in the bones and joints; sweating should be induced, and it must not be purged.

## Notes

Nearly the same text as paragraph 23 of the "Distinguishing the Pulse" chapter, here in the form of a prohibition of purgation. Cheng Wuji's commentary is in the "Distinguishing the Pulse" chapter.
