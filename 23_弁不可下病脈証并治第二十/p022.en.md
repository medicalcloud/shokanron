# §22 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Combined disease of taiyang and shaoyang with hardness below the heart and stiff neck and nape with dizziness must not be purged.

## Notes

Corresponds to Clause 171 of the lower taiyang chapter ("dual disease of taiyang and shaoyang with hardness below the heart and stiff neck and nape with dizziness: needle Dazhui, Feishu, and Ganshu; be careful not to purge"). Here it is called "combined disease." Cheng Wuji's commentary is on Clause 171.
