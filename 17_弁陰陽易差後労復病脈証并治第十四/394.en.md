# Clause 394 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When after recovery from cold damage there is fever again, Xiao Chaihu tang governs it. When the pulse is floating, resolve it by sweating; when the pulse is sunken and replete (one version writes "tight"), resolve it by purgation. Formula 3.

Chaihu 8 liang; renshen 2 liang; huangqin 2 liang; gancao 2 liang (mix-fried); shengjiang 2 liang; banxia half a sheng (washed); dazao 12 pieces (broken).

Boil the seven ingredients above in 1 dou 2 sheng of water to obtain 6 sheng, remove the dregs, decoct again to obtain 3 sheng, and take 1 sheng warm, three times a day.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

When after recovery the residual heat is not yet exhausted and there is fever again, Xiao Chaihu tang is given to harmonize and resolve it. A floating pulse means the heat is in the exterior, so it is resolved by sweating. A sunken pulse means the heat is in the interior, so it is resolved by purgation.

## Notes

For fever recurring after illness, one first harmonizes with Xiao Chaihu tang, and selects treatment by pulse: sweating if floating, purgation if sunken and replete. In this clause's Xiao Chaihu tang, renshen, huangqin, gancao, and shengjiang are 2 liang each, differing from the 3 liang of Clause 96 and elsewhere.
