# Clause 398 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the patient's pulse has already resolved but there is slight vexation at dusk, it is because the illness has just recovered, people forced grain on the patient, and the spleen and stomach qi, still weak, cannot digest the grain, thus causing slight vexation. Reducing the grain cures it.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The yangming is in its prime at shen, you, and xu. Because there is abiding food in the stomach, there is slight vexation at dusk. One should purge slightly to reduce the abiding grain.

## Notes

The last clause of the main text of the *Shanghan lun* (the chapters on the six channel diseases, sudden turmoil, and post-recovery). It closes with a precept of convalescent care: diet after illness should be restrained according to the recovery of stomach qi. Cheng Wuji says one should purge slightly, but the text states that "reducing grain" (eating less) cures it.
