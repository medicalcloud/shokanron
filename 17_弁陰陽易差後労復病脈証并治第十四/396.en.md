# Clause 396 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When after recovery from a major illness there is frequent spitting that does not clear up for a long time, there is cold above the chest, and it should be warmed with pills; Lizhong wan is appropriate. Formula 5.

Renshen, baizhu, gancao (mix-fried), and ganjiang, 3 liang each.

Pound and sift the four ingredients above, mix with honey to make pills about the size of an egg yolk. Mix one pill with several ge of boiling water, grind it, and take warm, three times a day.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

After sweating, yang qi is insufficient and the stomach is vacuous and cold, unable to take in the fluids, so there is frequent spitting that does not clear up. Lizhong wan is given to warm the stomach.

## Notes

Frequent, persistent thin spittle after illness is attributed to vacuity cold of the spleen and stomach being unable to control water, and Lizhong wan warms it gently. Pills are said to be more suitable than decoctions for long-term convalescent care.
