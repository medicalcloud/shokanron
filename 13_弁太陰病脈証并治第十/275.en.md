# Clause 275 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The time when taiyin disease tends to resolve is from hai to chou.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The spleen is yin earth and is in its prime at chou. Hai and zi turn toward yang, so it is called the time of resolution.

## Notes

One of the six channels' "times of resolution." Hai to chou corresponds roughly to 9 p.m.–3 a.m., the period when yin reaches its extreme and begins to turn to yang.
