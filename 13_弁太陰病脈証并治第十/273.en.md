# Clause 273 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyin disease is abdominal fullness with vomiting, food not going down, spontaneous diarrhea that becomes increasingly severe, and periodic spontaneous abdominal pain. If it is purged, there will certainly be binding and hardness below the chest.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Taiyin disease is yang evil transmitted into the interior. The taiyin vessel spreads through the stomach. The evil qi congests and becomes abdominal fullness; when what is above cannot descend, there are vomiting and food not going down; when what is below cannot ascend, spontaneous diarrhea becomes increasingly severe, with periodic spontaneous abdominal pain. When yin obstruction within causes abdominal pain, the pain is constant. This is yang evil invading the interior, so although there is pain it is not constant, but only periodic spontaneous abdominal pain. If it is purged, the yin evil remains below the chest and forms binding and hardness. The classic says: "When the disease arises in the yin and, on the contrary, it is purged, glomus is thereby produced."

## Notes

Regarded as the outline clause of taiyin disease. Abdominal fullness, vomiting, food not going down, diarrhea, and periodic abdominal pain are generally explained as due to vacuity of spleen yang with cold-damp stagnating in the middle burner. Mistaking the fullness for repletion and purging is said to further damage the middle burner and cause glomus and hardness below the chest (below the heart), so purgation is warned against. Cheng Wuji explains it as "yang evil transmitted into the interior."
