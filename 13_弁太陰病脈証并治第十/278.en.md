# Clause 278 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cold damage with a floating slowdown pulse and spontaneously warm hands and feet is tied to the Taiyin. Taiyin should produce yellowing of the body, but if urination is spontaneously free, yellowing cannot arise. By the seventh or eighth day, even with sudden vexation and diarrhea more than ten times a day, it will certainly stop of itself, because the spleen is in excess and the putrid filth should depart.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

When Taiyin disease reaches the seventh or eighth day with hard stool, the Taiyin has entered the bowel and been transmitted to the Yangming. Now when by the seventh or eighth day there are sudden vexation and diarrhea more than ten times, the spleen is in excess and the putrid filth is departing. One with diarrhea and vexation-agitation dies, [it is said,] but here the spleen qi is harmonized and expels the pathogen, draining it downward; so even with sudden vexation and diarrhea more than ten times a day, the diarrhea will certainly stop of itself.

## Notes

In the course of Taiyin disease, when spleen yang recovers, turbid matter in the body may be discharged as diarrhea and the disease cures itself. "The spleen is in excess" here is generally interpreted not as pathogen excess but as recovery of spleen qi. The first half on jaundice is almost identical to Clause 187 in the Yangming chapter.
