# Clause 280 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In taiyin disease, when the pulse is weak and the person continues to have spontaneously free bowels, even if dahuang and shaoyao should be used, they should be reduced, because the person's stomach qi is weak and easily stirred. (For diarrhea, first boil the shaoyao three times.)

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Abdominal fullness and pain are taiyin disease. When the pulse is weak and the person continues to have spontaneously free bowels, although the evil is in the interior it has not yet formed great repletion. One who wishes to give dahuang and shaoyao to attack the fullness and pain should give only a little, because the stomach qi is still weak and easily stirred into diarrhea.

## Notes

Even when using Guizhi jia Shaoyao tang or Guizhi jia Dahuang tang from the previous clause, the amounts of dahuang and shaoyao should be reduced for weak persons with a weak pulse and loose stool. It suggests that shaoyao was also considered to loosen the bowels.
