# Clause 279 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When a disease originally Taiyang is, on the contrary, purged by the physician, and as a result there are abdominal fullness and periodic pain, it belongs to the Taiyin; Guizhi jia Shaoyao tang (Keishikashakuyakuto) governs it. When there are great excess and pain, Guizhi jia Dahuang tang (Keishikadaioto) governs it. 3.

Guizhi jia Shaoyao tang formula:

Guizhi (keishi) 3 liang (remove the bark); shaoyao (shakuyaku) 6 liang; gancao (kanzo) 2 liang (mix-fried); dazao (taiso) 12 pieces (broken); shengjiang (shokyo) 3 liang (cut).

Boil the five ingredients above in 7 sheng of water to obtain 3 sheng, remove the dregs, and take warm in three divided doses. Originally called Guizhi tang (Keishito); now shaoyao is added.

Guizhi jia Dahuang tang formula:

Guizhi 3 liang (remove the bark); dahuang (daio) 2 liang; shaoyao 6 liang; shengjiang 3 liang (cut); gancao 2 liang (mix-fried); dazao 12 pieces (broken).

Boil the six ingredients above in 7 sheng of water to obtain 3 sheng, remove the dregs, and take 1 sheng warm, three times a day.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

When the exterior pathogen has not yet ceased and the physician purges, the pathogen takes advantage of the deficiency and is transmitted to the Taiyin; the interior qi is not harmonized, so there are abdominal fullness and periodic pain. Guizhi tang is given to resolve the exterior, with shaoyao added to harmonize the interior.

Great excess and great fullness may of course be removed by purgation; therefore dahuang is added to purge the great excess.

## Notes

The clause describes treatment of abdominal fullness and pain when mistaken purgation of Taiyang disease has extended the disease to the Taiyin.

- Guizhi jia Shaoyao tang is Guizhi tang with double shaoyao, said to relax abdominal pain (spasm).
- When the pain is strong with a tendency to excess ("great excess and pain"), dahuang is further added to free the bowels.
- "Originally called Guizhi tang; now shaoyao is added" is a standard Song-edition note showing the original formula name and the modification.
