# Clause 277 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Spontaneous diarrhea without thirst belongs to the taiyin, because its viscus has cold. It should be warmed; it is appropriate to take formulas of the Sini type. 2.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Spontaneous diarrhea with thirst belongs to the shaoyin, with cold in the lower burner. Spontaneous diarrhea without thirst belongs to the taiyin, with cold in the middle burner. Sini tang and similar decoctions are given to warm its viscus. [The commentary's "目利" is read as an error for "自利" (spontaneous diarrhea).]

## Notes

The clause shows the characteristic of taiyin diarrhea (no thirst), its mechanism (vacuity cold of the spleen viscus), and its treatment (warming). "Formulas of the Sini type" is understood not as one specific formula but as the class of interior-warming formulas such as Lizhong tang (pill) and Sini tang.
