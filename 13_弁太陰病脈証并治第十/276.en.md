# Clause 276 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyin disease with a floating pulse may be sweated; Guizhi tang is appropriate. Formula 1.

Guizhi 3 liang (remove the bark); shaoyao 3 liang; gancao 2 liang (mix-fried); shengjiang 3 liang (cut); dazao 12 pieces (broken).

Boil the five ingredients above in 7 sheng of water to obtain 3 sheng, remove the dregs, and take 1 sheng warm. After a short while, sip 1 sheng of hot thin gruel to assist the power of the medicine, and cover warmly to obtain sweating.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The classic says: "Floating means [the disease] is in the exterior; sunken means it is in the interior." Taiyin disease with a floating pulse means the evil is in the channel; therefore it should be dissipated by sweating.

## Notes

Even in taiyin disease, if the pulse is floating and the exterior pattern predominates, the exterior may be resolved with Guizhi tang. It is often explained that Guizhi tang, containing shengjiang, dazao, and gancao which regulate the spleen and stomach, is easy to use even in those with interior vacuity.
