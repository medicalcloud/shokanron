# §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the yang pulse is floating, large, and soft, and the yin pulse is floating, large, and soft, and the yin and yang pulses are equal, it is called moderate.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The yang pulse is the cun kou; the yin pulse is the chi. When above and below are equal with no one-sided prevalence, the qi of yin and yang is harmonious and moderate—unlike the slow-moderate pulse that has evil. One-sided prevalence of yin or yang produces bound or skipping; contention of yin and yang produces stirred; harmony of yin and yang qi produces moderate. Learners must know this.

## Notes

The clause defines "moderate" as the cun and chi pulses being equally relaxed and soft, a pulse of harmony between yin and yang. It is distinguished from moderate as a disease pulse (moderate close to slow).
