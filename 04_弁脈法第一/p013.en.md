# §13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The pulse is wiry and large. Wiry means reduction; large means hollow. Reduction means cold; hollow means deficiency. When cold and deficiency contend, it is called tympanic. In women there are half-term birth (miscarriage) and metrostaxis; in men, blood loss and seminal loss.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Wiry means reduction, and reduction means cold; cold means scant yang qi. Large means hollow, and hollow means deficiency; deficiency means scant, insufficient blood. What is called tympanic means that when there is both cold and deficiency, qi and blood are altered and do not follow their normal measure. In a man who has it, true yang is reduced and cannot secure the interior, so it governs blood loss and seminal loss. In a woman who has it, yin blood is deficient and cannot nourish, so it governs miscarriage and metrostaxis.

## Notes

The clause defines the "tympanic pulse," taut and hard outside (wiry) but empty inside (hollow), as indicating declining yang qi and insufficiency of essence and blood. Almost the same text appears in the *Jingui Yaolüe* chapter on blood impediment and deficiency/consumption, among others.
