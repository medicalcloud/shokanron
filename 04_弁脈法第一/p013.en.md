# §13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The pulse is wiry and large. Wiry means reduction; large means scallion-stalk. Reduction means cold; scallion-stalk means vacuity. When cold and vacuity contend, it is called drumskin. In women there are half-term birth (miscarriage) and spotting; in men, blood loss and seminal loss.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Wiry means reduction, and reduction means cold; cold means scant yang qi. Large means scallion-stalk, and scallion-stalk means vacuity; vacuity means scant, insufficient blood. What is called drumskin means that when there is both cold and vacuity, qi and blood are altered and do not follow their normal measure. In a man who has it, true yang is reduced and cannot secure the interior, so it governs blood loss and seminal loss. In a woman who has it, yin blood is vacuous and cannot nourish, so it governs miscarriage and spotting.

## Notes

The clause defines the "drumskin pulse," taut and hard outside (wiry) but empty inside (scallion-stalk), as indicating declining yang qi and insufficiency of essence and blood. Almost the same text appears in the *Jingui Yaolüe* chapter on blood impediment and vacuity taxation, among others.
