# §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

A pulse that flickers like fat floating on broth means the yang qi is faint.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

It is light and floating, with faint yang.

## Notes

A pulse that is light, floating, and forceless like fat floating on the surface of broth indicates weak yang qi.
