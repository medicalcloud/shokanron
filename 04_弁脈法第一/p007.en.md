# §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

A pulse that is entwined like a spider's thread means the yang qi is debilitated. (One version says "yin qi.")

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

*Yingying* means stagnant—like being entangled and caught, not flowing freely. "Like a spider's thread" means extremely fine. Feeble is feebleness of yang; thready is debility of yang. The *Maiyao* says: "Feeble means qi stuffiness"—it has not yet reached debility. The *Neijing* says: "Thready means scant qi." That extreme fineness is taken as debility of yang is fitting.

## Notes

An extremely thready pulse like a spider's thread indicates debility of yang qi. One version has "yin qi," which relates to the view that a thready pulse indicates insufficiency of yin blood.
