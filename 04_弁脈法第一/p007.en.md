# §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

A pulse that is entwined like a spider's thread means the yang qi is debilitated. (One version says "yin qi.")

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

*Yingying* means stagnant—like being entangled and caught, not flowing freely. "Like a spider's thread" means extremely fine. Faint is faintness of yang; fine is debility of yang. The *Maiyao* says: "Faint means qi glomus"—it has not yet reached debility. The *Neijing* says: "Fine means scant qi." That extreme fineness is taken as debility of yang is fitting.

## Notes

An extremely fine pulse like a spider's thread indicates debility of yang qi. One version has "yin qi," which relates to the view that a fine pulse indicates insufficiency of yin blood.
