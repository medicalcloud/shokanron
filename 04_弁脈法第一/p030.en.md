# §30 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When all the pulses are floating and rapid, there should be fever with shivering aversion to cold. If there is a painful spot and eating and drinking are as usual, there is accumulated pus.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

A floating rapid pulse governs the evil being in the channels, and there should be fever with shivering aversion to cold. When the patient's whole body aches and there is no desire to eat or drink, it is cold damage. If, although there are fever and aversion to cold, the pain is localized in one place and eating and drinking are as usual, it is not cold damage; the evil qi is depressed and bound between the channels and network vessels, blood and qi are congested and blocked, and they are about to gather and form a welling-abscess with pus.

## Notes

Even with a floating rapid pulse and aversion to cold with fever, if the pain is confined to one spot and appetite is preserved, it is distinguished as an abscess rather than cold damage. A similar clause appears in the *Jingui Yaolüe* chapter on sores and abscesses.
