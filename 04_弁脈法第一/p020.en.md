# §20 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Question: In general, how can one know when a disease was contracted and when it will recover? Answer: Suppose one contracts the disease at midnight; it recovers at midday the next day. One who contracts it at midday recovers at midnight. Why so? Contracting it at midday and recovering at midnight is because yang resolves when it obtains yin; contracting it at midnight and recovering at midday the next day is because yin resolves when it obtains yang.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

One who contracts the disease at midday receives it in yang; one who contracts it at midnight receives it in yin. When yang is not harmonized, it is harmonized by obtaining yin, so it resolves at midnight; when yin is not harmonized, it is harmonized by obtaining yang, so it resolves at midday. The classic says: "Use yang to harmonize yin; use yin to harmonize yang."

## Notes

The clause shows a prognostic idea based on the complementarity of yin and yang: a disease recovers at the time of the opposite polarity from its onset (day = yang, night = yin).
