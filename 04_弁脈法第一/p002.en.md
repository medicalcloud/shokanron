# §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Question: When the pulse shows yang bind and yin bind, how are they distinguished? Answer: When the pulse is floating and rapid, one can eat, and there is no bowel movement, this is repletion and is called yang bind; it should become severe on the seventeenth day. When the pulse is sunken and slow, one cannot eat, the body is heavy, and the stool is, on the contrary, hard (pronounced like 鞕; likewise below), it is called yin bind; it should become severe on the fourteenth day.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

"Bind" means qi is one-sidedly bound and solidified, so that the qi of yin and yang cannot intermingle. There is yang within yin and yin within yang; intermingling of yin and yang is harmony, failure to intermingle is binding. Floating and rapid are yang pulses; being able to eat but having no bowel movement is interior repletion; yang qi is bound and solidified and yin cannot intermingle with it, so it is called yang bind. Sunken and slow are yin pulses; being unable to eat and a heavy body are yin disease. When a yin pulse is seen in yin disease there should be diarrhea; now the stool is hard because yin qi is bound and solidified and yang cannot intermingle with it, so it is called yin bind. As for the number of days: in cold damage disease, day 1 taiyang, day 2 yangming, day 3 shaoyang, day 4 taiyin, day 5 shaoyin, day 6 jueyin; on day 6 the transmission through the channels is complete, and on day 7 it should recover. When it does not recover on day 7, it is called repeated transmission through the channels—that is, it is transmitted again from taiyang, and by day 12 again reaches jueyin, completing the transmission, and on day 13 it should recover. When it does not recover on day 13, it is called passing the channels—that is, after the repeated transmission it passes the taiyang channel and is again transmitted in sequence. Yang bind is fire; on day 17 it is transmitted to shaoyin water; water can restrain fire, so when the fire evil resolves and dissipates there is recovery. Yin bind belongs to water; on day 14 it is transmitted to yangming earth; earth can restrain water, so when the water evil resolves and dissipates there is recovery. If the binding of the evil qi is severe, and water cannot restrain fire nor earth restrain water, it should become severe. The *Neijing* says: "One period late, there is disease; two periods late, the disease is severe; three periods late, the disease is critical."

## Notes

The clause divides constipation into "yang bind," arising from heat repletion, and "yin bind," arising from bound cold, distinguished by pulse and signs. Cheng Wuji explains the days of worsening (seventeen and fourteen) by the transmission through the six channels and the five-phase restraint cycle, but views on the basis of these day counts vary.
