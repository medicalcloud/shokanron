# §18 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Question: To know from the pulse whether a disease will recover or not, how is it distinguished? Answer: When at the three places—cun kou, guan, and chi—the size, floating or sunken, and slow or rapid are equal, even if cold and heat have not resolved, the pulse shows yin and yang in harmony and balance, and even if severe it should recover.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

When the pulses of the three positions are even, the upright qi is already harmonized; even if residual evil remains, what harm can it do? [The commentary's "巳和" is read as "已和."]

## Notes

A prognostic clause stating that if the pulses of the three positions—cun, guan, and chi—are balanced, the upright qi is harmonized, so recovery will occur even if the symptoms are severe.
