# §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

A pulse that is luxuriant like a carriage canopy is called yang bind. (One version says "autumn pulse.")

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Luxuriant like a carriage canopy means large and soft and fluttering. It is yang qi depressed and bound on the outside, not harmonizing and intermingling with yin qi.

## Notes

Paragraphs 4–8 are regarded as a group likening the shape of the pulse to familiar things to indicate yang bind, yin bind, faint yang qi, debilitated yang qi, and blood loss. This paragraph likens a large, swelling, floating pulse to the canopy of a carriage.
