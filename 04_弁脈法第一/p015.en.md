# §15 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Question: Why is it that some diseases resolve through sweating without shivering? Answer: Because the pulse is large, floating, and rapid, one knows it will resolve through sweating without shivering.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

When yang prevails there is heat; when yin prevails there is cold; when yin and yang struggle there is shivering. Large, floating, and rapid pulses are all yang. When yang qi fully prevails and yin has nothing to struggle with, how could there be shivering?

## Notes

Continuing the previous paragraph, it states that one with ample upright qi resolves with sweating alone, without passing through shivering.
