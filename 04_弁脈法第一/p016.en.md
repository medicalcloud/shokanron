# §16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Question: Why is it that some diseases resolve without shivering or sweating? Answer: The pulse is spontaneously faint because there has already been sweating, or vomiting, or purgation, or blood loss, so there are no fluids within. Here yin and yang harmonize spontaneously and it will certainly recover by itself; therefore it resolves without shivering or sweating.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

A faint pulse means the evil qi is slight. The evil qi is already slight and the upright qi is also weak, so the pulse is faint. Having already undergone sweating, vomiting, or purgation, losing yang and blood, with no fluids within, one cannot produce sweat, and recovers spontaneously through the harmonization of yin and yang qi. [The commentary's "巳微" is read as "已微."]

## Notes

Those whose fluids have become scant through sweating, vomiting, purgation, or bleeding lack the fluids to sweat, so they recover through spontaneous harmonization of yin and yang without shiver sweating or sweating.
