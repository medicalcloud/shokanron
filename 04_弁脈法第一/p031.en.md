# §31 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the pulse is floating and slow, the face is hot and red, and there is shivering and trembling, sweating should resolve it in six or seven days. If, on the contrary, there is fever, recovery is delayed. Slow means there is no yang, so one cannot produce sweat, and the body will certainly itch.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

A floating pulse with a hot red face means the evil qi floats outward in the exterior. A slow pulse with shivering and trembling means the original qi is insufficient. Six or seven days is when the transmission through the channels is complete and sweating should resolve it. If one should sweat but does not, and, on the contrary, has fever, the interior is vacuous and the fluids are not abundant, so sweat cannot be produced. Without sweating the evil has no way out, so recovery is delayed. Fever means the evil qi floats in the skin, which will certainly cause itching of the body. The classic says: "Because one cannot get a slight sweat, the body will certainly itch."

## Notes

With evil in the exterior but insufficient yang qi to sweat, recovery is delayed and itching of the skin arises. It is the same idea as "because one cannot get a slight sweat, the body will certainly itch" in Clause 23 of the taiyang chapter (the Guizhi Mahuang Geban tang clause).
