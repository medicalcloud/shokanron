# §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

A pulse that is strung like stroking a long pole is called yin bind. (One version says "summer pulse.")

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Strung like stroking a long pole means continuous and stiffly straight. It is yin qi depressed and bound inside, not harmonizing and intermingling with yang qi.

## Notes

A hard, straight, continuous pulse is likened to a long pole, indicating yin bind. It pairs with the previous paragraph.
