# §37 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cold damage with cough, counterflow, and rising qi: when the pulse is scattered, it is fatal, because the body is damaged.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The *Qianjin fang* says: "Panting with coughing is taken as cough, counterflow, and rising qi." [This is] lung disease. Scattered is the heart pulse; this is heart fire punishing lung metal. The *Neijing* says: "[A disease passing] from the heart to the lung is called dead yin; those of the dead-yin type die within three days." It is because the damage is visible in the body.

## Notes

In lung disease with cough and panting, the appearance of a scattered pulse (dispersed and rootless) is the five-phase adverse relation of heart fire restraining lung metal, a sign that bodily damage has reached its extreme, and the clause deems it fatal. It is the last paragraph of the "Distinguishing the Pulse" chapter.
