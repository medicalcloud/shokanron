# §17 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Question: In cold damage of three days, when the pulse is floating, rapid, and faint, and the patient's body is cool and calm, what does it mean? Answer: It is about to resolve; it resolves at midnight. When it resolves with a floating pulse, there is a drizzling sweat; when it resolves with a rapid pulse, one will certainly be able to eat; when it resolves with a faint pulse, there will certainly be great sweating.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Three days of cold damage is the time when yang leaves and enters yin. When the patient's body is hot and the pulse floating, rapid, and large, the evil qi is being transmitted. If the body is cool and calm and the pulse floating, rapid, and faint, the evil qi is not being transmitted and is about to resolve. It resolves at midnight because yang is born at zi. A floating pulse governing resolution with drizzling sweat means the evil dissipates from the outside. A rapid pulse governing resolution with the ability to eat means the stomach qi is harmonized. A faint pulse governing resolution with great sweating means the evil qi is slight.

## Notes

On the third day of cold damage, when the pulse loses force and the body temperature falls to calm, it is a sign that the disease will recover without passing to the three yin; the clause shows, by pulse quality, how recovery manifests.
