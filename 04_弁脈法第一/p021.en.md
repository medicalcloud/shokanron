# §21 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the cun kou pulse is floating, [the disease] is in the exterior; deep, in the interior; rapid, in the bowels; slow, in the zang organs. Suppose the pulse is slow—this means it is in the zang organs.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The classic says: "All yang [pulses], floating and rapid, assail the bowels; all yin [pulses], slow and hesitant, assail the zang organs."

## Notes

The clause shows the basics of pulse diagnosis: judging exterior and interior by floating and deep, and zang-fu organs by slow and rapid.
