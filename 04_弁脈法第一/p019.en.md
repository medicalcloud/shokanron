# §19 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The master said: To obtain a surging (one version writes "floating") large pulse at the beginning of summer is its proper position. When such a person falls ill with distressing pain and heaviness of the body, sweating must be induced; if the next day the body is no longer painful or heavy, sweating need not be induced; if sweat comes out drizzling by itself, it will resolve the next day. Why so? The surging large pulse at the beginning of summer is the seasonal pulse, which makes it so. The four seasons follow this.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

When the pulse comes in accordance with the season, the upright qi is secure within; even if evil qi is contracted from outside, it resolves merely with slight spontaneous sweating. The *Neijing* says: "When the pulse accords with the four seasons, the disease has no other [complication]."

## Notes

If the seasonal pulse (surging and large in summer, etc.) appears, the upright qi is preserved, so even if an external evil is contracted the illness is mild and cured by spontaneous sweating.
