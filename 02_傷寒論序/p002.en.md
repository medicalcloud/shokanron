# §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Respectfully submitted by your servants Gao Baoheng, Right Admonisher to the Heir Apparent; Sun Qi, Vice Director of the Bureau of Military Farms of the Department of State Affairs; Lin Yi, Director of the Bureau of Honors of the Department of State Affairs and Collator of the Imperial Archives; and others.

## Notes

The signatures of the Song officials' preface. Gao Baoheng, Sun Qi, and Lin Yi are known as those who collated many medical books in the Northern Song Bureau for Revising Medical Texts, including the *Suwen*, *Shanghan lun*, *Jingui Yaolüe*, *Qianjin fang*, and *Waitai miyao*.
