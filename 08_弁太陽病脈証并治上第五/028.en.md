# Clause 28 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When, after taking Guizhi tang or being purged, there are still a stiff and painful head and nape, feather-warm fever, no sweating, fullness and slight pain below the heart, and inhibited urination, Guizhi qu Gui jia Fuling Baizhu tang (Cinnamon Twig Decoction minus Cinnamon Twig plus Poria and White Atractylodes) governs it. Formula 15.

Shaoyao 3 liang; gancao 2 liang (mix-fried); shengjiang (sliced), baizhu (white atractylodes), and fuling (poria), 3 liang each; dazao 12 pieces (split).

Of the six ingredients above, boil with 8 sheng of water down to 3 sheng, remove the dregs, and take 1 sheng warm. When urination becomes free, [the patient] recovers. Originally called Guizhi tang; now guizhi is removed and fuling and baizhu are added.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

A stiff and painful head and nape and feather-warm fever mean that, despite sweating and purgation, the evil qi is still in the exterior. Fullness and slight pain below the heart with free urination would mean chest bind is about to form. Here the external signs have not yet ended, and with no sweating and inhibited urination, the fullness and slight pain below the heart are due to collected fluid. Guizhi tang is given to resolve the exterior, and fuling and baizhu are added to free urination and move the retained fluid.

## Notes

This clause uses Guizhi tang with guizhi removed and fuling and baizhu added for a patient in whom, after sweating or purgation, a stiff and painful head and nape, fever, and no sweating remain, along with fullness and slight pain below the heart and inhibited urination.

- Inhibited urination and fullness below the heart are attributed to collected water (water-rheum) in the body; the idea is that moving the water will also resolve the exterior. The phrase "when urination becomes free, [the patient] recovers" is regarded as the key point.
- "Removing guizhi" has long been debated; some hold that shaoyao, not guizhi, should be removed (Guizhi qu Shaoyao jia Fuling Baizhu tang), but the Song edition reads as here. Cheng Wuji speaks of adding fuling and baizhu to Guizhi tang and does not mention removing guizhi.
