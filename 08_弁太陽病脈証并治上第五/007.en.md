# Clause 7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When a disease has fever and aversion to cold, it arises in the yang; when there is no fever but aversion to cold, it arises in the yin. What arises in the yang is cured in seven days; what arises in the yin is cured in six days. This is because the number of yang is seven and the number of yin is six.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Yang is heat; yin is cold. Fever with aversion to cold means cold has damaged the yang. Aversion to cold without fever means cold has damaged the yin. Yang follows fire; yin follows water. The completion number of fire is seven, and that of water is six. A yang disease is cured in seven days because the number of fire is fulfilled; a yin disease is cured in six days because the number of water is fulfilled.

## Notes

A clause that broadly divides diseases into yin and yang by the presence or absence of fever.

- Fever with aversion to cold is generally taken to indicate disease of the yang (the three yang, especially taiyang), and aversion to cold without fever disease of the yin (the three yin). What exactly "yang" and "yin" refer to (taiyang and shaoyin, defensive and nutritive qi, etc.) is debated.
- "The number of yang is seven, the number of yin six" is explained by the numbers of the River Chart and Luo Writing (the completion number of fire is seven, of water six), which Cheng Wuji follows. It is generally held that these day counts are not to be applied literally in clinical practice.
