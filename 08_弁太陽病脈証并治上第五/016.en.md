# Clause 16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease of three days: when sweating has already been induced, or vomiting, or purgation, or warm needling [applied], and it still does not resolve, this is a spoiled disease (*huaibing*), and Guizhi [tang] is not suitable for it. Observe the pulse and signs, know what mistake has been committed, and treat according to the pattern. Guizhi [tang] is fundamentally for resolving the flesh. If the person's pulse is floating and tight, with fever and no sweating, it must not be given. Always keep this in mind and do not let mistakes be made. 5.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

When, within three days of taiyang disease, sweating, vomiting, purgation, and warm needling have already been applied, vacuating the upright qi, and the disease still does not resolve, it is called a spoiled disease—meaning a disease spoiled by the physician. Guizhi tang must not be given again. Observe the pulse and signs carefully, know what mistake has been committed, and treat it. "Mistake" (*ni*) means rescuing according to what has gone wrong.

A floating pulse, fever, sweating, and aversion to wind are wind-strike; Guizhi tang may be given to resolve the flesh. A floating, tight pulse with fever and no sweating is cold damage; Mahuang tang may be given. Always keep this in mind and do not treat recklessly.

## Notes

This clause states two important principles.

- Spoiled disease: when repeated mistaken treatments have broken down the original taiyang pattern, it is called a "spoiled disease." In that case one does not rely on a fixed formula but follows "observe the pulse and signs, know what mistake has been committed, and treat according to the pattern." This phrase is very often cited as expressing the principle of pattern differentiation and treatment in the *Shanghan lun*.
- Contraindication of Guizhi tang: Guizhi tang is a formula for "resolving the flesh" (releasing evil from the flesh and exterior) and must not be used for cold damage with a floating, tight pulse, fever, and no sweating (the Mahuang tang pattern).
