# Clause 25 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When, after taking Guizhi tang, there is profuse sweating and the pulse is surging and large, give Guizhi tang as in the method above. If the condition is like malaria, with attacks twice a day, it will certainly resolve once sweat comes out; Guizhi er Mahuang yi tang (Two Parts Cinnamon Twig and One Part Ephedra Decoction) is appropriate. Formula 12.

Guizhi 1 liang 17 zhu (remove the bark); shaoyao 1 liang 6 zhu; mahuang 16 zhu (remove the nodes); shengjiang 1 liang 6 zhu (sliced); xingren 16 kernels (remove the skins and tips); gancao 1 liang 2 zhu (mix-fried); dazao 5 pieces (split).

Of the seven ingredients above, with 5 sheng of water first boil the mahuang once or twice, remove the froth on top, add the other ingredients, boil down to 2 sheng, remove the dregs, take 1 sheng warm, twice a day. Originally it said: combine two parts of Guizhi tang and one part of Mahuang tang to make 2 sheng, and take in two doses. Now they are combined into one formula; care as in the method above. Your servants [Lin] Yi and others respectfully note: the Guizhi tang formula is guizhi, shaoyao, and shengjiang 3 liang each, gancao 2 liang, and dazao 12 pieces; the Mahuang tang formula is mahuang 3 liang, guizhi 2 liang, gancao 1 liang, and xingren 70 kernels. Calculating now, taking five twelfths of Guizhi tang gives guizhi, shaoyao, and shengjiang 1 liang 6 zhu each, gancao 20 zhu, and dazao 5 pieces. Taking two ninths of Mahuang tang gives mahuang 16 zhu, guizhi 10 and two-thirds zhu, rounded to 11 zhu, gancao 5 and one-third zhu, rounded to 6 zhu, and xingren 15 and four-ninths kernels, rounded to 16 kernels. Combining what is taken from the two decoctions gives guizhi 1 liang 17 zhu, mahuang 16 zhu, shengjiang and shaoyao 1 liang 6 zhu each, gancao 1 liang 2 zhu, dazao 5 pieces, and xingren 16 kernels, matching the formula.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The Classic says: "If the disease signs are still present after taking one preparation, the original decoction should be made again and taken." When, after sweating from Guizhi tang, the pulse is surging and large, the disease is still present. If the condition is like malaria, with attacks twice a day, the evil qi is lodged between the nutritive and defensive. Guizhi er Mahuang yi tang is given to resolve and disperse the evil between the nutritive and defensive.

## Notes

This clause describes two courses after taking Guizhi tang.

- Even with profuse sweating and a surging, large pulse, if there are no other signs of interior heat (such as great vexation and thirst, the pattern of Baihu jia Renshen tang in Clause 26), it may still be treated as an exterior pattern and Guizhi tang continued.
- When malaria-like chills and fever occur twice a day, a slight exterior evil is taken to remain, and Guizhi er Mahuang yi tang—Guizhi tang and Mahuang tang combined in a 2:1 ratio—is used to induce a very light sweat. Its sweating power is considered even weaker than that of Guizhi Mahuang Geban tang in Clause 23.
