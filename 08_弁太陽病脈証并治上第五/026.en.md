# Clause 26 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When, after taking Guizhi tang and sweating profusely, great vexation and thirst do not resolve and the pulse is surging and large, Baihu jia Renshen tang (White Tiger Decoction plus Ginseng) governs it. Formula 13.

Zhimu (anemarrhena) 6 liang; shigao (gypsum) 1 jin (crushed, wrapped in cotton); gancao (mix-fried) 2 liang; jingmi (non-glutinous rice) 6 ge; renshen (ginseng) 3 liang.

Of the five ingredients above, with 1 dou of water boil until the rice is cooked and the decoction is done, remove the dregs, take 1 sheng warm, three times a day.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

When there is profuse sweating and a surging, large pulse but no thirst, the evil qi is still in the exterior, and Guizhi tang may be given again. If there is profuse sweating with a surging, large pulse and vexation and thirst that do not resolve, there is heat in both exterior and interior, and Guizhi tang must not be given again. Baihu jia Renshen tang is given to engender fluids and stop thirst, harmonize the exterior and disperse the heat.

## Notes

This clause pairs with Clause 25. After profuse sweating from Guizhi tang, if the pulse is equally surging and large but intense thirst (great vexation and thirst) is added, the evil is taken to have entered the interior as yangming heat while the sweating has also damaged the fluids, and Baihu jia Renshen tang is used.

- Baihu tang (zhimu, shigao, gancao, jingmi) clears the great heat of the yangming; renshen is added to supplement the damaged qi and fluids.
- The presence or absence of thirst decides whether to continue Guizhi tang or switch to Baihu jia Renshen tang.
