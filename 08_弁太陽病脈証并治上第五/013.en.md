# Clause 13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease with headache, fever, sweating, and aversion to wind: Guizhi tang governs it. Formula 2. Use the first formula above.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Headache is [a sign of] Taiyang. Fever, sweating, and aversion to wind are wind attack. Guizhi tang is given to resolve and disperse the pathogenic wind.

## Notes

A concise statement of the indications for Guizhi tang. Whereas Clause 12 describes the pulse and signs in detail, this clause lists only four signs: headache, fever, sweating, and aversion to wind. Because it says "Taiyang disease" rather than "wind attack," some read it as meaning that Guizhi tang may be used whenever these four signs are present, not only in wind attack.
