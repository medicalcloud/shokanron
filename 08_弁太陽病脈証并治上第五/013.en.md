# Clause 13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease with headache, fever, sweating, and aversion to wind: Guizhi tang governs it. Formula 2. Use the first formula above.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Headache is [a sign of] taiyang. Fever, sweating, and aversion to wind are wind-strike. Guizhi tang is given to resolve and disperse the wind evil.

## Notes

A concise statement of the indications for Guizhi tang. Whereas Clause 12 describes the pulse and signs in detail, this clause lists only four signs: headache, fever, sweating, and aversion to wind. Because it says "taiyang disease" rather than "wind-strike," some read it as meaning that Guizhi tang may be used whenever these four signs are present, not only in wind-strike.
