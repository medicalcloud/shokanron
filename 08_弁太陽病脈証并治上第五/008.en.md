# Clause 8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In taiyang disease, when a headache of seven days or more cures itself, it is because the course through its channel has been completed. If [the disease] is about to make a second course through the channels, needle the foot yangming so that it is not transmitted along the channels, and [the patient] will recover.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Cold damage is transmitted through all the channels of the three yang and three yin from the first to the sixth day, and should be cured on the seventh day. The Classic says: "On the seventh day the taiyang disease declines and the headache is slightly relieved." If it is not cured on the seventh day, the taiyang evil is transmitted again to the yangming. Needling the foot yangming meets the evil and seizes it, so that it is not transmitted along the channels, and [the patient] recovers.

## Notes

This clause describes spontaneous recovery from taiyang disease and what to do when it does not recover and is about to be transmitted again.

- "The course through its channel has been completed": read either as the end of the period during which the evil stays in the taiyang channel, or as one complete round through the six channels; opinions differ.
- "Needle the foot yangming": needling a point of the foot yangming stomach channel (often said to be Zusanli, ST-36) to prevent the evil from being transmitted to the yangming. It is cited as an example of heading off the disease where it is about to go.
