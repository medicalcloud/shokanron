# Clause 30 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Question: The pattern resembled *yangdan* [Yangdan tang], so it was treated according to the method, but it became worse, with reversal counterflow, dryness in the throat, hypertonicity of both shins, and delirious speech. The master said that at midnight the hands and feet would become warm and both legs would stretch, and later it was as the master said. How was this known? Answer: The cunkou pulse was floating and large. Floating means wind; large means vacuity. Wind produces slight heat; vacuity makes both shins hypertonic. The form of the disease resembled Guizhi [tang], so fuzi was added and mixed in, and guizhi was increased to make sweat come out. Fuzi warms the channels; it was because of loss of yang. For reversal counterflow, dryness in the throat, vexation and agitation, internal bind of the yangming, and delirious speech with vexation and derangement, Gancao Ganjiang tang was given to drink again; at midnight the yang qi returned, and both feet should become hot. The shins were still slightly hypertonic, so Shaoyao Gancao tang was given again, and then the shins stretched. With Chengqi tang the stool became slightly loose, and then the delirious speech stopped. Therefore it was known that the disease could be cured.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

*Yangdan* is another name for Guizhi tang. In the preceding pattern [Clause 29], a floating pulse, spontaneous sweating, frequent urination, vexation of the heart, slight aversion to cold, and hypertonicity of the legs resemble the Guizhi tang pattern; this is "the pattern resembled *yangdan*." When Guizhi tang was given, it became worse, and the cunkou pulse became floating and large. Floating is wind evil; large is blood vacuity. So fuzi was added to Guizhi tang to warm the channels and supplement the vacuity, and guizhi was increased to bring out sweat and expel the wind. For one whose reversal increased through this mistaken treatment, Gancao Ganjiang tang is given; the yang is restored and the feet become warm. Then Shaoyao Gancao tang is given; the yin is harmonized and the shins stretch. When the exterior evil has already resolved and yin and yang have been restored, but there is internal bind of the yangming with delirious speech and vexation, a little Tiaowei Chengqi tang is given to cause slight loose stool and harmonize the stomach; then the qi of yin and yang are all harmonized and the evils of the interior and exterior are entirely removed. Therefore it is known that the disease can be cured.

## Notes

This clause re-explains the course of Clause 29 in question-and-answer form.

- *Yangdan* is generally taken as another name for Guizhi tang (as Cheng Wuji does); some hold that Yangdan tang is Guizhi tang plus huangqin (scutellaria).
- The text has many obscure passages, and some think it was added later as a commentary on Clause 29.
- The main point is the same as in Clause 29: for yang vacuity from mistreatment, first restore the yang with Gancao Ganjiang tang, then regulate the yin with Shaoyao Gancao tang, and for delirious speech from stomach dryness-heat, use a small amount of Chengqi tang.
