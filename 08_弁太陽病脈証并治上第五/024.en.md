# Clause 24 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease: when, on first taking Guizhi tang, there is on the contrary vexation and [the disease] does not resolve, first needle Fengchi and Fengfu, then give Guizhi tang again, and [the patient] will recover. 11.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Vexation is heat. After taking Guizhi tang there should be sweating, and the body should become cool and calm. If instead there is vexation and no resolution, the wind is severe and cannot yet be dispersed. First needle Fengchi and Fengfu to free the taiyang channel and discharge the wind qi, then give Guizhi tang to resolve and disperse it, and [the patient] will recover.

## Notes

This clause combines acupuncture and medicine when Guizhi tang has instead caused vexation without resolution.

- It is taken not as a wrong prescription but as strong evil blocking the channel: first Fengchi (GB-20, foot shaoyang gallbladder channel) and Fengfu (GV-16, governor vessel) are needled to free the channel qi, and then Guizhi tang is given again.
- It is often cited as an example of combining acupuncture and medicine in the *Shanghan lun*.
