# Clause 5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When, after two or three days of cold damage, the patterns of yangming and shaoyang have not appeared, there is no transmission.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

If after two or three days of cold damage there are no yangming or shaoyang patterns, one knows that the evil has not been transmitted and remains within the taiyang channel.

## Notes

Like Clause 4, this clause states that transmission is judged by the patterns, not by the number of days. Even if, by the day count, it is time for transmission to the yangming or shaoyang, the disease is still treated as taiyang disease if their patterns (for yangming, generalized heat, sweating, thirst, etc.; for shaoyang, bitter taste, alternating chills and fever, etc.) are absent.
