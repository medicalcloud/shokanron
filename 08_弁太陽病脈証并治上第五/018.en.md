# Clause 18 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

For a person with dyspnea (*chuanjia*), make Guizhi tang (Keishito) and it is good to add houpo (koboku; magnolia bark) and xingzi (apricot kernel). 6.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Taiyang disease governs the qi of all the yang. When wind is severe and the qi is blocked, dyspnea arises. Guizhi tang is given to disperse the wind, and houpo and xingren (kyonin) are added to lower the qi.

## Notes

When a person with a chronic tendency to dyspnea (asthma-like breathing) contracts Taiyang wind attack, houpo and xingren are added to Guizhi tang (Guizhi jia Houpo Xingzi tang (Keishikakobokukyoshito)).

- *Chuanjia* is generally taken to mean a person with a chronic dyspnea disorder.
- The formula is regarded as the same as Guizhi jia Houpo Xingzi tang in Clause 43.
