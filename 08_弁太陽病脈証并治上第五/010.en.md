# Clause 10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When, for a person with wind (*fengjia*), the exterior has resolved but [the patient] does not feel clear, he will recover in twelve days.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

When a person with wind-strike, after resolution by sweating, has not yet fully recovered, in twelve days the major evil is completely gone, the six channels are all harmonized, and [the patient] recovers.

## Notes

This clause concerns the convalescent period after the exterior pattern of wind-strike has resolved, when mild discomfort remains. It is often understood to mean that once the exterior has resolved, the remaining discomfort will clear on its own with time, and no further medicine is needed.

- *Fengjia*: generally taken to mean a patient with wind-strike (some take it as a person who habitually catches wind).
- "Does not feel clear" (*bu liaoliao*): not feeling clear and refreshed.
- "Twelve days": one explanation is that it is the number of days for two rounds through the six channels.
