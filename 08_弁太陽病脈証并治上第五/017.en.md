# Clause 17 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

If a habitual drinker (*jiuke*) is ill, Guizhi tang must not be given; if he takes it he will vomit, because habitual drinkers do not like sweet things.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Habitual drinkers have internal heat; they like acrid things and dislike sweet things. Guizhi tang is sweet, so when a drinker takes it, there is fullness in the middle and vomiting.

## Notes

One of the contraindications of Guizhi tang. It is generally explained that habitual drinkers have damp-heat within, and the sweet Guizhi tang (containing licorice and jujube) aggravates the damp-heat and tends to cause vomiting. It is widely read as a caution not only for drinkers but for anyone with internal damp-heat.
