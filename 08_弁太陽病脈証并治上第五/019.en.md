# Clause 19 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Whenever someone vomits after taking Guizhi tang, he will later certainly vomit pus and blood.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

When someone with internal heat takes Guizhi tang, he vomits, like the habitual drinker. Having already lost fluids, and being further assailed by heat, he will later certainly vomit pus and blood. Vomiting pus and blood is called lung wilting. The *Jingui yaolüe* says: "Heat in the upper burner causes lung wilting," meaning that it is contracted when the fluids are repeatedly lost through sweating or vomiting.

## Notes

This clause warns that if the acrid-warm Guizhi tang is given to someone with internal heat, the heat will intensify and later lead to vomiting of pus and blood. Following Clause 17 (habitual drinkers), it cautions against using Guizhi tang for those with internal heat. Although it says "certainly," it is often read as an emphatic expression of the danger of misuse.
