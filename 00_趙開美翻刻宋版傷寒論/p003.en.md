# §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In Northern Song official printings, classics and their commentaries were all in large characters, while stand-alone subcommentaries were all in small characters, so as to distinguish the honored from the humble. The Zhiping official edition of the *Shanghan lun* is thus a large-character classic, while the *Qianjin fang* and *Waitai miyao* are both small-character subcommentaries. Lin Yi and the others were profound in medicine; how could anyone after the Southern Song have matched them?

## Notes

A second colophon by the same owner. It states that Northern Song official printings distinguished rank by cutting classics in large characters and commentary-like works in small characters, so that the *Shanghan lun* issued by the Bureau for Revising Medical Texts in the Zhiping era (1064–1067) was treated as a large-character classic, and it praises the insight of Lin Yi and the other editors.
