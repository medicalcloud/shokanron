# §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Recorded by Fang. Date: mid-autumn day of the wushen year, a wuchen day.

## Notes

The signature and date of the preceding colophon. "Wushen" is thought to correspond to 1908 in the late Qing (on the view that the owner was Xu Fang).
