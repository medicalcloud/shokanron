# §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

There is no good edition of the *Shanghan lun* in the world. Apart from the traced copy of the large-character official Zhiping edition in my collection, there is only this Zhao Qingchang edition. My late friend, Chancellor Boxi of the imperial clan, once offered a great sum to buy this edition but could not obtain it, and obtained only the Japanese Ansei reprint. (Now there is also a printed edition in Shu, which likewise derives from the Japanese edition.) This summer I obtained this book from Wei Zimin, a bookseller of the [Liuli]chang; it is complete with nothing missing. Alas that Boxi did not live to see it.

## Notes

A colophon written by a late-Qing owner in one copy of the Zhao Kaimei edition (the reprint of the Song-edition *Shanghan lun* in the *Zhongjing quanshu*), the base text of this work. There is no Cheng Wuji commentary.

- The signatures "Fang" and "Ju'an" are said to refer to the late-Qing book collector Xu Fang.
- "Chancellor Boxi of the imperial clan" is thought to refer to Shengyu (courtesy name Boxi), a Qing imperial clansman who served as Chancellor of the Imperial Academy.
- The "Japanese Ansei reprint" refers to the edition reprinted in Japan from the Zhao Kaimei edition in the Ansei era at the end of the Edo period. It suggests that Song-lineage *Shanghan lun* texts had nearly vanished in China at the time and the Japanese reprint was being reimported.
- "Chang bookseller" is understood as a book merchant of Liulichang in Beijing.
