# §44 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cold damage of six or seven days with fever, slight aversion to cold, vexing pain of the limb joints, slight retching, propping bind below the heart, and the external pattern not yet gone: Chaihu Guizhi tang governs it.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 146 of the lower taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
