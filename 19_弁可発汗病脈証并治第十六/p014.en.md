# §14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When taiyang disease does not resolve and heat binds in the bladder, the person is as if manic; if blood descends spontaneously, one in whom it descends recovers. When the external has not yet resolved, it still must not be attacked; the external should first be resolved; it belongs to Guizhi tang.

## Notes

The same content as the first half of Clause 106 of the middle taiyang chapter (the Taohe Chengqi tang clause). Cheng Wuji's commentary is found there.
