# §41 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cold damage with water qi below the heart, cough with slight panting, fever without thirst: when after taking the decoction there is thirst, the cold has departed and it is about to resolve. It belongs to Xiao Qinglong tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 41 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
