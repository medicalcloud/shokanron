# §16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cold damage with a floating tight pulse, not sweated, leading to nosebleed: it belongs to Mahuang tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 55 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
