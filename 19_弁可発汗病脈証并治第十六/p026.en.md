# §26 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease, after purgation, when the qi surges upward: it belongs to Guizhi tang.

## Notes

The same content as the first half of Clause 15 of the upper taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
