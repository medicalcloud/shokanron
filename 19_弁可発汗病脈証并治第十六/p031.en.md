# §31 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Combined disease of taiyang and yangming will certainly have spontaneous diarrhea; when there is no retching, it belongs to Gegen tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 32 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
