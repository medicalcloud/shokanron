# §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

As a general rule, sweating is appropriate in spring and summer.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

In spring and summer yang qi is outside, and the evil qi is also outside, so sweating may be induced.

## Notes

The general rule at the beginning of the chapter "Distinguishing Pulse, Signs, and Treatment of Diseases That May Be Sweated." In accordance with where seasonal yang qi resides, sweating is said to suit spring and summer. The later "may vomit" and "may purge" chapters have similar general rules (vomiting in spring, purgation in autumn).
