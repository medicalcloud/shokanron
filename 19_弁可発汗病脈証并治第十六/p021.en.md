# §21 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Diarrhea with abdominal distension and fullness and generalized body pain: first warm the interior, then attack the exterior. To warm the interior, Sini tang is appropriate; to attack the exterior, Guizhi tang is appropriate.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 372 of the jueyin chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
