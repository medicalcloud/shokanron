# §33 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease with the Guizhi (keishi) pattern, which the physician, on the contrary, purged, so that the diarrhea did not stop: when the pulse is skipping, the exterior has not yet resolved; with dyspnea and sweating, Gegen Huangqin Huanglian tang (Kakkonogonorento) is appropriate. ("Skipping" is written "loose" in one version.)

## Notes

The same content as Clause 34 of the middle Taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
