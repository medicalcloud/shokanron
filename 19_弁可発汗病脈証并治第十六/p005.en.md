# §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease with the external pattern not yet resolved and a floating weak pulse should be resolved by sweating; Guizhi tang is appropriate.

## Notes

The same text as Clause 42 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found. This chapter regathers the clauses on sweating from the three yin and three yang chapters.
