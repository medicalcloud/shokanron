# §25 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease with fever and sweating: this is weak ying-nutrient and strong wei-defense, which causes sweating. To rescue from the pathogenic wind, it belongs to Guizhi tang (Keishito).

## Notes

The same content as Clause 95 of the middle Taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
