# §25 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease with fever and sweating: this is weak nutritive and strong defensive, which causes sweating. To rescue from the evil wind, it belongs to Guizhi tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 95 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
