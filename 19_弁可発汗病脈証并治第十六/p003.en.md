# §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In general, when taking a decoction to induce sweating, stop as soon as it hits the disease; it is not necessary to finish the preparation.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

Profuse sweat leads to yang collapse.

## Notes

The clause states the principle that a sweating formula should not be taken further once it has taken effect.
