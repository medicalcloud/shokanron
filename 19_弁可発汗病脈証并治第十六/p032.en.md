# §32 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Combined disease of Taiyang and Yangming with no diarrhea but only retching: Gegen jia Banxia tang (Kakkonkahangeto) is appropriate.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 33 of the middle Taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
