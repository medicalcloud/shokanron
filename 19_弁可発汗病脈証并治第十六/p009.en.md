# §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In cold damage, when the pulse is not wiry and tight but weak, one with a weak pulse will certainly be thirsty; if subjected to fire, there will certainly be delirious speech. When one with a weak pulse has fever and the pulse is floating, to resolve it, sweating will cure it.

## Notes

Nearly the same content as Clause 113 of the middle taiyang chapter (Clause 113 reads "in the form of cold damage, the pulse is not wiry and tight but weak"). Cheng Wuji's commentary is found there.
