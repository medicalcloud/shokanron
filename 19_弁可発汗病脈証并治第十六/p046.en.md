# §46 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the pulse is floating, urination inhibited, and there are slight heat and dispersion-thirst, give Wuling san to promote urination and induce sweating.

## Notes

The same purport as the second half of Clause 71 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found. It is the last paragraph of the chapter "Distinguishing Pulse, Signs, and Treatment of Diseases That May Be Sweated."
