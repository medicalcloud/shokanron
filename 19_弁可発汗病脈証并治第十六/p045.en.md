# §45 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyin disease, two or three days after contracting it: induce slight sweating with Mahuang Fuzi Gancao tang. Because at two or three days there are no [interior] signs, slight sweating is induced.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 302 of the shaoyin chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
