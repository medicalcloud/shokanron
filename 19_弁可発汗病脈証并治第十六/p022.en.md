# §22 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When after diarrhea there is body pain and the stool has spontaneously become regular, one should urgently rescue the exterior; it is appropriate to induce sweating with Guizhi tang.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The *Waitai* says: "When the interior is harmonious and the exterior diseased, sweating cures it."

## Notes

A clause with the same purport as the second half of Clause 91 of the middle taiyang chapter. When after the diarrhea has healed and the interior is harmonious an exterior pattern remains, the exterior is resolved with Guizhi tang.
