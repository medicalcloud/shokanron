# §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yangming disease with a slow pulse, profuse sweating, and slight aversion to cold: the exterior has not yet resolved; sweating may be induced; it belongs to Guizhi tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 234 of the yangming chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
