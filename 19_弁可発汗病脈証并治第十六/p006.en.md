# §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the pulse is floating and rapid, sweating may be induced; it belongs to Guizhi tang (Keishito). (One method uses Mahuang tang [Maoto].)

## Notes

Corresponds to Clause 52 of the middle Taiyang chapter ("when the pulse is floating and rapid, sweating may be induced; Mahuang tang is appropriate"). This clause gives Guizhi tang.
