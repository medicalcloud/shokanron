# §39 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cold damage with a floating moderate pulse, no body pain but only heaviness that at times becomes lighter, and no shaoyin pattern: Da Qinglong tang may be given to effuse it.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 39 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
