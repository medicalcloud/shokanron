# §23 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease with headache, fever, sweating, and aversion to wind and cold belongs to Guizhi tang.

## Notes

Nearly the same text as Clause 13 of the upper taiyang chapter (Clause 13 reads "aversion to wind"). Cheng Wuji's commentary is found there.
