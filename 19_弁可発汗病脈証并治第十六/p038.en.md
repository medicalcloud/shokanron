# §38 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease after ten days or more with a floating fine pulse and somnolence: the external has already resolved. If there are chest fullness and rib-side pain, give Xiao Chaihu tang; if the pulse is only floating, give Mahuang tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 37 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
