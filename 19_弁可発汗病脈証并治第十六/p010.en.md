# §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the patient has vexing heat that resolves as soon as there is sweating, but again is malaria-like with fever around late afternoon, it belongs to the yangming. When the pulse is floating and vacuous, sweating should be induced; it belongs to Guizhi tang.

## Notes

The same content as part of Clause 240 of the yangming chapter. Cheng Wuji's commentary is found there.
