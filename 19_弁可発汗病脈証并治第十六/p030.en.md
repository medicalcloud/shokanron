# §30 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease with stiff and rigid nape and back, no sweating, and aversion to wind belongs to Gegen tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 31 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
