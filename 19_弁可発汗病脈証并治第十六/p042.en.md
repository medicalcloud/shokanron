# §42 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Wind strike with alternating chills and fever, or cold damage after five or six days, with distressing fullness of the chest and rib-sides, taciturnity with no desire for food or drink, heart vexation and frequent retching, or vexation in the chest without retching, or thirst, or abdominal pain, or glomus and hardness below the rib-sides, or palpitations below the heart with inhibited urination, or no thirst with slight body heat, or cough: it belongs to Xiao Chaihu tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 96 of the middle taiyang chapter (with minor differences in wording). Cheng Wuji's commentary is found there.
