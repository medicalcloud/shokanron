# §40 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cold damage with the exterior unresolved and water qi below the heart, with dry retching, fever and cough, and perhaps thirst, or diarrhea, or choking, or inhibited urination with lower-abdominal fullness, or panting: Xiao Qinglong tang is appropriate.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 40 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
