# §28 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When a burning needle is used to induce sweating and the needled spot is exposed to cold, with lumps rising and turning red, there will certainly be running piglet. When qi rises from the lower abdomen to strike the heart, moxa one cone on top of each lump and give Guizhi jia Gui tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 117 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
