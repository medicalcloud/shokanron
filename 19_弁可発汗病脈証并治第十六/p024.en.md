# §24 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang wind strike: yang floating and yin weak. Yang floating means heat arises spontaneously; yin weak means sweat comes out spontaneously. With huddled aversion to cold, shivering aversion to wind, feathery fever, noisy nose, and dry retching, it belongs to Guizhi tang.

## Notes

The same text as the first half of Clause 12 of the upper taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
