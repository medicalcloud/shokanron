# §15 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease that after purgation has slight panting: the exterior has not yet resolved; Guizhi jia Houpo Xingzi tang is appropriate.

## Notes

The same text as Clause 43 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
