# §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the patient's viscera have no other disease, but there are periodic fever and spontaneous sweating that do not recover, the defensive qi is not harmonious; induce sweating before the time [of the fever] and there will be recovery. It belongs to Guizhi tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 54 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
