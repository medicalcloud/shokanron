# §18 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease with a floating tight pulse, no sweating, fever, and body aches, unresolved after eight or nine days, with the exterior pattern still present: sweating should be induced again. After taking the decoction, it is slightly relieved, but the person becomes vexed and the eyes are dim; in severe cases there will certainly be nosebleed, and with the nosebleed it resolves. The reason is that the yang qi is heavy. It belongs to Mahuang tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 46 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
