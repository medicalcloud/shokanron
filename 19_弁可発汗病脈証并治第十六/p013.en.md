# §13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The pulse is floating and tight. Floating means wind; tight means cold. Wind damages the defensive; cold damages the nutritive. When both the nutritive and the defensive are diseased, there is vexing pain in the bones and joints; sweating may be induced; Mahuang tang is appropriate.

## Notes

Nearly the same text as paragraph 23 of the "Distinguishing the Pulse" chapter; here the use of Mahuang tang is made explicit. Cheng Wuji's commentary is in the "Distinguishing the Pulse" chapter.
