# §37 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yangming wind strike with a wiry, floating, large pulse, shortness of breath, fullness of the whole abdomen, pain below the rib-sides and in the heart, qi not passing even with prolonged pressure, dry nose, inability to sweat, somnolence, yellowing of the whole body and eyes, difficult urination, tidal fever, frequent hiccup, and swelling before and behind the ears: needling brings slight improvement, but the external does not resolve; after ten days, if the pulse remains floating, give Xiao Chaihu tang; if the pulse is only floating without other signs, give Mahuang tang; with no urination, abdominal fullness, and added hiccup, it is untreatable.

## Notes

The same content as Clauses 231 and 232 of the yangming chapter combined. Cheng Wuji's commentary is found there.
