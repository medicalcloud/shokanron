# §19 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the pulse is floating, the disease is in the exterior, and sweating may be induced; it belongs to Mahuang tang (Maoto). (One method uses Guizhi tang (Keishito).)

## Notes

The same content as Clause 51 of the middle Taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
