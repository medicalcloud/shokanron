# §34 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease with headache, fever, body aches, lumbar pain, joint pain, aversion to wind, and dyspnea without sweating belongs to Mahuang tang (Maoto).

## Notes

The same content as Clause 35 of the middle Taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
