# §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When in disease there is constant spontaneous sweating, the nutritive qi is harmonious; when the nutritive qi is harmonious but the outside is not in accord, it is because the defensive qi is not in harmony with the nutritive qi. Since the nutritive moves within the vessels and the defensive outside the vessels, induce sweating again; when the nutritive and defensive are harmonized, there is recovery. It belongs to Guizhi tang.

## Notes

Nearly the same text as Clause 53 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
