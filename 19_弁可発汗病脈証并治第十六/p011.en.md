# §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When in disease there is constant spontaneous sweating, the ying-nutrient qi is harmonious; when the ying-nutrient qi is harmonious but the outside is not in accord, it is because the wei-defensive qi is not in harmony with the ying-nutrient qi. Since the ying-nutrient moves within the vessels and the wei-defense outside the vessels, induce sweating again; when the ying-nutrient and wei-defense are harmonized, there is recovery. It belongs to Guizhi tang.

## Notes

Nearly the same text as Clause 53 of the middle Taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
