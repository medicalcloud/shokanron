# §43 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Cold damage of four or five days with body heat and aversion to wind, stiff neck and nape, fullness below the rib-sides, and warm hands and feet with thirst belongs to Xiao Chaihu tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 99 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
