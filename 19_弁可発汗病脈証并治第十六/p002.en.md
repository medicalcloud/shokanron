# §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In general, when inducing sweating, one should make it reach the hands and feet all over, coming out moistly from time to time for about one period; this is best. Do not let it flow and drip like water. If the disease does not resolve, sweating should be induced again. One with profuse sweat will certainly suffer yang collapse; when yang is vacuous, sweating must not be induced again.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

When sweat comes out gently, the evils of exterior and interior are all removed; when sweat comes out profusely, the evil qi is not removed and there is only yang collapse. Yang vacuity means there are no fluids, so sweating must not be induced again.

## Notes

As a basic rule of sweating, the clause states that a gentle, moist sweat over the whole body is best, and profuse sweating damages yang qi. It has the same purport as the administration of Guizhi tang in the upper taiyang chapter (Clause 12): "a moist, faint sweat over the whole body is best; do not let it flow and drip like water."
