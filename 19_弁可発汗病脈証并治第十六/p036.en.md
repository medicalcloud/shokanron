# §36 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang wind attack with a floating tight pulse, fever, aversion to cold, body aches, and restlessness without sweating: Da Qinglong tang governs it. If the pulse is feeble and weak with sweating and aversion to wind, it must not be taken; taking it causes cold limbs and twitching of sinews and jerking of flesh; this is an adverse treatment.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 38 of the middle Taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
