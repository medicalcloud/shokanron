# §36 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang wind strike with a floating tight pulse, fever, aversion to cold, body aches, and vexation and agitation without sweating: Da Qinglong tang governs it. If the pulse is faint and weak with sweating and aversion to wind, it must not be taken; taking it causes reversal counterflow and twitching of sinews and jerking of flesh; this is an adverse treatment.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 38 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
