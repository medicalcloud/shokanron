# §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In general, when it is said that sweating may be induced and there is no decoction, pills or powders may also be used; the essential is that sweating brings resolution. However, they are not as good as a decoction, which follows the signs and is effective.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

The *Shengji jing* says: "Decoctions are fundamentally for treating congestion and depression in the interstices; for removing evil qi, decoctions are suitable." The *Jingui Yuhan* says: "Water can cleanse all things; hence decoctions are used."

## Notes

The clause states that decoctions are best for sweating, but pills and powders may be used if unavoidable. It shows the view on differences in effect by dosage form.
