# §35 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Combined disease of taiyang and yangming with panting and chest fullness must not be purged; it belongs to Mahuang tang.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 36 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
