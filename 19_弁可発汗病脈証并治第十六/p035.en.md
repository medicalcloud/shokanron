# §35 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Combined disease of Taiyang and Yangming with dyspnea and chest fullness must not be purged; it belongs to Mahuang tang (Maoto).

## Notes

The same content as Clause 36 of the middle Taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
