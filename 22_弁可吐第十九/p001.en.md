# §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

As a general rule, vomiting is appropriate in spring.

## Translation of Cheng Wuji's commentary

In spring yang qi is above, and the evil qi is also above, so vomiting is appropriate.

## Notes

The general rule at the beginning of the chapter "May Induce Vomiting." In accordance with where seasonal yang qi resides, vomiting is said to suit spring.
