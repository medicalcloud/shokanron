# §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyin disease in which food and drink are vomited as soon as they enter the mouth, with queasiness in the heart and a desire to vomit, yet one cannot vomit: it is appropriate to induce vomiting.

## Notes

Corresponds to the first half of Clause 324 of the shaoyin chapter (the case of repletion in the chest). Cheng Wuji's commentary is found there.
