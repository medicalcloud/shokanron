# §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the disease resembles the Guizhi (keishi) pattern but the head does not ache, the nape is not stiff, the cun pulse is slightly floating, there is stuffiness and hardness in the chest, and qi rises to strike the throat so that one cannot breathe, there is cold, and it should be treated by vomiting. (One version says: "This is because there is long-standing phlegm within; it is appropriate to induce vomiting.")

## Notes

Nearly the same content as Clause 166 of the lower Taiyang chapter (the Guadi san (Kateisan) clause). Cheng Wuji's commentary is found there.
