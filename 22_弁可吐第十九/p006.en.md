# §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When abiding food is in the upper stomach duct, it should be treated by vomiting.

## Notes

A short clause stating that food stagnating in the upper part of the stomach should be removed by vomiting. It has the same purport as the *Yuhan* passage cited in Cheng Wuji's commentary two paragraphs earlier. "上管" is the same as "上脘."
