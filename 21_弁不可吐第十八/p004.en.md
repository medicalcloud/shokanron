# §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

All cases of counterflow of the four limbs and reversal must not be treated by vomiting; the same applies to those of vacuous constitution.

## Notes

Corresponds to Clause 330 of the jueyin chapter ("must not be purged"); here it takes the form of prohibiting vomiting. It is the last paragraph of the chapter "Must Not Induce Vomiting."
