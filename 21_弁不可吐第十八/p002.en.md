# §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease treated by vomiting: taiyang disease should have aversion to cold, but now, on the contrary, there is no aversion to cold and one does not want clothes near; this is internal vexation from vomiting.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 121 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found.
