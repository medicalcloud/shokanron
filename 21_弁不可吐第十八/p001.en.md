# §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Taiyang disease should have aversion to cold and fever. Now there is spontaneous sweating and, on the contrary, no aversion to cold or fever, and the guan pulse is fine and rapid; this is the fault of the physician's inducing vomiting. If vomiting was induced one or two days after contracting the disease, the abdomen is hungry but the mouth cannot eat; if vomiting was induced on the third or fourth day, one does not like gruel, wants cold food, and vomits in the evening what was eaten in the morning. This is caused by the physician's inducing vomiting; it is a minor adverse treatment.

## Notes

The same content as Clause 120 of the middle taiyang chapter, where Cheng Wuji's commentary is found. The chapter "Must Not Induce Vomiting" is a short chapter collecting clauses on the contraindications of the vomiting method.
