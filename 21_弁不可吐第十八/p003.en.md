# §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyin disease: when food and drink enter the mouth they are vomited; there is queasiness in the heart with a desire to vomit, yet one cannot vomit. When just contracted, with cold hands and feet and a wiry slow pulse, this is repletion in the chest and must not be purged. If there is cold rheum above the diaphragm with dry retching, vomiting must not be induced; it should be warmed.

## Notes

Nearly the same content as Clause 324 of the shaoyin chapter (lacking that clause's "should be treated by vomiting" and "Sini tang is appropriate"). Cheng Wuji's commentary is found there.
